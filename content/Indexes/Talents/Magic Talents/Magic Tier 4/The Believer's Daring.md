**Tier:** 4
**Activation:** Active (Incidental)
**Ranked:** No

When your [[Character]] uses an [[Actions|Action]] or are [[Attack Check|attacked]] (even if out-of-turn), they may use this [[Talents|Talent]] to spend three [[Advantage]] or a [[Triumph]] (or three [[Threat]] or a [[Despair]] on an enemy [[Attack Check]] targeted at them) to instantly cast a [[Relocate]] [[Spell Creation|Spell]] on their [[Character Sheet]] on themselves or an [[Engaged Range|Engaged]] ally.
