**Tier:** 2
**Activation:** Passive
**Ranked:** Yes

When purchasing this [[Talents|Talent]], pick one of the [[Spell Creation|Base Powers]] listed in [[Spell Creation]]. Every [[Spell Creation|Spell]] created using that Base Power now has its Magic Points cost permanently reduced by a number equal to the ranks in Power Mastery spent on that Base Power.

Each rank of Power Mastery can be spent on a different Base Power. For example, with three ranks of Power Mastery, two could be spent on [[Attack]] and one spent on [[Life]].
