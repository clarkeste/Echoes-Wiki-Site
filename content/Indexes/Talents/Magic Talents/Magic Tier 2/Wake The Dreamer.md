**Tier:** 2
**Activation:** Passive
**Ranked:** Yes

When purchasing this [[Talents|Talent]], pick one [[Spell Creation#Base Powers|Base Power]]. [[Spell Creation|Spell]] [[Skill Checks|Checks]] to [[Dispel]] effects made with that Base Power have their ability [[Dice Upgrade|upgraded]] twice.

Each additional rank of Wake The Dreamer can be used to pick an additional Base Power, to which the same effects will be applied, or to the same Base Power, in which case the effects will be duplicated. For instance, if two ranks of Wake the Dreamer are used to better the effects of [[Dispel]] on [[Invoke]] Spells, then all Dispel Spells from this [[Character]] targeting Invoke Spells have their [[Dice Pool]] upgraded four times.
