**Tier:** 4 
**Activation:** Active (Action) 
**Ranked:** No 

Once per [[Encounter]], may make a [[Difficulty|Hard]] (3p) [[Discipline|Discipline]] or [[Leadership|Leadership]] [[Skill Checks|Check]]. If successful, your [[Character]] plus one ally per [[Success|Success]] within [[Medium Range]] adds +1 [[Defense]] for the remainder of the encounter. This cannot exceed the maximum Defense of 4. Three [[Advantage]] or a [[Triumph]] can increase the Defense given by 1. Effects expire for any targets that move beyond Medium Range.
