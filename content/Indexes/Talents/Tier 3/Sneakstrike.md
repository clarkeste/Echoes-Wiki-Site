**Tier:** 3
**Activation:** Active (Action)
**Ranked:** No

When [[Attack Check|Attacking]] an [[Adversary|enemy]] within [[Engaged Range|Engaged Range]] and when hidden, a [[Character]] may use this [[Talents|Talent]]. This adds +3 [[Damage]] to the a successful Attack, and immediately deals a [[Critical Injury|Critical Injury]] so long as the Attack is successful. This will instantly kill targets that are [[Adversary#Minions|Minions]], or some that are [[Adversary#Rivals|Rivals]].
