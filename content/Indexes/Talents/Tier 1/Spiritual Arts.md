**Tier:** 1
**Activation:** Passive
**Ranked:** Yes

Each rank of Spiritual Arts allows a [[Character]] to add +1 [[Spell Creation|Spell]] to their [[Character Sheet|sheet]]. Additionally, it can be taken by Characters who are **not** Spellcasters and allows them to cast minor Spells.

For non-Spellcasters, each rank of Spiritual Arts will increase their Magic Points that can be used on Spells by 1. Their base amount of Magic Points start at 4, as per usual. They may *only* have Spells on their Sheet equal to their ranks in Spiritual Arts, and casting them costs 3 [[Strain]]. Additionally, all Spells have [[Inaccurate (Passive) (Quality)|Inaccurate]] 1 when used.

When using [[Manipulate]], [[Relocate]], roll using [[Coordination]]. When using [[Endow]], [[Telepathy]], [[Prophecy]], or [[Dispel]], use [[Discipline]]. When using [[Invoke]], use [[Craftsmanship]]. When using [[Life]], use [[Medicine]]. When using [[Attack]], use [[Discipline]] if the Spell has a Range of [[Short Range]] or longer, and use [[Melee-Light]] if it has a Range of [[Engaged Range|Engaged]].

May not use [[Enchantment]].
