A steel trap is a device like a claw-trap, or some other type of trap which can hurt or incapacitate the individual trips it temporarily. Often used for hunting, but also used on sapient beings—even if [[Mine|Mines]] are more common.

![](https://i.imgur.com/pX2IIgl.png)

[[Encumbrance|Encumbrance]]: 1
Effect: Can be set down and armed on any piece of mostly-flat terrain as a [[Maneuvers|Maneuver]]. When a target enters within [[Engaged Range]] of it, that [[Character]] (if it is a [[Nemesis]] or skilled Rival) may make an [[Opposed Check|Opposed]] [[Athletics]], [[Coordination]], or [[Vigilance]] [[Skill Checks|Check]] against the trapper’s [[Operating]] [[Skills|Skill]]. On a [[Success]], nothing occurs. Although the trap is not disarmed on a Success, any [[Character]] that knows of its existence add an automatic [[Success]] when trying to avoid it. On a [[Failure]], the Character suffers 4 [[Wounds|Wounds]] + uncanceled [[Failure|Failures]], and may not take another [[Maneuvers|Maneuver]] on their turn, or falls [[Prone]], depending on the nature of the trap.

If the Character or Minion group that triggered the trap does not roll a [[Skill Checks|Check]] for whatever reason, the trapper can choose to quickly spend a [[Sacred Points|Sacred Point]] to roll an Operating Check with [[Difficulty|Average]] (2p) [[Difficulty]], each uncanceled [[Success]] resulting in an additional point of [[Damage]].

If someone is intentionally looking for a trap and has good reason to, they may use [[Perception]] to spot it as well. Hiding it cleverly in the environment can add [[Setback Dice]] to [[Skill Checks|Checks]] to avoid it, and placing it in difficult spots can add Setbacks to Checks to avoid it—although not necessarily to spot it.
