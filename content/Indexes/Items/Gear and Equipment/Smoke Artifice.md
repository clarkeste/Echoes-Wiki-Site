A smoke artifice is a type of [[Bomb (Small)|Bomb]] or explosive which, when thrown and triggered, quickly creates a cloud of smoke around itself. This acts as [[Light Haze]] or [[Thick Haze]] depending on the exact nature of the Smoke Artifice.
![](https://i.imgur.com/f10hbND.png)
[[Encumbrance|Encumbrance]]: 2
Effect: May be thrown with a [[Ranged]] [[Skill Checks|Check]], with [[Difficulty]] determined by [[Range Bands|Range]] and a maximum range of [[Medium Range|Medium]]. On a [[Success]], the Smoke Artifice creates a [[Light Haze]] or [[Thick Haze]] of [[Short Range]] in size where it landed. On a [[Failure]], it is thrown one [[Maneuvers|Maneuver]]’s worth of space in the wrong direction, activating elsewhere, or falls into a position where the smoke, when released, has no effect. On a [[Despair]], it may be delayed or not trigger automatically.

Price: 200 [[Rupees]]
