Alcohol is a compound that many find relaxing and that act to soothe their worries, but can cause intoxication if too much is drunk.
![|231x821](https://i.imgur.com/chTvfOy.png)
[[Encumbrance]]: 0
Effect: When drunk, reduce [[Strain]] by 5. On the next use during that same day, reduce Strain only by 4, then 3, then 2 for each subsequent drink. After each usage of Alcohol, add a [[Setback Dice|Setback Die]] to all [[Skill Checks|Checks]] for the next [[Encounter]] or hour, whichever is longer. This stacks. If three bottles are drunk, gain two Setbacks that remain for the entire day. Each drink after the fifth requires an [[Difficulty|Easy]] (1p) [[Resilience]] [[Skill Checks|Check]] to be made when drunk, with the [[Difficulty]] increasing by 1 after each additional drink. On a [[Failure]], the [[Character]] will fall [[Incapacitated|unconscious]]; [[Threat]] or [[Despair|Despair]] may result in proportionate health issues. 

Must be stored within a container; glass [[Bottle|Bottles]] are not necessary but can be used. A [[Bottle]] can hold two servings of Alcohol. Wooden or ceramic containers can be used. Alcohol comes with its own impure glass bottle that is suitable for drinking alcohol out of. Three Alcohol bottles count as 1 [[Encumbrance]].

Price: 30 [[Rupees]]
