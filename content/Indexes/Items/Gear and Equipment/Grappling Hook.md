Grappling Hooks are often used by spelunkers and adventurers who need to traverse difficult, vertical terrain. Those climbing steep mountains as well as descending down cavernous sinkholes will often make use of a Grappling Hook.
![](https://i.imgur.com/03hKlmR.png)
[[Encumbrance|Encumbrance]]: 2
Effect: Grappling Hooks can be used to grab onto things from a distance, allowing its user to swing from where it has hooked, or pull themselves up, or use it as a steady point from which to descend from, using the rope connected to the hook. 

The user of the Grappling Hook may throw it at any location within [[Medium Range]]. Throws during dangerous situations or towards tricky spots or spots just out of range might require a [[Coordination]] or [[Ranged]] [[Skill Checks|Check]] to succeed. Once a Grappling Hook has hooked onto something, if there is something to hook onto, the user may manipulate the connection in various previously described ways to traverse the environment around them.

Using a Grappling Hook in a way that would cause physical harm to the person, such as using it in a last-ditch attempt to stop a free-fall or in such a way that it crashes the person holding onto the Grappling Hook into a wall, may result in the user of the Grappling Hook being forced to suffer [[Wounds]] or [[Strain]] to represent the toll on their body. In some cases, it may cause a [[Critical Injury]] or outright break their arm.
