Whetstones are important for blade maintenance. Beyond that, though, some type of whetstones made from sedimentary rock mined in western [[Hyrule]] are able to sharpen a blade better and quicker than most other whetstones, and must be rinsed with water before use. However, they are softer and can be used a few times before becoming useless compared to regular whetstones used for maintenance. Officers or warriors sometimes carry them to sharpen their [[Weapons]] just before a battle.

![](https://i.imgur.com/tXjEEmh.png)

[[Encumbrance|Encumbrance]]: 1
Effect: Each whetstone can be used a total of three times, which must be noted on a [[Character]] [[Character Sheet|sheet]]. The whetstone can be used on a [[Melee-Light|Melee]] [[Weapons|Weapon]] with a blade or point. When used, one usage of the whetstone is consumed, and the [[Weapons|Weapon]] increases its [[Damage]] by 1 during the next [[Encounter]]. Afterwards, the Damage returns to normal.

Price: 400 [[Rupees]]
