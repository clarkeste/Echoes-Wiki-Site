A cooking pot is a metal pot heated by a fire or stove of some sort that is then used to cook various meals, foods, or to mix the ingredients necessary to create [[Potion Creation|Potions]].

![](https://i.imgur.com/CYvS0Qq.png)

[[Encumbrance|Encumbrance]]: 2
Effect: Can be used to create meals or [[Potion Creation|Potions]] with [[Potion Creation|cooking]] and the [[Culinary]] [[Skills|Skill]]. Without a device like a cooking pot or similar cauldron-like device, one is unable to create Potions or cook. Must be put over a flame or otherwise heated to be used in that way. Only adds +2 Alchemy Points.

Price: 70 [[Rupees]]
