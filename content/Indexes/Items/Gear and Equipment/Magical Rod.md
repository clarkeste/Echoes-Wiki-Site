These Magical Rods, or Wands, are used by [[Spell Creation|Spellcasters]] to enhance their sorcery. Why exactly carrying a sometimes heavily-ornamented wooden stick is helpful is something of a mystery to scholars; but most suspect the effect is psychological and due to cultural association, like how an actor may feel better embedded into a role once he dons a cape.

These Magical Rods are also often used with a wide-brimmed, puffy hat that has a similar effect on people. Both young and old Spellcasters, particularly those that received a formal education in [[Spellcasting]], often use this wardrobe.
