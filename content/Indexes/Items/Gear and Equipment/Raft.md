Small, interconnected logs or pieces of wood make up a raft which can be used by a single person—sometimes two if one is pushing it—to ride along bodies of water and cross them.

![](https://i.imgur.com/cZGvfCP.png)

Effect: Can be placed on bodies of river to keep one from having to swim or touch the water. May paddle with hand or with oars. Rarely requires a [[Skill Checks|Check]], but in the situations where it does, [[Athletics]] is used.
