An instrument is used by a musician or instrumentalist to create music, often for entertainment or ceremonies. A variety exist.

![](https://i.imgur.com/f5OKWbU.png)

[[Encumbrance|Encumbrance]]: 2
Effect: May play music on the instrument. [[Skill Checks]] are resolved using [[Cool]], [[Discipline]], or [[Operating]]. Music can be used for a variety of purposes, and legends state it was used by the Hero of Time; it can also be used to make a few quick [[Rupees]]. Some larger instruments, such as pianos or organs, are more expensive and cannot be moved around as easily, but can be more impressive to listeners.
