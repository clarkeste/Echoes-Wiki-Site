A saddle and harness are necessary to properly ride a [[Horses and Mounts|Horse]], or most mounts. Most domesticated horses are used to having one and being led by its reins.


[[Encumbrance|Encumbrance]]: 2
Effect: Without a saddle or harness, all [[Riding]] [[Skill Checks|Checks]] with a [[Horses and Mounts|Horse]] and most other mounts add three [[Setback Dice]]. With them, these Setbacks are removed. Additionally, the [[Encumbrance]] capacity of the Mount is increased by +4.
