A piton is a metal spike that is meant to be embedded into a wall, usually a rock one such as a cliff- or mountain-side, and used as handholds to aid in climbing or as protection to loop one’s climbing [[Rope]] around. Most pitons have holes to thread the rope through, so that if a climber falls, they are caught by the embedded piton.

![](https://i.imgur.com/kBTSFv5.png)

[[Encumbrance|Encumbrance]]: 1
Effect: Can be embedded into a wall, floor, or ceiling as an [[Incidentals|Incidental]]. If the surface is durable, it may take more time, require a difficult [[Skill Checks|Check]], or in rare cases be impossible. The mallet required is usually assumed to already be with a [[Character]] who would carry a piton, but may be purchased separately for 10 [[Rupees|Rupees]]. A Character may use a piton to climb up areas that would otherwise be impossible due to lack of handholds, or use a Maneuver to climb a short way when it would otherwise require a single [[Actions|Action]] of [[Difficulty|Hard]] (3p) [[Difficulty]] or lesser. Harder [[Skill Checks|Checks]] may also have their [[Difficulty]] decreased by one or two by the presence of a piton. 

If combined with a [[Rope]] or similar [[Items|Item]] which is properly threaded around the piton, it will catch a falling Character and prevent them from suffering [[Fall Damage]], although they may still suffer reduced [[Strain]] or [[Wounds]] due to the shock of the sudden stop, depending on how far they fall before being caught. After a piton has been used, attempting to recover it will damage and deform it. It cannot be used a second time unless the attempt to extricate it from the wall is masterful.

As metal spikes, pitons can be used for a variety of purposes aside from its original intent.
