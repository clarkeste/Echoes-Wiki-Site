Yellow Potions are a compound that allows the body to stabilize its temperature; either making it colder in a warmer area, or warmer in a cold area, and protecting it from things such as frostbite or toxic fumes. Often used by travelers heading to [[The Gorons|Goron City]], where it is subsidized and bought in bulk.
