**Tier:** 4
**Activation:** Passive, Active (Maneuver)
**Ranked:** No

When using [[Manipulate]], a [[Character]] with this [[Talents|Talent]] no longer needs to use [[Maneuvers]] every turn to keep a target Gripped; they are Gripped indefinitely until they escape manually. Additionally, may spend 3 [[Strain]] instead of 2 to cast a [[Manipulate]] [[Spell Creation|Spell]] as a Maneuver.
