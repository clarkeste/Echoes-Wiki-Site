**Tier:** 2
**Activation:** Active (Incidental)
**Ranked:** No

May suffer 1 [[Strain]] to instantly see the past and echoes of a future of any object or person that the [[Character]] with this [[Talents|Talent]] is currently touching, and time’s effect on them. This will show a brief vision and flash of images and sensations that quickly tell the [[Character]] the history of the object or the person. Just like with regular [[Prophecy]] [[Spell Creation|Spells]], this vision may be clouded depending on the desire to hide the information.
