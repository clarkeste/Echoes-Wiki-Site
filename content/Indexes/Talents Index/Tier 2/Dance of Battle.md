**Tier:** 2
**Activation:** Passive
**Ranked:** No

If a [[Character]] used at least one [[Maneuvers|Maneuver]] on movement during their turn, they gain a [[Boost Dice|Boost Die]] to their next [[Attack Check]] made during that same turn. This effect stacks if the Character moves multiple turns consecutively, with a max of 3 Boost Dice. For example, if they move two turns in a row and then Attack, they gain two Boost Dice on that Attack.
