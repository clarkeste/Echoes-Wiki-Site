**Tier:** 2
**Activation:** Active (Maneuver)
**Ranked:** Yes

On the active [[Character]]’s turn, they may choose to take the Overwatch [[Maneuvers|Maneuver]] to enter ‘Overwatch’. When doing so, pick one Weapon currently held by the character in ‘Overwatch’. After taking this [[Maneuvers|Maneuver]], any enemy [[Character]] who takes a move [[Maneuvers|Maneuver]], and who while doing so is within [[Medium Range]], is within sight, is within [[Range Bands|Range]] of a [[Weapons|Weapon]] currently wielded by the character in ‘Overwatch’, and is not behind any type of [[Cover]], immediately triggers Overwatch. This means the moving adversary suffers Damage equal to the [[Damage|Base Damage]] of the Weapon which was used for Overwatch; if this Weapon is a [[Melee-Light|Melee]] Weapon, it also adds the [[Brawn]] of the character in ‘Overwatch’, as if it were a regular attack. If a Weapon is not [[Prepare (Passive) (Quality)|Prepared]], becomes unprepared such as by being used on ‘Overwatch’, is lost, or is no longer held, it cannot be used for ‘Overwatch’, and ‘Overwatch’ ends.

At the beginning of the active character next turn, ‘Overwatch’ ends until reactivated. ‘Overwatch’ also ends before the active Character’s next turn if it is triggered a number of times equal to the amount of ranks in the Overwatch [[Talents|Talent]]. This means that a [[Character]] with 2 ranks in Overwatch can inflict [[Damage]] with it twice before ‘Overwatch’ ends. An adversary can only be hit once with this [[Talents|Talent]] once per applicable movement [[Maneuvers|Maneuver]]. Overwatch can be [[Parry|Parried]], [[Block|Blocked]], [[Reflect|Reflected]], etc.
