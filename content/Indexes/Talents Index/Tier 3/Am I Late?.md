**Tier:** 3
**Activation:** Active (Incidental, Maneuver)
**Ranked:** No

Once per [[Encounter]], spend a [[Maneuvers|Maneuver]] and a [[Sacred Points|Sacred Point]] to activate. If you are inflicted with 20 [[Damage]], including Damage from [[Attack Check|Checks]] that failed, or more before the end of the next Round, you gain an additional 2 [[Actions]] on your next turn.

Additionally, once per Encounter an [[Incidentals|Incidental]], may spend 4 [[Strain]] to immediately move to [[Engaged Range]] of any [[Character]] within [[Medium Range]], or vice versa.
