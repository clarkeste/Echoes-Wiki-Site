**Tier:** 3
**Activation:** Active (Maneuver)
**Ranked:** No

Once per Encounter, use a [[Maneuvers|Maneuver]] and spend a [[Sacred Points|Sacred Point]] to trigger. Each time your [[Attack Check]] is ‘blocked’ (it fails or deals no [[Wounds]]), add +2 [[Damage]] to your next successful Attack. This stacks until +12.
