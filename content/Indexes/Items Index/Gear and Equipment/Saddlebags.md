Saddlebags are put onto horses or pack-animals so that they can carry more things; however, this usually comes at the cost of speed.


[[Encumbrance|Encumbrance]]: 2
Effect: Multiplies the [[Encumbrance]] capacity of a [[Horses and Mounts|Mount]] by 2. Unless the Mount description explicitly states that they can handle saddlebags without losing speed, putting a saddlebag on a [[Horses and Mounts|Horse]] or other Mount results in their Speed being decreased by 1.

Price: 250 [[Rupees]]
