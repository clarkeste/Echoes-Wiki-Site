Small metal objects with four spikes jutting out. One spike is always facing up because of this design. It is intended to be an area-denial tool, and to hurt the feet of anyone foolish enough to step on them.

![](https://i.imgur.com/P8xHzdW.png)

[[Encumbrance|Encumbrance]]: 1
Effect: Each pack of Caltrops contains about twenty and is good for one use. May throw anywhere within [[Engaged Range]] as a [[Maneuvers|Maneuver]] when equipped. Caltrops cover an area of [[Short Range]] in size. When a [[Character]] spends a [[Maneuvers|Maneuver]] to do anything in Caltrop-covered terrain, such as to move into them or exit them, or are Shoved into the Caltrops or otherwise enter them, they suffer 3 [[Wounds]]. As an [[Actions|Action]], may make an [[Difficulty|Average]] (2p) [[Coordination]] [[Skill Checks|Check]] to avoid taking [[Damage]] and move one Maneuver’s worth of space through the Caltrops. 

Caltrops are usually easy to see and obvious. However, in dark areas, they may not be obvious and require a [[Perception]] or [[Vigilance]] [[Skill Checks|Check]] from some Characters. Someone placing down Caltrops may suffer 2 [[Strain]] out of [[Combat]], or take an [[Actions|Action]] in Combat, and roll a [[Streetwise]] [[Skill Checks|Check]] with variable Difficulty to place the Caltrops in a hidden manner.
