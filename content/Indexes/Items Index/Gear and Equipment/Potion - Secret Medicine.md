The Secret Medicine is a type of Potion that, when drunk, has a very small effect. However, if its consumer suffers a grave injury sometime within the next day, the Potion takes effect and gives them the fortitude to keep going.

![|480x863](https://i.imgur.com/NqG371X.png)

[[Encumbrance]]: 0
Effect: When drunk, restore 3 [[Wounds]]. If a [[Character]] exceeds their [[Wound Threshold]] and would otherwise be [[Incapacitated]] sometime in the next day, restore 7 [[Wounds]] immediately. Consumes the Potion when drunk. Must be stored in a bottle, otherwise its effects deteriorate.
