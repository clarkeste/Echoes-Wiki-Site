A wagon, or a wheelbarrow, is a wooden vehicle with two wheels that is pulled along by a person, allowing them to transport much more than they otherwise would on their person.


[[Encumbrance|Encumbrance]]: 7
Effect: Can be pulled along to transport things. A wagon can hold up to 20 [[Encumbrance]] worth of [[Items]], but is unwieldy on its own to move and pull. Anything put into the wagon that does not exceed its 20 Encumbrance capacity does not add Encumbrance to the personal Encumbrance rating of the [[Character]] pulling the wagon. 

Any excess Encumbrance will be put on the pulling Character. For example, if the Wagon is carrying 24 Encumbrance worth of Items, the wagon will add 11 Encumbrance (the excess 4 plus its usual 7) to the pulling Character’s Encumbrance.

Price: 100 [[Rupees]]
