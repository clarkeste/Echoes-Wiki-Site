Mines are devices that, when triggered by an enemy while walking, trigger an explosion that can harm or kill the individual who stepped on it. They often take the form of small trip-wires that, when bumped, trigger a nearby explosive. It can be combined with other explosives covered by dirt or mud, such as [[Bomb (Medium)|Bombs]], to create a larger effect.

![](https://i.imgur.com/qQyGEmx.png)

[[Encumbrance|Encumbrance]]: 2
Effect: Can be set down and armed on any piece of mostly-flat terrain as a [[Maneuvers|Maneuver]]. When a target enters within [[Engaged Range]] of it, that [[Character]] (if it is a [[Nemesis]] or skilled Rival) may make an [[Opposed Check|Opposed]] [[Athletics]], [[Coordination]], or [[Vigilance]] [[Skill Checks|Check]] against the trapper’s [[Operating]] [[Skills|Skill]]. On a [[Success]], nothing occurs. Although the mine is not disarmed on a Success, any [[Character]] that knows of its existence add an automatic [[Success]] when trying to avoid it. On a [[Failure]], the Character suffers 10 [[Wounds|Wounds]] + uncanceled [[Failure|Failures]], has their turn instantly end, and falls [[Prone]]. [[Threat]] may trigger a [[Critical Hit]] roll, [[Despair|Despairs]] may trigger particular [[Critical Injury|Critical Injuries]]. Mines have a [[Critical Rating]] of 1. If any [[Bomb (Small)|Bombs]] are nearby, they are also trigged by the explosion. 

If the Character or Minion group that triggered the trap does not roll a [[Skill Checks|Check]] for whatever reason, the trapper can choose to quickly spend a [[Sacred Points|Sacred Point]] to roll an Operating Check with [[Difficulty|Average]] (2p) [[Difficulty]], each [[Success]] resulting in an additional point of [[Damage]].

If someone is intentionally looking for a trap and has good reason to, they may use [[Perception]] to spot it as well. All Checks to spot (such as when using [[Perception]] or [[Vigilance]]) a mine add three [[Boost Dice]]. Hiding it cleverly in the environment can add [[Setback Dice]] to [[Skill Checks|Checks]] to avoid it, and placing it in difficult spots can add Setbacks to Checks to avoid it—although not necessarily to spot it.
