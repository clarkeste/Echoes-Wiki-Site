A concoction of chemicals that, when thrown, instantly erupts into flame. It can set an area around it on [[Fire]], especially if it is made of flammable substance like wood, or covered in something like [[Lantern Oil|oil]] or tar.
![](https://i.imgur.com/vIWPxD3.png)
[[Encumbrance|Encumbrance]]: 1
Effect: [[Thrown Weapons|Thrown]] with a [[Ranged]] [[Attack Check]], [[Medium Range]] being the maximum throwable distance. When successfully hitting its [[Character|target]] [[Character]] or area, it creates an area of [[Fire]] of [[Short Range]] in size, of medium severity, using the regular Fire rules. It may grow in size and severity or sustain itself for longer than expected due to its surroundings. Characters who are within Short Range of where the Firebomb lands count as having entered the Fire and must immediately suffer the effects.
