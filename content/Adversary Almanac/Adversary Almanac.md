The Adversary Almanac is where the information about different Enemies and Monsters are collected after [[Insight]] has been used on them by any [[PC|Player Character]].

It includes access to their stats, in addition to a brief description of them. For Characters, this includes their history, and includes additional information for Monsters of non-descript Enemies. It additionally includes basic tactical information about how these Adversaries usually operate.
