**Sage Medallions** are objects that, according to legend, were tied to a [[Sages|Sage]] and awarded to the [[Hero of Time|Hero of Time]] as a token of that Sage’s power. It is believed that they were used, in ancient times, to prove that a person had authorization or approval from a Sage. In modern days, sources say that the modern-day [[Ganondorf (Historia)|Ganondorf]] recovered a Sage Medallion by an unknown method from the [[Lost Woods (Historia)|Lost Woods]] that he used to enter a [[Temple in the Desert (Historia)|Temple]] deep in the [[Desert of Doubt (Historia)|Desert of Doubt]]. In that Temple, he found a [[The Triforce (Historia)|Triforce]] Shard, before returning to [[Hyrule|Hyrule]] and dying by the [[Master Sword (Historia)|blade]] of [[Link Steadfast (Historia)|Link Steadfast]].
