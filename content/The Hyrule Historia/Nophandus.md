**Nophandus** was a man who served over a decade as the Minister of Hylia, a position involving the supervision and approval of thing such as [[Goddesses and the Gods|religion]], books and newspapers, as well as handling the military, war efforts, and espionage. He was among the longest-serving members of the [[Last King of Hyrule|King]]'s [[The Council|Council]] by the time of the [[Revolution and Second Civil War|Revolution]]. However, despite his zeal, there are records indicating that his compatriot [[Inl Astor|Astor]] believed him to be involved with the [[The Followers of Dragmire, Revolutionaries|Followers of Dragmire]] as its leader, [[The Pragmatist|the Pragmatist]]. Such a thing, however, is unlikely, as Nophandus died along with the other councilmen during the Revolution. He was described as hardened, loyal and with a quiet zeal.

In the final days of Hyrule, Nophandus worked to [[The Draft, Mobilization|mobilize the Hyrulean army]] after a draft was announced. He was present during the Follower raid at [[Hyrule Castle|Hyrule Castle]], and oversaw both the interrogation and executions of the Followers captured at Lon Lon Ranch. While it is unclear what happened in the last day of the [[Former Hyrulean Government and Royal Family|Royal Government]], it is believed that Nophandus most likely died to an assassin before the Followers arrived and shelled the city. Like the other council members, his body was recovered from the rubble and cremated.
