**King’s Port** is a port city located on the eastern coast of [[Hyrule]]. It was founded by the [[Last King of Hyrule|King of Hyrule]] and his civilian supporters decades ago to act as a more reliable port-city alternative to the annoyingly defiant [[Kakariko City]]. Unlike Kakariko, King’s Port is not located in an [[Autonomy Clause|autonomous zone]].

Despite the King’s lofty goals for the King’s Port, it never reached the same heights of trade as Kakariko City, nor came close to overtaking it. While for political reasons it was beneficial to have an alternative directly under the control of the King, it became clear that few [[Guilds|companies]] were interested in importing and exporting through a new Port that lacked the proper infrastructure, both material and personal.

After the King’s Port failed to live up to expectations, the [[Former Hyrulean Government and Royal Family|Hyrulean government]] changed strategies and primarily focused on bringing Kakariko City to heel. 

Now, however, during the [[Revolution and Second Civil War|Civil War]], King’s Port has become more important than ever before. With [[Kakariko City]] under the control of the [[The Followers of Dragmire, Revolutionaries|Revolutionaries]], King’s Port is the only major city with port facilities available to [[The Royalists|the Royalists]]. It plays an incredibly important role in the Royalist government, as it is where troops from overseas arrive, as well as supplies and aid from [[Holodrum]], [[Calatia]], and [[Koridai]]. The Royal Navy, too, had made this their main civilian base.

Therefore, despite being smaller in terms of population than [[Akkala City]], King’s Port is essentially the de-facto capital of the Royalists. The [[Governor of Kakariko]] resides here and oversees the political aspects of their faction.
