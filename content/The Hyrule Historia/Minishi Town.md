**Minishi Town** is a settlement in the east of [[Hyrule|Hyrule]], near the [[Lost Woods|Lost Woods]] and close to the frontline of the [[Revolution and Second Civil War|war]]. It is currently under [[The Royalists|Royalist]] control. [[Commission for the Reclamation of the Golden Power|The Commission for the Reclamation of the Golden Power]] passed by this city days before the Revolution, when heading for the Lost Woods.

Minishi Town is on the outskirts of the Great Hyrule Forest, otherwise known as the Lost Woods. The bridge to enter the Lost Woods is very nearby to the town, and Minishi was a well-off town as a result to the war, receiving economic boosts from tourism. However, they were quickly secured by the Royalists, and the town put under martial law, where it remains to this day. The city is regularly sieged and attacked by partisans and artillery from the Followers. Prior to the war, the Minishi people were considered to have negative sentiments towards the [[Former Hyrulean Government and Royal Family|Royal Family]]; however, most information indicates that is changing and that they are now seen as defenders.
