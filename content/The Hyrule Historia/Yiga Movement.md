**The Yiga Movement** refers to [[the Sheikah|Sheikah]] who grew disillusioned with the [[Former Hyrulean Government and Royal Family|Royal Family]] and society in [[Hyrule|Hyrule]] due to increases in bigotry among other citizens of the country. They often blame the Royal Family for this, seeing them as partly responsible, or condemn them for doing essentially nothing to help a group of people who has historically been extremely loyal to the monarchy, but has seen little of that loyalty rewarded. The [[Master Kohga|Believer]] is a member of this movement. 

Yiga means 'Betrayer' in the Sheikah language, and—despite initially being a disparaging phrase—was embraced by the supporters of the movement.

While the first mention of the Yiga movement can be traced to over a hundred years ago, its numbers exploded six years ago. At that time, the [[Last King of Hyrule|King]] officially banned the vast majority of Sheikah from holding governmental positions, or working in government or government-contracted [[guilds]]. This laid off approximately 1958 Sheikah workers in the government—approximately 3% of its workforce—and over twenty-thousand country-wide. Individuals who identified as Sheikah also reported a rise in discrimination and hate speech directed at them, as the most fervant bigotry had essentially been codified by the highest authority in the land.

While a few individuals were spared—such as the Believer disguised as General Ahoga, who was personally vouched and advocated for by [[Chancellor Collin Phurn|Chancellor Phurn]]—most were not, including those closest to the Royal Family. A notable example was the traditionalist Sheikah, [[Impa|Impa]], head of the [[Princess Zelda|Princess]]' Royal Guard. Despite her dismissal, she remained loyal to the crown, as did many traditionalist Sheikah.

Most members of the Yiga movement joined the [[The Followers of Dragmire, Revolutionaries|Followers of Dragmire]], as—by the time the movement exploded in popularity—almost all non-violent groups had been outlawed by the Royal Family, and other violent groups subsumed by the Followers in the name of forming a united front for revolution. Eventually, the Yiga got their wish and overthrew the Royal Family during the [[Revolution and Second Civil War|Revolution]].

Today, the Yiga Movement still exists, hoping to secure new rights in the Revolutionary government. Essentially every member of the movement supports the Revolutionary government over the [[The Royalists|Royalists]].

Many Yiga dye their hair black, to symbolically separate themselves from Sheikah in the past who served the Royal Family. This is because then natural hair color of Sheikah is almost always white.
