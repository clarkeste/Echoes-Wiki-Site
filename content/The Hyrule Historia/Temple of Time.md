**The Temple of Time** is an ancient sacred site, and the resting place of the [[Master Sword|Master Sword]] in legend. It is a cathedral-like structure, with a main room leading into the chamber of the Master Sword. History and legend both suggest that only certain virtuous individuals can pull the Master Sword from its pedestal. It is said that it was once the gateway between the world, and the [[Sacred Realm|Sacred Realm]]. It is said that the [[Hero of Time|Hero of Time]] used this gateway to recruit the [[Sages|Sages]]. The Hero of Time's title is derived from this Temple.

The Temple of Time is currently located in the [[Lost Woods|Lost Woods]], or Great Hyrule Forest as it is known officially. Due to the confusing nature of the woods, there are only a few people in recent times that can attest to seeing the Temple of Time. None that have been able to enter the supposed Master Sword chamber.

That all seemed to change, however, shortly before the [[Revolution and Second Civil War|Revolution]]. At some point, it is believed that [[Link Steadfast|Link Steadfast]], a member of the [[The Followers of Dragmire, Revolutionaries|Followers of Dragmire]], was able to pull the Master Sword from its slumber. Then, in the infamous '[[Broadcast|broadcast]]', he revealed that he wielded the Master Sword to the entire world, beginning the Revolution. Declassified Royal documents from shortly before indicate that the [[Commission for the Reclamation of the Golden Power|Commission for the Reclamation of the Golden Power]] investigated the Temple of Time shortly before and found that the Master Sword was missing from its pedestal. Additionally, one of [[Princess Zelda]]’s bodyguards, [[Link Sahon|Sahon]], was killed inside the Temple.
