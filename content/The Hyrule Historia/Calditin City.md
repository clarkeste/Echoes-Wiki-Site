**Calditin City** is a city in the [[Desert of Mystery, Gerudo Desert|Gerudo Desert]], currently controlled by the [[The Followers of Dragmire, Revolutionaries|Followers]] despite their population overall supporting the [[The Royalists|Royalists]].

Calditin City was built following the [[Hylian-Gerudo War|Hylian-Gerudo War]] by [[Hyrule|Hyruleans]] who wished to colonize the land they had expelled the [[The Gerudo|Gerudo]] from. Despite the difficulty of the desert, the superior technology of the mostly [[The Hylians|Hylian]] settlers and support from the larger Kingdom allowed them to build a settlement far more impressive and beautiful than the paltry towns of the Gerudo that had previously inhabited the region.

Despite its distance from [[Hyrulean Geography|Central Hyrule]], Calditin became a large city. It is a hub of tourism, from those seeking to observe and explore ancient and damaged Gerudo architecture that remains buried in the sand. The city has also marketed itself to those who supported the [[Former Hyrulean Government and Royal Family|monarchy]] and felt tired or alienated of the more liberal cities, such as [[Castle Town|Castle Town]] and [[Kakariko City|Kakariko City]].

Due to its geography, Calditin was forced under the control of the Revolutionary government, despite majority support in its populace for the [[Former Hyrulean Government and Royal Family|Royal Family]]. There are fears among its people that the Revolutionary Followers may try to invite the Gerudo back into the lands they now inhabit, which many local leaders threaten would lead to a rebellion against the Followers. Many citizens of Calditin, particularly those above the age of twenty, yearn for the day when the Royalists will liberate their city and bring them back into the fold of the Royal Family, with all the security and liberty they believe it will secure them.
