**The Desert of Doubt** refers to the deeper, most isolated regions of the [[Desert of Mystery, Gerudo Desert|Gerudo Desert]]. It is where the [[The Gerudo|Gerudo]] were forced to flee after the [[Hylian-Gerudo War]] forced them to leave their charred homes. It is usually considered even more dangerous than the parts of the Gerudo Desert closer to [[Hyrule|Hyrule]]—now controlled by the Kingdom of Hyrule—due to its distance from civilization, clearer skies, and more dangerous wildlife. In the Desert of Doubt exists the primitive Gerudo settlements of the modern day.

Information on these settlements is hard to come by, mostly because there is essentially no contact between them and Hyrule. For obvious reasons. Most of our information comes from the [[Zuna]], another largely isolated people, as they are the closest companions of the Gerudo in the modern day.

Up to a thousand years ago, before they lived on [[Zuna Republic (Historia)|Zunari Island]], it is believed that the Zuna people lived in the Desert of Doubt and constructed various grand structures, some of which still exist today.

Somewhere in the Desert of Doubt is a [[Temple in the Desert (Historia)|Temple]] that [[Ganondorf (Historia)|Ganondorf]] visited as part of his duties in [[Commission for the Reclamation of the Golden Power|The Commission for the Reclamation of the Golden Power]].
