Despite being a crime in almost every country—from [[Hyrule|Hyrule]] and [[Holodrum|Holodrum]] to the smallest island-nation—the potential [[Rupees, Currencies|profits]] gained from **piracy and smuggling** are too much for many individuals to pass up. They engage in these illicit activities to gain goods that would otherwise be heavily taxed, or illegal in some jurisdictions.

Many different pirate groups exist, with many running independently. While smugglers often are simply unlicensed and unregistered convoys, pirates often raid merchant or cargo vessels to steal their wares and sell them at a later date, or used by themselves.

Various clandestine ports exist in which these sea-faring criminals reside; including some in barren, undocumented islands, or those hidden in otherwise civilized cities and islands. There, mayors and governors are incentivized to keep the presence of the criminals hidden by coercion, or bribery.

Despite the naturally antagonistic relationship between government and these criminals, sometimes the two groups enter an uneasy alliance. For example, there is a precedent in governments hiring Privateers—essentially, pirates paid by a state—to destroy, raid, or steal ships and supplies from a rival power, without declaring war. This gives the aggressor nation plausible deniability, with the responsibility laying only on the pirates. Likewise, some countries support smugglers to bypass the taxes and bans of other nations.
