**The Royalists** is the colloquial name for the side in the [[Revolution and Second Civil War|Civil War]] that claims to be the legitimate government, upholding the ideals of the [[Former Hyrulean Government and Royal Family|monarchy]]. Their current leaders are [[General Noril|General Nori]]l and the former [[Governor of Kakariko|Governor of Kakariko]]; their stated goal is to take the [[Hyrule|country]] back from the [[The Followers of Dragmire, Revolutionaries|Revolutionaries]], and they have stated that they intend to invite the next-in-line to the throne to the country once they can guarantee their safety.

Following the death of [[Princess Zelda]] and her [[Last King of Hyrule|father]] at [[Hyrule Castle|Hyrule Castle]], the Royal Military was expelled from the [[Castle Town|capital of Hyrule]]. The Revolutionaries successfully rebuffed the attempts of General Noril to retake the city, and pushed the General's army across the river to Mount Crenel. There, Noril communicated with other military commanders, and they agreed to promote General Noril to Chief of Staff for the purposes of prosecuting the war. Thus, the Royalist movement was born—with the humble General Noril handling the military aspects, and the Governor of Kakariko—one of the few experienced politicians who still lived and remained loyal to the crown—handling political aspects, such as petitioning [[Calatia|Calatia]] and [[Holodrum|Holodrum]] for supplies and equipment.

The ever-increasing demands from the theaters of battle forced General Noril to move his base of operations to a more central spot. Seeing nowhere better, Noril turned [[Lanayru Citadel|Lanayru Citadel]] into a fortress and headquarters for the Royalist military. Nearby, [[King's Port|King's Port]] serves as a political capital, where the former Governor works with foreign diplomats to gain support. 

The Royalists have successfully forced the war into a near stand-still, with them controlling much of western Hyrule. Mountains and rivers make it challenging for the Followers to progress, bleeding out the forces of their enemies while the Royalists gather more and more supplies, munitions, and legitimacy on the world stage.
