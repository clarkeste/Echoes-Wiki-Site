**Aadin Steadfast** was a [[The Hylians|Hylian]] [[Crafting|blacksmith]] who lived in [[Castle Town]], and the father of [[Link Steadfast]]. His wife died in complications during the childbirth of their son and daughter, Link and [[Aryll Steadfast]], leading Aadin to raise their children alone. Those who knew Aadin say he was a soft-spoken and kind man, one who deeply believed in certain principles. If those principles were the ones that led his son to join the [[Castle Town Parliamentarians]], we cannot say.

Aadin was something of an 'underrated' blacksmith. Not large enough to be noticed and put into the employ of the [[Former Hyrulean Government and Royal Family|government]], he served independent contracts, such as creating gleaming weapons for [[Capitalist and Nobleman Class|nobility]], or designing practical blueprints for [[Guilds|blade manufacturing companies]]. At some point when he visited with a noble client, his young boy--Link Steadfast--met a wandering [[Princess Zelda]]. The two got along excellently, with Aadin reportedly not even realizing the girl was the Princess until the very end. However, a friendship had been made, and the two young children met many times--until Zelda's scheduling and allowed areas came under heavier restrictions for her own safety. This made them unable to meet.

Later, when both children were approximately fourteen, Zelda appeared at Aadin's workshop. She and the blacksmith's son hit it off yet again, even when Zelda learned that Link Steadfast had joined the Castle Town Parliamentarians.

However, things went so very wrong when the Castle Town Parliamentarians went out of line, resulting in arrests of their members and the burning of the building they met in. Aadin's son was forced to flee to avoid arrest and imprisonment. Neighbors reported that Steadfast visited his father many times covertly, but only for short periods of time, before leaving. 

Aadin was made a pariah in society and business by the [[Last King of Hyrule|King's]] office, due to his own link to the Parliamentarians, through his son. Despite Princess Zelda's attempts to support Aadin financially and her visits with him, the elder Steadfast was a man with no work, and his son a criminal on the run. His finances eventually dwindled to nothing. Four years later after his son fled, he contracted an illness and died. His son was not present, but Aadin’s neighbors report that the Princess was. Despite what Aadin’s own son would later do to end Zelda’s life.
