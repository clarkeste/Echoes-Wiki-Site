**Humans** are the most numerous race on the planet. Various sub-groups of humans exist, but they are all clearly identifiable as humans. These groups include the [[The Hylians|Hylians]], the [[The Gerudo|Gerudo]], the [[The Sheikah|Sheikah]], and the people of [[Holodrum (Historia)|Holodrum]], [[Labrynna (Historia)|Labrynna]], and [[Koridai (Historia)|Koridai]]. It is debated if the [[Zuna]] from the [[Zuna Republic (Historia)|Zuna Republic]] are close enough to humans to be counted as a sub-group.
