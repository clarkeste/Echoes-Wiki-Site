According to Royal documents that were declassified following the [[Revolution and Second Civil War|Revolution]], [[Ganondorf (Historia)|Ganondorf]] spoke of a **Temple deep in the desert**. He believed it to hold a [[The Triforce (Historia)|Triforce]] Shard, but was unable to enter without a [[Sage Medallions|Sage Medallion]].

After traveling with the [[Commission for the Reclamation of the Golden Power|Commission for the Reclamation of the Golden Power]], Ganondorf recovered a Sage Medallion from inside the [[Lost Woods (Historia)|Lost Woods]]. How is unknown. After he gained it, he traveled to the Temple in the Desert. He presumably opened it with the Sage Medallion, for he returned to [[Hyrule|Hyrule]] days later with the Triforce Shard.

When Ganondorf returned, the [[Revolution and Second Civil War|Revolution]] had reached [[Castle Town (Historia)|Castle Town]]. Unfortunately, the [[The Gerudo|Gerudo]] died attempting to stop the Revolution.
