**Daphnes Nohansen Hyrule** was a [[Former Hyrulean Government and Royal Family|King]] of [[Hyrule|Hyrule]], and the reigning monarch when [[Ganondorf Dragmire|Ganon]], the Demon King, returned after 100 years of being sealed. He was a measured King, and despite his strict tone with many subordinates, his actions spoke louder than words—the King was such a compassionate man that, in the eyes of many historians, it was what killed him.

During the era of the [[Hero of the Flood|Hero of the Flood]], Daphnes advocated for peaceful relations with the [[The Gerudo|Gerudo]], and a policy of attempting to disassociate them with the actions of Dragmire, and making it clear they were not responsible.

Eventually, when Ganon returned, he and Gerudo loyal to him attacked [[Hyrule Castle (Historia)|Hyrule Castle]] and captured both the King and his daughter, the [[Princess Zelda (Historia)|Princess Zelda]] of the era.

In his captivity, Daphnes Nohansen died, as did his daughter. The Hero of the Flood stopped Ganon, but he was too late to save the members of the Royal Family who been captured.

While Daphnes Nohansen is respected by most regardless of political opinions, most Royalists in the modern day use him as a warning—a word to the wise on healthy fear and toxic compassion.
