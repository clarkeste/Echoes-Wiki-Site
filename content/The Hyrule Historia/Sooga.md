Sooga is a member of the [[The Followers of Dragmire, Revolutionaries|Followers of Dragmire]], holding the rank of [[The Messengers, Follower Ranks|Messenger]], and is now a member of the new Revolutionary government. He was the right-hand man of the [[Master Kohga|Believer]], and is a [[the Sheikah|Sheikah]], therefore part of the [[Yiga Movement|Yiga movement]]. He uses electrical and lightening magic, and is agile despite his large and muscular stature.
