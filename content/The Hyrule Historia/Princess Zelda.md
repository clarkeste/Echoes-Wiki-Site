**Princess Zelda** is a very common name for the women of the [[Hyrule|Hyrulean]] [[Former Hyrulean Government and Royal Family|Royal Family]]. The most notable Zeldas include the woman who, in legend, is said to have founded Hyrule and been a descendent or incarnation of the [[Goddesses and the Gods|Goddess Hylia]], the Princess during the era of the [[Hero of Time|Hero of Time]], the Princess during the era of the [[Hero of the Flood|Hero of the Flood]], or the last Princess of Hyrule, who died in the [[Revolution and Second Civil War|Revolution]].


The Zelda who lived during the era of the Hero of Time was a woman who spent much of her childhood in hiding. Her first nine years of life were expected, if turbulent; her [[King Arkenalan Bosmison Hyrule|father]] and mother, two of the most important people in the realm, attempted to calm the flames of the [[First Hyrulean Civil War|Hyrulean Civil War]]. Despite the fact that by the time she was four the war was over, her parents still remained extremely busy, attempting to codify the new peace. Most of Zelda's mentoring was done by her [[Sheikah|Sheikah]] nursemaid, whose name has been lost to time.

Only a few years later, however, an even greater tragedy would strike. Using his newfound sway with the King, [[Ganondorf Dragmire|Ganondorf Dragmire]] eliminated him and almost the entirety of the Hyrulean government. Zelda, saved by her bodyguards and nursemaid, were among the only ones to survive. For [[Seven Year Reign|seven years]], she scrounged and stayed in hiding to survive, trying to help where she could. However, after those seven years had passed, Zelda re-connected with her childhood friend, who wielded the [[Master Sword|Sword of Evil's Bane]]. He was now known as the Hero of Time. Along with the power of seven wise [[Sages|Sages]], they were able to defeat Ganondorf and seal him away into the [[Sacred Realm|Sacred Realm]] for one hundred years.

This Zelda abdicated her right to the throne shortly after her 18th birthday, before she was to be coronated. Instead, the throne passed to her brother who had been safe in a distant land. Zelda spent the rest of her life serving as a diplomat between the Hyrulean and [[The Gerudo|Gerudo]] people, hoping to prevent the conditions that lead to the rise of an individual like Ganondorf to begin with. She died while middle-aged, 53, and never married or had children. Instead, the royal lineage was continued by her brother.


One hundred years later, during the [[The Flood|Era of the Flood]], another girl named Zelda was born into the Hyrulean Royal Family. She was the granddaughter of the previous Zelda's brother, and unlike her great-aunt, lived through a peaceful childhood. Hyrule was in an era of peace after reconstructing from the vicious seven years of tyrannical rule, and the Hyrulean Civil War before that. She was great friends with the Hero of the Flood, a tradesman from the outskirts of Hyrule who was skilled with a blade, and most agree the two romantically loved one another. However, her life tragically came to a sudden stop when torrential downpours fell from the heavens. She and her father, [[King Daphnes Nohansen|Daphnes Nohansen]], were kidnapped by the newly-returned Ganondorf for the purposes of extracting Zelda's [[The Triforce|Triforce of Wisdom]].

However, Princess Zelda would rather die than hand over the Triforce. And that she did. Under interrogation, she died before giving it over. Ganondorf used and killed Daphnes to draw out the Hero of the Flood, who then avenged them and slew Ganondorf. Some records claim that the Hero confessed that the Demon King allowed him to do this, encouraging it, as he knew it would end the torrential downpour. This theory is often questioned, however, as the most likely cause of the rain was Ganondorf's own magic. This implies he could’ve controlled it into ceasing himself.

Twenty-one years ago, the Princess Zelda of the modern day was born. She was kind and beloved by many, and different from her [[Last King of Hyrule|father]] in innumerable ways. Yet the [[The Followers of Dragmire, Revolutionaries|Revolutionaries]] did not spare her, and she suffered the same fate: death.

Zelda spent the majority of her life living in [[Hyrule Castle|Hyrule Castle]] and [[Castle Town|Castle Town]]. Despite her limited contact with others for safety reasons, she met and took a liking to a commoner boy, named [[Link Steadfast|Link Steadfast]], when the two of them were children. However, after knowing each other for a few years, the boy was disallowed from seeing Zelda. This was due to concerns arising from his station in society and potentially posing a risk to the Princess. Despite this delay, the Princess continued her studies and grew into a young woman. Concerns both from her father and the gossip-mongers ran wild, for she had not yet manifested the Triforce of Wisdom, the birthright of the female members of the Royal Family. It is said she had a close friendship with [[Impa]], her lead bodyguard, although Impa was eventually replaced by [[Link Sahon]]. Additionally, Zelda was known to be on good terms with [[Chancellor Collin Phurn|Chancellor Phurn]].

When Link and her were both in adolescence, they met once more when the Princess was allowed to leave the Castle with less supervision. This boy lived with his [[Aadin Steadfast|father, a blacksmith]]. Not much is known of his early life, but his destiny became extremely important to the [[Revolution and Second Civil War|Revolution]]. In a way, Link Steadfast betrayed his childhood friend for ideological convictions when he supported the revolution, the very same individuals that ended up killing Zelda.

The details of their relationship is unknown to us. Suffice to say, when the authorities decided that the [[Castle Town Parliamentarians|group that Steadfast belonged]] had become dangerous, Steadfast fled town along with his [[Aryll Steadfast|sister]]. As far as we know, the Link and our Princess did not meet again.

Six years later, Zelda was present at the fateful [[Festival of the Flood|Festival of the Flood]] where the new [[Ganondorf|Ganondorf]] made his appearance. The arrival of the new Gerudo King caused a stir. However, after it was learned that this Ganondorf was not a violent man, he was brought into the Castle for negotiations along with the King and Zelda. Once he was there, the Followers of Dragmire conducted a raid on Hyrule Castle, likely emboldened by the presence of a man bearing the same as their god. Using dozens of secret agents that had infiltrated as soldiers, the Followers infiltrated the Castle. The [[The Sorcerer|Sorcerer]], the leader of the raid, kidnapped Princess Zelda and defeated all of the King's strongest warriors, including [[Inl Astor]]. However, before the Sorcerer could strike at the King of Hyrule, Ganondorf intervened and saved the King.

Regardless, Zelda was captured. Where exactly she was taken is unknown; however, it is known that another one of [[The Chosen|the Chosen]], the [[The Confessor|Confessor]] interrogated her during this time. The Confessor forcefully took the Triforce of Wisdom from Zelda's wrist, who had manifested it in captivity. Later, however, through Zelda's own cunning, she managed to escape her restraints, and met with the [[Commission for the Reclamation of the Golden Power|Commission for the Recovery of the Golden Power]]. This group that included her old bodyguard and friend, Impa, and Ganondorf. Despite being encouraged to return to the Castle, she chose to go along with the Commission through their journeys to recover Shards in [[Zora|Zora's Domain]] and the [[Lost Woods|Lost Woods]]. While the Confessor was present to attempt to stop the Commission in the Lost Woods, she and Zelda did not face off once again.

Days later—along with the rest of the Royal Government—Zelda was present in Hyrule Castle when the [[Revolution and Second Civil War|Revolution]] began in [[Kakariko City|Kakariko City]]. She remained there, along with her father, the government, and [[The Council|the Council]], as the Followers approached. 

Whether it was during the assault by the Follower army or by spies inside the Castle, Zelda was killed when an explosive went off. The bomb destroyed a large part of the Castle and left her charred body behind. She was later buried in the Sacred Grounds, a fate better than her father's body, which was burned and scattered to the winds.

Today, Zelda is a rallying cry for the [[Should my character believe in the Royalists, or the Followers?|Royalist]] faction, the martyr for the crimes of the Followers. However, even among the Followers who do not curse her for being a part of the Royal Family, seem to regret her fate, and respect her for supporting the [[Castle Town Parliamentarians|Castle Town Parliamentarians]] earlier in life.
