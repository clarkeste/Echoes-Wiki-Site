**Koridai** is an island-nation in the [[The Great Sea|Great Sea]], between [[Hyrule|Hyrule]] and [[Holodrum|Holodrum]]. Due to its strategic position, almost all intercontinental [[Economy|trade]] passes through Koridai—making it the most wealthy country in the world, and extremely important for its small size. It has often been courted by competing powers, primarily Holodrum and Hyrule, who seek to have the most influence over the vital island. Koridai itself is very mountainous in terrain, with various ports and harbors on its coasts. It is ruled by a monarchy, but the most powerful people on the island are the merchant [[Guilds|companies]]. Almost all groups appreciate the attention received from Hyrule and Holodrum, allowing the country to grow even richer.

Three years ago, [[Labrynna|Labrynna]] attempted to play alongside the larger countries of Hyrule and Holodrum when it came to influencing Koridai. They established an embassy, and various diplomats met with members of the Koridian government and plutocracy. However, after less than a year of operation, vigilantes in Koridian security forces took it as their duty to eliminate Labrynnan influence, and killed over a dozen members of the Embassy, including important politicians and diplomats.

Koridai refused to prosecute these vigilantes, or punish them beyond removing from their posts. While reasons are debated, the official stance remains that the proof was inconclusive. The real reason that most geopolitical observers theorize, however, was that the Koridian government wished for there to be a nationalistic rallying point, and chose the vigilantes to be that point.

When tensions inevitably rose, Koridai knew that Holodrum and Hyrule would bend over backwards to protect them. Indeed, after months of back and forth, Labrynna blockaded Koridai. The island quickly retaliated, and so began the Koridai War. Hyrule and Holodrum, two countries courting Koridai's favor, immediately joined the war on the side of Koridai.

It took less than a week for the Labrynnan navy around Koridai to be destroyed and routed, with all attempts to land at the island turning into massacres for the Labrynnan forces. Both the navies of Hyrule and Holodrum continued to pummel Labrynna's ships, trade, and coastline—eventually landing on the western coast and capturing the capital, Lynna City.

In a humiliating defeat, the Labrynnan government and monarchy capitulated, and accepted a conditional surrender. This was seen as a great success in Koridai. However, an issue would arise soon after, when—only months after the terms of the surrender were agreed upon—the [[Revolution and Second Civil War|Revolution]] in Hyrule began. While Koridai was largely immune to the revolutionary fervor that hit some other countries, Hyrule's new state of war meant that there was only one nation left to dominate Koridai: Holodrum. This gave the island-nation much less leverage, and thereby, much less independence.

To this day, Koridai remains the center of trade. However, without a rival to compete with, Holodrum's influence over Koridai has undeniably grown. While they have not done anything to upset or jeopardize their relationship with Koridai, most observers say that this is simply a mercy from the Empire of Holodrum that could technically end at any time.

Just like Holodrum, Koridai supplies the [[The Royalists|Royalist]] in Hyrule and [[Hytopia|Hytopia]] with munitions. Often, almost all support sent to those countries by Holodrum are shipped through Koridai. The people of Koridai are largely apolitical to the world, happy to sell to anybody. However, the majority do still express sentiments of approval for the Royalist cause in the world.
