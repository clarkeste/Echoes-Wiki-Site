**Rupees** are the main currency of [[Hyrule|Hyrule]], and are often used world-wide due to Hyrule's large influence on the world. It is the main currency used in [[Koridai|Koridai]]—although the locals spell and pronounce it as 'Rubies’—and slightly edges out the [[Holodrum|Holodrumian]] currency as the most used currency internationally. Most [[Capitalist and Nobleman Class|entrepreneurship]] and [[Market Research|market research]] is done with Rupees.

Rupees are common gemstones that are said to have some [[Spellcasting|magical]] power, which was the original reason for their use as currency in eras past. It seems there is truth to this, as some items—such as the [[Magic Armor|Magic Armor]] crafted by certain artisans—drain Rupees, turning them 'dun'. A dun Rupee is a black Rupee that has no color. Dun Rupees are worthless, and not accepted as currency, since they can be easily forged and be illegitimate. Some Rupees may be so devoid of color that they actively drain the Rupees around them of their own color, turning them dun. These Rupees are, fittingly, called Rupoors. Rupees can also be shaken to determine if they are not officially issued by the government, as mining and collecting the gemstones that make up a Rupee, and then attempting to pass it off as legally issued currency is a [[Criminal Activity|crime]] and considered forgery.

In Holodrum, Rupees are also used. To denote the difference, they are usually called "Holodrumian Rupees". Hyrulean Rupees are usually referred to as just Rupees. Holodrumian Rupees are sometimes called Rupoors derisively.

In [[Labrynna|Labrynna]], coinage is used, with the face of historical [[Queen Ambi|monarchs]] and Prime Ministers on its front, and a seal from the Labrynnan government on the back. These are rarely used outside of Labrynna, although of course currency exchanges exist.

Various smaller domains may have their own currencies, such as the [[Zuna|Zuna]], who use Shillings. The [[The Gerudo|Gerudo]] are unique in that they have no formalized currency, as far as we know, and primarily use a bartering system. The rest of the world, however, uses either Hyrulean or Holodrumian Rupees, with a minority using Labrynnan coinage.

The exchange rates are intentionally not listed in the [[Beginning - There is more to this Folder than meets the eye|Hyrule Historia]], as—considering current events—it is quite volatile.

The different denominations of Hyrulean Rupees are the following:

### Green Rupee
The base amount in the country's [[economy]], a Green Rupee corresponds to one Rupee.

### Blue Rupees
Blue Rupees are slightly bigger, useful when storing larger amounts of capital, but without being excessive. It is worth 5 Green Rupees.

### Red Rupees
These are heftier in purchasing power, worth about 20 Green Rupees.

### Purple Rupee
It is rare to see these Purple Rupees outside of the hands of the rich, or a [[Capitalist and Nobleman Class|middle-class]] person about to make a significant purchase. Purple Rupees are worth 50 Rupees.

### Silver Rupees
Silver Rupees are brilliant jewels that call the eye of many who flaunt it for its value. While not an uncommon denomination for a member of the [[Capitalist and Nobleman Class|nobility]], it is almost never seen in poorer areas. Their value is 100 Rupees.

### Gold Rupees
Using Gold Rupees is considered a sign of wealth, even among individuals of means. If a commoner is carrying a Gold Rupee, they either stole it, are about to get stolen from, or are on their way to make a life-changing purchase, such as a new house. Its value is 300 Rupees.
