**The Arbiter’s Grounds** was a prison used by the [[Former Hyrulean Government and Royal Family|Royal Family]] during and after the [[First Hyrulean Civil War|firstHyrulean Civil War]], until it was abandoned during [[Ganondorf Dragmire|Dragmire]]’s return 300 years ago. The Arbiter’s Grounds was located in the [[Desert of Mystery, Gerudo Desert|Gerudo Desert]], and built off ancient ruins made by the [[The Gerudo|Gerudo Tribe]].

Only the worst criminals were sentenced to imprisonment in Arbiter’s Grounds. Almost always, criminals sent to the Arbiter’s Grounds were awaiting trial, execution, had been given a life sentence, and were otherwise too dangerous to hold anywhere near society.

The ancient [[Sages|Sages]] were said to be connected to the site, with there being recorded instances of—when particularly dangerous criminals were tried—the Sages appearing to act as the jury and decide the fate of the indicted criminal. This ceased after Ganondorf Dragmire killed the ancient Sages at the beginning of his [[Seven Year Reign|seven year dictatorship]].

The new Sages, who had worked alongside the [[Hero of Time|Hero of Time]] to end Ganondorf’s dictatorship, never attended a trial in the Arbiter’s Grounds. Perhaps they never saw a criminal that required their attention, or the Sages died after their task was complete, unlike the ancient ones.

Some executions were carried out by dropping convicts into pits with large beasts native to the desert, or various other unusual means. It is believed that these beasts starved or were killed by invading forces when the [[Hylians|Hylians]] were forced to flee during the [[The Flood|Flood]]. Likewise, in the chaos of the Flood and Dragmire’s return, the Arbiter’s Grounds was damaged and its prisoners freed or killed. The facility was left derelict for the next 300 years, and is still an abandoned wreckage in the desert to the day of writing, shortly after the [[Revolution and Second Civil War|Revolution]]. The government under the [[Last King of Hyrule|King]] officially warned would-be tourists and treasure hunters to steer clear of the Grounds, citing its weak structural integrity and the chance of an accident.

The appearance of the Arbiter’s Grounds is that of a coliseum, with the interior consisting of a mix of Hylian architecture and ancient Gerudo sandstone, particularly in the lower floors. Much of its interior, especially its bottom half, is likely collapsed and heavily worn. While there are some accounts of daredevils who enter despite conventional and official wisdom, the accounts of such people are to be considered untrustworthy and embellished.
