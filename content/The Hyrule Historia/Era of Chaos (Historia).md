The Era of Chaos is an era of [[Hyrule]]’s history which is considered to have occurred approximately from the years 1150 to 850 before the Hyrulean restoration and [[Seven Year Reign|seven year reign]], although historians suspect that it had no definitive start-date, and records of the era—especially its beginning—are rare and unreliable.

It is believed that prior to the Era of Chaos, a period called the ‘[[Era of Peace (Historia)|Era of Peace]]’ by some, civilization in [[Hyrule]] was beginning to grow and flourish. Tribes that would one day form Hyrule developed towns and communities. Society was largely decentralized, but there is some evidence of cooperation and systems in place for the resolution of disputes between different localities, as well as travel and trade or interchange. Largely, these developments were peaceful and the presence of these decentralized institutions, such as ‘Arbiters’, allowed [[The Hylians|Hylians]] and other species in this era to avoid armed conflict. Some of the towns and villages of the modern day are even based on, or evolutions of, the serene settlements of these ancient Hyruleans.

However, it is believed that this began to change at around 1150 years before the Hyrulean restoration. This date is the agreed-upon estimation for the nebulous beginning of the Era of Chaos, an era which would be marked by conflict which eventually spiraled into a series of bloody wars and deadly power struggles which engulfed the continent in the flames of war.

It is unknown exactly what caused the beginning of the Era of Chaos. Many suspect that the conditions of the preceding era allowed for one or more warlords to rise, who raised deadly armies that matched their mounting ambition for conquest and power. However, in popular mythology, a certain event has been repeated as the cause, and most historians now agree it was the likely initiator of the Era of Chaos.

At some point, word spread throughout Hyrule of a location known as the [[Sacred Realm (Historia)|Sacred Realm]]. Worshipping [[Goddesses and the Gods|Hylia]] in this ancient time, many were familiar with the majestic power of the [[Goddesses and the Gods|Golden Goddesses]] which had protected them from Demons in ancient times, even according to the lore understood at the time. 

The Sacred Realm, they knew, held a treasure of incalculable value: The [[The Triforce (Historia)|Golden Power]], the Triforce. These whispers reached the ears of the warlords. Legends of the power within the Sacred Realm whetted the appetites greedy and ambitious men, as well as the powerful warlords of the area. Sorcerous individuals, and those who employed them, used the extent of knowledge held in this era to divine the location of the Sacred Realm, to interfere and intercede upon the realm of the Gods. 

It was common in this era for every army, or even the locals, to burn their own libraries and books to deny any information to an adversary, which has left us with scant few records of this time or the proceeding eras. We knew that they had great libraries from surviving writings, but alas; they are now ash floating in the wind, pulled by wind for millennia.

Not long into the Era of Chaos, it is suspected that those jockeying for power found the ability to enter the Sacred Realm. By what method or to what extent is unknown, but the few records produced by these power-seekers that remain indicate that for a large part of this time period, those who sought the Power of the Gods believed themselves to be making tangible progress towards gaining ultimate power, and dominion of the Sacred Realm. Among those who came the closest to total victory were a band of interlopers who used their powerful sorcery to become dominant in the race and subdue others. While these strongmen fought and killed for power, the common villagers of the area were pressed into service, killed into the crossfire, or starved as once pure waters turned bitter and tainted with blood.

The Era of Chaos only ended with the intervention of the Ancient [[Sages]]. The inheritors of the [[Goddesses and the Gods|Goddess Hylia]]’s blessings of wisdom, the modern-day [[Former Hyrulean Government and Royal Family|Royal Family]], sought to use their status to banish and diminish the warlords, thereby restoring order to the war-torn nation and unifying the country so that such heinous acts could never occur again. At this same time, Sages emerged from the population and were blessed by the [[Goddesses and the Gods|Goddesses]] and their representatives on this planet to quell the suffering and protect the Sacred Realm from intrusion, or so the story says.

Foremost among these Sages was [[Rauru]], the Sage of Light. Using their awesome might, they dismantled the blood-soaked empires of these petty warlords and interlopers. Rauru closed the gateways to the [[Sacred Realm (Historia)|Sacred Realm]], separating the two worlds. People of the era believed that this was a permanent, irrevocable separation; however, it has become clear, with the actions of [[Ganondorf Dragmire]], that the Sage Rauru was merely able to obscure the entrance to the Sacred Realm. He constructed the [[Temple of Time (Historia)|Temple of Time]] and hid the entrance inside, behind a complicated series of locks. Working in tandem with [[Former Hyrulean Government and Royal Family|the Royal Family]], which founded the country of [[Hyrule]] at the tail end of the Era of Chaos, they built the capital of the country, [[Hyrule Castle Town]], alongside the Temple of Time. Thereby, the secretive structure became merely another church in a growing settlement.

With this action from Rauru to close off the entrance to the Sacred Realm and dispel the mythologies claiming that anyone could gain the Triforce, as well as the actions of the divine forces to disrupt the most progressed of these forces, the stage was set. The Kingdom of Hyrule was founded, led by a group which had popular support from many of the denizens of Hyrule, and who promised they could quell the violence.

The founding of Hyrule is considered, formally, to be the end of the Era of Chaos. However, while the country was founded, it did not take the modern form we know today, as the country was much smaller in size and did not include [[The Zora|Zora]] or [[The Gorons|Goron]] tribes.

The cost of the Era of Chaos was great. Much of our records relating the history of [[Hyrule]] prior to the Era of Chaos is, unfortunately, lost due to this tragic event. Libraries and repositories of knowledge across the continent were burned and scoured, and much of what remained of knowledge was largely unreliable oral histories and personal accounts, passed down from father to son, from mother to daughter. This has made it challenging, in our study to understand the past, to differentiate between what is history, and what are legends.
