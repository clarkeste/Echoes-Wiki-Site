**Akkala Citadel** is a tall structure built near [[Akkala City (Historia)|Akkala City]], in the [[Hyrulean Geography|region]] of the same name. It is on top of a small plateau that overlooks the valley path towards Akkala proper, as well as the parade grounds directly south. Therefore, it is in an extremely strategic position to cut off access in or out of the [[Hyrulean Geography|Akkala region]]. It is currently controlled by the [[The Royalists|Royalist]] faction, and is considered to be almost impossible for [[The Followers of Dragmire, Revolutionaries|the Followers]] to take by conventional means.

As of the time of writing, it is currently the headquarters for the Royalists in the north.
