**Akkala City** is a city in the north-east part of [[Hyrule|Hyrule]], located in the [[Hyrulean Geography|Akkala region]]. It is situated in Torin Valley, straddling Lake Akkala. Nearby, between it and the Follower frontlines, it is protected by [[Akkala Citadel (Historia)|Akkala Citadel]], an imposing fortress manned by the [[The Royalists|Royalists]].

Akkala City is controlled by the Royalists. As a city primarily made up of [[Hylians|Hylians]] and still under Royalist control, it is more supportive of the Royalists than most other cities. It is one of the larger cities in Hyrule, only a little bit smaller than [[Castor City (Historia)|Castor City]].

It is nearby to and supports Akkala Port, which, especially after the fall of [[Kakariko City]] to the [[Hyrulean Republic (Followers)|Revolutionaries]], has seen an increase in activity.
