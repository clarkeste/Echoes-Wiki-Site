The **Commission for the Reclamation of the Golden Power**, sometimes called the **Commission for the Recovery of the Golden Power**, and abbreviated as the **CRGP**, was a team created by the [[Former Hyrulean Government and Royal Family|Royal Family]] to recover the [[The Triforce|Triforce]] in the last days of the monarchy.

When [[Ganondorf]] appeared to the [[Last King of Hyrule|King]] during the [[Festival of the Flood]], he made a request that the King assist him in recovering the Triforce to undo some wicked curse on Hyrule. While initially skeptical, the King’s mind changed when Ganondorf saved him from the following attack on [[Hyrule Castle]] by [[The Followers of Dragmire, Revolutionaries]]. The Last King of Hyrule then founded the Commission for the Reclamation of the Golden Power for one purpose: to find the Triforce pieces and undo a wicked curse placed upon Ganondorf, the Gerudo, and all of Hyrule since time immemorial.

The members of the Commission were [[Princess Zelda|Princess Zelda’s]] current lead bodyguard, [[Link Sahon|Link Sahon]], Ganondorf himself, [[Inl Astor|Inl Astor]]—who was the Minister of Justice—and [[Impa|Impa]], Zelda’s previous lead bodyguard. Impa had been fired due to suspicions against the [[Sheikah|Sheikah]], but at Sahon’s request, she was recruited to serve in the Commission. Despite the capture of his daughter Zelda during the attack on the Castle, the King told the Commission to focus on recovering the Triforce pieces and leave the recovery of Zelda to his agents. Ganondorf used a strange device he referred to as the [[The Timekeeper|Timekeeper]] to help locate the Triforce shards.

The Commission first traveled to [[Kakariko City|Kakariko City]] and recovered a piece in the Sheikah catacombs there.

During that, however, Ganondorf had split off to trail a suspicious figure. This figure was a member of [[The Followers of Dragmire, Revolutionaries]], who had just saved one of their own from a forest fire. Who is unknown, however most agree it was a skilled warrior of theirs—likely, [[Link Steadfast]] or the [[The Confessor|Confessor]].

Regardless, they led Ganondorf directly to a Follower base at Lon Lon Ranch. Ganondorf quickly discovered it had been nothing more than a front for Follower operations, and burned the ranch to the ground. He trapped the Followers inside and requested the Royal authorities recover them. It is thought that he battled the Confessor here and discovered that she had stolen the Triforce of Wisdom from Zelda, but the woman escaped and the piece was not recovered.

Later, the Commission once more united and began their journey towards [[The Zora|Zora’s Domain]]. On the way, they found Princess Zelda, having escaped from the shackles of the Followers with her own skill. She accompanied them to the temple beneath Zora’s Domain, where Ganondorf claimed the next shard hid. Despite Follower attempts to gain the Triforce shard from the structure beneath the Domain before the Commission could, the Followers were foiled.

The following events have only one source—Impa—as all others on this mission died before they could make any recording of it.

Ganondorf took the Triforce Shard from beneath Zora’s Domain despite the warnings of a Follower agent, matching the description of Steadfast. Pulling it out, as the boy had warned, caused the ground the city rested on above them to shake and collapse, destroying large parts of the city. Engineers this author has consulted on the matter believe that the Triforce was powering a device that kept the ground steady, but how is not known. Other observers believe that it was the Followers present who took the piece out first, damaging the Domain. The truth is unknown.

After attempting to alleviate the suffering and requesting Royal humanitarian aid, the Commission departed from Zora’s Domain for their next destination: [[Lost Woods|The Lost Woods]].

Once passing through [[Minishi Town]], the Commission was joined by [[Master Kohga|General Ahoga]]. Inside the woods, they split up. Following details are scarce; however, we know that Ganondorf entered deep into the forest and recovered a shard of the Triforce, while other members—including Astor, Sahon, and General Ahoga—entered the [[Temple of Time]]. After discovering the [[Master Sword]]’s pedestal was empty, Ahoga betrayed them by killing Sahon, and fled. The Commission, down a man, returned to Hyrule Castle.

Only a day prior to [[Revolution and Second Civil War|Revolution]], the Commission arrived at Hyrule Castle. The King, believing correctly that conflict was near, ordered most of the members of the Commission to stay and fulfill their usual roles.

Ganondorf was the only one who continued seeking the Triforce Shards, heading to the [[Desert of Doubt|deep into the Gerudo Desert]] to recover the last one. Upon his return, Hyrule was in the flames of its Revolution.
