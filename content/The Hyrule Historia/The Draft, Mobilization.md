Colloquially, **the Draft** refers to a decree issued by the [[Last King of Hyrule|King]] days before his death and the [[Revolution and Second Civil War|Revolution]], in which he ordered the manpower of the [[Hyrule|Hyrulean]] military to be increased threefold to maintain order in Hyrule. He authorized the use of conscription and drafting to achieve this end. While tens of thousands were levied to this end, the goal was not met before the [[Castle Town|capital]] was overwhelmed.

Under the [[The Royalists|Royalist forces]], a continuation of the old [[Former Hyrulean Government and Royal Family|government]], this decree has remained in effect. While the leader of the Royalist military, [[General Noril|General Noril]], has not attempted to reach the original number, he has used random lotteries and other such conscription tactics to fill the gaps in the army. While both sides of the military attempt to primarily recruit volunteers into the military, with promises of benefits, both have had to resort to limited conscription.
