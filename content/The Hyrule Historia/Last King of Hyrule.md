**The King of Hyrule** was a controversial, but oft admired to man, whose life met a sudden end less than one year ago when he was killed during the [[Revolution and Second Civil War|Revolution]]. He was and is usually referred to only as 'the King' or 'King Hyrule', although his name was Aldriech Calodmir Hyrule. He is only monarch much of [[Hyrule|Hyrule]] remembers.

The King came to power in his thirties, following the death of his father, and ruled for nearly fifty years. He was credited for various things, such as tightening the criminal code and prosecuting the unlawful. He also oversaw the newest wave of increased Hyrulean presence in world politics, and the accompanying growth in the Hyrulean [[Economy|economy]]. However, he was not completely beloved; some criticized his actions as authoritarian, often citing the harsh response to disruptive and sometimes violent protests.

In the decades before the revolution, sentiment against the [[Former Hyrulean Government and Royal Family|monarchy]] began to grow more and more. Around this time, the King had a daughter, who he would name [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]]. The girl's mother and he divorced, however, when the King had suspicions that infidelity induced the mother's second pregnancy, which led to the birth of [[Prince Lusand]]. After their divorce, Lusand and the former Queen departed [[Hyrule Castle]], the home of the monarchy, and would eventually leave the country entirely. The King continued to raise Zelda alone, encouraging her to be the best leader she could be. However, as the threat of rebels became more pressing, King Hyrule became focused on dealing with them. One group, [[The Followers of Dragmire, Revolutionaries]], became dominant.

When [[Ganondorf (Historia)|Ganondorf]] interrupted the last [[Festival of the Flood|Festival of the Flood]] the King oversaw, a little more than a week before the Revolution, things began to change for the King. While initially fearing that Ganondorf would be like his name sake, Ganondorf saved the King from an assassin during a Follower raid on the Castle. After that, the King began to trust Ganondorf and agreed to help him with what he wanted: finding the [[The Triforce|Triforce]], to undo a curse that Ganondorf claimed bound him, Hyrule, and the [[The Gerudo|Gerudo]] into endless conflict.

While Ganondorf's team were recovering the pieces, they also exposed a Follower base at Lon Lon Ranch. Royalist authorities made many arrests.

With those captured there, the King engineered a public execution at Castle Town. However, it went awry when [[Link Steadfast]], the [[Master Kohga|Believer]], and others escaped, saving many of their comrades and fleeing the capital shortly after.

Later, when Ganondorf's team arrived back at Hyrule Castle, the King saw Ganondorf depart for the [[Desert of Doubt]]. The Revolution began in [[Kakariko City|Kakariko City]] soon after, heralding the end of the King and all he held dear. As the Revolutionary forces approached, the King ordered [[Impa|Impa]] to apprehend the [[The Confessor|Confessor]] and redoubled his efforts to find the [[The Pragmatist|leader of the Followers]], whom he believed was living a double life as a member of his [[The Council|Council]]. However, despite capturing the Confessor, the King's men were seemingly not able to kill her before the Revolutionaries arrived. What happened in the final hours at Hyrule Castle is unknown; but the next day, the King was discovered having died from a dagger stab to the back, right through his heart.

Two days after the assault on Hyrule Castle, the King's body was paraded through Castle Town inside of a transparent coffin, and publicly burned in a central square, before his ashes were spread to the wind, to confirm that the man had died.
