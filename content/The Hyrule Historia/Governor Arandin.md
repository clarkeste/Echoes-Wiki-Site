Arandin is a veteran politician and an elder statesman. An activist when he was young, he was elected to be Governor of [[Kakariko City]] fifteen years ago, becoming one of the most well-known and famous governors in all of [[Hyrule]]. He achieved an extremely high approval from his citizenry, who had always been more progressive than other places in Hyrule. They saw Arandin as representing a hope for a better future. As a result, he was consistently re-elected. He even spoke in support of the [[Castle Town Parliamentarians|Parliamentarians in Castle Town]], and often defied the [[Last King of Hyrule|King]]’s orders.

His existence, and Kakariko’s own tradition of mob-rule government, were in many ways inherently an insult to the King’s [[Former Hyrulean Government and Royal Family|government]]. His antics were still tolerated.

However, after he refused to institute a curfew and authorize a surveillance program following a [[The Confessor|rebel attack]], the King chose to sack him from his position. The King ruled that the law that allowed Kakariko to have elections for its governors was not allowed within their [[Autonomy Clause|Autonomy Clause]]; and therefore, appointed the next Governor directly. The Governor after Arandin was named [[Governor of Kakariko|Kakan Rarurn]], and served as Governor for four years up until the [[Revolution and Second Civil War|Revolution]].

Arandin lived the intervening years as a private citizen, occasionally endorsing certain movements or petitions, but largely staying out of public life—believing that there was little he could do.

After the Revolution threw out his replacement and soon overthrew the King, Arandin was instrumental in preventing chaos in the city. Due to his legacy, the [[The Followers of Dragmire, Revolutionaries|Revolutionaries]] agreed to put Arandin back in control shortly after the revolution. As of now, Arandin has been Governor of Kakariko again for a year. The veteran, cunning, but according to his friends, compassionate politician has struggled with his city, but is reportedly glad he is the one entrusted with it in this difficult time.

However, his position has only been temporary; the intention of the Revolutionary government is to soon host an election in Kakariko City. Arandin has expressed his interest in re-election, but it seems clear he will have competition.
