**Reincarnation** refers to the [[Goddesses and the Gods|religious]] belief that, after death, life begins anew in a new body with limited to no memories of past lives. The exact mechanics of this belief vary between different faiths, with some thinking that one soul can inhabit multiple bodies, or that life only begins again after death. This belief is present in most popular religions across the world.

The origin story for this belief is found in the legend of Hylia's rebirth to save the planet. It is believed by some that the Goddess Hylia, who was entrusted to guard the world and the [[The Triforce|Triforce]] by the Golden Goddesses, was unable to use the Triforce when demonic forces threatened to destroy the world. Her godhood prevented her from using the power. And so, she was reborn as a human woman the forebearers, and lived an entire life from infant to adulthood. When she was ready, her memories were restored. She used the Triforce to then banish the demonic forces. Some versions of this story include a hero clad in green being reborn alongside the Goddess, but this may have been added to the legend later. 

It is believed by those who subscribe to this story that the descendants Hylia reborn became the [[Former Hyrulean Government and Royal Family|Royal Family]]. However, there are various other stories for the original matron of the Royal Family; while many agree that she was guided or related to the Goddess Hylia in some way, most involve her being a descendent or simply a disciple of the Goddess Hylia. In truth, the reincarnation theory of the Goddess Hylia is highly suspect and unlikely.

The second myth most related to this theory is that of green-clothed Heroes, such as the [[Hero of Time|Hero of Time]] and the [[Hero of the Flood|Hero of the Flood]]. While there are legends of a Hero in green prior to the Hero of Time, they are difficult to verify and therefore are not considered to be proven parts of the historical record. 

It is believed that every time a Hero, clad in green, saves the world using the [[Master Sword|Master Sword]], it is the same soul of the same boy, reborn into a new body.

There is a significant amount of scientific research that suggests the reincarnation theory may be true. This includes a few recorded cases of children knowing information about dead individuals that—at the time—were completely unknown by public records.
