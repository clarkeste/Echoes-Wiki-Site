**The Hero of Time** was a figure born over four hundred years ago, during the end of the first [[First Hyrulean Civil War|Hyrulean Civil War]]. While little of his childhood is known, it is known that he was orphaned only months after birth by the Civil War and grew up somewhere inside the [[Lost Woods|Lost Woods]]. When he was 9 years old, only weeks before [[Ganondorf Dragmire|Ganondorf Dragmire]] killed the [[King Arkenalan Bosmison Hyrule|King of Hyrule]], he had a brief encounter with the [[Princess Zelda|Princess Zelda]] of that era. However, the two would not meet again until [[Seven Year Reign|seven long years]] later, when the Hero of Time returned, wielding the [[Master Sword|Sword that Seals the Darkness]].

Together, he and the Zelda evaded Ganondorf’s attempts to end their lives and awoke [[Sages|seven wise individuals, called Sages]], from each of the tribes across Hyrule. Shortly after awakening all seven of them, Ganondorf forced their hand into action by capturing the Princess. The Hero of Time stormed Ganondorf’s remodeled [[Hyrule Castle|Hyrule Castle]], encountering the Demon King at the top of the spire. There, the two did battle. When the Hero of Time, with all his strength, couldn’t beat Ganondorf alone, the Sages and Zelda arrived to assist him in the fight. Together, they sealed away Ganondorf, a seal that would create peace in Hyrule for one hundred years.

With this action complete, the Hero of Time disappeared from the world. For the rest of their lives, those who knew him best said little of his fate; all we know is that those close to him, including Zelda, believed he was robbed of a proper life. They felt that the world had taken his heart and soul away; that he would wander through a wasted life, with no words to say. That he would never truly feel or love, due to what others did to him.

Regardless, the Hero of Time became an icon—*the* icon—of bravery, courage, and resolve in Hyrule. His tales are still sung, his legacy insurmountable. While there are legends and myths of heroes in green in the distant past, just like the Hero of Time, this individual is the first that history knows for sure made those legends come true. His inspiration is eternal; one hundred years later, [[Hero of the Flood|another hero clad in green saved Hyrule]].

The Hero of Time’s name was [[The Name Link|Link]]. His exploits inspired thousands, all the way to the modern day, to name their sons Link. However, due to his time as an orphan, his last name is unknown.
