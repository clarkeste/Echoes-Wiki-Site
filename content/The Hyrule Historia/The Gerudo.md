**The Gerudo** are a desert-dwelling people, who have historically been seen as thieves and outlaws. They were the people of [[Ganondorf Dragmire|Ganondorf Dragmire]], although very few Gerudo were directly involved in Dragmire's [[Seven Year Reign|seven year reign]]. After [[The Flood|the Flood]], [[The Hylians|the Hylians]] sought revenge and [[Hylian-Gerudo War|invaded]] the [[Desert of Mystery, Gerudo Desert|Gerudo Desert]].

The Gerudo, being [[The Humans|human]] a common ancestor with the [[The Hylians|Hylians]]. They have lived in [[Hyrule|Hyrule]] or its surrounding lands for just as long as the Hylians. At some point, due to a quirk of biology we are not familiar with, it is believed that the birth rate of Gerudo males slowly fell until it was almost non-existent. As a necessity, [[Economy|trade]] and cultural exchange increased among the newly-born [[Hyrule|Kingdom of Hyrule]] and the Gerudo people. Many Hylians and Gerudo married, both out of necessity and kinship. For centuries, only one Gerudo male has been born to the tribe every one-hundred years. Due to this rarity, they are often made King as a matter of birth.

During the [[First Hyrulean Civil War|Hyrulean Civil War]], the Gerudo were as split as their Hylian siblings. As the war progressed, most rallied around the leadership of their king, Ganondorf Dragmire, the first man born into their tribe in a century. 

Under Dragmire’s orders, the Gerudo sided with the victorious [[Former Hyrulean Government and Royal Family|Royal Family]], gaining a reputation as thieves for their tactics. While Ganondorf’s strategies were brutal and drew criticism from his kin, the king of the Gerudo and future Demon King was undeterred. He pledged fealty to the [[King Arkenalan Bosmison Hyrule|King of Hyrule]], who was thankful to the Gerudo. Ganondorf used his newfound rapport with the King to move throughout Hyrule freely. 

With this freedom, he discovered the location of the [[The Triforce (Historia)|Triforce]], a source of near limitless power. Then using hired mercenaries and elite Gerudo, he betrayed and killed Hyrule’s King, conquering [[Castle Town (Historia)|Castle Town]] and [[Hyrule Castle (Historia)|Hyrule Castle]] in the process. He then accessed the Triforce through the [[Sacred Realm (Historia)|Sacred Realm]], and gained one third of it—the Triforce of Power. Using it, Ganondorf completed his coup d'état by defeating what remained of the Royal army, and forcing the rest of the country, such as the [[The Gorons|Gorons]] and [[Zora]], to bend to his iron will.

Ganondorf Dragmire reigned over the Kingdom for seven long years. Using his influence, he sent his agents across the Kingdom to find both the runaway [[Princess Zelda (Historia)|King’s daughter]], who held another piece of the Triforce, and the young [[Hero of Time|Hero of Time]], who bore the final piece.

Ganondorf promised his people, the Gerudo, that they would one day inhabit the green pastures of Hyrule, a land far more hospitable than the desert. However, that day never came; until the final day of his rule, he believed that the lands were too dangerous to invite the Gerudo into. Even the Gerudo in Ganondorf’s forces were slowly phased out in favor of [[The Monsters|monstrous beings]] who Ganondorf spawned, or were allured by his overwhelming, intoxicating malice.

After the Hero of Time spoke with [[Sages|Nabooru]], an influential lieutenant of Ganondorf’s, the Gerudo began to turn on Ganondorf. Following the lead of Nabooru, they ousted Ganondorf from power alongside the Hero of Time, the King’s daughter, and all the other peoples of Hyrule. Despite their role in ending his reign, distrust was sowed between the rest of Hyrule and the Gerudo.

A century later, their former King, Dragmire, miraculously returned from his assumed death. He was slain by the [[Hero of the Flood]], but not before he laid waste to the Kingdom. The people of Hyrule chose to exile the Gerudo people for the actions of their former leader. When they, of course, refused to leave their homes, violence ensued.

Very few of the Gerudo joined together with Dragmire during the [[The Flood|Flood]] and Dragmire’s rampage of terror. Yet, Hyrule Kingdom still deemed that the Gerudo were dangerous. [[Hylian-Gerudo War|Hyrulean forces invaded the Desert in which the Gerudo resided]], and forced the Gerudo people out of their homes. Through a series of bloody battles, the [[The Hylians|Hylians]] pushed the Gerudo deep into the desert, and away from civilization. This place became known as the [[Desert of Doubt (Historia)|Desert of Doubt]].

Since then, the Gerudo have rotted in this isolated desert, living by scavenging and scrounging the lifeless dunes. Their only neighbor is the island-nation of the [[Zuna Republic (Historia)|Zuna Republic]], through which the Gerudo can engage in international [[Economy|trade]] and diplomacy. It is through the Zuna Republic that the Gerudo have any voice in the world stage at all, although both are extremely minor groups, of little consideration by most world leaders.

Gerudo are considered to be an ethnicity of human, much like the Hylians. They tend to have dark skin, amber or green eyes, red hair, a tall-stature, and a sharp nose. However, as with any people, this is not absolute. For example, their skin color, comes in many different tones, such as the common light brown, an olive-greenish variation, or various lighter tones. 

Due to a quirk of their biology, the Gerudo have dominant genes; this means the Gerudo are a tribe that consists almost entirely of women, with a male being born only once every hundred years. According to tradition, this male is destined to become their king. When there is no male to be their king, the Gerudo are led by a chief with the position passed on from mother to daughter. 

The most recent male is [[Ganondorf (Historia)|Ganondorf]], who left his people in the care of a Chief while he traveled to Hyrule. There, attempting right the wrongs of the past, he met his demise at the hands of [[Link Steadfast (Historia)|Link Steadfast]]. 

The lack of males of their own ethnicity means that the Gerudo primarily enter relationships with other peoples. Before, this was [[The Hylians|Hylians]]; now, many Gerudo take [[Zuna]] husbands. 

Gerudo names are structured in reverse name order compared to most of Hyrule, with their family name coming before their given name. For example, a Gerudo with the given name Hajei would refer to herself as "Makeela Hajei", with Makeela as the family name. Gerudo have a lifespan nearly identical to that of humans, but with a quicker old age; likely due to the harsh conditions in which they live.

Many rights groups in Hyrule have petitioned the Royal Family to return the Gerudo their lands, or at least to allow them to once more live in Hyrule. However, to this day, this has not happened. The Revolutionary Government has technically lifted the exile, but—despite saying they intend to—have not yet created any program to facilitate the return of Gerudo, mor formally invited them back into their old lands.  Whether the Gerudo are even interested in accepting this Hylian charity after centuries of mistreatment, and the death of Ganondorf, is unknown.
