How long the **Royal Family** and previously sole **Hyrulean government** reigned is unknown; however, most historians agree that it began long before the first [[First Hyrulean Civil War|Hyrulean Civil War]], putting it at hundreds of years. Despite its ideals being succeeded by the [[The Royalists|Royalist faction]], the **Royal Family**, its [[The Council (Historia)|council]], and other institutions prior to the [[Revolution and Second Civil War|Revolution]] are no longer surviving or in power. The sole, most legitimate survivor is [[Prince Lusand (Historia)|Prince Lusand]].

The structure of the Hyrulean government is that of a monarchy, with a strong, active leader in the form of the head of the Royal Family. This monarch is called the King or Queen. The sons and daughters, or Princes and Princesses, of the monarch succeed them upon their death, the eldest rising to the throne regardless of gender. The siblings of the monarch, likewise, usually are given powerful positions in the Royal government that the Royal Family oversees, and in some rare cases, they or a trusted confidant may act as a Regent if the heir is too young or not ready.

The Royal Family has always resided in [[Hyrule Castle (Historia)|Hyrule Castle]], and despite their influence, they were far from the only organ of the government. Beneath them were many in important positions running agencies or branches of the government. Most important among these were the [[The Council (Historia)|Council of Ministers]], the King’s closest advisors. These Ministers, or Councilors, ran different agencies of the government. At the time of the Revolution, there were four members of the Council besides the King: [[Lakoh (Historia)|Lakoh]], the Minister of the Interior, [[Inl Astor|Astor]], the Minister of Justice, [[Nophandus]], Minister of Hylia, and [[Chancellor Collin Phurn|Collin Phurn]], Chancellor of Economics.

The origins of the Royal Family are not clear. The Royal Family is, in legend, thought to be of divine lineage. Their wisdom is said to be one of the reasons for [[Hyrule|Hyrule]]’s success. Additionally, a piece of the [[The Triforce (Historia)|Golden Power]]—the Triforce of Wisdom—has been carried down their ancestry, manifesting itself in the female members of the Royal Family [[Princess Zelda (Historia)|Zelda]], supposedly the name of the first Princess of Hyrule, is also a name given to many women in the family.

The Royal Family has always been the final authority in Hyrule, except for when they have been deposed—such as [[Ganondorf Dragmire|Dragmire]]’s [[Seven Year Reign|seven year reign]] following the death of the [[King Arkenalan Bosmison Hyrule|King]] of the era, or the [[Revolution and Second Civil War|current day Revolution]]. Therefore, the consequences of Hyrule’s policies have often been laid at their feet. Among these was a policy that is still criticized to this day; the expulsion of the [[the Gerudo|Gerudo]] from Hyrule and their desert, and the Hylian colonization of the area.

Following the [[Hero of the Flood|Hero of the Flood’s]] victory over Ganondorf Dragmire, the Kingdom was devastated; its [[King Daphnes Nohansen|King]] and Princess both died at the hands of Ganondorf Dragmire. The Princess’ brother took the throne and sought revenge, as did most of Hyrule.

Seeking to prevent another Dragmire from ever existing, the newly-crowned King [[Hylian-Gerudo War|invaded the Gerudo Desert]] and razed their structures, forcing the Gerudo to flee [[Desert of Doubt (Historia)|deeper into the Desert]]. Their forces were pursued by [[The Hylians|Hylians]], eventually leading to a climactic battle at the Dunes of the Dead. Since then, the land the Gerudo once lived in has been settled by Hyruleans, creating [[Calditin City (Historia)|Calditin City]], and the Gerudo have been cut off from most of the world.

In the three centuries since, more and more criticism for the Royal Family has emerged despite Hyrule’s growing prosperity on the world stage. While it was mostly tolerated by those in power, certain organizations had, in the eyes of the Royal Family gone too far and merited police action by the King and his Ministers, known as the Council. However, the actions of the Royal government to maintain order were clearly not enough, as just one year ago, the King was overthrown and Hyrule Castle fell into enemy hands. Just as when Dragmire attacked three hundred years ago, the Revolution killed both [[Last King of Hyrule|the last King of Hyrule]] and the Princess, Zelda.

Currently, the most direct heir to the throne is the King’s son, who lives with his mother in [[Holodrum (Historia)|Holodrum]]. It is thought by gossip-mongers that the King divorced his wife because he believed it was possible that his son was conceived with another individual, but there is no evidence that this is the case. [[General Noril (Historia)|General Noril]] and the Royalist faction in the civil war have announced their intentions to invite the King’s son to the country as soon as it is safe.
