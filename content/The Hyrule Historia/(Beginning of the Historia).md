### Foreword
This is a book, that in our era, is needed more than ever. In light of the tumultuous changes around [[Hyrule|Hyrule]], it is necessary for us to look in the past so that may determine what to do in the present.

It is therefore that I and other authors have revised this centuries-old document once more, the Hyrule Historia, to provide as unbiased a view as possible of Hyrulean history, and all the events leading up to the modern day. I would encourage anyone with the means to, to read this book. It is vital to stay informed in these difficult times.

This document covers a variety of topics; important figures, historical events, organizations, and other terminology. It acts as an encyclopedia for a world of thieves and [[The Followers of Dragmire, Revolutionaries|Revolutionaries]], of the [[Capitalist and Nobleman Class|noble]], despicable, and all those between.

If there is a particular topic you wish to read about, it is suggested you consult the table of contents. Another way to learn from this Historia, however, and the way suggested by this author, is to investigate starting with basic or relevant topics, and using keywords to learn related things. When a keyword is mentioned for the first time in an entry, and it has its own separate entry in the Hyrule Historia, a page number will exist next to the word. For instance, the first mention of the [[Revolution and Second Civil War|Revolution]] in a given entry will have a page number that will allow you to navigate to that page, as shown with that mention of the Revolution.
