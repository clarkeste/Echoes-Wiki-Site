**Inl Astor** was the Minister of Justice following the death under strange circumstances of the previous minister, [[Ealden Frendell|Ealden Frendell]]. He took over when dissidence against the [[Former Hyrulean Government and Royal Family|Royal Family]] was at an all-time high, approximately six years ago. Collaborating with his colleague, [[Nophandus|Nophandus]], Astor did much to prevent the spread of such groups. One of the groups that always eluded the [[Former Hyrulean Government and Royal Family|Royal government]] was the [[The Followers of Dragmire, Revolutionaries|Followers of Dragmire]], which eventually started absorbing members from other groups that were easier to quash. Astor believed for a long time that a member of the [[The Council|Council]] was secretly the leader of the Followers of Dragmire, an individual who they only knew as “[[The Pragmatist|The Pragmatist]]”, and whose existence they knew only from intercepted letters. Eventually, Astor grew to suspect Nophandus. Astor himself was well-liked by the [[Last King of Hyrule|King]], with some going so far as to say that the King treated him more like a son than he treated [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]] like a daughter.

Astor was a member of the [[Commission for the Reclamation of the Golden Power|Commission for the Recovery of the Golden Power]], technically the leader of the team. Despite his resistance to working with [[Ganondorf|Ganondorf]], he successfully worked with [[Impa|Impa]], Ganondorf, and [[Link Sahon|Link Sahon]]—who he and the King had proclaimed to be the new Hero—to recover the [[The Triforce|Triforce]] shards at [[Kakariko City|Kakariko City]], [[The Zora|Zora’s Domain]], and the [[Lost Woods|Lost Woods]]. At the Lost Woods, the Commission was joined by [[Master Kohga|General Ahoga]]; all of its members, except for Ganondorf, attempted to find the [[Master Sword|Master Sword]], but found it nowhere inside the [[Temple of Time|Temple of Time]]. During the diversion, Ahoga revealed himself to be a turncoat member of the Followers, and eliminated Link Sahon.

Later, Astor returned to [[Hyrule Castle|Hyrule Castle]] at the request of the King as the situation in [[Hyrule|Hyrule]] quickly deteriorated. He was present when the Followers [[Broadcast|broadcast]] an image through every torch and watery reflection in [[Castle Town|Castle Town]] that claimed that [[Link Steadfast|Link Steadfast]] was the [[Hero of Time|Hero of Time]], reborn. Proved by Steadfast holding what looked to be the Master Sword.

What happened in the Castle day between that and the assault on Castle Town is largely unknown. However, we know that there was panic inside the Castle, and it was about this time that [[Lakoh|Lakoh]] defected, and Nophandus was assassinated inside Hyrule Castle. Astor stayed in the Castle until the very end, likely perishing during the Follower assault on the Castle. His body was exhumed from the wreckage and burned, like that of the King and other council members. Now, he is a martyr, much like Princess Zelda. Many believe he should’ve been married to her highness.
