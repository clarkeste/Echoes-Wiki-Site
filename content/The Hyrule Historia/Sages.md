In [[Hyrule|Hyrulean]] history, there were two groups of **Sages**. First, the Ancient Sages, who are said to have watched over Hyrule since before the reign of [[Former Hyrulean Government and Royal Family|the Royal Family]], and the modern Sages, who were recruited by the [[Hero of Time]].

Little is known about the Ancient Sages, except that there were six of them and they often appeared in times of great strife or danger to consul the King or Queen of that era. They often stood judgement on particularly vile criminals at [[Arbiter's Grounds]]. One of these Sages was called Rauru. While he is often portrayed as their leader in popular myth, it is more likely he was a junior member.

The Ancient Sages, at some point, were killed. It is most likely that [[Ganondorf Dragmire]], the Demon King, killed them when he entered the [[Sacred Realm]] and began his [[Seven Year Reign|seven years]] of evil. All except Rauru were killed.

And so, new Sages had to be found to stop the Demon King. 

The six Sages of modern times were a group of individuals recruited by the Hero of Time and who assisted in battling and sealing away the [[Ganondorf Dragmire|Demon King]]. They were usually notable persons among their people, who had no knowledge of their power and destiny as a Sage until it was awoken during a particularly dire event. They assisted the Hero of Time at [[Hyrule Castle|Hyrule Castle]], and were joined by a seventh Sage, who was none other than the [[Princess Zelda|Princess]] of that era four hundred years ago. The surviving ancient Sage, Rauru, continued his service to the world in this new group.

Some believe that the Sages still watch over us to this day, with most surviving text indicating that the Sages become ageless when awoken. If they do, then they likely live inside the [[Sacred Realm|Sacred Realm]], and a building called the 'Temple of Light', where the Hero of Time is said to have first met Rauru.

The following is information about each Sage.

### Rauru
Rauru was the Sage of Light, and an ancient Sage. This means he pre-dates all the other modern Sages, and was the only Ancient Sage who survived Dragmire’s murders. It is believed that Ganon killed the other five Ancient Sages during his [[Seven Year Reign|seven year reign]]. 

Rauru was an old [[The Hylians|Hylian]] man, who wielded great magical power—however, his most important trait was his wisdom which he used to guide Hero of Time.

### Saria
Saria was the Sage of the Forest, and the first Sage awoken by the Hero of Time. The exact nature of Saria's life beforehand is unknown, however it is believed she was a childhood friend of the Hero of Time. Some believe she was a [[Kokiri|Kokiri]], however that is not confirmed.

### Darunia
Darunia was the Sage of Fire, and the second Sage awoken by the Hero of Time. Prior to his awakening, the [[The Gorons|Goron]] named Darunia was a mighty and beloved chieftain of his people, who led them through the tribulations of the [[First Hyrulean Civil War|Hyrulean Civil War]] and the cruel reign of Ganon. When his people were at their most desperate—starved and trapped by Ganon—the Hero of Time saved them. In the process, Darunia awoke as a Sage.

### Ruto
Ruto was the Sage of Water, and the third Sage awoken by the Hero of Time. Prior to her awakening, she was a headstrong [[Zora|Zora]] Princess, growing up in the difficult times of the Hyrulean Civil War and the seven year reign of Ganon. For the subversive resistance of Ruto and her people, Ganon froze Zora's Domain. However, with the Hero of Time's help, Ruto was able to free her people from the ice, and awoke as the Sage of Water.

### Nabooru
Nabooru was the Sage of Spirit, and the fourth Sage awoken by the Hero of Time. Prior to her awakening, the charismatic Nabooru was the second-in-command of the [[The Gerudo|Gerudo]], and Ganon's right-hand 'man'. However, she grew disillusioned and attempted to move against Ganon shortly before his rise to power after discovering of his plans to conquer and dominate Hyrule. Unfortunately, the plan was for naught, and she spent the next seven years in her former headquarters that had been turned into a penal colony. However, the Hero of Time soon helped Nabooru escape and defeat her captors, leading to her awakening as a Sage.

### Sheikah Sage
The name of the sixth Sage awoken by the Hero of Time is lost to entropy. However, we know that she was a [[the Sheikah|Sheikah]]—among the last of the Sheikah operating Hyrule during this time—and was a former bodyguard for Princess Zelda. In the latter half of Dragmire’s reign, she became the protector of [[Kakariko Village|Kakariko Village]]. Alongside the Hero of Time, she descended down into Village's well and defeated a dark demon who threatened to destroy Kakariko. She then became the Sage of Shadow.

Later on, reports of those seeing the Sages report she was succeeded by a another Sheikah Sage, whose identity is unknown.

### Princess Zelda
Zelda was the final, seventh Sage. She, the Princess of all [[Hyrule|Hyrule]] on the run, likely knew of her destiny for many years. She was awakened during the final confrontation with Ganon. What she was the Sage of is unknown.

In honor of both the ancient Sages and those that assisted the Hero of Time, the [[Sage Temple]] was constructed.
