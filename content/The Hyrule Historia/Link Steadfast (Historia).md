**Link Steadfast** is an influential [[The Followers of Dragmire, Revolutionaries|Revolutionary]] that sought the destruction of the old [[Former Hyrulean Government and Royal Family|monarchy]], and served as the right-hand man of [[The Confessor (Historia)|Confessor]]. He is alleged to be wielding the [[Master Sword (Historia)|Master Sword]].


Link Steadfast was a child born to a [[Crafting|blacksmith]] of middling class called [[Aadin Steadfast|Aadin Steadfast]]. Due to coincidence and the adventurous nature of both children, as well as Aadin taking a few contracts for swords from [[Capitalist and Nobleman Class|noblemen]], he and [[Princess Zelda (Historia)|Zelda]] met. Despite reportedly being great friends, concerns over the security of the Princess led to her no longer being allowed to see Steadfast.

In their early teen years, the two met once more. However, by this time, Link Steadfast had joined a group of dissidents called the [[Castle Town Parliamentarians|Castle Town Parliamentarians]]. While this did not seem to initially come between their very close friendship—or romance, if the gossipmongers are to be believed—that changed as soon as a report was filed to the [[Former Hyrulean Government and Royal Family|King]]. The report discovered clandestine activities by the Parliamentarians, and retaliation was carried out. Zelda ceased her relationship with Steadfast. Whether Link and Zelda were truly in a romantic relationship is unknown, but it is a scandalous idea that has gripped the imaginations of many, without good reason. Why a Princess would fall for a commoner is beyond this author.

Steadfast, with most of his colleagues arrested, fled, unable to take his father. His father fell into poverty despite the support of Zelda and later died. His neighbors, however, reported that his son visited him often, under aliases—not realizing that Steadfast was a wanted man they should've reported.

There are no official or unofficial records of what happened to Link Steadfast during this period. It is likely, as he was a wanted criminal, he used aliases to evade the authorities. However, it is now known that sometime during this period, he was seduced by ideology and joined [[The Followers of Dragmire, Revolutionaries|the Followers of Dragmire]]. Later, he became the right-hand man of one of its radical leaders, the [[The Confessor (Historia)|Confessor]]. 

Steadfast was likely corrupted into a cynical man during his time on the run. He chose to ally with a group that wanted to kill a friend who trusted in him, and her entire family. So much that he became indispensable to a cruel woman who sought to kill what he’d once held dear. He chose the Parliamentarians over our Princess. 

Years later, shortly before the [[Revolution and Second Civil War|Revolution]], [[Ganondorf (Historia)|Ganondorf]] reported fighting who would later be identified as Steadfast when he discovered the Follower base at Lon Lon Ranch. Steadfast, however, escaped.

On the day of the Revolution, hours before it began, Steadfast was in a raid along with the Confessor and the [[Master Kohga (Historia)|Believer]] on [[Lanayru Citadel (Historia)|Lanayru Citadel]] to free prisoners, including some of his old Parliamentarian compatriots. At the evening of that day, he and the Confessor staged the now famous—or infamous—[[Broadcast|speech which was broadcast]] into every water reflection and fiery torch in every major city in [[Hyrule|Hyrule]], where he showed that he had gained the [[Master Sword (Historia)|Master Sword]]. This was the last push the Revolutionaries needed to launch their attack.

Link Steadfast was present at the battle for [[Hyrule Castle (Historia)|Hyrule Castle]]; whether or not he met the Princess one last time, or was the one to kill her, is unknown. However, it is known that—alongside the Confessor—he fought Ganondorf as the Castle collapsed around them, only barely managing to slay the [[The Gerudo|Gerudo]] King.

Since then, less is known about Steadfast's activities. He was present in the early months, still serving as a bodyguard and right-hand man for the Confessor. However, he is seen more rarely now and likely is active on missions away from Hyrule Castle.

The issue of whether he truly is the reincarnation of the [[Hero of Time|Hero]], as his ownership of the Master Sword would imply, is in great contention. Many on the [[The Royalists|Royalist]] side, naturally, say he is not; for how could a man who overthrows the monarchy be the Hero, when all previous Heroes’ quests have ended in its restoration?

Like many others, Steadfast shared [[The Name Link|the name Link]].
