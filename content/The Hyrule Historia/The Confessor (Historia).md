**The Confessor** is the leader of the [[The Followers of Dragmire, Revolutionaries|Revolutionary government and Followers of Dragmire]]. She is an individual with seemingly no name or history. She first appeared six years ago when she intervened during the Royal Army's attempt to quell a protest in [[Kakariko City (Historia)|Kakariko City]]. She engaged the Royalist Forces and destroyed much of their equipment, foiling their attempt. Bearing the same symbols and appearance as members of the Followers of Dragmire, she was identified as another [[The Chosen (Historia)|Chosen]].

Later that week, [[The Council (Historia)|councilman]] [[Ealden Frendell|Frendell]], the Minister of Justice who had been assigned to the case, died under mysterious circumstances in his study. Some have proposed the theory that the Confessor was involved in the murder, but there is no evidence of this being the case.

The Confessor spent the following years attacking Royalist targets, and recruiting her own group of the Followers, creating another branch in addition to the [[Master Kohga (Historia)|Believer]]'s and the [[The Sorcerer|Sorcerer]]'s. This branch is considered to have been more liberal, less strict, and more individualistic compared to the other branches of the Followers. It included [[Link Steadfast (Historia)|Link Steadfast]], who acted as the Confessor's right-hand man. The Confessor and her minions spent many months attacking Royalist forts, transports, and supply lines; often targeting their ability to respond to civil unrest.

In the days before the [[Revolution and Second Civil War|Revolution]], the Confessor fought [[Ganondorf (Historia)|Ganondorf]] and the [[Commission for the Reclamation of the Golden Power|Commission for the Reclamation of the Golden Power]] in [[Lost Woods (Historia)|The Lost Woods]]. She was attempting to stop them from gaining the [[The Triforce (Historia)|Triforce]] Shard in the forest, likely believing that the monarchy gaining the Triforce would be the end of their revolutionary movement. She was unsuccessful in her attempt, but managed to escape with her life.

However, as the [[Last King of Hyrule|King]] became more militant, violent, and dedicated in crushing the Followers, the Confessor—perhaps under the orders, or after conspiring with the [[The Pragmatist|Pragmatist]]—began the Revolution. To start this, she issued a '[[Broadcast|broadcast]]' by using magic to show moving images in every torch and watery reflection in every major city in [[Hyrule|Hyrule]]. This broadcast showed Link Steadfast, wielding the [[Master Sword (Historia)|Master Sword]]. This was intended to reduce the divine legitimacy of the [[Former Hyrulean Government and Royal Family|Royal Family]], and it succeeded.

At the same time, the Confessor but likely also the Pragmatist, used their resources and contacts to stage a revolution in [[Kakariko City (Historia)|Kakariko City]] that forced the [[Governor of Kakariko (Historia)|Governor]] to flee. The city was soon captured by Revolutionary militants. Using the city as a base, the Followers—all branches now united—marched on [[Castle Town (Historia)|Hyrule Castle Town]]. There, they sieged the city and, using spies inside Hyrule Castle, killed the King and [[Princess Zelda (Historia)|Princess Zelda]]. It is believed that the Confessor and Steadfast were among those inside the Castle, and that the two of them dealt the final blow to the new and loyal [[Ganondorf (Historia)|Ganondorf]], who died attempting to protect the King and the remaining members of the [[The Council (Historia)|Council]].

In the weeks following, the Followers secured the east of the country and pushed the [[The Royalists|Royalist]] to the west. They began the transition into becoming a provisional government, with the stated intention of holding democratic elections. The Confessor then became the acting Head of State, tasked with stabilizing and creating a new government. Afterwards, she appointed the defector, [[Lakoh (Historia)|Lakoh]], and—after political pressure—the Sorcerer, among others, to high-ranking political positions in the new government. The Confessor still acts as a leader for the ‘New Hyrulean’ government, but her role is becoming less and less important as she seeks to soon host democratic elections.

The Confessor is still the ultimate authority in the Revolutionary government, however. She knows the secrets of the Royal Family and is a powerful, well-loved member. She is considered the Follower leader who has most pushed for democracy and freedom; with Lakoh being too unwilling to make large changes, and Sorcerer too radical and fervent to burn everything of the old government down, regardless of who suffers.
