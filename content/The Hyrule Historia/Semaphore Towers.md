Semaphores, or Semaphore Towers, are a system of visual telegram towers that runs much of [[Hyrule]], particularly its most important routes. It is used for high-priority civilian messages, news, and government or military usages. It allows a message that would otherwise take a week to transmit via courier to reach the other side of a continent in a matter of hours.

These optical telegraphs function by using the aforementioned chain of towers. In each tower is an operator, who has access to at least two mounted telescopes pointing to at least two other towers; one behind them in the chain, and one ahead. Using these highly magnified telescopes, the operator watches for visual signals from the other Tower that spell out words or represent phrases.

Once they’ve read or understood this encoded message, they re-transmit visually on their own tower in a way that is visible to the next Semaphore tower in the chain. The process then repeats. Information this way can travel thirty to forty times faster than a courier, allowing information to travel around the country in a matter of hours or even minutes. Commanders can ask the General Staff of their military questions and recieve answers or new orders in a matter of an hour.

These Semaphore Towers are approximately 6-8 kilometers apart from one another, and are 10-20 meters tall. They use strong telescopes to see textual details. 

The main way that Semaphore Towers transmit information visually is by by using written messages boards visible to the next Tower in the chain, written using standardized lettering.

Once they the other Tower has read this encoded message, they re-write it on a board on their own tower that is visible to the next Semaphore Tower in the chain. The process then repeats.

However, this method often fails during fog, rain, or other heavy weather. 

These optical telegraph Towers are therefore also equipped with strong lime-light spotlights, which are directed at the next Tower in the chain, as well as the previous. With this light, the Tower operator is able to use a series of flashes and pauses to transmit a message, even through heavy weather.

If the lime-lights also fail, the Towers are equipped with an older form of communication that these Towers used. These are mechanical arms that can be aligned in different positions to represent different symbols, letters, phrases, or words.

Semaphores Towers are equipped with a variety of material for the purposes of easing communication, including codebooks for different mechanical arm positions, for interpreting limelight messages, and a standardized printing of the Hylian alphabet. Tower operators must show a proper understanding of all three systems of communication, including the ability to properly write clear letters in the same font and style as the printed-out alphabet in each tower, so that minimal information is lost between Semaphores.

Semaphore Towers are often built in or near to villages, towns, cities, or stables and other minor settlements.
