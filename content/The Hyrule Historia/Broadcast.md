'**Broadcast**' was the name given to the [[The Confessor|Confessor]] and [[Link Steadfast|Link Steadfast]]'s message that set off the [[Revolution and Second Civil War|Revolution]].

At the eve of that Revolution, he and the Confessor staged the now famous—or infamous—speech which was broadcast into every watery reflection and fiery torch in every major city in [[Hyrule|Hyrule]], where he showed that he had gained the [[Master Sword|Master Sword]]. This was the last push the [[The Followers of Dragmire, Revolutionaries|Revolutionaries]] needed to launch their attack and gain enough popular support to make victory possible.

It is believed the Confessor achieved this goal by having various Follower sympathizers place a magic powder into fountains, torches and other sources of fire and water. Then, using magic, the Confessor telepathically connected with the sources of fire and water, facilitated by the powder, and 'broadcasted' the events into a vision and image anyone could see by looking into the flames. A similar technique was implemented at [[Lanayru Citadel|Lanayru Citade]] by the [[The Warden|Warden]] to quickly deliver orders, as revealed in [[Former Hyrulean Government and Royal Family|Royal]] documents that were unclassified following the establishment of the Revolutionary government.
