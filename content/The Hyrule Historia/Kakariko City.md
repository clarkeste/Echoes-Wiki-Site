**Kakariko City** is the biggest, most diverse city in [[Hyrule|Hyrule]], judged by land that is considered a part of the city zone. It is a close second in terms of population, with [[Castle Town|Castle Town]] barely beating it out by most estimates.

Kakariko City is in the south of Hyrule, and is the country's biggest, and most important port, handling the vast majority of international [[Economy|commercial activity]] in the country. It was founded shortly after the time of the [[Hero of Time|Hero of Time]], named after [[Kakariko Village|Kakariko Village]]—an old [[Sheikah|Sheikah]] settlement near [[Death Mountain|Death Mountain]]—by its founders, that contained many Sheikah among them. It soon began to flourish due to its proximity to its coast, thanks to the newly-established intercontinental trade. By the time of the [[Hero of the Flood|Hero of the Flood]], it was among the ten largest cities in Hyrule. In the centuries since, it has grown to be second only to Castle Town. Due to its initial founding by Sheikah, it has been considered an [[Autonomy Clause|Autonomous Zone]], like [[Gorons|Goron City]] or [[Zora|Zora's Domain]], albeit with less privileges than the latter two.

Kakariko City retains a significant Sheikah population—often attracting Sheikah with more liberal sentiments, as opposed to the traditional conservatism of smaller Sheikah villages in the north. The majority of individuals are still [[The Hylians|Hylians]], with a [[The Gorons|Goron]] and [[The Zora|Zora]] minority. Kakariko City is often stereotyped, paradoxically, both as a place of business and hard-work and as the birth-place of radicalism, or enlightenment depending on one’s perspective. Kakariko City has historically been considered the most willing to accept anti-Royalist ideals, and was the starting place of the [[Revolution and Second Civil War|Revolution]]. It has a wide variety of individuals, but tends to lean towards those wanting political change and improved worker's rights. Kakariko City was the first city to decide to host elections for its governor and city government.

Kakariko City, years prior to the Revolution, had its beloved governor—[[Governor Arandin|Arandin]]—forcefully removed by the [[Last King of Hyrule|King]], and replaced with one loyal to the King. This was officially due to security threats caused by Arandin acting sympathetically towards dissident groups. This fostered a dislike among the population, both for the King and the new [[Governor of Kakariko|Governor, Kakan Rarurn]]. Later, days prior to the Revolution, the Governor closed off the port to prevent escaped [[The Followers of Dragmire, Revolutionaries|Followers]] from escaping the country under the orders of the King and [[Nophandus|Nophandus]].

Kakariko City was where [[Link Steadfast|Link Steadfast]] and the [[The Confessor|Confessor]] where during their infamous '[[Broadcast|Broadcast]]' that revealed that Steadfast held the [[Master Sword|Master Sword]]. As a result, the people of Kakariko rose up against the [[Former Hyrulean Government and Royal Family|Royal government]], the city falling into the possession of the Followers. Using Kakariko City as a staging ground, they invaded Castle Town and killed the King.

Today, Kakariko City is still under the control of the Revolutionaries. Its former beloved governor, Arandin, has returned to politics as Governor. Kakariko City is generally supportive of the Followers, but there is still much doubt—in a city so diverse, the opinions, too, are diverse. Some believe they have gone too far by killing members of the Royal Family, while others wish they had gone further; more, still, simply blame them for the ill-effects of the Civil War on their livelihood.
