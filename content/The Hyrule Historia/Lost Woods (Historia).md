**The Lost Woods**, or the **Great Hyrule Forest** are a mysterious woods in the central east of [[Hyrule|Hyrule]] near [[Minishi Town (Historia)|Minishi Town]] and between [[Hyrulean Geography|Lanayru]] and [[Akkala City (Historia)|Akkala]]. It is oft the focus of folk stories, legends, and mythologies. It is the largest forest in Hyrule, and its shroud is largely impenetrable. Its inside is dark due to its heavy canopy and covered in a blueish fog. Tourists have reported that it is very easy to get lost, and it is suggested to enter with a guide. However, even the guides cannot penetrate far into the forest. While people sometimes find landmarks, such as the [[Temple of Time (Historia)|Temple of Time]] or other ruins, very few people can successfully recreate their journeys and their discoveries of certain locations. It is said that the [[Hero of Time|Hero of Time]] lived near, or inside, the Lost Woods for much of his early life. Legends from this time speak of a protector of the forest, named the [[Deku Tree (Historia)|Great Deku Tree]], and [[The Kokiri|fairy children]] that inhabited the forest. These mythological and questionable legends continue to propagate to the modern day, although there is some evidence from historical sources that some sort of Deku Tree did indeed exist, or perhaps still does.

In the last days of the [[Former Hyrulean Government and Royal Family|monarchy]], the [[Commission for the Reclamation of the Golden Power|Commission for the Reclamation of the Golden Power]] traveled to the Lost Woods to recover a shard of the [[The Triforce (Historia)|Triforce]]. It is here that the group found the Temple of Time, with the [[Master Sword (Historia)|Master Sword]] missing, and encountered one of the [[The Chosen (Historia)|leaders]] of the [[The Followers of Dragmire, Revolutionaries|Followers of Dragmire]]—[[The Confessor (Historia)|the Confessor]]. Despite the Confessor's escape, Ganondorf and the Commission managed to recover the Triforce Shard and returned it to the [[Hyrule Castle (Historia)|Hyrule Castle]].

Now, during the Civil War, tourism to the Lost Woods has largely dried up due to it being uncomfortably close to the frontline of the war. Those who do enter report that the forest feels darker than ever. Some even report seeing inexplicable scorch marks on the forest floor. There have been some reports of dangerous wildlife, and the majority who enter have been logged as missing after not returning from the Lost Woods for months on end. The official statement of the [[The Royalists|Royalist]] movement urges citizens to not enter the Lost Woods or approach it at all.
