As with any society, **crime** has been present in [[Hyrule|Hyrule]] since the very beginning. While the [[Former Hyrulean Government and Royal Family|Hyrulean government]] and authorities have often been good at busting organized crime, or keeping crime syndicates small, some always slip through the cracks. In particular, in the modern day—with the Royal government overthrown by the [[The Followers of Dragmire, Revolutionaries|Revolutionaries]]—crime and smuggling become more profitable and prevalent.

While little is known about the rising underworld—in part because it is constantly shifting during the time of writing—it is known that, since the Civil War, smuggling has become far more prevalent. This smuggling has been to both the Followers and the [[The Royalists|Royalists]], as well as civilians. Additionally, these criminal groups will often accept contracts from the different factions in the war, exchanging money for weapons, or an advantage.
