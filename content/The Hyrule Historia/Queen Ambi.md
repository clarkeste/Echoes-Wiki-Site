**Queen Ambi** was the previous monarch of [[Labrynna (Historia)|Labrynna]], before the current [[Queen Lakra (Historia)|Queen Lakra]], who was Ambi’s daughter.

Thoughts on Ambi are mixed, often split down between Rebels and the Royalists. She is considered to have been an absent-minded monarch, but one who sought her goals no matter what. Critics portray her as the epitome of an individual so privileged that her cruelty towards her people was something that she never even noticed, despite the ample signs. Conversely, others see her as someone who was willing to tackle the difficult tasks and finish them no matter what.

Possibly Ambi’s most clear legacy, besides fanning the flames of rebellion, is the [[The Black Tower (Historia)|Black Tower]]. It is a massive structure near Lynna City that towers even above some of the tallest mountains, its imposing dark form casting a shadow over the world. Why Ambi ordered it constructed is unknown, with the Queen giving many contradictory answers.

Ambi died due to natural causes approximately twenty years ago.
