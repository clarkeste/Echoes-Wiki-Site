**Prince Lusand** is the crown prince of [[Hyrule|Hyrule]], and brother to [[Princess Zelda|Princess Zelda]]. He is a young man, nineteen years of age, and with the death of his sister and [[Last King of Hyrule|father]], the next in line to the throne.

However, the line of succession was complicated when Lusand, along with his mother, were all but banished from Hyrule by the King of Hyrule, Lusand’s own father. Believing that Lusand was not his son and the product of an affair, the King divorced his wife. She and Lusand then moved across the sea to the [[Holodrum|Empire of Holodrum]], where they have lived since.

The former Queen—now a Duchess—and her son live in Holodrum as respected guests of the [[Emperor of Holodrum|Emperor]], and members of his court. It is said that Lusand and the nearly-decade older Emperor Kanadei are good friends, and share many ideological agreements. Some gossipmongers go so far as to describe Kanadei as a mentor, or bigger brother, to the boy.

Recent events such as the death of Princess Zelda have returned the call for Lusand to return to Hyrule and seek the throne. Legally-speaking, he is entitled to it. However, complications arise, such as legitimacy. While most scholars agree that Lusand is almost certainly his father’s son, the King seemed skeptical to his final days. Additionally, [[General Noril|General Noril]], while making a few controversial statements referring to Lusand positively and swearing to restore the monarchy, has not officially or publicly invited the Prince back into the country. Likely this is because he fears for the Prince’s safety in a war-torn country, although some cynics claim Noril is planning on establishing a military dictatorship should he win.

Regardless, Lusand has much to be angry about—furious about. [[The Followers of Dragmire, Revolutionaries|Revolutionaries]] killed his father, and his beloved sister. Everything he could’ve missed or loved from his home country was killed in a matter of hours. Any sane boy would want revenge after that.
