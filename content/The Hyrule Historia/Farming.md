Farming is an extremely vital part of any civilization, as it produces a steady supply of food that allows beings to take up residence and build homes in a particular area, without being forced to move due to the requirements of food.

Food security was once a major fear in [[Hyrule|Hyrule]], and stunted not just its growth, but the growth of every civilization on the planet. However, it was discovered approximately six hundred years ago by academics in [[Holodrum|Holodrum]] that individuals with the power to cast magic could use the same [[Magic|Spells]] they used to heal or protect [[The Humans|humans]] on plants. This essentially had a fertilizing and sterilizing effect on the plants, warding off illnesses and infections that would previously spell the end of crop seasons for up to a year, and increasing growth and reliability. Even harvests that seemed too far gone could be spared if the farmer had enough coin to hire a sorcerer.

As this knowledge spread to the rest of the world, including Hyrule, it revolutionized farming and made magic even more important than it once was. Now, its techniques are normalized and used by most farmers, even independent ones, to greatly improve food security.
