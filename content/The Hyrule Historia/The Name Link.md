**The name Link** is a popular name given to boys. Before the [[Hero of Time|Hero of Time]], the name represented courage and determination, and there may have been past historical figures who used the name. After the Hero of Time, the name increased in popularity drastically. Currently, about 2.1% of the male population of Hyrule is named Link—an incredibly high percentage for a name.

The [[Hero of the Flood]] was named Link; so are several important figures listed in this Historia, such as [[Link Steadfast (Historia)|Link Steadfast]] and [[Link Sahon]].
