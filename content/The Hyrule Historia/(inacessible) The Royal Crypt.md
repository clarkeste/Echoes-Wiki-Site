While there are rumors of a special crypt created specifically for the [[Former Hyrulean Government and Royal Family|Royal Family]] to bury their dead, as well as that of their most vital servants, it has never been confirmed and remains a fanciful legend. That said, documents declassified by the [[The Followers of Dragmire, Revolutionaries|Followers]] featured several mentions of non-standard burials for the Royal Family, but never described details or the location of this supposed crypt.

Most recently, [[Princess Zelda|Princess Zelda]]—killed during the [[Revolution and Second Civil War|Revolution]] one year ago—was buried underneath the Sacred Grounds just south of [[Castle Town|Castle Town]].
