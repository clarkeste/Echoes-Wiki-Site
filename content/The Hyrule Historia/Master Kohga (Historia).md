**The Believer**, also known as **Kohga of Kakariko Village**, **Master Kohga**, or **General Ahoga** is a member of the [[The Followers of Dragmire, Revolutionaries|Followers of Dragmire]] and the new Revolutionary government.

It was early during his life that Kohga became [[Yiga Movement|disillusioned]] with the Royal Family, for using the Sheikah as a scapegoat. His position was lost due to this increase in stigma against the Sheikah [[Goddesses and the Gods|religious]] minority. It was during this time that Kohga became more distant from society, and no longer attempted to continue his professional career. It is suspected that it was around this time that Kohga was radicalized and joined the Followers of Dragmire. 

Eventually, likely due to his knowledge of Sheikah mystic arts, experience in the Sheikah community, and combat techniques, Kohga was promoted to a [[The Chosen (Historia)|Chosen]]—and took the name of the Believer. While there is no evidence he was as dedicated as some of his colleagues, he seemed to believe in [[Ganondorf Dragmire|Dragmire]] as, at least, a symbol of resistance and change in the face of the Royal Family.

As a Chosen, he was given his own branch of the Followers to control, along with the [[The Sorcerer|Sorcerer]]. His ally [[Sooga (Historia)|Sooga]] was a member of this branch. The Believer's branch is considered to have been significantly more lax than that of the Sorcerer's, with members rarely being punished for smaller infractions. While this led to the Believer's branch being less effective in missions than that of the Sorcerer's, it also maintained a larger, constantly growing membership, when the Sorcerer's branch often stagnated. Additionally, disgruntled Sheikah were almost always directed to the Believer's branch. The [[The Confessor (Historia)|Confessor]] and the Believer, however, got along much better with each other than either did with the Sorcerer, and the two branches worked together much more often.

It was about this time that it is thought that the Believer created the Ahoga persona. Ahoga was an alias made by the Believer that acted as an entirely new identity, without any connections to the real Kohga; this alias joined the Hyrulean Military and quickly rose through the ranks due to the Believer's own knowledge and expertise in the subject, becoming a General shortly before the final [[Festival of the Flood|Festival of the Flood]].

The Believer was present the raid on [[Hyrule Castle (Historia)|Hyrule Castle]] that occurred during the Festival, weeks before the Revolution. While he faced [[Ganondorf (Historia)|Ganondorf]] to try and convince the [[The Gerudo|Gerudo]] to join them, Ganondorf was not receptive. Rather than fight his supposed god, Kohga fled, escaping with the [[Princess Zelda (Historia)|Princess]] as his captive. His ally who stayed behind—the Sorcerer—was not so lucky, seemingly dying in a battle with Ganondorf. Members of the Followers report that the Believer was really not that broken up, as he did not enjoy the company of the Sorcerer. Unfortunately for him, she didn’t seem to properly have died.

The Believer was present later at Lon Lon Ranch, which was raided by Royalist agents. He escaped, later launching a mission inside of [[Castle Town (Historia)|Castle Town (Historia)]] to rescue as many of his captured comrades as he could. He participated, during the day of the [[Revolution and Second Civil War|Revolution]], in a prison break at [[Lanayru Citadel (Historia)|Lanayru Citadel]] along with the Confessor. He was there when [[Link Steadfast (Historia)|Link Steadfast]] was [[Broadcast|announced]] to be the Hero. He later led the Revolutionary army during their march to Castle Town. When their army was devastated by Ganondorf, the Believer was heavily injured and taken out of the battle; but despite his injuries, the Followers pulled through and won the battle, with Link Steadfast killing Ganondorf.

Now, the Believer is in charge of security for Hyrule Castle has a role in the military under the new regime. He reports directly to the Confessor, and is technically on a separate hierarchy from [[Lakoh (Historia)|Lakoh]] and the Sorcerer—meaning, he no longer has to listen to his annoying "friend", the Sorcerer.

Despite his name, Kohga was not born in [[Kakariko Village (Historia)|Kakariko Village]].
