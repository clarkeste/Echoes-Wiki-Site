**The Rito** are a sapient, avian species that live in the cold eastern reaches of northern [[Holodrum|Holodrum]]. They are masters of the sky, and often use bows. Using their wings—which have finger-like appendages on them that allow them to manipulate objects just as well as a [[The Humans|Human]] or [[The Zora|Zora]]—they can fly in the air, gliding on currents and weaving in and out of obstacles with precision. 

They are considered to have the best bow craftsmen among them; Rito bows are considered premium and are in high demand by nearly the whole world. They also fetch an extremely nice [[Rupees|price]].

While Rito can fly forward, they cannot ascend on their own and rely on the wind to lift them upwards. The art of creating an updraft to soar high into the sky without the assistance of a tool is considered to be a masterpiece of aerial techniques by the Rito.

Rito have poor night vision and struggle to see in dimly-lit places, causing problems at night. However, their feathers retain heat, helpful for both the chilly climate they inhabit, and for when they fly at high altitudes. A Rito's feathers molt once per year, and the feathers have formed a thriving industry for the Rito who use them to craft clothes for others, paragliders, or create particularly soft mattresses and sheets. 

Despite their harsh terrain, the Rito manage excellently, maintaining [[Economy|trade]] through the world with the help of companies in Holodrum and [[Labrynna|Labrynna]], and surviving fairly comfortably in the cold. Their little corner of the continent is filled with tall, stout plateaus and over upraised platforms, allowing them to weave between massive pillars. Their largely vertical home of Rito Village is on one such pillar.

The Rito are considered to be a gentle but adventurous people. Despite their geographical isolation, they tend to be welcoming of visitors to their home. Many Rito travel the world to spread their wings—literally! However, despite this, Rito foreign policy has always valued their independence. They have strong ties with Holodrum. Despite this cultural friendship, the Rito have never agreed to a proposal that they believed could make them a vassal of Holodrum. However, in recent years, Rito and many geopolitical experts in Holodrum and the rest of the world have warned that Holodrum seems to be taking steps in an attempt to monopolize their trade with the Rito. They have indirectly limited the Rito’s ability conduct foreign policy or trade without first going through the [[Emperor of Holodrum|Holodrumian Imperial Family]]. The Rito have fervently tried to oppose these actions, but often they are left with little choice.

Despite this, Rito culture remains strong. Rito pass down legends in the form of songs, and have libraries that have remained well-preserved in the cold. Unfortunately, they are not particularly long-lived—their average life-span a little bit short than a [[The Hylians|Hylian]]'s.
