While it would be shocking that anyone would read the [[(Foreword and Contents)|Hyrule Historia]] without knowing of the titular country, it bears repeating for the sake of completeness: Hyrule is a country on the western continent, bordering [[Calatia]] to the west, [[Hytopia]] to the north, the sea to the east, and the Desert of Mystery—or more commonly known, the Gerudo Desert—to the south.

Hyrule is a country with a long, storied history, much of which is recounted in this book. From the ravages of [[Ganondorf Dragmire]] to the pillaging of the [[First Hyrulean Civil War|first Hyrulean Civil War]], it has a country that has suffered much, but also experienced booms and golden ages. Up until a year ago, it was undisputedly a great power, rivaled only by [[Holodrum]].

Things changed a year ago, however, when the [[Revolution and Second Civil War|Revolution and Second Civil War]] began and started another Civil War. Now, Hyrule’s greatest threat is from within; the country is divided, and chaos is the law of the land.
