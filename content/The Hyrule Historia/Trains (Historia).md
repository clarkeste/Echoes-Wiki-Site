**Trains** are a new mode of transportation devised by engineers in [[Hyrule|Hyrule]] and [[Holodrum (Historia)|Holodrum]]. They go on rails and can travel at fast speeds.

They are a recent invention, and so most of the infrastructure for them is not yet in place. However, some areas in Holodrum and Hyrule do have trains for carrying heavy loads, and eager entrepreneur in both countries are working on raising funding and expertise for passenger and cargo rail lines.
