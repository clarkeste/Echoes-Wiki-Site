**The Hylian-Gerudo War** was an event shortly after the Era of [[The Flood|the Flood]], when the then monarch of [[Hyrule|Hyrule]]—the Prince ascendant, after his [[King Daphnes Nohansen|father]] and [[Princess Zelda|sister]] were killed by [[Ganondorf Dragmire]]—ordered an invasion of the [[the Gerudo|Gerudo]] in retaliation for the actions of their former leader. While the majority of then-living Gerudo did not support Dragmire, the Royal Army rolled into the desert all the same.

Despite being a warrior people used to surviving, the Gerudo were no match for the overwhelming firepower of the Royal Army, including their heavy artillery and mass number of troops. The Gerudo were soon forced out of [[Desert of Mystery, Gerudo Desert|Gerudo Town]], the Hyruleans reducing their former homes to nothing more than smoking ruins. Despite significant casualties relative to the amount of enemy troops they faced, the Hyrulean Army continued on, pursuing the Gerudo as the warrior tribe fled further and [[Desert of Doubt|further into the desert]].

As the chase continued, the Hyrulean soldiers inflicted severe casualties on the Gerudo. It is estimated that forty-percent of the Gerudo population was slain during these attacks. However, the harsh desert and dunes were terrain that the Hyruleans were unfamiliar with, allowing the Gerudo to slowly pick off their opponents. The Gerudo believed they could outlast their [[The Hylians|Hylian]] opponents, with many Royal soldiers dying of heat strokes or dehydration.

The Hyruleans, however, were determined to end the war before the conditions deterred them. The Hyruleans caught up to the Gerudo at what is now called the Dune of the Dead, and barred the Gerudo’s escape with well-placed artillery blasts. From there, the Hyruleans killed thousands upon thousands of the Gerudo. Eventually, the Gerudo gained the upper hand in the battle and destroyed much of the siege equipment, forcing the Royal Army to flee. So did the Gerudo flee, deeper into the desert—what is now called the Desert of Doubt. It is believed that the Gerudo were nomadic for a time, losing more people to the harsh desert, before eventually forming the settlements that they are reported to now inhabit. However, despite the survival of some of the Gerudo, the King was satisfied that they had been taught a lesson, and recalled the troops. The Gerudo Desert was later settled by Hylians, who founded [[Calditin City|Calditin City]] there. The ruins of Gerudo Town are now a regulated tourist attraction.

During the war, there were attempts by those on the Hylian side to convince the government to end it. Everything from the group that would eventually become the [[The Followers of Dragmire, Revolutionaries|Followers of Dragmire]], to the [[Hero of the Flood|Hero of the Flood]], publicly requested the [[Former Hyrulean Government and Royal Family|Royal government]] to cease. However, they—and many in Hyrule—saw only red after Dragmire attempted to flood the world. They desired revenge on the Gerudo who had spawned him, and who Dragmire had once led.
