The **Hero of the Flood** is an individual who lived approximately three hundred years ago, and who saved [[Hyrule|Hyrule]] from the [[Ganondorf Dragmire|Demon King, Ganondorf]], and a [[The Flood|torrential downpour]] that could've eliminated the entire Kingdom. He was a tradesmen from Lurelin Village who, on his first visit to [[Hyrule Castle]], successfully dueled and bested a drunk guard who attempted to harass him. During his visit, he was noticed by [[Princess Zelda|Princess Zelda]], whose bodyguards had hired the guard the soon-to-be Hero bested. They grew close, and most historians agree that there may have been a blooming romantic love between them, particularly when the Hero's tradesmen family chose to stay in [[Castle Town|Castle Town]] for business reasons. At some point during this, the Hero recovered the [[Master Sword|Master Sword]], but it was kept a secret from the public and everybody except Princess Zelda.

However, after years of this, things went horribly wrong. Ganondorf, the being who had [[Seven Year Reign|conquered Hyrule a century prior]], returned with a vengeance. Quickly after his return, he conquered the [[Desert of Mystery, Gerudo Desert|Gerudo Desert]], recruiting an army of [[Gerudo|Gerudo]] who still supported him, and [[The Monsters|monsters]] he created from the purest of Malice. He invaded Hyrule and reached Castle Town by destroying any forces in his way, capturing both Princess Zelda and her father, [[King Daphnes Nohansen|King Daphnes]], returning them to the Forbidden Fortress in the Gerudo Desert.

When the Hero of Floods arrived, he had found that the two had died, with Ganondorf failing to extract the piece of the [[The Triforce|Triforce]] he sought from Zelda. Seemingly believing that only his death could stop the downpour he most likely caused, he allowed the Hero to slay him with the Master Sword.

After this, the Hero of Floods went into a reclusive life. Despite his fame and acclaim, he lost much of what he held dear. The only appearances he made between finishing his journey and his death were when he publicly spoke out against the [[Hylian-Gerudo War|Hylian-Gerudo war]] that the monarch, after the death of his sister Zelda and father Daphnes, waged in response to Ganondorf's return. However, despite his disapproval, the Hyrulean government continued that war to its conclusion. The Hero of Floods is thought to have returned to the Master Sword to the [[Temple of Time|Temple of Time]] within the [[Lost Woods|Lost Woods]], and died a peaceful death.

The Hero of the Flood was [[The Name Link|named Link]] after the [[Hero of Time|Hero of Time]]. As was not uncommon at the time, his full name was simply "Link of Lurelin Village".
