**Castor City** is a city in the west of [[Hyrule|Hyrule]], close to the Tabantha Frontier Bridge. It is the westernmost large city in Hyrule, established centuries ago as an effort to increase development in the lands to the west. It was conquered by the [[The Followers of Dragmire, Revolutionaries|Revolutionaries]] after the Royal forces guarding it fled further west, into [[Hyrulean Geography|Tabantha]] and [[Hyrulean Geography|Hebra]]. 

Castor City, as a large city, naturally leans towards change. Polls show that the denizens of Castor City slightly support the Followers over the [[The Royalists|Royalists]]. Despite this, Castor City is very close to the Royalist resistance movement, who make their home in the Hebra and Tabantha regions. This group—colloquially known as the Royalist Partisans—disrupt Follower [[Economy|trade]], supplies, and infrastructure, often destroying important routes like bridges. Taking advantage of the cold, snowy, and misty mountains of Hebra, they avoid and kill the Followers sent to deal with them.
