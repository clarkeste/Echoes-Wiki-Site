**Calatia** is a minor power to the west of [[Hyrule|Hyrule]]. It has always been an ally of Hyrule. Hyrule’s loyalty, both diplomatically and militarily, is likely due to Calatia’s strategic position. The country of Calatia controls various naturally-formed mountain passages that, if in the wrong hands, could be used to launch a swift and likely successful invasion of the Hyrulean mainland.

Hyrule is aware of these paths and has made many efforts to garrison and fortify them. Still, it has been the consistent foreign policy of all its wise [[Former Hyrulean Government and Royal Family|monarchs]] to remain on friendly terms with Calatia. Part of this has been, however, through threat; even if Calatia was in a powerful position that could be exploited, war between the two countries would favor Hyrule. This was due to Hyrule’s dominance militarily and significantly larger navy. This fear of Hyrulean might, however, waned after the beginning of the [[Revolution and Second Civil War|second Hyrulean Civil War]].

Calatia has its own monarchy and, so, was appalled at the actions of the [[The Followers of Dragmire, Revolutionaries|Revolutionaries]] in Hyrule and the death of the ruling Royal Family. Exercising their control, Calatia suppressed any revolutionary sentiment in their own country that may have arisen from the actions of the Followers in Hyrule. They have supported the [[The Royalists|Royalists]] in the Hyrulean Civil War. Due to their status as the only country on the western continent not at war, however, their pre-existant [[Piracy, Smuggling|smuggling]] problems have become significant. These smugglers will sell to any side or government, no matter how radical or dangerous.

Without the power or influence Hyrule once wielded, Calatia is no longer bound to be such strong allies with Hyrule. Instead, [[Holodrum|Holodrum]] has been filling the roles that Hyrule once did, with Holodrum and Calatia now forging a strong partnership. Both countries have collaborated with one another to support the Royalists in both the Hyrulean and [[Hytopia|Hytopian]] Civil Wars.

There are some concerns that Calatia may use the mountain passages it controls to invade the Follower-controlled parts of Hyrule, entering the war on the side of the Royalists. However, that has not yet occurred—the Calatian government and monarchy may be concerned that such a direct intervention will herald the return of revolutionary instability in their own country.
