**The ‘Sunken City’** is an uncommonly mentioned part of [[Hyrule|Hyrulean]] history. While most myths about it that aren’t accurate, it does truly exist.

It refers to an area off the coast of Hyrule that, prior to the [[The Flood|Flood]], was known as Eventide Island. It was home to a large Hyrulean settlement. However, when the Flood came, the island was buried underneath waves of water, and the island as a whole sunk into the water further. Separated from the mainland as they were, the denizens of Eventide could only stay and die.

Now referred to as the Sunken Village, or Sunken City, it is the product of many popular ghost stories. A few investigators and sailors have confirmed that its wreckage still exists below, but no reliable reports of anyone diving deep into it exist. Some treasure hunters have tried, but the challenges of breathing so long underwater have dissuaded them. The most successful ones have been [[Zora|Zora]], who have brought back soaked trinkets. Many are off-put by the atmosphere, however, and do not linger for long. Others never surface, likely becoming trapped by rubble falling from the dilapidated buildings.
