**The Revolution** was an event in [[Hyrule]] that happened one year ago, as of the publication of [[(Beginning of the Historia)|Hyrule Historia]]. It started the **Second Hyrulean Civil War**, and was enacted by the [[The Followers of Dragmire, Revolutionaries|Followers of Dragmire]] for the purpose of overthrowing the [[Former Hyrulean Government and Royal Family|Royal government]]. Some blame the Royal Family for its passive—and increasingly active, in their point of view—cruelty, while others square the blame entirely on the Followers.

The exact architect of the Revolution is unknown. However, it seems most likely that the shadowy figure of [[The Pragmatist|the Pragmatist]] used [[The Confessor|the Confessor]] and the rest of his [[The Chosen|Chosen]] minions to complete the task, before fading into obscurity.

A mere week prior to the Revolution, two of the most influential leaders in the Followers, the [[The Sorcerer|Sorcerer]] and the [[Master Kohga|Believer]], chose to strike at [[Hyrule Castle|Hyrule Castle]]. They believed that the newly arrived [[Ganondorf|Ganondorf]] was a herald of what was to come, and sign that they must act. During the raid, however, Ganondorf proved his loyalty to the [[Last King of Hyrule|King]] by allegedly killing the Sorcerer, although she later appears to have survived the encounter. The King, convinced by the threat, ordered a Kingdom-wide [[The Draft, Mobilization|mobilization]].

In the following week, the Followers prepared and moved their resources to enact the Revolution. Their final move, however, was one that nobody—even almost no member of the Followers of Dragmire—saw coming. The Confessor issued a '[[Broadcast|broadcast]]' by using magic to show moving images in every torch and watery reflection in every major city in Hyrule. This broadcast showed one of the members of the Followers, [[Link Steadfast|Link Steadfast]], wielding the [[Master Sword|Master Sword]]. This was intended to reduce the divine legitimacy of the Royal Family, and it succeeded. Steadfast urged the people of Hyrule to rise up against the [[Last King of Hyrule|King of Hyrule]].

The leaders of the Followers, largely the Believer and the Confessor but likely also the Pragmatist, used their resources and contacts to stage a revolution in [[Kakariko City|Kakariko City]] that forced the [[Governor of Kakariko|Governor]] to flee. The city was soon captured by Revolutionary militants. Using the city as a base, the Followers—all branches now united—marched on [[Castle Town|Hyrule Castle Town]]. There, they sieged the city and, using spies inside Hyrule Castle, killed the King and [[Princess Zelda|Princess Zelda]]. It is believed that the Confessor and Steadfast were among those inside the Castle, and that the two of them dealt the final blow to the new and loyal [[Ganondorf|Ganondorf]], who died attempting to protect the King and the remaining members of the [[The Council|Council]].

In the weeks following, the Followers secured the east of the country and pushed the [[The Royalists|Royalist]] to the west. They began the transition into becoming a provisional government, with the stated intention of holding democratic elections. The Confessor then became the acting Head of State, tasked with stabilizing and creating a new government. Afterwards, she appointed the defector, [[Lakoh|Lakoh]], and—after political pressure—the Sorcerer, among others, to high-ranking political positions in the new government. The Confessor still acts as a leader for the ‘New Hyrulean’ government, but her role is becoming less and less important as she seeks to soon host democratic elections.

The Confessor is still the ultimate authority in the Revolutionary government, however. She knows the secrets of the Royal Family and is a powerful, well-loved member. She is considered the Follower leader who has most pushed for democracy and freedom; with Lakoh being too unwilling to make large changes, and Sorcerer too radical and fervent to burn everything of the old government down, regardless of who suffers.
