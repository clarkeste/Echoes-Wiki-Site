Death Mountain, or Eldin Volcano, is the name of an active volcano in the northwest of [[Hyrule]] in the [[Hyrulean Geography|Eldin]] region. It is the highest mountain in Hyrule, and is the home of [[The Gorons|the Gorons]] and Goron City. Its lava flows are basaltic, and it has many zeolite deposits around it. The Goron authorities have long cautioned against entering its caves for fear of foul air, but while it is inadvisable to explore them due to other dangers, the Mountain’s tunnels are well-ventilated enough that this is not often a concern.

Almost all of Death Mountain is a part of the Goron Neutral Zone, including some surrounding areas. This includes [[Kakariko Village (Historia)|Kakariko Village]], which is at the base of the volcano.

Death Mountain is about 3.4km tall.
