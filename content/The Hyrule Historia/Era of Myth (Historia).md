The Era of Myth is a time period prior to the [[Era of Peace (Historia)|Era of Peace]] in Hyrulean history. As the Era of Peace is already largely shrouded in mystery with few valuable records, the Era of Myth is foreign enough to us to be essentially impenetrable to any attempt at modern understanding.

Most of what remains of this era is architectural in nature, as most other records of the past have been lost to time.

Proceeding the Era of Peace, when the continent that would become [[Hyrule]] was first settled, archeological evidence from the Era of Myth would indicate that some sapient beings still called Hyrule home prior to the modern settlement by [[The Hylians|Hylians]]. While the [[The Gorons|Gorons]] and some other tribes have basic histories dating all the way back to this time period, none of it would seem to explain some of the rare advanced structures found beneath the earth which seem to date to this era.

For many years, the public consciousness began to tell tales of some mythical group of [[Ancient Civilizations|Ancient Hyruleans]] sent by the [[Goddesses and the Gods|Gods]] to live in Hyrule before disappearing or being cast out. However, in the modern day, most scholars and archeological agree that these ancient inhibitors of [[Hyrule]] were like ancient [[The Hylians|Hylians]], or were otherwise [[The Humans|human]] in some way and are the ancestors to the modern Hylians inhabiting Hyrule. At some point, these ancient Hyruleans left or were forced to abandon the continent, before returning thousands of years later during the Era of Peace. Indeed, many of these ancient structures, many with functions or parts that seem entirely alien and purposeless to us, show signs of damage as a result of battle or war; it is possible that a pitched battle between the ancient peoples who resided in the continent forced a departure of many of its residents.

A few scholars have put forward the theory that the enemy of the ancient Hyruleans were the [[The Gorons|Gorons]] and other ancient tribes, but the Gorons’ own records, vague as they are, show no sign or indication of a large-scale war against Hylians. Instead, only of a great tumult in this era and a series of related calamities that seem to be supernatural in origin—potentially extreme weather, earthquakes, or other such cataclysms that ancient peoples would understand as not being man-made.

The ‘Era of Myth’ in scholarly debate encompasses all time prior to the Era of Peace. Even further before in time, before the time when these ancient structures were constructed—which is believed to have been a few thousand years—our understanding of the past is incredibly limited and comes down purely to archeology, especially that of ancient bones and remains that have been dug up. It is the realm of [[Goddesses and the Gods|religion]] and mythology, such as the creation story of the three [[Goddesses and the Gods|Golden Goddesses]]. Verification of any of these myths is impossible, but many accept that they are somewhat metaphorical. To what degree of allegory, however, is unknown.
