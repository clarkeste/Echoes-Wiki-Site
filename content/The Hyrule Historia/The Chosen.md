**The Chosen** were the leaders of the [[The Followers of Dragmire, Revolutionaries|Followers of Dragmire]]. The group was led by the [[The Pragmatist|Pragmatist]], who themselves was a Chosen. While there have been various past individuals who were Chosen who bore the title [[The Sorcerer|Sorcerer]] or [[Master Kohga|Believer]], there is—as far as we know—only one Pragmatist, who took control of the Followers about thirty years ago.

The Chosen during the [[Revolution and Second Civil War|Revolution]] consisted of four individuals: The Believer, the Sorcerer, the [[The Confessor|Confessor]], and the Pragmatist.

Each Chosen led their own branch of the cult, except for the Pragmatist, who organized all branches together.
