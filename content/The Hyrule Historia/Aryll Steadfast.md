**Aryll Steadfast** is [[Link Steadfast]]’s sister and daughter of [[Aadin Steadfast]]. Little about her is known, although it is thought she was Link’s twin. During the birth of her and her brother, Aryll’s mother died, leaving Aadin Steadfast to raise the two children alone. Aryll was a member of the [[Castle Town Parliamentarians]] and so, like her brother, was forced to flee [[Castle Town]] and abandon her father.

She was thought to be kind and, perhaps a little more rudely, air-headed.

This author must confess that she greatly detested scrounging through idle gossip from Steadfast’s neighbors. This was done, of course, for information pertaining to the contents of this book. Little is known about the lives of the Steadfasts. No one expects peasants to change the world, and so few think to catalogue their lives.

It is the opinion of this author that while gossip is not as reliable as primary sources, it is the best we have in the face of this pure and unrelenting chaos. I did not dare seek out an interview with Link Steadfast, the so-called Hero of the Revolution, nor his sister, for obvious reasons. Likewise, I did not interview their father, for I am afraid I cannot speak to the dead.
