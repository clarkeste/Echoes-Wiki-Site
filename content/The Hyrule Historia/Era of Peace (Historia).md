The Era of Peace, believed to have occurred from about 1500 to 1150 years before Hyrulean restoration and the [[Seven Year Reign|seven years reign]], is the earliest era in the history of the Hyrulean continent that historians have some reliable knowledge of.

Despite this, however, the Era of Peace is still shrouded in mystery by many, not least of which that what led to it is the subject of contentious scholarly debate. Additionally, during the [[Era of Chaos (Historia)|Era of Chaos]], many written records from this time period were destroyed. What we know of what is called the Era of Peace is largely from archeological ventures and the few written works which survived the Era of Chaos and that are believed to be contemporaneous with the Era of Peace. Many oral histories exist of the Era of Peace, and of the era proceeding it, but are considered to be unreliable and are often so contradictory that they cannot be reconciled.

The Era of Peace, which name we derive from oral stories, was when civilization congruous with ours began to form to a recognizable degree. It is believed that [[The Hylians|Hylians]] and other groups such as the [[The Sheikah|Sheikah]], [[The Gorons|Gorons]], and [[The Zora|Zora]] either migrated to the continent shortly before or during this era, or were previously before it living a more tribal, nomadic life-style either due to a smaller population or a lack of the technology required.

However, as the [[The Hylians|Hylians]] migrated to the continent, many towns and settlements were founded around the continent, independent and decentralized but with communication between them facilitated by messengers. Some surviving letters of the era indicate that it was not uncommon for two directly related family members to live in two different towns far apart from one another, but despite this were still able to maintain contact and visit one another. For many years, anthropologies theorized that this meant most of the ancient [[The Hylians|Hylian]] population in [[Hyrule]] had an innate connection to one another, perhaps owing to their large ears. However, in recent years, this theory has come under scrutiny. Many believe that this interconnectedness, completely opposite what [[Holodrum (Historia)|Holodrumian]] anthropology would indicate occurred in the Holodrumian continent—although some cast aspersions on those findings—would instead imply that Hylians were not native to the continent of Hyrule, but instead migrated to it from a common origin point and spread out over the nation, thereby explaining how families could be spread out over the continent.

Whether this is the same for the [[The Humans|non-Hylian human]] minority in Hyrule and human majority in [[Hytopia (Historia)|Hytopia]] and [[Calatia (Historia)|Calatia]] is unknown, as less research has taken place in those nations. For other tribes, such as the Gorons, Zora, and Sheikah, it is hard to know; most scholars say that these peoples were not numerous enough, or spread out enough across the continent, to say for certain, at least with the level of evidence that currently exists.

The [[The Gerudo|Gerudo]] are a different case. Little evidence has been uncovered of activity in the [[Desert of Mystery, Gerudo Desert||Desert of Mystery]] from before the settlement of humans during the Era of Peace, excluding [[Era of Myth (Historia)|truly ancient findings]]. It is suspected, therefore, they arrived or came into existence at a similar time as the Hylians.

The Era of Peace, therefore, was characterized by a sort of peaceful pastoralism. There is little evidence of widespread conflict in this time period, and no one village, settlement, or collection of thereof, had the power to wage war or otherwise act in a systemically cynical matter. Slowly, over time, these rural communities and farming villages grew in size, never to resemble the cities of the modern day, but to hold several thousand inhabitants.

This is not to say it was utopian, however. Several remaining records are complaints towards mayor, pontiffs, or others with authority who acted in a corrupt manner. Additionally, little technological progress was made during this time period that we know of. Finally, the conditions of the Era of Peace allowed for the arrival of the destructive [[Era of Chaos]].

The Era of Peace, therefore, ended with violence, although its descent into it was a gradual one that did not embroil the entire continent at once.

The ancestors of [[Former Hyrulean Government and Royal Family|the Royal Family]] were known and well-respected during this time period, due to their relation to the [[Goddesses and the Gods|Goddess Hylia]] and her order of priests and priestesses. Surviving letters and writing show the Goddess was worshipped in this time with more dedication than the modern day, alongside of course the [[Goddesses and the Gods|Golden Goddesses]]. Those who would become the Royal Family, much like the rest of Hyrule, had their kin in many different villages across many different settlements, and held no institutional influence and rarely any political power, but were greatly respected. The exact reasons why is still in debate today among scholars, as much of the information for *why* comes from contradictory oral histories that tell tall tales from the [[Era of Myth (Historia)|Era of Myth]].
