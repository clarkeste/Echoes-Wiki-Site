**The Seven Year Reign** usually refers to the reign of [[Ganondorf Dragmire|Ganondorf Dragmire]] over [[Hyrule]] during the era of the [[Hero of Time|Hero of Time]]. It began after Ganon, abusing the trust of the [[King Arkenalan Bosmisen|King]], murdered him, most of the [[Former Hyrulean Government and Royal Family|Royal Family]] and many important ministers and politicians with the assistance of elite [[The Gerudo|Gerudo]] soldiers and bribed guards. 

After this, Ganon started a dictatorial reign for seven long years. It is believed it is around this period, or shortly before, that Ganon gained the [[The Triforce (Historia)|Triforce of Power]] by infiltrating the [[Sacred Realm (Historia)|Sacred Realm]], and grew determined to gain the other two pieces from their holders. As Hyrule's new king, Ganondorf gained even more knowledge regarding the Triforce that he sought. Because his heart was not in balance, he was only bestowed one third of the Triforce: the Triforce of Power. The Triforce of Courage was bestowed to the Hero of Time, and the Triforce of Wisdom bestowed to the [[Princess Zelda (Historia)|Princess Zelda]] of the era. 

Shortly after his coup, parts of the Royal Army that were away from the [[Castle Town (Historia)|capital]] during the coup attempted to stop him. However, Ganon easily dispatched them using [[The Monsters|monstrous beings]] such as Moblins, Re-Deads, and Stalfos who he had used magic to force to serve him. Castle Town, the site of their battle, was largely reduced to rubble, with civilians fleeing the city to take refuge in [[Kakariko Village (Historia)|Kakariko Village]]. With his dominance complete, he forced regional leaders—such as [[Sages|Darunia]] and the King of the [[The Zora|Zora]]—to swear fealty to him.

However, one girl—Zelda, the wielder of one third of the Triforce—had escaped his purge, along with her nursemaid. Ganon sought to find and capture the Princess, but he had no success during his seven years. Instead, he focused on strengthening his rule. Despite his stated purpose being to conquer a new, more hospitable home for his people, Ganon never deemed Hyrule safe for the Gerudo to migrate to it.

During his reign, regional leaders—such as the aforementioned Darunia—continued to attempt to subvert Ganon's reign. In response, the Demon King cracked down. He starved the Gorons. He froze Zora's Domain, trapping the Zora within the ice and cutting off water to the refugees in Kakariko Village.

Only one person could stop Ganon—the [[Hero of Time|Hero of Time]], a boy wielding the final third of the Triforce and the [[Master Sword (Historia)|Master Sword]]. The Hero of Time reversed many of Ganon's crimes, such as the freezing of the Zora. He recruited individuals from across Hyrule, and helped them complete their destiny to become [[Sages|Sages]] who could help him in the final battle.

However, with all of the luxury and power of the subdued kingdom at his disposal, Dragmire did not directly move against the Hero of Time. Instead, he was able to wait patiently for the moment when the two remaining Triforce keepers, the Hero of Time and the Princess, would reveal themselves. Eventually, he found them in the [[Temple of Time (Historia)|Temple of Time]]. There, he captured Princess Zelda, who had evaded his pursuit for seven years. There, he challenged the Hero of Time to save her.

The Hero of Time met the challenge and fought Ganon inside the occupied [[Hyrule Castle (Historia)|Hyrule Castle]]. There, the two battled, and—after proving to be no match for Ganon on his own—was assisted by Zelda and the Sages he had recruited previously. Together, they sealed Ganon away for one hundred years, and ended the seven year reign of darkness.

The next decades were ones of reconstruction and rehabilitation of a land greatly damaged by the actions of Dragmire. However, with the wisdom of the Zelda of the era and those who followed her, Hyrule began to flourish once more. Places such as Hyrule Castle and Castle Town were restored to the versions we know in the present day.
