This entry refers to the first **Hyrulean Civil War** that occurred shortly before the adventures of the [[Hero of Time|Hero of Time]], over four hundred years ago.

The Hyrulean Civil War was a bloody civil conflict inside of the [[Hyrule|Kingdom of Hyrule]].

Approximately four hundred and fifty years ago—and at the end of this nearly thousand-year era of growth—the Kingdom was rocked by the Hyrulean Civil War. This Civil War lasted for decades. Various groups, even within the [[Hylians|Hylians]] themselves, sought dominance, with the eventual victors—[[Former Hyrulean Government and Royal Family|the Royal Family]]—forced to ally with neighboring peoples to win a bloody victory. As a result, the country eventually unified to become the Hyrule we know today with its modern borders.

The cause of this war is usually attributed to an attempt by the monarchy of the time to consolidate power from various [[Capitalist and Nobleman Class|nobles and their domains]]. Pressured by an [[Economy|economy]] struggling from taxes and a population with limited rights due to competing domains, the King of the time attempted to reel in the independence of these noble domains. Despite attempting to do so diplomatically, much of these landowners grew angry and staged an armed insurrection, beginning the war.

Despite this being the cause, it is not what made the war rage on for as long as it did. This dispute was resolved in years; however, other groups, seeking to place separate monarchs on the throne, or extremists from neighboring tribes such as the [[Zora|Zora]], or raids from the [[Gerudo|Gerudo]] that led to large battles, caused the entire land of Hyrule to be dangerous. The war finally ended when [[Ganondorf Dragmire|Ganondorf Dragmire]]—the leader of the Gerudo—agreed to assist the Kingdom. He invaded the flank of the Hylian dissidents and decisively ended the Civil War. Over the next decade, the [[King Arkenalan Bosmison Hyrule|King]] sought to prevent a future war from breaking out by making sure that all lands in the Kingdom, now including the [[Desert of Mystery, Gerudo Desert|Gerudo Desert]], would answer to the monarchy. The [[Gorons|Gorons]], Gerudo, and even the Zora were forced to pledge themselves as [[Autonomy Clause|autonomous regions]] under the Kingdom of Hyrule. With Ganondorf Dragmire having been the deciding factor that won the Civil War, his pledge of loyalty was the most important of any. It was expected he would do so for several reasons. Namely, to secure protection for the Gerudo, because he had already supported the King in his hour of need, and because the Gorons and the Zora had already done so.

However, Ganondorf was a cunning man. Using his status as a decisive ally during the Civil War, he manipulated the monarch into allowing him to usurp the Kingdom. Using his free access into Hyrule as a result of his pledge, his agents assassinated the King and took over [[Castle Town|Castle Town]]. Somehow, Dragmire managed to enter the [[Sacred Realm|Sacred Realm]] and gain the [[The Triforce|Triforce of Power]] for himself. With it and his control of the country, he plunged it into [[Seven Year Reign|seven long years of despair]]. This destruction resulted in the ravaging of [[Hyrule Castle|Hyrule Castle]] and its surrounding city, Castle Town. All peoples were forced to subservience to Ganondorf; while the King of Evil—as he has been called—claimed to seek new, better lands for his people, the time where he allowed the Gerudo to enter the occupied Hyrule never came. According to Gerudo documents recovered following their banishment, it appears that the land was considered too dangerous by Ganondorf up until the very end of his reign.

Ganondorf’s reign was overthrown after seven years by the [[Princess Zelda|Princess-in-hiding]], and the Hero of Time—a child of the Civil War, orphaned by it. With the rebuilding that happened afterwards, the final echoes of the Civil War were put to rest.

This Hyrulean Civil War which happened four hundred years ago, also referred to as the First Hyrulean Civil War, is distinct from the [[Revolution and Second Civil War|current Civil War]].
