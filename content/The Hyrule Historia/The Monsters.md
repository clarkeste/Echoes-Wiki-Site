While the purpose of the [[(Foreword and Contents)|Hyrule Historia]] is to provide an accounting of historical events in [[Hyrule|Hyrule]] and not as a biology textbook, the authors of this book understand the utility in detailing basic facts about some of the most commons monsters in Hyrule. 

It is believed that monsters are, to a certain extent, immortal. At least during the era of the [[Ganondorf Dragmire|Demon King]], a blood moon would revive all the slain monsters, usually close to where they died.

The following is a list of the most common types; some of which still roam the world today, although in much lesser numbers. Their sightings are becoming more common.

### Bokoblins
Bokoblins are among the most common type of monster in Hyrule, especially in the centuries without Dragmire. They come in various colors, and seem to have some degree of intelligence. They are clearly a sociable species, as they form together in groups and make makeshift strongholds. They carve clubs from wood—or steal weapons from travelers they attack. They also hunt animals to remain fed.

### Moblins
These creatures are form the same family as the Bokoblins, but are much larger. They are at least two times the size of a [[The Hylians|Hylian]], and a little larger than even a [[The Gorons|Goron]]. They often make camp with Bokoblins, and wield much heavier weapons.

### Lizalfos
Lizalfos are chameleon-like bipeds who are often described as cunning. They are extremely agile, able to jump large distances, and wield sharp weapons with more finesse than Bokoblins and Moblins. They will often lie in wait, using their biology to camouflage themselves to their surroundings. When a traveler approaches, they jump and assault them. There has never been a recorded case of Lizalfos working with Bokoblins or Moblins, outside of some reports during the [[The Flood|Great Flood]] three hundred years ago.

### Stalfos
Stalfos are skeletal [[The Humans|humanoids]], sometimes fallen knights or soldiers, who have been re-animated and attack others. Stalfos usually exist in abandoned structures, in underground caves, or any area where sunlight does not reach, as they seem to actively avoid it. Stalfos often use weapons once made for humans, befitting of their appearance, such as old spears or swords and shields.

### Chuchu
Chuchus are water-based monster can be found all over Hyrule, its body acting like gel. It tends to spring its attacks on unsuspecting prey from the ground or from trees. Its strength varies by size, and the type of jelly it drops varies depending on whether the Chuchu was heated up, cooled down, or electrocuted—all things that will also change its appearance and abilities. Chuchus are slow, and it is advised to flee from them—although smaller blue ones can often be dealt with in a few strikes from even an impromptu weapon.

### Keese
Keese are the most common type of bat in Hyrule, and are distinct from other types of their species in that they only have one, large yellow-colored eye and often attack individuals on sight. 

It is debated whether Keese should be characterized as monsters or as an animal, however it is almost certain that it did not come about naturally in the same way many animals did. Keese often fly at night or hang from the ceiling underground, or in dark, abandoned structures. They are weak, and—despite trying to ram into or bite anything that gets too close—can often be felled in one strike. However, Keese may sometimes organize into swarms and attack together, all at once.
