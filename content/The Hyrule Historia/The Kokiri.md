**The Kokiri**, also known as the **Kokiri Tribe**, the **Children of the Forest**, or **Fairy Folk**, are, according to myth, small child-like beings that inhabit the [[Lost Woods (Historia)|Lost Woods]]—or ‘Kokiri Woods' as some call them when referring to the Kokiri. There, legends state, they live in treehouses within the woods' acres and clearings.

The Kokiri are supposedly an ageless race that takes the form of [[The Humans|human]] children. Like [[The Hylians|Hylians]], they possess long, pointed ears. It is said that one of the [[Sages|seven sages]] was a Kokiri, a girl named Saria. Despite their agelessness, Kokiri are still said to be mortal beings that can be killed.

It is said—through some unknown process—that the [[Deku Tree (Historia)|Great Deku Tree]] gave life to the Kokiri. The Deku Tree, their guardian, watches over their home in the Lost Woods. It is said in children’s tales and popular myth that the [[Hero of Time|Hero of Time]] was raised as a Kokiri and grew up among them, before leaving them to save the Kingdom of [[Hyrule|Hyrule]] in its darkest hour. While there is large amounts of evidence to indicate that the Hero of Time lived with a forest-dwelling tribe, the Kokiri themselves and the characterization of a living tree can seem a little far-fetched. Despite its unconfirmed status, it has not been completely dismissed as false.
