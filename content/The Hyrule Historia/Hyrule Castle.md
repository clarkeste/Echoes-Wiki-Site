**Hyrule Castle** is a fortress overlooking [[Castle Town|Castle Town]], [[Hyrule|Hyrule]]’s capital, and is visible from almost anywhere in [[Hyrulean Geography|Hyrule Field]]. It is the center of Hyrulean power, an icon of its state. Since time immemorial, it served as the seat of power for the [[Former Hyrulean Government and Royal Family|Royal Family]]. However, during the [[Revolution and Second Civil War|Revolution]], it was captured by the [[The Followers of Dragmire, Revolutionaries|Revolutionaries]]. It served its original masters faithfully; an adorned grave for the [[Last King of Hyrule|King]] and the [[Princess Zelda|Princess]].

Since then, the Castle has served as the main base of the Follower Revolutionaries. It is arguably the most important symbol in all of the Kingdom. If you control Hyrule Castle, you control Hyrule—as far as many are concerned.
