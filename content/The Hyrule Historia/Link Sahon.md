Link Sahon was a bodyguard who served under Lady [[Impa|Impa]] as a member of [[Princess Zelda|Zelda]]'s royal guard. He was promoted to being the leader of her bodyguard unit, after large amounts of [[the Sheikah|Sheikah]] were removed from [[Former Hyrulean Government and Royal Family|government]], including Impa. He was close to the [[Last King of Hyrule|King]] and a loyal member of the Royal government. In the last days of the [[Hyrule|Hyrulean Kingdom]], Sahon was announced by the Royal government to be the most likely candidate for the reincarnation of the [[Hero of Time|Hero of Time]] and [[Hero of the Flood|Flood]]. However, such a reality seems unlikely now, as he died before pulling the [[Master Sword|Master Sword]], was not involved in the defeat of [[Ganondorf (Historia)|Ganondorf]], and was not able to save the Kingdom from the [[The Followers of Dragmire, Revolutionaries|Revolutionaries]].

Link Sahon was present during the Follower raid on [[Hyrule Castle|Hyrule Castle]], and attempted to protect Princess Zelda. However, despite his skill, he was little matched for the Follower's warriors, like the [[The Sorcerer|Sorcerer]], and was unable to reach Zelda in time. Following the raid, the King—on the behest of Ganondorf—created the [[Commission for the Reclamation of the Golden Power|Commission for the Recovery of the Golden Power]], and unassigned Sahon from searching for Zelda, instead wanting him to find the Triforce.

Sahon traveled along with the other members of the Commission, including Ganondorf, [[Inl Astor]] and his old boss, Impa—who he recommended for the job. At the Sheikah Catacombs underneath [[Kakariko City]], Impa led the Commission through the Sheikah church and the catacombs underneath, until eventually reaching an area with various trials. While Ganondorf was distracted with a fire outside the forest, Impa and the others recovered the [[The Triforce|Triforce]] Shard.

Later, the team once more united and began their journey towards [[The Zora|Zora’s Domain]]. On the way, they found Princess Zelda, having escaped from the shackles of the Followers with her own skill. She accompanied them to the underwater temple, where Ganondorf claimed the shard hid. The Followers attempted to gain the piece for themselves, but were foiled. Ganondorf took the Triforce Shard shard the warnings of a Follower boy, matching the description of [[Link Steadfast]]. Pulling it out, as the boy had warned, caused the ground the city rested on above them to shake and collapse, destroying large parts of the city. Most engineers believe that the Triforce was powering a device that kept the ground steady, but how is not known.

After attempting to alleviate the suffering and requesting Royal humanitarian aid, the Commission departed from Zora’s Domain for their next destination: [[Lost Woods|The Lost Woods]].

Once passing through [[Minishi Town|Minishi Town]], the Commission was joined by [[Master Kohga|General Ahoga]]. Inside the woods, they split up. Following details are scarce; however, we know that Ganondorf entered deep into the forest and recovered a shard of the Triforce, while other members—including Astor, Sahon, and General Ahoga—entered the [[Temple of Time]]. After discovering the [[Master Sword]]’s pedestal was empty, Ahoga betrayed.

As described by Impa, in a single moment, Sahon was rammed through the chest with a blade. He fell to the ground, dead, before being able to say a single word.

Like many others, Sahon shared [[The Name Link|the name Link]].
