**The Great Sea** is the colloquial name of the oceans of the world. The most well-explored part is to the east of [[Hyrule|Hyrule]], and to the west of [[Holodrum (Historia)|Holodrum]] and [[Labrynna (Historia)|Labrynna]]. In this part of the Great Sea, resides many islands, such as the important [[Economy|trade]] hub of [[Koridai (Historia)|Koridai]]. The Great Sea is littered with islands many independent, and is extremely important for international trade. Despite this, due to its massive nature, many parts of it remain unexplored, even between Hyrule and Holodrum. This is particularly truth to the west of Hyrule and east of Labrynna and Holodrum.

Individual islands are intentionally not mentioned in the [[(Beginning of the Historia)|Hyrule Historia]], due to the countless number, and their limited relevance to the history of Hyrule, the main purpose of this volume. The exception to this rule are Koridai and [[Zuna Republic (Historia)|Zunari]], due to their important relations to [[Economy|trade]] and the [[The Gerudo|Gerudo]] respectively.
