Currently, the economy of the [[The Followers of Dragmire, Revolutionaries|Revolutionary]]-controlled areas of [[Hyrule|Hyrule]] is already struggling heavily due to the war, with the [[The Royalists|Royalists]] faring much better because of the economic aid received from [[Holodrum|Holodrum]] and [[Calatia|Calatia]].

The international economy is also suffering greatly from the Civil Wars in various countries, the most damaging being [[Revolution and Second Civil War|Hyrule’s]]. Places and countries like [[Koridai|Koridai]] hope the crisis is resolved quickly so that the economic woes they are suffering quickly pass.

[[Rupees|Rupees]] are still the most important currency in the world in which international business is done, but that may change in the coming years. The currency of Holodrum is a proposed alternative.
