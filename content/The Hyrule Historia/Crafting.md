**Crafting** refers to the art of creating something new and useful out of raw materials. Often, this is with metallurgy to create weapons, worked by a Blacksmith.

[[The Gorons|Gorons]] are usually considered among the best blacksmiths, but skilled smiths can be found in all peoples, in all places, from all walks of life. In recent years, with mass production, the production of many weapons of medium quality has been largely streamlined, using manual laborers following precise instructions. However, blacksmiths still find work in constructing unique blades for individuals or creating new designs that they can sell to a [[Guilds|Guild]] for them to produce. The biggest of these Guilds sells the Fighter line and are usually considered the best, most premium weapons.
