**Collin Phurn** was the Lord Chancellor of Economics up until the [[Revolution and Second Civil War|Revolution]], where he perished—a tragic end to a life-long career in economic philosophy and service to the crown.

Phurn was the son of two minor [[Capitalist and Nobleman Class|noblemen]]. As a young man, Phurn worked for various [[Guilds|Guilds]], before eventually becoming a statistician and auditor. Later on, he jumped around various economic jobs in the government. This included: budgeter for the [[Hyrulean Geography|Central Hyrulean region]], advisor on tax policy, managing and overseeing grain supply lanes, a position in labor-dispute in which he was very successful for many years, master of Guild-government relations, budgeter for the entire [[Former Hyrulean Government and Royal Family|Royal government]], and many other positions. Finally, towards the end of his long and storied career, he was made a Royal Administrator, a position directly underneath the Chancellor of Economics, a [[The Council|council]] position. When his superior on the council resigned—which was shortly before the death of [[Ealden Frendell|Frendell]]—Phurn was handpicked by the [[Last King of Hyrule|King]] for his long resume to replace the retiring councilman. Collin became Chancellor Phurn, a member of the council. Officially, his title was Lord Chancellor of Economics. He served honorably and stably in that position. Phurn became a friend of [[Princess Zelda|Princess Zelda]] in this position, greatly respecting her wisdom and measured nature. Despite his humble origins and birth, Phurn became one of the highest-ranking individuals in the government.

However, as it did for many things, the [[The Followers of Dragmire, Revolutionaries|Followers of Dragmire]] marked the beginning of the end for Phurn. The Chancellor was present when [[Ganondorf|Ganondorf]] appeared at the [[Festival of the Flood|Festival of the Flood]], and survived the following raid on [[Hyrule Castle|Hyrule Castle]]. Rumors say he knocked the [[The Sorcerer|Sorcerer]] unconscious during a fight prior to her apparent death, but such a thing is almost certainly false. Members of the court acknowledge that Phurn was skeptical about Ganondorf and his motives.

Later, despite his concerns, the Chancellor authorized emergency economic measures and [[The Draft, Mobilization|mass conscription]]that was in response to the impending [[Revolution and Second Civil War|Revolution]]. Chancellor Phurn, unfortunately, was at Hyrule Castle during the Followers' attack on the Castle, and died along with his King, and the Princess he so admired.
