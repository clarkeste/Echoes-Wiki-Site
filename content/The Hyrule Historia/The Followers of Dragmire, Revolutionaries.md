**The Followers of Dragmire**, officially calling their government the **“Republic of Hyrule”**, and colloquially known as “**the Revolutionaries**”, were a rebellious organization in [[Hyrule]]. Their leaders, most notably [[The Pragmatist|the Pragmatist]], staged the [[Revolution and Second Civil War|Revolution]] in which they overthrew the [[Last King of Hyrule|King of Hyrule]] and his [[Former Hyrulean Government and Royal Family|Royal government]]. They are named after [[Ganondorf Dragmire]]. In the current day, they are one of the factions in the Civil War, led by [[The Confessor (Historia)|the Confessor]].

The first mentions of the Followers as an organization can be traced to approximately three hundred years ago, to the [[Hylian-Gerudo War|Hylian-Gerudo War]]. Their very beginnings were as a group advocating for peace between the [[The Gerudo|Gerudo]] and the [[The Hylians|Hylians]]. 

Sometime after, however, they transformed into a cultist group that saw [[Ganondorf Dragmire|Ganondorf Dragmire]] as a metaphor for resistance against the [[Former Hyrulean Government and Royal Family|Hyrulean Royal Family]]. They rejected the widely-accepted truth that Dragmire was behind the attempt to [[The Flood|flood]] [[Hyrule|Hyrule]]. Dragmire's legacy was seen as a symbol for resistance against the supposed tyranny of the Royal Family. Many members developed a religious fanaticism to the Demon King, believing him to be the great avenger of the wronged.

Despite this, the Followers were still a small group, compromised of perhaps a hundred members. This is the way it would continue for several hundred years, until closer to the modern day. With more and more abuses, perceived or otherwise, from the Royal Family and the [[Last King of Hyrule|King of Hyrule]], as well as the distance of time obfuscating Dragmire's crimes, the stage was ripe for the growth of the Followers of Dragmire.

Several decades ago, despite their increased size, the Followers remained aimless. Squabbles emerged between those who had joined the group due to their hatred of the Royal Family, seeing Dragmire as simply a symbolic metaphor, and those who genuinely revered the Demon King of old. These squabbles threatened to tear the Followers apart.

However, a new figure emerged; one who would turn cultists into Revolutionaries, one who would unify the rowdy, one who would besiege the world and bring nations to their knees through only his words. The [[The Pragmatist|Pragmatist]]. 

The Pragmatist united the Followers decades ago solely behind their goal of overthrowing the government by various means, including appointing various leaders—referred to as [[The Chosen (Historia)|Chosen]], to lead their own branches of the Followers of Dragmire, who would cooperate in operations organized by the Pragmatist and lead [[The Messengers, Follower Ranks|their own devotees]]. This meant that those of varying ideologies would not be disturbed by those more or less devoted. Additionally, the Pragmatist chose to avoid fantasies of what their new, supposedly ‘fairer’ world would look like. Instead, he spoke of what unified the disparate, would-be Revolutionaries: hatred for the Royal Family and odium for the leaders of society. These appointed Chosen were the [[Master Kohga (Historia)|Believer]]—a Sheikah with a veneer of moderation—and the [[The Sorcerer|Sorcerer]], a fanatic of the highest calling.

Testimony from former members of the Cult confirm that the Pragmatist tailored his words to his audience very carefully. He always sought new recruits. He did this by absorbing other resistance groups into the fold. Initially, many refused to work with the Followers. This was always due to either the use of Dragmire in their name, or due to their willingness to use violence. Over time, this resistance was shown for what it was; a front, a veil of morality. Those who seek to change the world always end up speaking the same language.

The last [[The Chosen (Historia)|Chosen]] to join their ranks was six years ago, when a woman who identified herself as the Confessor attacked [[Kakariko City (Historia)|Kakariko City]] with impressive power and who assembled her own branch. The Confessor's was reportedly the only branch where mentions of the Demon King of old were sparse, though the author of this book is skeptical of this claim which seems intended to rehabilitate their image.

Over time, the membership of all three branches of the Followers were bolstered and increased when the Royal Family outlawed and persecuted more and more peaceful dissidents, discriminated against the [[Sheikah|Sheikah]], and burned down protester’s buildings and posters. An example of this was the [[Castle Town Parliamentarians|Castle Town Parliamentarians]], a supposedly peaceful group that was hit with the full force of the law shortly before the Confessor appeared. With no legal and non-violent resistance organizations left, and often being wanted for their crimes, many of these activists and sympathizers chose to align with an illegal, revolutionary organization. The only one among those groups that had the organization and gall to operate all throughout Hyrule were the Followers of Dragmire—and so, the only reasonable choice. Many of these individuals joined the Confessor's branch, and some the Believer's branch; not an insignificant amount, however, were radicalized by their experiences and later joined the Sorcerer's branch of the Followers.

An example of this is the now famous—or, infamous—[[Link Steadfast (Historia)|Link Steadfast]], who was a member of the Castle Town Parliamentarians before joining the Confessor's branch and remaining there.

The Followers’ growth and increased presence in mainstream political conversation—as well as fear of them among the [[Capitalist and Nobleman Class|nobility]], [[Guilds|guilds]], and government—increased over the six years between the Confessor's appearance and the Revolution. On the orders of the Pragmatist, all operations that could hurt the citizenry were paused, as to not alienate the population. Despite this, most in Hyrule still saw the Followers as a frightening organization, especially after the Sorcerer and the Believer chose to strike at [[Hyrule Castle (Historia)|Hyrule Castle]] when the new [[Ganondorf (Historia)|Ganondorf]] arrived in Hyrule. This was a result of their tendency to worship Dragmire, who bore the same first name as Ganondorf. During the raid, Ganondorf proved his loyalty to the King by killing the Sorcerer, although she later appears to have unnaturally extended her life to escape the valley of death.

In the following week, the Followers prepared and moved their resources to enact the Revolution. Their final move, however, was one that nobody—even members of the Followers of Dragmire—saw coming. The Confessor issued a '[[Broadcast|broadcast]]' by using [[Spellcasting|sorcery]] to show moving images in every torch and watery reflection in every major city in Hyrule. This broadcast showed one of the members of the Followers, [[Link Steadfast (Historia)|Link Steadfast]], wielding the [[Master Sword (Historia)|Master Sword]]. This was intended to reduce the divine legitimacy of the Royal Family. It succeeded. Steadfast urged the people of Hyrule to rise up against the [[Last King of Hyrule|King of Hyrule]].

The leaders of the Followers, largely the Believer and the Confessor but likely also the Pragmatist, used their resources and contacts to stage a revolution in [[Kakariko City (Historia)|Kakariko City]] that forced the [[Governor of Kakariko (Historia)|Governor]] to flee. The city was soon captured by Revolutionary militants. Using the city as a base, the Followers—all branches now united—marched on [[Castle Town (Historia)|Hyrule Castle Town]]. There, they sieged the city and, using spies inside [[Hyrule Castle (Historia)|Hyrule Castle]], killed the King as well as [[Princess Zelda (Historia)|Princess Zelda]]. It is believed that the Confessor and Steadfast were among those inside the Castle, and that the two of them dealt the final blow to the new and loyal [[Ganondorf (Historia)|Ganondorf]], who died attempting to protect the King and the remaining members of the [[The Council (Historia)|Council]].

In the weeks following, the Followers secured the east of the country and pushed the [[The Royalists|Royalists]] to the west. They began the transition into becoming a provisional government, with the stated intention of holding democratic elections. The Confessor then became the acting Head of State, tasked with stabilizing and creating a new government. Afterwards, she appointed the defector, [[Lakoh (Historia)|Lakoh]], and—after political pressure—the Sorcerer, among others, to high-ranking political positions in the new government. The Confessor still acts as a leader for the ‘New Hyrulean’ government, but she intends, or so she claims, for her role to become less and less important as she seeks to soon host democratic elections. The truth of these claims is unknown. So is whether mob rule, or ‘democracy’, is even a desirable or sustainable system of governance.

The Confessor is still the ultimate authority in the Revolutionary government, however. She is a powerful and liked leader—for now, at least. She is considered the Follower leader who has most pushed for democracy and their own brand of freedom; with Lakoh too ‘moderate’ and cautious on every issue regardless of severity, and Sorcerer too radical and fervent to burn everything of the old government down regardless of who suffers.
