Death Mountain, or Eldin Volcano, is the name of an active volcano in the northwest of [[Hyrule]] in the [[Hyrulean Geography|Eldin]] region. It is the highest mountain in Hyrule, and is the home of [[The Gorons|the Gorons]] and Goron City.

Almost all of Death Mountain is a part of the Goron Neutral Zone, including some surrounding areas. This includes [[Kakariko Village]], which is at the base of the volcano.
