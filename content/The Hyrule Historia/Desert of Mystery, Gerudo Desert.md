**The Gerudo Desert**, officially known as the **Desert of Mystery** following the [[Hylian-Gerudo War]], is a region in the southwest of [[Hyrule]]. It, as the name implies, is a largely barren desert, although it has been colonized by [[The Hylians|Hylians]] who founded the now-thriving [[Calditin City]].

In the past, the Gerudo Desert was inhabited by the [[The Gerudo|Gerudo]], a group thought of in Hyrule proper as a roving band of thieves. However, after the [[The Flood|Flood]], it was decided that the Gerudo could not be trusted, and had to be forcibly expelled.

After this, the Gerudo Desert was fully incorporated into Hyrule. Old structures and towns belonging to the Gerudo are now mere tourist attractions; coffins long buried by time.

Deep into the desert—usually defined as where Hyrule’s official claim to the territory ends—is the [[Desert of Doubt]]. This is where the Gerudo fled after being exiled, and is even more inhospitable than the Desert of Mystery.
