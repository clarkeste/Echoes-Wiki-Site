The Carndetti Massacre occurred about three weeks prior to the [[Revolution and Second Civil War|Revolution]] which toppled the Hyrulean [[Former Hyrulean Government and Royal Family|monarchy]] and began the Hyrulean [[Revolution and Second Civil War|Civil War]]. In response to a growing protest in the large town of Carndetti, located between [[Castle Town (Historia)|Castle Town]] and [[Hagansville]], the Royal forces tasked with quelling the uprising used violence that some would characterize as a massacre. It is believed that the events at Carndetti was one of the reasons why, despite lacking wide popular support, many citizens’ quiet hostility to the Revolution materialized only as apathy instead of opposition.

Carndetti, a town with a population of about sixty thousand, is an important waystop between Hagansville and Castle Town. Due to rising discontent regarding higher prices for various goods, especially a spike in the last year caused by the war in [[Koridai (Historia)|Koridai]] with [[Labrynna (Historia)|Labrynna]], many citizens of Carndetti felt wronged. However, there is evidence to suggest that the protest at Carndetti was also attended by many from outside the town, due to the presence of a garrison in the town and its reputation as a hub for the wealthy. 

Additionally, evidence highly suggests that various agitators, largely from the [[The Followers of Dragmire, Revolutionaries|Followers of Dragmire]], acted as plants inside the protest to steer it towards their own ends, including potentially violence to act as a rallying cry for the nation.

The protestors approached the garrison on the eve of dusk in Carndetti, along with some of the mansions in the area. They began pelting the mansion and soldiers with rocks. Shortly after, a soldier among those attempting to maintain order fired a shot from his Crossbow. It is widely believed that this was an accidental discharge, although some have put forward theories that this soldier was either an agitator for the Followers recruited to the garrison, or an individual who pretended to be a soldier.

The shot set off a panic which resulted in fights and brawls with the security forces that left 2 soldiers dead, and 41 protestors dead, and another 53 wounded. The [[Former Hyrulean Government and Royal Family|Royal government]] stated that the blame for these tragic deaths lied solely on the protestors and agitators in the crowd, and declined to prosecute any soldiers involved in the affair. It is undeniable, in this author’s opinion, that the Royal authorities acted with reckless abandon for sense and logic during this event, which legitimized the claims of radicals who used their mistakes and coldness for their own benefit.

A majority of the deaths were due to chaos and trampling as the demonstrators jumped over one another to flee, but this did not change that in the public consciousness, the garrison soldiers and the [[Former Hyrulean Government and Royal Family|Royal government]] were seen as bearing responsibility. Along with the appearance of [[Ganondorf]], the unease regarding his presence, the institution of [[The Draft, Mobilization|mobilization]], as well as the surprise announcement of [[Link Steadfast (Historia)|Link Steadfast]], it is thought by scholars of the Revolution that this was how the Revolutionary forces managed to gain enough support with which to revolt.

After witnessing such tumult, many analysts and scholars asked the same question: How? How could a country in relative health be the victim of such a Revolution?

In the lead-up to the Revolution, many factors caused discontent among the populace, such as the Carndetti Massacre. However, it is believed that despite this the Revolution was not a movement with widely popular support. Indeed, the Kingdom, for whatever flaws it may have had, completed with the basic duties of a state. It provided security, a stable economy, and access to lodgings and food for those who worked. Instead, the Revolutionaries fostered and acted upon only the bare minimum of support required, which manifested as apathy or indecision among most of the population rather than approval.

So the Revolution did not have wide or far-reaching popular support, regardless of what the Revolutionaries may wish to claim. Yet, the Revolution succeeded. Why? Largely, due to the organization and underhandedness of the Followers of Dragmire who had been planning for years for just the coup. Indeed, with Hyrulean armies in [[Koridai (Historia)|Koridai]] and [[Labrynna (Historia)|Labrynna]], the Revolutionaries were able to move swiftly and act upon decades of preparation; their spies allowed them to kill the leadership structure of the Kingdom of Hyrule; and their ploys of such as the Broadcast allowed them to paralyze the opposition.
