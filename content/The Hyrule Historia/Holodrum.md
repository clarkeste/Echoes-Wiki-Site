**The Empire of Holodrum**, colloquially known as just **Holodrum**, is one of the most powerful nations in the world. Located to the east, across the ocean from [[Hyrule|Hyrule]], it has historically been one of world's most influential country, trading the spot with Hyrule depending on the circumstances of any given time.

Prior to the [[Revolution and Second Civil War|Revolution]], Holodrum and Hyrule were rivals for influence in the world, however this rivalry never broke out into armed conflict. They competed for influence in various nations and islands, and clashed most recently in [[Koridai|Koridai]]. However, both united to support Koridai militarily when [[Labrynna|Labrynna]] attempted to invade the country after vigilantes in the island-nation executed Labrynnan diplomats and visiting politicians.

It is usually thought that having such a troop presence in Koridai is one of the reasons why, during the Hyrulean Revolution, there was a lack of troops to stop the [[The Followers of Dragmire, Revolutionaries|Followers]]. Indeed, after the Revolution split Hyrule in half, Holodrum became the uncontested champion of the world stage. They have exerted influence over countries that were previously firmly in the Hyrulean sphere of influence, such as [[Hytopia|Hytopia]] and [[Calatia|Calatia]].

They have supported the [[The Royalists|Royalists]] in the Second Hyrulean Civil War, as well as the Royalists in the Civil War in Hytopia, hoping to gain governments favorable to them. In Hytopia, a large reason for the victories—and predicted total wipeout of the Rebels—is attributed to not just the support of Holodrum in the form of munitions, but direct military intervention on the behalf of the Royalists, leaving the soon-to-be undisputed government of Hytopia in their debt. 

Despite not doing anything quite so bold in Hyrule yet, they have consistently sold weapons, equipment, and warships to the Royalist faction at extremely low prices--some even gifted, or leased without immediate charge. They have collaborated with Calatia in delivering these munitions and maintaining ships at the Royalist coast of Hyrule, often-times escorting Royalist ships to dissuade Follower raiders. There are even rumors of Holodrum hiring [[Piracy, Smuggling|privateers]] to disrupt trade and sink ships delivering supplies in and out of [[Kakariko City|Kakariko City’s]] ports. 

The young [[Emperor of Holodrum]], Kanadei, likely seeks not just ideological consistency in the factions he supports, but to build governments that will be indebted to him and under the influence of the Empire of Holodrum.

Kanadei himself came into power after the death of his father. His father was seen as an ailing and weak old man who faltered in the face of adversity, an image not at all applicable to his son. His son, Kanadei, has instead been called the voice of the nation.
