**The Sorcerer** was a [[The Chosen|Chosen]], and therefore one of the leaders of the [[The Followers of Dragmire, Revolutionaries|Followers of Dragmire]]. Now, after seemingly cheating death, she is an influential member of the Revolutionary government, and one of the most vocal proponents of the earliest tenets of the Followers. Her real name is **Agnin Carolin**. She often clashed with her compatriots the [[Master Kohga|Believer]] and the [[The Confessor|Confessor]], but ultimately still followed the same master of the [[The Pragmatist|Pragmatist]].

She joined the Followers at a young age, and served in the organization for over a decade. While little is known of her life before, it is known she was raised as a powerful sorcerer, likely among the [[Capitalist and Nobleman Class|nobility]]. As an invaluable and ideologically fanatic member of the Followers, she was quickly promoted to the rank of Chosen and was given the name the Sorcerer by the cult's leader, the [[The Pragmatist|Pragmatist]]. She was—and is—a 'true believer' in their cause, particularly as it relates to [[Ganondorf Dragmire|Dragmire]].

She was reportedly responsible for the Follower operations in the [[Desert of Mystery, Gerudo Desert|Gerudo Desert]], including the mission that led to the death of [[Ganondorf|Ganondorf]]'s sister, which caused the Gerudo's hatred of the Followers of Dragmire. Attempting to revive their dark lord, the Dragmire of old, was an attempt that ended in failure; however, that did not deter the Sorcerer from her cause. Instead, Carolin continued serving the Followers. She was involved in various missions, including raids in the Gerudo Desert and [[Hyrulean Geography|Central Hyrule]]. She was dangerous and wanted across the country for her subversive actions. 

She met her 'end' at the [[Festival of the Flood|Festival of the Flood]] last year, when she and her compatriot, the [[Master Kohga|Believer]], attempted a raid on [[Hyrule Castle|Hyrule Castle]] along with their cultists. However, such a raid ended in disaster as Ganondorf, whom the Sorcerer had made an enemy of so long before, foiled their attempts. In a climatic battle, Ganondorf killed the Sorcerer.

Yet, that was not the end of her. Following the successful [[Revolution and Second Civil War|Revolution]] and the death of the [[Last King of Hyrule|King]] and his [[Princess Zelda|daughter]]—something Carolin had long wanted—Sorcerer returned. Sources who knew her prior to the war report that she looks... different than how she did previously, although if this is a side-effect from how she survived is unknown. 

She now serves as one of the most important members of the new government, alongside the [[The Confessor|Confessor]], and the Royalist defector, [[Lakoh|Lakoh]]. She often represents a more radical branch of the government, that seeks to wipe away traces of the old regime at nearly any cost. Some are concerned about if she still follows Dragmire as her religion. Additionally, many worry about her authoritarian tendencies. Despite this, she provides an alluring message for her supporters: Complete change and the destruction of shackles and all the systems of injustice and suffering.
