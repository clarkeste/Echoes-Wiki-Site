**General Noril** is the current leader of the [[The Royalists|Royalist Armed Forces]]. He is a veteran of the [[Hyrule|Hyrulean]] Army and was a Captain in the [[Last King of Hyrule|King]]’s Royal Guard. In the years leading up to the [[Revolution and Second Civil War|Revolution]], he was changed to independent command, becoming a General in the panic shortly prior to the Revolution. He was tasked with leading the armed forces in [[Castle Town (Historia)|Castle Town (Historia)]] to meet the marching revolutionaries; however, his army was routed due to a sneak attack, and he was forced back to [[Hyrule Castle (Historia)|Hyrule Castle]].

He was taken prisoner during the assault on Hyrule Castle, but in the chaos, managed to escape and regroup near a garrison outside of Castle Town. There, he took command of forces that attempted several times to re-take the city. After repeated failures, he and his troops fled across the river and announced that they would continue to fight.

With much of the Hyrulean chain of command either dead or serving in [[Koridai (Historia)|Koridai]], Noril was made the Chief of Staff, in charge of all [[The Royalists|Royalist]] operations—essentially, their leader. After months of fighting, the ever-increasing danger demanded that he moved to a more central spot; seeing nowhere better, Noril turned [[Lanayru Citadel (Historia)|Lanayru Citadel]] into a fortress and headquarters for the Royalist military.

Today, Noril still leads the Royalist military. Despite being outranked by four generals stationed in Koridai, they and their allies in [[Holodrum (Historia)|Holodrum]] have been hesitant to send them, for fear of assassination by the [[The Followers of Dragmire, Revolutionaries|Followers]]; and Noril’s experience and familiarity with the war currently make him the best pick.

Noril serves alongside the [[Governor of Kakariko (Historia)|former Governor of Kakariko]], who primarily handles the foreign affairs of the Royalist cause. Additionally, Noril had pledged to restore the [[Former Hyrulean Government and Royal Family|monarchy]], and—somewhat controversially—said he intends to invite [[Prince Lusand (Historia)|Prince Lusand]] back into the country to do so.
