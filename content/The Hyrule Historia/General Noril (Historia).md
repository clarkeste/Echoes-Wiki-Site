**General Noril** is the current leader of the [[The Royalists|Royalist Armed Forces]]. He is a veteran of the [[Hyrule|Hyrulean]] Army and was a Captain in the [[Last King of Hyrule|King]]’s Royal Guard. In the years leading up to the [[Revolution and Second Civil War|Revolution]], he was changed to independent command, becoming a General in the panic shortly prior to the Revolution. He was tasked with leading the armed forces in [[Castle Town (Historia)|Hyrule Castle Town]] to meet the marching revolutionaries; however, his army was routed due to a sneak attack, and he was forced back to [[Hyrule Castle (Historia)|Hyrule Castle]].

He was taken prisoner during the assault on Hyrule Castle, but in the chaos, managed to escape and regroup near a garrison outside of Castle Town. There, he took command of forces that attempted several times to re-take the city. After repeated failures and a counter-attack, he and his troops fled across the river and announced that they would continue to fight.

With much of the Hyrulean chain of command either dead or serving in [[Koridai (Historia)|Koridai]], Noril was made the acting Supreme General-Commander or Chief of Staff, in charge of all [[The Royalists|Royalist]] operations—essentially, their leader. After months of fighting, the ever-increasing danger demanded that he move to a more central spot to oversee the army; seeing nowhere better, Noril turned [[Lanayru Citadel (Historia)|Lanayru Citadel]] into a fortress and headquarters for the Royalist military.

Today, Noril still leads the Royalist military. Despite being technically outranked by some generals stationed in Koridai, they and their allies in [[Holodrum (Historia)|Holodrum]] have been hesitant to deploy them, for fear of assassination by the [[The Followers of Dragmire, Revolutionaries|Followers]]. Noril’s experience and familiarity with the war currently make him the best pick, even if initially a power-struggle of sorts occurred and many, both within and outside Hyrule, are not satisfied with Noril’s leadership.

Noril serves alongside the [[Governor of Kakariko (Historia)|former Governor of Kakariko]], who primarily handles the foreign affairs of the Royalist cause. Additionally, Noril had pledged to restore the [[Former Hyrulean Government and Royal Family|monarchy]], and—somewhat controversially—said he intends to invite [[Prince Lusand (Historia)|Prince Lusand]] back into the country to do so. Official sources from the Royalist movement have stated there is no official decision yet taken.
