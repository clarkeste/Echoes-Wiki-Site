Staffs are long wooden poles or rods that are used by [[Spell Creation|Spellcasters]] to enhance their sorcery. Why exactly carrying a large wooden stick is helpful is something of a mystery to scholars; but most suspect the effect is psychological and due to cultural association, like how an actor may feel better embedded into a role once he dons a cape.

These staffs also often come up with a wide-brimmed, puffy hat that has a similar effect on people. Both young and old Spellcasters, particularly those that received a formal education in [[Spellcasting]], often use this wardrobe.
![](https://i.imgur.com/gtbQkZu.png)
[[Encumbrance]]: 3
Effect: Whenever rolling a [[Force]] [[Skill Checks|Check]], may suffer one [[Strain]] to add one [[Boost Dice|Boost Die]] to the results. Must be held in hand to use; one-handed. If used to attack directly, use [[Improvised Weapons]] rules.

Price: 150 [[Rupees]]
Affiliation: None
