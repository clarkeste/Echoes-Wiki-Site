Torches are a larger and brighter version of the Lantern, much more versatile with its rod-like shape. It can also serve as a weapon in a pinch.
![](https://i.imgur.com/gtbQkZu.png)
[[Encumbrance]]: 1
Effect: Creates light up to [[Medium Range]] and decent heat. If used as a [[Weapons|Weapon]], has the same Weapon profile as the [[Dull Rod|Dull Rod]] with the removal of the [[Flammable (Passive) (Quality)|Flammable]] [[Qualities|Quality]], and the addition of [[Burn (Active) (Quality)|Burn]] 2 if the Torch is lit. Will run out faster than a lamp.

Price: 20
Affiliation: None
