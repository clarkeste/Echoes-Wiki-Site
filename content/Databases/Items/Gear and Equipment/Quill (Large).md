A large Quill that fits on the back of an individual. It holds a large amount of arrows, especially for its size, letting a soldier operate without a refill for a long time.
![](https://i.imgur.com/ujzMuPw.png)
[[Encumbrance|Encumbrance]]: 1
Effect: If a [[Despair]] or three [[Threat]] causes you to run out of arrows, may refill using the spare arrows in your Quill (Large) twice before needing to restock. Only one Quill can be worn at a time.

Price: 499 [[Rupees]]
