Alcohol is a compound that many find relaxing and that act to soothe their worries, but can cause intoxication if too much is drunk.
![](https://i.imgur.com/chTvfOy.png)
[[Encumbrance]]: 0
Effect: When drunk, reduce [[Strain]] by 5. On the next use on that day, reduce Strain only by 4, then 3, then none. After each usage of Alcohol, add a [[Setback Dice|Setback Die]] to all [[Skill Checks|Checks]] for the next [[Encounter]]. This stacks. If three bottles are drunk, gain two Setbacks that remain for the entire day.

Must be stored within a container; glass [[Bottle|Bottles]] are not necessary but can be used. A Bottle has two serving of Alcohol. Wooden or ceramic containers can be used. Every container will be at least 1 [[Encumbrance]].

Price: 20 [[Rupees]], if buying wooden container +10 Rupees
