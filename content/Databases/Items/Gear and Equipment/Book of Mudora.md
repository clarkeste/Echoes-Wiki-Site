The Book of Mudora is a publication that is a few decades old. When it released, it was lauded for its extreme effort in translating ancient Hylian, Gerudo, and other languages to modern Hylian. Editions of it are still published today. Its author, still alive, has refused to sell the rights to any major [[Guilds|guild]], allowing this tome to be largely accessible and affordable.
