With this tool, it becomes possible to attempt to pry open most modern locks, and many old ones as well. However, its restricted nature has made finding and purchasing them very expensive, unless one has the proper contacts.

[[Encumbrance|Encumbrance]]: 1
Effect: Can be used on any door or gate that uses a traditional lock to begin a [[Skulduggery]] [[Skill Checks|Check]] to open the door. On both success or failure, the Lockpick is consumed. [[Difficulty]] depends on the quality of the lock.

Price: 350 [[Rupees]] [[Restricted|(R)]]
[[Affiliations|Affiliation]]: None
