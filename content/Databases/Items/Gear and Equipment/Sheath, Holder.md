Storage for a sword, spear, bow, or [[Shields|Shield]]. Sheaths and their equivalents often match with their swords to fit perfectly, often tied to the wielder’s back or their side.

[[Encumbrance|Encumbrance]]: 0
Special: May ignore the [[Encumbrance|Encumbrance]] of a single Sword, Spear, Bow, other [[Weapons|Weapon]] or [[Shields|Shield]] while inside its sheath so long as the Weapon is [[Encumbrance|Encumbrance]] 5 or less. A [[Character]] may only wear two sheaths at a time.

Price: 199 [[Rupees]]
