A box, bag, or pouch, this bag is useful for storing items inside and making them more convenient to carry.

[[Encumbrance|Encumbrance]]: Varies
Effect: May put in up to 5 [[Items]] of any type into the container. While inside, the total [[Encumbrance|Encumbrance]] of all items and the container is reduced to half. If all or most of the Items are of the same type and are easily foldable or stackable, reduce Encumbrance to 3 in total. Cannot place [[Weapons]], [[Shields]], [[Armor]], or other unwieldy Items inside.

Price: 99 [[Rupees]]
[[Affiliations|Affiliation]]: None
