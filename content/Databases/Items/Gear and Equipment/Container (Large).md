A larger container, such as a crate or large backpack, that can carry many things inside of it.
![](https://i.imgur.com/stullwf.png)
[[Encumbrance|Encumbrance]]: 6
Effect: May put in up to 8 objects or Items of any type into the container. While inside, the total [[Encumbrance|Encumbrance]] of all items and the container is reduced to half. If all or most of the Items are of the same type and are easily foldable or stackable, may put many more than 8.

Price: 500 [[Rupees]]
[[Affiliations|Affiliation]]: None
