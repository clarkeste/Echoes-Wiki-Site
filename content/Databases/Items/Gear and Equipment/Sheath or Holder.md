Storage for a sword, spear, bow, or [[Weapons#Shields|Shield]]. Sheaths and their equivalents often match with their swords to fit perfectly, often tied to the wielder’s back or their side.
![](https://i.imgur.com/qmb5BbX.png)
[[Encumbrance|Encumbrance]]: 0
Special: May reduce the [[Encumbrance|Encumbrance]] of a single Sword, Spear, Bow, other [[Weapons|Weapon]] or [[Weapons#Shields|Shield]] while inside its sheath by 4. A [[Character]] may wear up to two sheaths at a time, but a single Weapon can only have its Encumbrance reduced by one Sheath.

Price: 400 [[Rupees]]
[[Affiliations|Affiliation]]: None
