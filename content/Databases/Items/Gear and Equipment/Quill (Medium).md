A Quilll for true enthusiast, and commonly used in armies for logistical purposes. It can hold more Arrows than [[Quill (Small)|small Quills]], allowing you more arrows in a pinch.

Effect: If a [[Despair]] or three [[Threat]] causes you to run out of arrows, may refill using the spare arrows in your Quill (Medium) once before needing to restock. Only one Quill can be worn at a time.
