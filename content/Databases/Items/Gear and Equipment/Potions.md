Potions, created via [[Potion Creation|alchemy]], are among the most valuable [[Items]] in Hyrule. They can serve a wide variety of services to a wide variety of people, such as increasing one’s energy, increasing one’s hardiness, or even something as simple as being used by insomniacs for a peaceful night of rest.

Any of the following types of Potions can be purchased, but must be stored in a [[Bottle]]. If they not stored in a glass Bottle their quality or their effects can be reduced by twice or more.

Potions have no [[Encumbrance]] on their own. Only one Potion can be stored in a Bottle at any given time.

All [[Potions]] listed and widely available must be ingested for their effects to occur.
### Red Potion

### Green Potion
Restores 7 [[Strain]].

Price: 80 [[Rupees]]
[[Affiliations|Affiliation]]: None

### Blue Potion
Restores 6 [[Wounds]] and 6 [[Strain]].

Price: 399 [[Rupees]]
[[Affiliations|Affiliation]]: None

### Purple Potion
Add a [[Boost Dice|Boost Die]] to every [[Attack Check]] for the next two [[Encounter|Encounters]] or the next day, whichever is shorter.

Price: 299 [[Rupees]]
[[Affiliations|Affiliation]]: None

### Yellow Potion
When drunk, the body temperature of the consuming [[Character]] is stabilized at a comfortable level for the next day.

Price: 199 [[Rupees]]
[[Affiliations|Affiliation]]: None

### Secret Medicine
When drunk, restore 3 [[Wounds]]. If a [[Character]] is exceeds their [[Wound Threshold]] and would otherwise be [[Incapacitated]] sometime in the next day, restore 7 [[Wounds]] immediately. 

Price: 499 [[Rupees]]
[[Affiliations|Affiliation]]: None

### Fairy
Should a Fairy choose to willingly help a [[Character]], they will activate when a Character receives the End Is Nigh or Dead [[Critical Injury]]. The Character will then reduce the result of the Critical Injury by -70, and the Fairy will leave. This may not apply in cases  where a Character’s body no longer exists, such as incineration. 

Price: ???
[[Affiliations|Affiliation]]: ???

### Stun Potion
A Stun Potion–usually used as anesthesia–naturally looks a dark and sickly blue. However, with the proper brew, it can be colored to look differently. When drunk, a [[Character]] suffers 8 [[Strain]].

Price: 299 [[Restricted|(R)]] [[Rupees]]
[[Affiliations|Affiliation]]: None

### Poison Potion
A Poison Potion naturally looks a dark and sickly purple; however, with the proper brew, it can be colored to look differently. When drunk, suffer 7 [[Wounds]] and make a [[Difficulty|Hard]] (3p) [[Resilience|Resilience]] [[Skill Checks|Check]]. Uncanceled [[Failure|Failures]] add [[Wounds]], uncanceled [[Threat]] adds [[Strain]] or the [[Disoriented|Disoriented]] effect, and [[Despair]] add a [[Critical Injury]]. These rules can be used for any poison, with a modified [[Difficulty]] and immediate Wounds suffered.

Price: 799 [[Restricted|(R)]] [[Rupees]]
[[Affiliations|Affiliation]]: None

### Sleep Potion
When drunk, the [[Character]] who consumed it must make a [[Difficulty|Hard]] (3p) [[Resilience]] or [[Discipline]] [[Skill Checks|Check]] if they wish to resist its effects. If the Check fails, or if they choose not to resist its effects, the [[Character]] falls [[Incapacitated|unconscious]].

Price: 99 [[Rupees]]
[[Affiliations|Affiliation]]: None
