A Quiver for true enthusiast, and commonly used in armies for logistical purposes. It can hold more Arrows than [[Quiver (Small)|small Quivers]], allowing you more arrows in a pinch.
![](https://i.imgur.com/i6hvQiA.png)
[[Encumbrance|Encumbrance]]: 0
Effect: If a [[Despair]] or three [[Threat]] causes you to run out of arrows, may refill using the spare arrows in your Quiver (Medium) once before needing to restock. Only one Quiver can be worn at a time.

Price: 1200 [[Rupees]]
[[Affiliations|Affiliation]]: None
