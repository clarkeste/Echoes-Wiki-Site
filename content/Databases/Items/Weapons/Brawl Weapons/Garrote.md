A short and thin wire, often from a piano, used for the nefarious art of assassination. It can be used to strangle an unsuspecting victim, and incapacitate or kill them.

[[Ensnare (Active) (Quality)|Ensnare]] 1
[[Stun (Active) (Quality)|Stun]] 4
[[Knockdown (Active) (Quality)|Knockdown]]
Special: If a target of an [[Attack Check]] is aware of the [[Character]] who is attacking them, an Attack Check using the Garrote suffers three [[Setback Dice]]. Additionally, if a Character is unconscious, may spend between four and ten minutes strangling them to deal a +200 [[Critical Injury]]. A Garrote generally requires two hands to wield.
