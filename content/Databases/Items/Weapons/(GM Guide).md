Hmm.

#### Traveler’s Line
These are like, the basics that anybody should buy, even not combat-oriented characters, just for utility.
[[Traveler's Sword]] is like that, particularly allowing for a shield.
[[Traveler's Claymore|Traveler’s Claymore]] is that same idea, but with a slight focus on just damage.
[[Traveler's Spear]] is the cheapest and most replaceable, as that’s what spears are like. Also, they are designed to be thrown, and are the spears best for that.

#### Mercenary/Mid-priced Line
This is the stuff between the Traveler’s Line, and the Soldier’s Line. They’re the better upgrade, but are still not as good.
[[Mercenary's Sword]] is a basic weapon, low crit, decent damage.
[[Mercenary's Claymore]] not sure if this should exist. it feels like the same thing, but just more damage and two-handed. double axe fills a similar price and is more different.
[[Steel-tipped Spear|Steel-tipped Spear]] is the more crit-focused version, with some swift-strike.

#### Utility Line
[[Wooden Club]] is more strain-focused, with an equalish balance
[[Metal Club]] same category, but with more focus on Strain
[[Double Axe]] would be the version for claymores. It should be more focused on effects like Knockdown, Disorient, etc.
[[Heavy Lancet]] is a mid-priced Spear that lets you go to Short Range., focus on damage, a little unwieldy. 

### Soldier’s Line
[[Soldier's Broadsword]] is a jack of all trades sword that you can afford at character creation. Like all the ones of the line, it has Quick Charge.
[[Soldier's Greatsword]] is the same idea, but more focused on damage to justify its two-handed nature.
[[Soldier's Spear]] has the same role, but sacrifices some effectiveness to be user to use, since spears are the main weapons of most armies.

#### Knight’s Line
[[Knight's Broadsword]] is an improved version, a little less user-friendly, and with a slight focus on critical injuries.
[[Knight's Claymore]] is the same idea, but a little bit better, and a good defensive mid-way between having a Shield and having a Claymore.
[[Knight's Halberd]] focus on increased range, and a good defensive mid-way between having a Shield and having a Spear.

#### Royal’s Line
[[Royal Broadsword]] is one of the two best general purpose swords, along with the fighter’s. It’s all around a very good weapon, with less of a focus on accessibility.\
[[Royal Claymore]] is among the best claymores. It is a claymore variation on the Broadsword, and it’s versatile with plenty of qualities.
[[Royal Halberd]] One of the best Spears, but it’s contrasted because it has a strong focus on dealing Damage and Qualities.

#### Fighter’s Line
[[Fighter's Sword]] is one of thee best general purpose swords, it’s focused on significant damage and ease of use. It’s a little easier to use, and less crit focused than the Royal Broadsword.
[[Rock Ruiner]] not actually a fighter’s line weapon, but fills a similar role. It’s very specialized on Damage and Qualities, at the cost of being hard to use and bad crit.
[[Fighter's Halberd]] The spear version, one of the best. It’s focused a little more on crits than the sword and royal ones, and also more focused on defense and those tactics.

#### Additional Line
[[Noble's Rapier]] is a good mid-priced sword for Defense, and strikes a decent balance between crits and damage. also very usable.
[[Giant's Blade]] really solid all-arounder, has some defense but not as much as the knight’s claymore, but hard to use. 
[[Hyrulean Pike]] is an advanced version of the heavy lancet. High piercing and good range, it’s primarily specialized on that.

#### Zora Line
[[Zora Spear]] is a Spear focused on defense + dealing Strain.
[[Silverscale Spear]] better version of the thing before, + some good Pierce.

#### Goron Line
[[Drillshaft]] has an insane amount of Pierce, and that is its main purpose. It is very specialized. You could kill even very armored rivals and minions by using crits.
[[Cobble Crusher]] focus on Damage, at the expense of usability and Crits.
[[Stone Smasher]]  better version of that, with a bunch of good Qualities for that.
[[Rock Ruiner]] the best highest damage claymore in the game. Hard to use and neglects crits, but one of the best in the game.

#### Sheikah Line
[[Sheikah Kodachi]] is one of the few weapons available at character creation with a focus on critical injuries. It’s probably the best sword at char creation, but is hard to wield.
[[Eightfold Blade]] a sword that is extremely specialized on critical injuries. It is the only weapon with a crit rating of 1.
[[Blade of Ferocity]] is one of the best weapons in the game, but is really hard to wield. Not a whole lot of qualities, but really good damage and crit. Best one-handed sword damage, tied with the fighter’s sword.

#### Gerudo Line
[[Gerudo Scimitar]] is like a cheaper version of the Kodachi, with some Swift Strike. It’s a low-tier crit weapon.
[[Moonlight Scimitar]] continues the focus on crits, but is more aggressive than the Knight’s sword and not particularly defensive.
[[Gerudo Spear]] focused on easy to use, hard to master, with there being a high skill ceiling. this is since gerudo have limited weapons to begin with. it is general purpose.
[[Gerudo Glaive]] hard to wield, but one of the best spears in the entire game.

#### Dagger Line
[[Dagger]] very situational weapon, good for hiding, good for swift strike, is best for critical injuries and not much else.
[[Parrying Dagger|Parrying Dagger]] the Parrying Dagger is an alternative to most shields, which is less defensive, but has one swift strike and lets you be a little more offensive than most shields.

#### Utility 2 Line
[[Ball And Chain]] extremely hard to use, but very strong. It’s similar to the Goron Rock Ruiner, but it’s also good for crowd control and is a little harder to use.
[[Boomerang]] is unique in that it’s very much both a Ranged and Melee weapon. It is mostly for harassing, with both Disorient and Concussive. It’s also for utility/puzzle solving, like in the games.
[[Dull Rod]] honestly should be changed to ‘wooden rod’ and be in the gear section. It has no business being in the weapon category.
[[Hammer]] is kind of a low-tier version of those very stun-heavy, bad crit weapons like the rock ruiner. it’s unique in that way.
[[Massive Hammer]] is like a better version. But I will have to check how it compares to stone smasher and rock ruiner
[[Whip]] is a whip, like Indiana Jones. You can lasso people up with it, but it also just works as a decent weapon, and tool for utility, like the boomerang. Best most effective way to get Ensnare without magic.

Alright. I think things are fairly balanced now. That said, there are two things that require attention.

1: Healing
The fact that PCs will have to buy a bottle and a red potion to get any healing items is kind of prohibitive to entry. Either I will have to make sure they each get a bottle for free early on in the adventure, or I will have to introduce easier healing, or make the first bottle cheaper, or *something*.

2: Encumbrance
Everything is a little bit cheaper in Echoes to encourage PCs to branch out and have plenty of different equipment. However, since the Encumbrance Threshold is unchanged, this can easily lead to pretty big problems. Ideally, Players should have a decent amount of Encumbrance free.

Either I could decrease the Encumbrance of most things (so that swords are Enc 1, for instance and daggers are Enc 0) or I could increase Base Encumbrance. I don’t really want to do the latter, though, because it would damage parity with base Genesys.
