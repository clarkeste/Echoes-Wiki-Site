Made for a prince of the Royal Family, the Magic Armor drains Rupees to power and provide its extremely impressive defense.

[[Soak|Soak]]: 20 
[[Defense|Defense]]: 2 
[[Encumbrance|Encumbrance]]: 2

Special: Every Round or minute that a [[Character]] is wearing Magic Armor, deduct 20 [[Rupees]] to represent the cost of powering the armor. The Rupees become dun. When hit, remove Rupees equal to ten times the amount of [[Damage]] the attack deals. When out of Rupees, the Magic Armor loses all benefits, and increases its [[Encumbrance|Encumbrance]] amount to 10, until either it is recharged with Rupees or removed from the Character.

Price: 242K [[Rupees]] 
[[Affiliations|Affiliation]]: None, [[Should my character believe in the Royalists, or the Followers?|Royalist]]
