A wingsuit made by the [[Echoes of the Departed/Hidden Peoples/Rito|Rito]] people of Northern [[Holodrum]]. It is useful in skydiving, and has most often found its home among non-Rito aviation enthusiasts.
![]()
[[Soak|Soak]]: 0
[[Defense|Defense]]: 1
[[Encumbrance|Encumbrance]]: 1

Special: Add three [[Boost Dice|Boost Dice]] to any [[Athletics|Athletics]] or [[Coordination|Coordination]] [[Skill Checks|Check]] related to jumping or [[Fall Damage|falling]]. Suffer -8 [[Damage]] when falling. Additionally, horizontal maneuverability in the air is greatly increased; for every two vertical [[Range Bands|Range Bands]] moved, may move one horizontal Range Band.

Price: 3999 [[Rupees]]
[[Affiliations|Affiliation]]: Neutral, [[Echoes of the Departed/Hidden Peoples/Rito|Rito]]
