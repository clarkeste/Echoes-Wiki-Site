Armor designed for stealth. Its defining feature is its soft, tight fabric suppresses sound coming from the wearer. The most famous example of Stealth Guard is the [[Sheikah|Sheikah]] Guard.



[[Soak|Soak]]: 1
[[Defense|Defense]]: 0
[[Encumbrance|Encumbrance]]: 2

Special: Add three [[Boost Dice]] to any [[Stealth]] [[Item Creation|Check]], and to all [[Attack Check|Attack Checks]] that are done while hidden. The [[Damage]] of [[Attack Check|Attack Checks]] while hidden is doubled.
