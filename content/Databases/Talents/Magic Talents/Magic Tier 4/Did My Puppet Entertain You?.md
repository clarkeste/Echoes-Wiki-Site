**Tier:** 4
**Activation:** Passive
**Ranked:** No

When using [[Invoke|Invoke]], may use the “Did My Puppet Entertain You?” [[Modifiers|Modifier]] (11MP cost) to make the Invoked [[Items|Item]] impervious to [[Damage]] for an hour.

Additionally, pick one Invoke [[Spell Creation|Spell]] on your [[Character Sheet]]. May now ignore Long-Lasting Spell costs associated with Invoke, and treat the [[Range]] of the Spell as unlimited.
