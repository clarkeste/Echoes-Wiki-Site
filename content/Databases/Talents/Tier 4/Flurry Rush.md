**Tier:** 4
**Activation:** Passive
**Ranked:** No

Your [[Character]] must have purchased the [[Parry (Improved)]] [[Talents|Talent]] to benefit from this. When using Parry (Improved), may add extra [[Damage]] to the attack equal to the amount of ranks in [[Parry]], and [[Failure|Failures]] on the [[Adversary|enemy]]’s [[Attack Check]].
