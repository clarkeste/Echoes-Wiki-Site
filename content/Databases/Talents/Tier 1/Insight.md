**Tier:** 1
**Activation:** Active (Action)
**Ranked:** No

In or out-of-[[Combat|combat]], spend an [[Actions|Action]] and succeed in an [[Difficulty|Average]] (2p) [[Knowledge Skills|Knowledge]] [[Medicine|Check]] or [[Vigilance]] Check to gain full access to the stat-block of one [[Adversary|enemy]] you are currently within [[Medium Range]] of. If the enemy is uncommon or unknown to even educated individuals, the [[The GM|GM]] may increase the [[Difficulty|Difficulty]] up to a max of [[Difficulty|Daunting]] (4p). Which Knowledge Skill, or if Vigilance must be used, depends on the type of enemy.

For each Range Band above [[Engaged Range|Engaged]] that the active [[Character|character]] is from the target, add 1 [[Setback Dice|Setback Die]] to the check.
