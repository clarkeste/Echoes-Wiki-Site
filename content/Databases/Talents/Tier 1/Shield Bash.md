**Tier:** 1
**Activation:** Active (Incidental)
**Ranked:** No

When your [[Character]] uses a [[Weapons#Shields|Shield]] to attack a [[Adversary#Minions|minion]] or [[Adversary#Rivals|rival]], you may spend four [[Advantage]] or a [[Triumph]] to either [[Staggered|stagger]] the target until the end of the target's next turn, or remove their [[Defense]] for the rest of the Round.
