**Tier:** 1
**Activation:** Active (Action)
**Ranked:** No

As an [[Actions|Action]], suffer 2 [[Strain]] and take the Hey! Listen! Action. Roll an [[Difficulty|Average]] (2p) [[Knowledge Skills|Knowledge]] [[Skill Checks|Check]] (which [[Skills|Skill]] is determined by the GM) in relation to a certain task, enemy, or situation that a [[Character]] is facing in the moment. On a [[Success]], automatically add a [[Triumph]] to the next [[Skill Checks|Skill Check]] you choose before the end of the next Round that relates to the chosen task/topic. If the Knowledge Check rolls a Triumph, treat the use of “Hey! Listen!” as a [[Maneuvers|Maneuver]].
