**Tier:** 1
**Activation:** Active (Incidental)
**Ranked:** No

At the beginning of a [[Social Encounters|Social Encounter]] or at the first [[Social Checks|Social Check]] in an [[Encounter]] that may have more, may use Smile Like The Sun. All [[Charm]], [[Negotiation]], and [[Leadership]] [[Skill Checks|Checks]] gain a [[Boost Dice|Boost Die]]. However, if your [[Character]] lies or bends the truth in a deceptive manner, they instantly suffer 4 [[Strain]] and lose the benefit for the rest of the Encounter.
