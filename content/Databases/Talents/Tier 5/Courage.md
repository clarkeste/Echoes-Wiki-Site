**Tier:** 5 
**Activation:** Active (Incidental) 
**Ranked:** No 

Once per [[Adventure]], may choose one [[Character]] in the [[Encounter]] and one [[Talents|Talent]] or ability (within reason) the target Character possesses. For the remainder of the Encounter, your Character counts as having that Talent or ability. Additionally, this Character is immune to fear effects, and abilities such as [[Do Not Look Away]] cannot be used on them.

Purchasing this [[Talents|Talent]] permanently increases your [[Virtues|Points in Courage]] by 1.
