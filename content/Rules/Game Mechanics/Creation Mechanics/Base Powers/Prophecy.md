Allows its user to see brief glimpses into the future, or past. 

When attempting to cast a Prophecy Spell, a [[Spell Creation|Spellcaster]] must first think about what they would like to see or learn of. Then, an Hour of time, or [[Downtime]], must be spent to activate a Prophecy [[Spell Creation|Spell]]. This usually involves the [[Spell Creation|Spellcaster]] meditating, sleeping outside of necessary hours, or otherwise preparing their mind or drifting off into the land of dreams and the subconscious.

After this time is complete, a [[Force]] [[Skill Checks|Check]] is done to complete the Prophecy Spell. The [[Difficulty]] is determined by the subject that the [[Spell Creation|Spellcaster]] picked, with more dangerous, distant, obscured topics gaining a higher [[Difficulty]].

The Spellcaster then sees a Vision related to what they were intending, such as of a particular [[Character]], location, or history, or potential future event. Usually this Vision is not entirely clear, may depict past, present, and future, and may be mired in symbolism or red herrings, especially if the topic is particularly obscured or hidden, even on a successful [[Skill Checks|Check]]. Generally, the fewer people who know or knew about the topic—and the more those who do know it want it hidden—the harder the [[Skill Checks|Check]] will be, and the less decisive a Vision will be.

[[Advantage]] can be spent to glean more details or interact in a sort of dream-like way with the contents of the Vision, while [[Threat]] can redact information or present dangers. In serious cases, such as with [[Despair]], the Spellcaster may be psychologically attacked. A [[Triumph]], on the other hand, may allow them to traverse this dreamlike world, give it some degree of order, and retain their awareness as a lucid dreamer might.

Other [[Skill Checks]] may occur in this realm of dreams and prophecy.

Default Range is [[Engaged Range|Engaged]].
