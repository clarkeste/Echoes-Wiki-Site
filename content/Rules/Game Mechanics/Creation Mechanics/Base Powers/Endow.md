This power allows the caster to give various buffs or benefits to targets of their choice, including themself or locations. 

Likewise, it can be used to curse individuals or locations, but the means of how this is done is not publicized. 

By default, uncanceled [[Success|Successes]] on an Endow [[Skill Checks|Check]] add an equal amount to the target’s [[Soak]]. Its default [[Range Bands|Range]] is [[Engaged Range|Engaged]], but most Spellcasters are quickly taught to increase it to [[Short Range|Short]] if their target is another being or object. 

Casting Endow on anybody removes any [[Soak]] benefit gained from the target’s [[Armor]], if the Endow Spell adds Soak at all.

The [[Difficulty]] of Endow, if the recipient is willing, is **Average** (2p) in most circumstances, although [[The GM|the GM]] may rule it to be higher depending on the circumstance. If the target is not willing, it is an [[Opposed Check]] against their [[Discipline]]. Casting an Endow Spell on yourself is **Hard** (3p) Difficulty, similar to how [[Medicine|Medicine Checks]] operate.

An Endow Spell lasts so long as the original Spellcaster is conscious and within the Range required to have initially cast the Spell, as per the [[Spell Creation#Long-Lasting Powers, Curses and Blessings|Long-Lasting Spell]] rules. Additionally, since Endow is a [[Spell Creation#Long-Lasting Powers, Curses and Blessings|Long-Lasting Spell]], at the beginning of each turn belonging to the Caster who cast the Endow Spell, that original Spellcaster must suffer 1 [[Strain]]. It can be Dismissed at any time with a [[Maneuvers|Maneuver]], which ends this cost.

Like almost any other Spell, an Endow Spell can be countered by [[Dispel]].

Endow is often used on crops to protect them from disease, which has done much to increase food security throughout Hyrule and the world.
