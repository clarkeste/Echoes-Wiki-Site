This power allows the caster to give various buffs or benefits to targets of their choice, including themself or locations. 

Likewise, it can be used to curse individuals or locations, but the means of how this is done is not publicized. 

By default, uncanceled [[Success|Successes]] on an Endow [[Skill Checks|Check]] add an equal amount to the target’s [[Soak]]. Its default [[Range Bands|Range]] is [[Engaged Range|Engaged]], but most Spellcasters are quickly taught to increase it to [[Short Range|Short]] if their target is another being or object.

The [[Difficulty]] of Endow, if the recipient is willing, is determined by Range as per Attack rules; if the target is not willing, it is an [[Opposed Check]] against their [[Discipline]].

An Endow Spell lasts so long as the original Spellcaster is conscious and within the Range required to have initially cast the Spell.

Additionally, an Endow Spell can be countered by [[Dispel]].

Endow is often used on crops to protect them from disease, which has done much to increase food security throughout Hyrule and the world.
