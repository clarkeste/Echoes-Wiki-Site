Manipulate allows you to pick up and move objects/people with [[Silhouette]] limitations. 

Default [[Range Bands|Range]] is [[Short Range|Short]], and targets can only pulled as high as Short Range off the ground. [[Modifiers]] that increase the Range will also increase how high they can be moved, to a maximum of [[Long Range|Long]]. 

The Range of the Spell also determines how far away from the Spellcaster an object or person can be moved using Manipulate.

- Silhouette 0: [[Difficulty|1p]]
- Silhouette 1: [[Difficulty|2p]]
- Silhouette 2: [[Difficulty|3p]]
- Silhouette 3: [[Difficulty|4p]]
- Silhouette 4: [[Difficulty|5p]]

Anything bigger is often impossible. 

If the target of Manipulate is a minor NPC or an object, [[Difficulty]] is determined by Silhouette using the Difficulties listed above.

If grabbing an important [[Adversary|Rival]] or [[Adversary|Nemesis]], as determined by the [[The GM|GM]], [[Difficulty]] is determined by the target’s [[Discipline]] or [[Resilience]], whichever is higher, in an [[Opposed Check]]. If this would result in the Manipulate check being easier, then the default Silhouette Difficulty is used instead.

If a Spellcaster manages to successfully complete a Manipulate Check on a target, that target is now considered Gripped. 

### Gripping
A Gripped target is a [[Character]] or object that is under the effect of a Manipulate Spell. Gripped targets cannot use the Move [[Maneuvers|Maneuver]]. They can instead be moved by the Spellcaster. 

By using a Maneuver, a Spellcaster can move a target in any direction, as if that target had spent a Move Maneuver. This is done with the added benefit that, unless the Spellcaster chooses otherwise, the target is mechanically speaking floating. This means that they are not subject to ground-based [[Hazards|Hazardous Terrain]] unless the Spellcaster states that their target is being dragged along the floor.

The Spellcaster must spend a [[Maneuvers|Maneuver]] every turn to keep the target in their Grip. This Maneuver can move the target, or simply keep it in place. When a Character is under the effect of a Manipulate Spell, the target can still use [[Actions]] and Maneuvers, but cannot use the Move Maneuver, thereby making them stuck in place.

A Character may attempt to escape being Gripped if they are a [[Nemesis]] or are a [[PC|PC]]. This is done by using an Action to make an opposed [[Discipline]] check against the Spellcaster’s [[Force]] Skill. If the Spellcaster is a Rival or Minion, it may instead be a flat [[Difficulty]]. [[Advantage]] and [[Threat]] in certain situations can also be used to free a Gripped Spellcaster target.

### Hurling
A target that is Gripped can be Hurled by the Spellcaster using an Action. Hurling is throwing the target harshly at something or someone, thereby dealing [[Damage]] to the Gripped object or person, and the destination of the Hurl.

Hurling can only be done on a target that is already Gripped. The Difficulty of Hurling a Gripped object or person is determined in the same way [[Attack Check|Attack Checks]] are determined, with the ‘target’ being the destination that the Gripped object or person is Hurled at. It is like the Hurled object or person is Bow and uses the same rules. If the Gripped object or person is thrown against an inanimate object, such as the wall, it is treated the same way, except that the inanimate object does not suffer Damage or breaks per the [[Sunder (Active) (Quality)|Sunder]] rules.

Gripped [[Items]] or [[Character|Characters]] suffer damage if Hurled or if they have something else Hurled at them. The [[Damage]] is equal to their silhouette x5. Silhouette 0 deals 3 Damage. When attacking with a Hurled item, it functionally has a [[Critical Rating]] of 4, but if the object is sharper, [[The GM]] may rule otherwise. Thrown Melee [[Weapons]] have their [[Damage|Base Damage]] added to the Hurl Damage, as well, and their [[Critical Rating]] determines the Hurl’s Critical Rating. Some Hurled objects may have qualities, such as a particularly thick rock having Concussive.

This means that a [[Silhouette]] 1 Object deals 5 Damage + [[Success|Successes]] on the check, a Silhouette 2 deals 10 Damage + Successes, Silhouette 3 deals 15 + Successes, etc.

[[Improvised Weapons]] or [[Weapons]] that are thrown additionally add their [[Damage|Base Damage]] to the Damage done.

When an object or person is Hurled, it ceases being Gripped.

For the rules regarding Hurling an enemy off an edge, please refer to the article: [[Characters Thrown off Edges]]

### Advantage Shortcuts
If, during the initial check to Grip a target using Manipulate, the Spellcaster gained 2 [[Advantage]] or a [[Triumph]], they may immediately Hurl the target as if that Check was an Attack Check. This can only be done if the Check to initially Manipulate the target was as hard, or harder, than the Check to Hurl would have been. If the Spellcaster wishes to bank on getting that 2 Advantage to instantly Hurl, they may accept the harder Hurling Difficulty so that instantly Hurling will be possible.

Additionally, when a target is Hurled, 2 Advantages or a Triumph may be spent to keep the Spellcaster’s Grip on the Hurled object or person, as opposed to losing the Grip after the Attack Check.

To do both these shortcuts in a single Check would require 4 Advantages or a Triumph.
