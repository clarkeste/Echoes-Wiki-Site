### [[Item Creation#Creating Armor|Armor Creation]]: Special - Scorch Protection (12)
Rarity: Rare

Active: No
Stacks: No
Limit: 1

An improved version of [[Special - Heat Protection (Modifier)|Heat Protection]], Scorch Protection gives extreme defense against warm climates, allowing individuals to stand near volcanic climates and remain well enough to function.

Provides Medium heat Resistance as described in [[Heated Temperatures]] If combined with [[Special - Heat Protection (Modifier)|Special - Heat Protection]], provides Major heat Resistance.
