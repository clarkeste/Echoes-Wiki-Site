Empowered Flanking is a [[Modifiers|Modifier]] used in [[Item Creation]]. It is based off the [[Empowered Flanking (Passive) (Quality)|Empowered Flanking Quality]].

### Empowered Flanking (5)
Active: No
Stacks: No
Limit: 1
When [[Attack Check|attacking]] an enemy that is unaware of their attacker’s presence or when [[Flanking]] an enemy, double [[Damage|Base Damage]] when attacking with a [[Weapons|Weapon]] using Empowered Flanking. Additionally, when [[Flanking]] a [[Character]], may flip a [[Sacred Points|Sacred Point]] to attack using the [[Stealth]] or [[Skulduggery]] [[Skills|Skill]] instead.

If a Weapon has no Base Damage, instead 2 [[Boost Dice]] to the [[Dice Pool]].
