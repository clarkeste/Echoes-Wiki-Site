### [[Item Creation#Creating Armor|Armor Creation]]: Special - Freeze Protection (8)
Rarity: Rare

Active: No
Stacks: No
Limit: 1

An improved version of [[Special - Cold Protection (Modifier)|Cold Protection]], [[Special - Freeze Protection (Modifier)|Freeze Protection]] gives extreme defenses against freezing effects. While an [[Armor|Armor]] with this quality is worn, the [[Character|Character]] with the armor cannot be frozen.

Provides Medium cold Resistance as described in [[Cold Temperatures]]. If combined with Special [[Special - Cold Protection (Modifier)|Cold Protection]], provides Major cold Resistance.
