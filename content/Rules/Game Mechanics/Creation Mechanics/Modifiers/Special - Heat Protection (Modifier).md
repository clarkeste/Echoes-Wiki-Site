### [[Item Creation#Creating Armor|Armor Creation]]: Special - Heat Protection (5)
Active: No
Stacks: No
Limit: 1
Rarity: Uncommon

Protects against heat, such as the heat in the Gerudo Desert or other notoriously warm areas.

Provides Minor heat Resistance as described in [[Heated Temperatures]] If combined with [[Special - Scorch Protection]], provides Major heat Resistance.
