#### [[Item Creation#Creating Armor|Armor Creation]]: Special - Cold Protection (4)
Rarity: Common
Active: No
Stacks: No
Limit: 1

Represents an overcoat or other warming clothing that protects against the cold, such as the frost found on Mount Lanayru or in the Hebra region.

Provides Minor cold Resistance as described in [[Cold Temperatures]]. If combined with [[Special - Freeze Protection (Modifier)|Special - Freeze Protection]], provides Major cold Resistance.
