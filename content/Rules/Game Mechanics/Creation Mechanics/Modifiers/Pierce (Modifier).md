Pierce is a [[Modifiers|Modifier]] used in [[Item Creation]]. It is based on the [[Pierce (Passive) (Quality)|Pierce Quality]].

#### [[Item Creation]]: Pierce (3)
Active: No
Stacks: Yes
Limit: 9
An [[Attack Check]] made with a [[Weapons|weapon]] that possesses the Pierce quality ignores one point of [[Soak]] for each rank of Pierce. 

The cost of increasing a Weapon’s [[Damage]] grows as it is increased. The first four ranks of [[Pierce (Modifier)|Pierce]] count as increasing Damage by 1 for this purpose. This means that Pierce can cost 3 Crafting Points, or 5 Crafting Points, depending on how much Damage the Weapon does when Pierce is added.

Check out [[Increase Damage (Modifier)|Increase Damage]] for the exact numbers.

After Pierce 4, each additional point of Pierce purchased costs only 1 [[Item Creation|Crafting Point]] as opposed to 3. After Pierce 4, it does not count as Damage, and you can ignore the aforementioned increased cost rules.
