Increase Damage is a [[Modifiers|Modifier]] used in Item Creation. It is similar to the [[Increase Power (Modifier)|Increase Power Modifier]].

#### [[Item Creation|Item Creation]]: Increase Damage (3)
Active: No
Stacks: Yes
Limit: None
Increases the [[Damage|Damage]] of the [[Weapons|Weapon]] by one.

The cost of Increase Damage grows as it is used more on a single Weapon. The first four ranks of [[Pierce (Modifier)|Pierce]] count as increasing Damage by 1 for this purpose.

Increasing Damage from +1 to +5 cost 3 Crafting Points. From +6 onwards costs 5 Crafting Points.

If the Damage value does not have a + in front of it (such as in the case of [[Weapon Template - Ranged Weapon]]), Increase Damage from 1-8 costs 3 Crafting Points; from 9 onwards, it costs 5 Crafting Points.
