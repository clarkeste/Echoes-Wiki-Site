**Materials** or **Ingredients** of varying rarities are required to make [[Potions]] and [[Items]] in the [[Potion Creation|Potion]] and [[Item Creation]] systems. Specifically, they are required to add certain [[Modifiers]]. How rare one of these Materials or Ingredients is determines how easy it is to find, how cheap it is to buy, and therefore how available it is to one who is trying to make a certain Potion or Item that requires them. 

While Materials and Ingredients are often abstracted to a certain extent, and while proper workspaces often have the basics required, this is not always the case—especially for Modifiers that have a Rarity of Rare or higher.

Different [[Modifiers]] require different Materials. For example, let us take the Modifier of [[Burn (Modifier)|Burn]]. It is listed to have a Rarity of “Rare”. This means that the raw Materials needed to construct it are rare to find, and difficult to get. It is not so simple acquire a Ruby gemstone, or a kilo of Firerock, or enough prized Flame Fruit to use to make a Burn Item or Potion.

Materials and Ingredients can be acquired in a number ways, depending on their Rarity and the particular quirks of the Material or Ingredient in questioned. They can often be bought, found, stolen, etc.

Sometimes, a Material used for an Item can also be used as an Ingredient in a Potion, but often these are separate things that must be acquired separately. The [[Shock (Modifier)|Shock]] Ingredient used in Potion Creation is not necessarily the same as the Shock Material used in Item Creation, for instance; one might be a fruit, whereas another may be a piece of technology. Likewise, different Materials and Ingredients, even ones that are for the same Modifier, can have varying [[Encumbrance]] ratings, or different effects when used as Items separately from any Creation system, if they have any effects to begin with. They may have their own monetary values, methods to get, and in some rare cases, additional effects on the Creation system they are being used for.

Different Materials or Ingredients might be good for only a certain rating of their [[Modifiers|Modifier]], requiring more of that Material to go above a certain rating.

### Rarities
Generally-speaking, there are four rarities.
##### Common
The Materials or Ingredients needed for this are common and easy to acquire. Most proper workstations will probably even come with this. For example, in Item Creation, this would be things like steel or charcoal; in Potion Creation, it may be things like simple foods or extracts.
##### Uncommon
These Materials or Ingredients are often not found lying around, or at least not in large quantities, and take some effort or initiative to find. Still, they are usually not hard to find if one is shopping, or knows where to look when foraging.
##### Rare
Rare Materials or Ingredients tend to be harder to find, sought after, or otherwise unknown enough that there is not a high supply of them. They are harder to find, and unless you know where to look or are willing to pay high prices, you might not be able to acquire them.
##### Obscure
Materials or Ingredients which are Obscure are either so rare as to not be widely-known about, or which are borderline legendary and which few have access to and many seek, but few have expectations of being able to access and find. The finest metal or the most elusive plants, which give effects that are legendary in their status—or which would be legendary, if the general public knew of them.
