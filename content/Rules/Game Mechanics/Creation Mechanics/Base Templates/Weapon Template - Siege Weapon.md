Special: Siege weapons are not carried and are mostly stationary, due to their larger size. During crafting, applying [[Increase Damage (Modifier)|Increased Damage]] to a Siege Weapon increases the [[Damage]] by 5 instead of 1.

**Crafter’s Note:** May [[Increase Damage (Modifier)|Increase Damage]] for a reduced cost of 1 Crafting Point, and reduce Damage to gain 1 Crafting Point. May spend 4 Crafting Points to remove the [[Artillery (Passive) (Quality)|Artillery]] [[Qualities|Quality]], but only if the Damage is below 15.

Siege weapons tend to take longer to make.
