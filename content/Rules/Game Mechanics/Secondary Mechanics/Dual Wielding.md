In [[Echoes of the Departed]], whenever a [[Character]] is wielding two one-handed [[Weapons]], one in each hand, they are technically Dual-wielding. However, there is a way to attack with both Weapons in a single [[Attack Check]]. This can be with two Swords, a Sword and a Shield, or any other combination.

First, as per usual, a [[Player]] selects a target and does the usual building of a [[Dice Pool]], as is outlined in [[Attack Check|Attack Checks]]. They must pick which Weapon of the two they are holding they want to be the ‘primary one’. Then, an additional [[Difficulty Dice|Difficulty Die]] is added to the final [[Difficulty]] to represent the challenge of using two Weapons at once. If the Weapons use two different [[Skills|Skills]], then the one the Character has the worst Dice Pool in is used.

If the Check is successful, then the ‘Primary’ Weapon does [[Damage]], as per usual. However, the attacker may also use two [[Advantage]] to attack with the second Weapon. The second Weapon deals Damage equal to its [[Damage#Base Damage|Base Damage]], plus any [[Success|Successes]] rolled on the Attack, as per usual.
