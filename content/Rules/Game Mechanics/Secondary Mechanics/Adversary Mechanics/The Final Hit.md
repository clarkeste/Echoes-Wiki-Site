Some [[Adversary|Adversaries]], particularly [[Nemesis]] Adversaries, must be finished off with a **Final Hit.**

This applies to particularly important enemies who are unlikely to give up the fight until they kill or are killed, or monstrous beings who, for one reason or another, are less prone to being knocked out after physical trauma.

While there are some ways to incapacitate non-lethally an enemy with **The Final Hit**, it is uncommon. Enemies who need a Final Hit to be defeated will likely need to be killed for the [[Encounter]] to end with them having lost.

When an [[Adversary]] who requires Final Hit is within 5% of their [[Wound Threshold]], or 3 points away from hitting it (whichever is higher, and subject to change depending on the enemy) then they become a target for a Final Hit. An Adversary who requires a Final Hit to be defeated cannot pass their [[Wound Threshold]], until they are the victim of a successful Final Hit, and therefore die in the process.

To achieve a Final Hit, target a [[Character]] who is vulnerable to it (due to the circumstances lifted above + having an Ability that explicitly mentions it). Then, the Attacker must score a [[Critical Hit]] on their [[Attack Check]]. If this is done, Final Hit is triggered, and the Adversary dies.
