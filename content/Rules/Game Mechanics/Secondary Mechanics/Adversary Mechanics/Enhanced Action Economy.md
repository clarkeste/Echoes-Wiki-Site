**Enhanced Action Economy** is an Ability that various [[Nemesis]]-level enemies will have throughout [[Echoes of the Departed]], but is also one that every [[PC]] has by default.

This ability functionally serves to level the playing-field when a single [[Character]] is fighting against several others.

A Character with Enhanced Action Economy may add extra allied [[Initiative]] slots up to 50% of the number of enemy initiative slots (rounding down). This can only be done until the Character’s side has at least 50% of the total Initiative Slots of the PC side. For instance, if allied Characters fulfill the difference, this Ability does not activate.
