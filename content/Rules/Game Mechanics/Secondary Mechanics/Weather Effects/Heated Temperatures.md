Various parts of [[Hyrule]] and the world have an ambient heat that is very uncomfortable or outright deadly to many of the living beings of the world; such as, for example, the [[Desert of Mystery, Gerudo Desert|Gerudo Desert]] or [[Death Mountain (Historia)|Death Mountain]]. These temperatures and areas have to be handled with care, or individuals not adapted to the environments might suffer from heat exhaustion or dangerously elevated body temperatures.

There are six severities of Heated Temperatures.

# Severities
## Severity 1 - Humid Heat
Warmth mostly due to humidity in the air as opposed to anything else can still be uncomfortable, distracting, and tiring. It is not life-threatening, but many tasks can become exhausting or more difficult in these conditions. Some coastal, tropical, or rainforest areas have this heat; so do many areas of [[Death Mountain (Historia)|Death Mountain]] when the Volcano is inactive, such as [[The Gorons|Goron City]].

Add a [[Setback Dice|Setback Die]] to all [[Skill Checks|Checks]] that use [[Agility]] or [[Brawn]] as their base [[Characteristics|Characteristic]]. Every time an unprotected [[Character]] suffers 2 [[Strain]] or more, they suffer +1 additional Strain.

## Severity 2 - Desert Heat
This drier heat is typical of the [[Desert of Mystery, Gerudo Desert|Gerudo Desert]], officially known as the [[Desert of Mystery, Gerudo Desert|Desert of Mystery]], especially those places in the desert closest to Hyrule proper such as [[Calditin City (Historia)|Calditin City]]. It is dangerous and it can kill the unprepared, especially if they wander the desert without a guide or direction.

Add a [[Setback Dice|Setback Die]] to all [[Skill Checks|Checks]]. Every time a [[Skill Checks|Check]] of any type is rolled, or a [[Character]] moves a significant distance (about 2km) suffer 1 [[Strain]].

## Severity 3 - Arid Heat
A much more punishing heat, found [[Desert of Doubt (Historia)|deeper into the Desert]], away from the fair winds of [[Hyrule]]. This will exhaust and collapse an unprepared individual much faster than the regular desert heat.

Add three [[Setback Dice]] to all [[Skill Checks|Checks]]. Every time a [[Skill Checks|Check]] of any type is rolled, or a [[Character]] moves a significant distance (about 2km) suffer 2 [[Strain]].

## Severity 4 - Searing Heat
On the worst of days within the desert of the world, or when approaching an area of magma within a Volcano, a burning wind punishes those who come near, searing them. This is an extremely dangerous heat to stay in for too long, and can cause burning.

Add an automatic [[Failure]] to all [[Skill Checks|Checks]]. Every 5 minutes or every Round, suffer 4 [[Strain]]. Each [[Skill Checks|Check]] rolled causes the [[Character]] who rolled it to suffer 2 [[Strain]]. If [[Incapacitated]] from [[Strain]], begin suffering [[Critical Injury|Critical Injuries]] instead.

## Severity 5 - Scorching
Scorching hot temperatures, which burn those who get near; get too close to magma, and one will feel this sensation. Common in many caves at [[Death Mountain (Historia)|Death Mountain]]; rarely felt outside of volcanic activity.

Add an automatic [[Failure]] to all [[Skill Checks|Checks]]. Each [[Skill Checks|Check]] rolled causes the [[Character]] who rolled it to suffer 2 [[Strain]]. Every minute or every Round, suffer 4 [[Strain]] and 4 [[Wounds]]. Objects made of wood, cloth, or which have the [[Flammable (Passive) (Quality)|Flammable]] [[Qualities|Quality]], will catch fire after a few moments.

## Severity 6 - Molten
Only those who get very close to lava experience this. Most who are not [[Gorons]] do not survive to tell the tale.

Add an automatic [[Failure]] to all [[Skill Checks|Checks]]. Every few moments, such as every Turn, suffer 5 [[Strain]] and 5 [[Wounds]]. A human and most [[Flammable (Passive) (Quality)|Flammable]] things they carry will catch fire if unprotected after a few moments.
# Resistances
Resistances from various sources stack, and can protect someone or diminish the effects of dangerous temperatures on them.

Resistances from various sources stack. Two Minor sources of Resistance, such as one from [[Armor]] and one from a [[Character]]’s [[Peoples|People]]-group, will come together to form a Medium Resistance. It scales linearly in this way; one, two, and three.
### Minor
Ignore all effects from Severity 1 and 2 weather. Halve the [[Strain]] effects of Severity 3.

### Medium
Ignore all of the effects of weather from Severity 1 to 4. Halve (rounding down) the [[Strain]] and [[Wounds|Wound]] penalties from Severity 5.

### Major
Ignore all effects of warm weather. In some cases, may have resistance to flames; but lava and magma will still harm a [[Character]].
