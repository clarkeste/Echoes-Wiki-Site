In cases where a storm and its accompanying rain are particularly harsh, Lightning might occur in such a manner that it becomes a threat to the safety of individuals, and the rain or darkness that sometimes pours down can limit visibility or create hazards.

There are six severities of Storms & Lightning.

# Severities
## Severity 1 - Rain
Rain, hardly more than a drizzle, falls upon the Earth—a regular meteorological phenomena. However, even this can provide problems in some situations. Climbing and holding onto things will become more difficult, usually adding a few [[Setback Dice]], due to the wetness and slipperiness. 

[[Ranged]] [[Skill Checks|Checks]] across distances of [[Long Range]] or farther often add at least one [[Setback Dice|Setback]], due to limited visibility.

## Severity 2 - Heavy Rain
This rain is heavier, more consistent, and the storm-clouds greater. Climbing and holding onto things becomes very difficult, although not impossible; the [[Difficulty]] may be increased in many cases, not just [[Setback Dice]] added.

[[Ranged]] [[Skill Checks|Checks]] targeting any target past [[Short Range]] add at least two [[Setback Dice]].

## Severity 3 - Storm
A heavy storm clouds the skies, usually accompanied by a significant amount of rain. Climbing anything too tall becomes impossible without special equipment.

[[Ranged]] [[Skill Checks|Checks]] gain one [[Setback Dice|Setback Die]] per [[Range Bands|Range Band]]. [[Engaged Range]] is 1 [[Setback Dice|Setback]], [[Short Range]] is 2, etc.

## Severity 4 - Lightning Storm
In addition to rain, mist, and cloudy skies, this storm also has Lightning. Sometimes, Lightning Storms occur even without rain; this makes any fires caused by them even more dangerous.

In addition to the drawbacks from Severity 3, when there is a Lightning Storm, there is a for buildings or high-up objects—or people—to be struck by Lightning. This is very rare, and usually a sparking on metal objects or the feeling of one’s hair standing up will alert them to the danger.

Being struck by Lightning immediately deals 20 [[Wounds]] and 20 [[Strain]] which bypass [[Soak]]. In addition to a [[Critical Injury]] caused by exceeding one’s [[Wound Threshold]], also roll a +60 [[Critical Injury]].

## Severity 5 - Intense Downpour
Intense rain and clouds, flashes of lightning accompanied by crashes of deafening thunder seconds later. Anyone standing in a downpour like this would be drenched almost immediately.

In addition to the drawbacks from Severity 4 and 3, add an additional [[Difficulty Dice|Difficulty Die]] to all [[Attack Check|Attack Checks]] done at Range. Additionally, for every Round or every 5 minutes spent in the rain, suffer 1 [[Strain]]—although this may be lessened with the proper clothing.

## Severity 6 - Supernatural Lightning
A storm that casts out a lightning bolt every few seconds—at a much higher rate than a usual downpour. While some geographical phenomena can cause this, it can also be a sign of dark things.

In addition to the drawbacks from the previous Severity, being struck by Lightning has a much higher chance, as it seems much more drawn to metal than previously. If a [[Character]] is doing something that might attract a lightning strike—such as holding exposed metal, or standing near a tall tree, or being the tallest object in an area—[[The GM]] may roll a d4 every Round, or every minute. If it rolls a 1 twice in a row, the [[Character]] is struck by Lightning.
# Resistances

Since the effect of Storms & Lightning are broader, there is no particular resistance to the weather conditions as a whole. However, certain gear might help with certain issues, such as climbing gear helping with the wetness of surfaces caused by the rain.
