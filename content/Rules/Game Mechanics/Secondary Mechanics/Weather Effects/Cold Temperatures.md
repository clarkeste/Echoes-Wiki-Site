Many parts of [[Hyrule]] and the world are chilly, cold, or freezing, and can seriously injure or harm individuals without the proper protections against the elements. Winter, too, can make the outside too cold for those without the proper clothing.

These locations, in [[Hyrule]], are most famously Hebra, [[Mount Lanayru (Historia)]], and parts of northern Tabantha. However, the tops of many mountains are cold as well.

# Severities
## Severity 1 - Chilled
A chilly breeze, a cold night; enough cold to make one uncomfortable, but not enough to seriously harm someone. It is experienced often in [[Hyrule]] and in many different regions, pushing people to seek fire, blankets, or the indoors to stay at a comfortable level of warmth.

Suffer 2 [[Strain]] every hour.

## Severity 2 - Cold
A more serious cold, usually in northern or higher altitude areas. Usually, areas with this level of chill are not covered in snow, or if they are, it is a very light amount.

Suffer 3 [[Strain]] every hour, and add a [[Setback Dice|Setback Die]] to all [[Skill Checks|Checks]].

## Severity 3 - Frigid
Snowy, cold areas. Tundras such as when one gets near to the Hebra [[Hyrulean Geography|region]], or at the lower parts of [[Mount Lanayru (Historia)|Mount Lanayru]]. It can be dangerous to be in this place for too long without protective equipment.

Add a [[Setback Dice|Setback Die]] to all [[Skill Checks|Checks]]. Every 5 minutes or every Round, suffer 2 [[Strain]].

## Severity 4 - Glacial
A freezing so off-putting that few live in these places, covering areas such as Hebra or many parts of Mount Lanayru. Frostbite can occur if one is not careful.

Add two [[Setback Dice]] to all [[Skill Checks|Checks]]. Every 5 minutes or every Round, suffer 4 [[Strain]] and 4 [[Wounds]].

## Severity 5 - Freezing
Only the coldest parts of [[Hyrule]], or under particularly bad weather, reach this low of a temperature. It is extremely dangerous and can cause an individual’s body to shut down very quickly. Cold liquids are also in this category, owing to their ability to soak through clothing.

Add an automatic [[Failure]] to all [[Skill Checks|Checks]]. Every few moments, such as every Turn, suffer 3 [[Strain]] and 3 [[Wounds]].

## Severity 6 - Below Freezing
Extremely deadly frost, such as water just short of becoming ice, which can soak through clothing and greatly injure a person, or supernatural cold.

Add an automatic [[Failure]] to all [[Skill Checks|Checks]]. Every few moments, such as every Turn, suffer 5 [[Strain]] and 5 [[Wounds]].

# Resistances
Resistances from various sources stack, and can protect someone or diminish the effects of dangerous temperatures on them.

Resistances from various sources stack. Two Minor sources of Resistance, such as one from [[Armor]] and one from a [[Character]]’s [[Peoples|People]]-group, will come together to form a Medium Resistance. It scales linearly in this way; one, two, and three.
### Minor
Ignore all effects from Severity 1 and 2 weather. Halve the Strain effects of Severity 3.

### Medium
Ignore all of the effects of weather from Severity 1 to 4. Halve (rounding down) the [[Strain]] and [[Wounds|Wound]] penalties from Severity 5.

### Major
Ignore all effects of cold weather.
