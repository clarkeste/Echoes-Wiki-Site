There’s only one thing more valuable than [[Rupees|Rupee]]—time. If that time is spent having fun, accompanying your family, working on yourself, or… well, getting more money, is up to you.

Downtime represents these moments when the focus is away, and when the [[PC|PCs]] are not on adventures, allowing themselves to continue their life—mostly—normally. Just like Rupees, Downtime is something of a currency that can be spent on certain things—by a [[Character|Character]] deciding what to do with their Downtime.

Downtime is broken into Days, Weeks, and Hours. Each Day is 12 Hours, and each Week, as you may imagine, is 7 Days. Each Month is exactly 4 Weeks, as each Month is 28 Days according to the [[Hylian Calendar|Hylian Calendar]]. The year consists of 14 Months, meaning each Year is 392 Days, or 56 Weeks. However, looking at Years and Months is excessive—what is often dealt with is Days and Weeks.

Days in the world are still 24 hours; however, it is assumed that the other 12 will be unproductive or spent resting.

Downtime can be spent on many different things, including hanging threads or thing decided on by the [[PC|PCs]] that seem minor enough to not require active game-time. They can be used to work a job, travel, seek leads, complete minor tasks, heal from injuries, etc.

When the PC says a specific activity they would like to do—such as reading a [[Proficiency Book|book]], going to train, brewing a [[Potion Creation|Potion]], or any other activity—the [[The GM|GM]] tells them how long this would take, usually in Weeks or Days, as well as any additional Rules. Then, as the [[Adventure]] is going on somewhere else—usually with a different character played by the same [[Player|Player]]—the Character experiencing Downtime continues their activity. They go along with whatever they want, along with spending ample leisure time.

Below is a common list of things that can be done with Downtime.

## Spending Downtime
It should be noted that the below is not an exhaustive list. Just like with [[Advantage|Advantage]] or [[Threat]], the idea is that these are simply benchmarks, or common activities you might want to pursue. They provide examples for other things a [[Player]] may want to actively pursue during Downtime.

### Resting
The body needs rests and to heal, and it often does that while you’re sleeping, or at least lazing around.

This is an outcropping of the [[Natural Recovery]] rules.

When **Resting**, do the following:
- Every Hour, heal 1 Strain, even if it is an ‘unproductive hour’. This means you heal 24 [[Strain]] if you spend a whole day Resting. Sometimes, in active time, this is simplified to: If you got a full night of sleep, you heal all [[Strain]]. This is not the case in Downtime, or with large amounts of Strain.
- Every Day, heal 2 Wounds. With proper medical care, this becomes 4 Wounds per day in addition to Items or [[Medicine|Medical]] checks attempted.
- Every Week, roll [[Resilience]] [[Skill Checks|Check]] with [[Difficulty]] equal to a [[Critical Injury]] severity you wish to try and heal.

### Working
As a great religious book once said, he who does not work, neither shall he eat. And as the great opponent of that religious book said… well, he said the same thing.

It is very likely that your [[Character]] may have a more traditional, comparatively boring job, or freelance work, that they take up at various points of the story to earn [[Rupees]] to purchase things they truly care about.

Different Jobs a Character might take on have different qualities and may impart different amounts of money.

When a Character spends a Day looking for a Job, [[The GM]] will roll a table three times to determine the options the Character finds. A Character may spend another Day looking to gain more options. The characteristics of the Jobs are the following and may vary.

- Wage - How much a [[Character]] is paid for each Hour spent.
- Skill - The [[Skills|Skill]] used to excel in the job. 
- Difficulty - The Difficulty of the job, often determining the [[Difficulty]] of [[Skill Checks|Checks]] relating to it.
- Duration - How long the Job will be willing to employ somebody.

Once a Job is selected and agreed upon, the Characters rolls a Skill Check to determine how effectively they do their work. Each [[Success]] increases hourly payment by 1 Rupee; each [[Failure]] reduces it by 1 Rupee. [[Advantage]] or [[Threat]] may be spent on a variety of things, same as [[Triumph]]. A [[Despair]] results in automatic termination.

After a Week of working, the Character must roll again, this time a [[Vigilance]] or [[Discipline]] Check, whichever is worse, which has the same effects. After another week, the Job’s Skill is rolled again. This continues until Downtime is over, the Character quits or takes a break, they are terminated, or the Duration of the Job expires.

### Item or Potion Creation
Various actions in [[Item Creation]] and [[Potion Creation]] take Downtime to enact. This is particularly true when crafting an Item.

### Training
There are various places in [[Hyrule]] and the surrounding Kingdoms where someone who wants to train in particular Skills may be able to find the educational assistance they want.

Mechanically, these Training areas have similar effects in that they give [[Experience Points|Experience Points, or XP]]. However, a Training area could be anything; A dojo, or a soldier’s training center, or a university, or an athletics class.

The difference between Training areas, mechanically, is their Quality. They can either be Decent, Medium, or High. Decent locations give 1 XP per Week of Downtime spent there. Medium give 1 XP per week of Downtime spent there, plus another 2XP at the end of the Month. High give 2XP per Week of Downtime spent there.

The cost is highly dependent on the Quality. Decent tend to be 30-100 per Week; Medium tend to be 200-400 per Week; High tend to be 500-700 per Week.

### Using Items
Some [[Items]], such as [[Proficiency Book|Proficiency Books]] and [[Masterworks Book|Masterworks Books]], take time to use and consume. This time is usually listed on their Item page.

### Traveling
Traveling Hyrule is time well-spent some might say, while others would consider it to be an annoying waste of time traveling to where you really want to be.

While Hyrule has a few train-lines in certain parts of the country, its railways are in their infancy. The most common mode of transportation will be on sea-worthy vessels between ports, or in carriages and on horseback.

### Tasks
‘Tasks’ is a catch-all name for anything a PC might want to do which is not listed here, but should be completed during Downtime. For example, sending a message to a loved one, or pouring through documents to try and find evidence on a criminal gang.

First, the [[Player]] states what they want their [[Character]] to do to the GM, who then decides the following:
- Will it an intrusive task? An intrusive task is something that needs to be tracked. Writing a letter is not an intrusive task and doesn’t need to interact with the Downtime system at all.
- How long will it take? A GM doesn’t necessarily need to tell the Player this. How many documents will they have to pour over to find the information they search? It’s impossible to know. But most often, the GM will state the time required, as the Character would likely have a decent idea.
- Is there a [[Skill Checks|Skill Check]] required? This is only necessary if it isn’t a task a laymen could do, or if there is a decent chance that something could be missed, or the task could be failed. Usually, these are done at the end of a Task. Failure doesn’t necessarily mean the end; perhaps it just means more Downtime must be spent on it.

### Change Spells
In [[Spell Creation]], some Downtime is required to be able to change out Spells. In active game time, this can be an hour; but in Downtime, it is considered to take a full Day. Once this day is passed, however, every Spell can be refreshed, changed, remove, modified, etc.
