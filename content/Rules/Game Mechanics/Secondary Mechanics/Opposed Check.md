An Opposed Check is when two [[Character|Characters]] do a [[Skill Checks|Check]] against each other, [[Skills|Skill]] against Skill. For example, if the two Characters ere trying to push each other off the platform, it would be an Opposed Check, with both characters using [[Athletics|Athletics]].

The opposer’s positive [[Dice Pool|Dice Pool]] is added to the check as negative [[Narrative Dice|Dice]]—[[Proficiency Dice|Proficiency]] becomes [[Challenge Dice|Challenge]], [[Difficulty|Difficulty]] becomes [[Ability Dice|Ability]], and [[Boost Dice|Boost]] becomes [[Setback Dice|Setbacks]]. This means that the difficulty is directly determined by the Skill of the opposer.

After the Dice Pool is constructed in this manner, the Check proceeds regularly.

## Opposed Checks in RPGSessions
Opposed Checks can be done fairly easily in [[RPGSessions]]. While often, especially if [[The GM|the GM]] wants to keep it hidden that a roll is an Opposed Check, the GM will simply tell you the Skill and the [[Difficulty]] to roll, they can also tell you what [[Skills|Skill]] to roll and what enemy Skill it should be opposed by.

For instance, they may tell you “roll a [[Charm]] [[Social Skills|Check]] against the target’s [[Cool]].” In this case, you can easily roll from RPGSessions the following way.

First, navigate to the [[Game Table]]. Then, open your [[Character Sheet]] by clicking on your Character’s icon.

![](https://i.imgur.com/DkYOXUw.png)

Then, click on [[Dice Pool]] icons next to the ‘Charm’ Skill (or next to any other Skill which the GM asked you to roll).

![](https://i.imgur.com/WCPB0XQ.png)

Afterwards, it will open the Dice Panel and the Rolling modal. At the top of this modal, you will see that you can pick a ‘target’ for this Check. From the list, pick the Character who you are targeting with the Opposed Check. If they’re not in the list, then the GM has made a mistake and has not properly added them to the Game Table.

![](https://i.imgur.com/eMYxz1b.png)

After selecting them, it will ask you what [[Skills|Skill]] your Check will be Opposed by. Click on the drop-down menu to open the list, and then select the Skill. In this case, [[Cool]] was selected as the Opposing skill by default, which is what the GM asked us to do.

Once the right opposing Skill has been selected, hit the “Add Dice” button.

This will add the correct dice to the Dice Pool, constructing the Opposed Check. Add any [[Setback Dice|Setback]] or [[Boost Dice]] if the GM stated any were relevant, or any other symbols that are relevant such as those gained from [[Talents|Talents]], and then hit “Roll as (Character).”

![](https://i.imgur.com/3WNeKrS.png)

Congratulations! You have now rolled an Opposed Check in RPGSessions.
