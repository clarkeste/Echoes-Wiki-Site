[[Potions]], regardless of whether they are purchased, found, or created using [[Potion Creation]], will deteriorate if they are not stored within the proper type of glass or another suitably chemically inert substance.

This includes most other containers which often are used for liquids, such as impure glass, wood, metal, ceramic, clay, or other such materials. 

This means that high-quality glass [[Bottle|Bottles]], specially designed to hold Potions, are of high value and highly sought-after for those who want to have and use Potions.

## Degradation
As soon as a Potion is stored in the wrong container, it already loses much of its efficacy. After only 10-20 seconds, it loses *half* of its effectiveness, rounding upwards. For example, a [[Potion - Red Potion|Red Potion]] will only heal 4 [[Wounds]] instead of 7. A [[Potion - Yellow Potion|Yellow Potion]], for instance, will have only last about halfway until the next day.

After 2 hour in an incorrect container, it loses another half. For example, a Red Potion will heal only 2 [[Wounds]] instead of 4. A [[Potion - Yellow Potion|Yellow Potion]] may only last two hours.

After 24 hours in an incorrect container, it will go to the minimum amount. A [[Potion - Red Potion|Red Potion]] will only heal 1 Wound; a [[Potion - Yellow Potion|Yellow Potion]] will last for thirty minutes at most.

Weeks of disuse in a particularly inappropriate container may lead to the Potion losing all its effects and becoming, essentially, colored water.

### Red Potion, Blue Potion, Green Potion
**Immediate:** Reduced to 4 [[Wounds]]/[[Strain]] healed.
**2 Hours:** Reduced to 2 [[Wounds]]/Strain healed.
**24 Hours:** Reduced to 1 [[Wounds]]/[[Strain]] healed.

### Purple Potion
**Immediate:** Lasts only 1 [[Encounter]], or for half a day
**2 Hours:** Lasts for only the next 2 [[Attack Check|Attack Checks]] or next 2 hours
**24 Hours:** Lasts for only the next [[Attack Check]], or next 30 minutes

### Yellow Potion
**Immediate:** Lasts for only half the rest of the day
**2 Hours:** Lasts for only the next 2 hours
**24 Hours:** Lasts for only the next 30 minutes

### Secret Medicine
**Immediate:** Heal only 2 [[Wounds]] immediately. Heal only 4 [[Wounds]] when exceeding [[Wound Threshold]].
**2 Hours:** Heal only 1 [[Wounds|Wound]] immediately. Heal only 2 [[Wounds]] when exceeding [[Wound Threshold]]. Effect only lasts for 2 hours after drinking.
**24 Hours:** Heal no [[Wounds]] immediately. Heal only 1 [[Wound Threshold|Wound]] when exceeding [[Wound Threshold]]. Effect only lasts for 30 minutes after drinking.

### Stun Potion
**Immediate:** Suffer only 4 [[Strain]]
**2 Hours:** Suffer only 2 [[Strain]]
**24 Hours:** Suffer only 1 [[Strain]]

### Poison Potion
**Immediate:** Suffer only 4 Wounds Immediately. Reduce [[Difficulty]] of [[Resilience]] [[Skill Checks|Check]] to [[Difficulty|Average]].
**2 Hours:** Suffer only 2 [[Wounds]] immediately. Reduce [[Difficulty]] of [[Resilience]] [[Skill Checks|Check]] to [[Difficulty|Easy]].
**24 Hours:** Suffer only 1 [[Wounds|Wound]] immediately. Reduce [[Difficulty]] of [[Resilience]] [[Skill Checks|Check]] to none.

### Sleep Potion
**Immediate:** Reduce [[Difficulty]] of [[Resilience]] or [[Discipline]] [[Skill Checks|Check]] to [[Difficulty|Average]]. 
**2 Hours:** May no longer accept the effects and willingly fall asleep. Reduce [[Difficulty]] of [[Resilience]] or [[Discipline]] [[Skill Checks|Check]] to [[Difficulty|Easy]].
**24 Hours:** May no longer accept the effects and willingly fall asleep. Reduce [[Difficulty]] of [[Resilience]] or [[Discipline]] [[Skill Checks|Check]] to none.
