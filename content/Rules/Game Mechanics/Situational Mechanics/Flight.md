Some [[Character|Characters]] in [[Echoes of the Departed]] are gifted with the natural ability to fly and soar through the air; to glide like birds, or flap and hover like bats.

Characters with Flight have a **Speed** rating, and therefore operate similarly to Mounts in [[Horses and Mounts]]. Their Speed Rating is determined by the [[Peoples]] they belong to.

Each turn while in Flight, a Character who is Flying gains free **Flight Maneuvers** equal to their Speed rating.

To begin Flying, a Character must be standing upright (not [[Prone]]) and in an area that has enough vertical space to allow for Flight. Then, they may spend a Maneuver to Fly up into the sky, putting them in [[Short Range|Short]] or [[Engaged Range]] of the ground (Character choice), so hovering a few meters off the floor. They may not benefit from Flight Maneuvers until their next Turn.

A flying Character, unless they are flying low to the ground or another enemy has flown with them (ie, within [[Engaged Range]],) cannot be targeted by [[Melee-Light|Melee]] or [[Brawl]] [[Attack Check|Attack Checks]]. In cases where they are, like mentioned previously, all Melee Attacks on them add 2 [[Setback Dice]]. This is due to a Flying Character having the high-ground over a land-bound Character, and due to the difficulty of one Flying Character attempting to strike another.

Flying Characters are not impacted by certain [[Hazards]], such as [[Difficult Terrain]], when in Flight.

Most Characters with Flight will have a Speed of 1-2.

### Flight Maneuvers
Flight Maneuvers are different from regular [[Maneuvers]], and cannot be spent on the same things as regular Maneuvers. Instead, the options for Flight Maneuvers are listed below.

Some Characters with Flight may be obligated to spend all Flight Maneuvers on their turn, or at least one on movement; however, this is not the case by default. Rito, for example, do not have this requirement, and can flap their winds to Fly in-place.

#### Move
Move between locations and [[Range Bands|Range Bands]] using the same rules as in [[Maneuvers#Move|the Move Maneuver]] for land-bound [[Character|Characters]]. In addition to moving horizontally, Characters with Flight can also move vertically; closer to or further from the ground.

If the Character in Flight chooses to use the Move Maneuver to take go through a particularly dangerous or precarious path, [[The GM|the GM]] may require that they make an [[Coordination]] [[Skill Checks|Check]] to successfully traverse it.

Regular Move Maneuvers (done with regular Maneuvers, not Flight Maneuvers) can also be used by a Character to move while Flying, and are functionally identical.
#### Evasive Flight
All [[Skill Checks|Checks]] targeting the [[Character]] who uses Evasive Flight adds one more [[Setback Dice|Setback Die]], until the beginning of that Character’s next turn. Can be used a maximum of twice on one Turn.
#### Aggressive Flight
The next [[Attack Check]] done by the [[Character]] in Flight adds one more [[Boost Dice|Boost Die]], although this effect expires at the beginning of their next Turn. Can be used a maximum of twice on one Turn.
#### Landing
When within [[Engaged Range]] of the ground, may take a Flight Maneuver to land, and end flight. May also do this using a regular [[Maneuvers|Maneuver]].

### Strain Cost
For each Turn ended while in the air, the Character in Flight suffers 1 [[Strain]].

### Encumbrance and Weight
When floating above the earth, the weights pulling you back down to the dirt become more troublesome than ever before.

When Flying, consider the [[Encumbrance|Encumbrance Threshold]] to be 3 points lower than it is by default. For example, a [[Character]] with an Encumbrance Threshold of 10 will consider it as 7 when flying.

In addition to all regular penalties from Encumbrance Threshold existing, there is an additional one. If over the Encumbrance Threshold while flying, the Character in Flight will descend to the ground one [[Range Bands|Range Band]] at the end of every turn, and may not spend Flight Maneuvers to move upwards. Once they reached [[Engaged Range]], the end of their next Turn will result in them Landing.

If a Character would be over their Encumbrance Threshold if they started to Fly, but are currently not, they cannot take a Maneuver to begin Flying. For example, let us say a Character has an Encumbrance Threshold of 10, and are currently have an Encumbrance of 8. Since Flying would cause their Encumbrance Threshold to drop to 7, and therefore leave them one point over their Encumbrance Threshold, they cannot take a Maneuver to begin to fly.

Picking up another Character adds to Encumbrance. Characters that are [[Silhouette]] 1 add +10 Encumbrance when held, in addition to any [[Items]] they are carrying. [[Silhouette]] 2 adds +20 [[Encumbrance]]. Silhouette 3 adds +30 Encumbrance. So on and so fourth. See [[Encumbrance#The Encumbrance of a Person|The Encumbrance of a Person]].
### Jumping over Hazards
Any [[Character]] with Flight who tries to jump over [[Hazards]] or gaps the regular way gain three [[Boost Dice]] to the attempt, unless their Flight is currently being blocked or denied to them.

### Knocking Character out of Flight
A [[Triumph]] can be spent on an [[Attack Check]] to knock a Character out of the sky. Some other effects, including some [[Critical Injury|Critical Injuries]], can result in this. This causes them to immediately fall to the ground and no longer be considered as Flying.

They suffer [[Fall Damage]], but treat the [[Range Bands|Range Band]] they fall is treated as one lower than it is in actuality. 

Three [[Advantage|Advantages]] can caused the forced descent effect mentioned previously in the Encumbrance section. A Character in Flight can attempt to revert course from that by using an [[Actions|Action]] to make a [[Difficulty|Hard]] (3p) [[Coordination]] [[Skill Checks|Check]].
