Horses and other ridable animals follow similar mechanics to those outlined in [[Ship Combat, and Vehicles]], but are simplified and at a more personal-scale.

Horses, just like any other living creature when relevant to track, have a [[Wound Threshold]] and [[Strain Threshold]]. These represent the same things as they do on any other [[Character]].

In addition to this, however, Horses have an additional stat, which is similar to one from Ships and Vehicles.

### Mount Stats
#### Speed
Similar to Max Speed, the Speed rating represents how fast an animal can go. The rider of a Mount immediately gains free Mount Maneuvers on their turn equal to the Speed rating of their Mount. 

Unlike with Max Speed, the animal is assumed to always be going at their Speed rating, and therefore the [[Character]] riding them always gets a number of free Mount Maneuvers equal to their Mount’s Speed. However, they are under *no obligation* to spend all of these Mount Maneuvers.

The Speed of most Mounts and Horses is 1-2. A Mount with Speed 0 must suffer 1 [[Strain]] each turn to gain one Mount Maneuver. 
#### Handling
Handling with Mounts and Horses represents the discipline of the animal, and its bond with its rider. Mechanically, it operates very similarly to Handling in Ships and Vehicles.

Handling can either be a + or - number, and adds either [[Boost Dice]] or [[Setback Dice|Setback Dice]] to a roll depending on its rating. For example, when riding a Horse with Handling +3, all relevant [[Skill Checks]] done on the back of the horse would gain three Boost Dice. When on a Horse with Handling -1, suffer 1 Setback Die on all relevant Checks.

A Mount has its Base Handling; this represents its training, natural biology, and its suitability for riding. In addition to this, it has Bond Handling, which is different for each [[Character]]. Bond Handling is determined by the relationship between the horse and its rider. This is a modifier that is added onto Base Handling, and can increase or decrease the overall Handling rating.

Handling is applied to any [[Riding]] [[Skill Checks|Checks]] done with the Mount, or to any Checks made in a hurry where the impact of the Mount’s movements is significant. For example, an [[Attack Check]] while on horseback.

The minimum Handling is -6; the max Handling is +6.
#### Bond
A bond between a Horse/Mount and any given person. This stat is unique in that it is only applicable when a Mount and a rider are both taken into account. A Mount that has a high Bond with one rider can have a low Bond with another.

A high Bond can improve Handling, [[Strain Threshold]], and in some cases even Speed. 

A Horse either **Gentle** (easier to Bond with) or **Wild** (and harder to Bond with). Neither of these impact the max potential of the horse, only the difficulty in reaching it.

Bond is a scale from 0 to 100. When certain thresholds are reached—generated randomly through a percentile table—benefits are incurred, also randomly generated, such as increased Handling, Speed, Strain Threshold, etc. Speed can never exceed 3.

Most wild Horses and Mounts start with a Bond of 5; however, an already tamed Horse may start with a higher Bond.

The process on how to increase a Bond is elaborated on below.

### Actions on Horseback
All the usual actions, such as personal [[Maneuvers]], [[Actions]], or [[Incidentals]], apply while on horseback or on a Mount. However, Move Maneuvers cannot be taken while on horseback. A Maneuver can be spent to Dismount or Mount (when in [[Engaged Range]]) a horse at any time.

Whenever riding a Horse or Mount, one hand is considered to always be on the reins of the animal. Two-handed [[Weapons]] can be used, but a [[Character]] must suffer 2 [[Strain]] to use them; in addition, add an extra [[Difficulty Dice|Difficulty Die]] to a [[Attack Check|Check]] to use them. Two one-handed [[Items]] cannot be held on horseback.

Whenever making an [[Attack Check]] or a physical Check related under pressure where being on a Mount would logically make it more challenging, add one Difficulty Die to the roll. Additionally, add [[Setback Dice]] or [[Boost Dice]] equal to the Handling on the Mount or horse.

### Mount Maneuvers
Earlier, it was mentioned that a [[Character]] riding on a Horse or Mount gains free Mount Maneuvers equal to that Mount’s speed. But what can you do with these Mount Maneuvers?

As mentioned earlier, you are under no obligation to spend all Mount Maneuvers on a turn.

#### Move
Move between locations and [[Range Bands|Range Bands]] using the same rules as in [[Maneuvers#Move|the Move Maneuver]] for individual [[Character|Characters]]. 

If the Mount’s rider chooses to use the Move Maneuver to take their Mount or horse through a particularly dangerous or precarious path, [[The GM|the GM]] may require that they make an Riding [[Skill Checks|Check]] to successfully navigate.

#### Gain The Advantage
Similar to the option of the same name in [[Ship Combat, and Vehicles]], Gain the Advantage here is a Maneuver, not an Action. Gain the Advantage represents maneuvering and guiding Horse or Mount in such a way that you gain a significant advantage in the [[Combat]], in comparison to your enemies, such as tailing behind a horseback enemy.

To make this Mount Maneuver, suffer 4 Strain. Then, roll a Riding [[Skill Checks|Check]] opposed to the [[Athletics]] or [[Coordination]] (if on foot) or [[Riding]] (if on a Mount) or [[Operating]] (if in a vehicle) of the enemy with the best [[Dice Pool]] for that Check in the [[Encounter]]. Add an automatic [[Success]] for each Speed point your Mount is above the enemy’s Mount. If the enemy is on foot, add 2 auto-[[Success|Successes]].

If there are various enemies in significantly different situations (such as some on foot and some on horseback), the GM may rule that a Gain The Advantage Check only applies to some of them. For instance, Gaining the Advantage over enemies on foot wouldn’t apply to enemies on Horseback.

If the initiating [[Character]] succeeds, they gain ‘The Advantage’ over the target. When they have this benefit, [[Dice Upgrade|upgrade]] all [[Attack Check|Attack Checks]] from this Mount targeting the other [[Character|Characters]] by two. If a horseback Character attacks another Mount or Horse that has ‘The Advantage’ over them, then that Character upgrades the [[Difficulty]] twice.

#### Positioning
The Mount suffers 2 [[Strain]] to move into a more advantageous position for an [[Attack Check]] or physical action. Gain an automatic [[Success]] on that Check.

#### Evasion
The Mount suffers 2 [[Strain]] to move into a more advantageous position for defense, or avoiding enemies. The next [[Attack Check]] targeting the Mount or its rider add an automatic [[Failure]].

### Strain
At the end of [[Encounter|Encounters]], Horses and Mounts do not heal strain. Instead, if they were active to a significant degree and were used, they suffer Strain.

Roll 2 [[Difficulty Dice]]. The Mount adds Strain equal to the amount of [[Failure|Failures]] rolled on that Check.

Additionally, time spent using a Horse, such as when traveling or during [[Downtime]], may increase its Strain, per the GM’s ruling. Higher [[Encumbrance]] loads may also increase the Strain suffered.

The only way for a Mount or Horse to heal Strain is for it to be able to rest, or via [[Advantage]]. In the case of Mounts and horses, two Advantage on a Check on horseback must to spent to heal 1 [[Strain]].

If a Mount exceeds its [[Strain Threshold]], it does not necessarily collapse or fall [[Incapacitated|unconscious]]. Instead, it may simply become too tired to keep moving, or it may buck its rider off. Mounts usually have a Strain Threshold of 6 to 12.

### Building a Bond
Building trust and a Bond with a Horse or Mount is important; it can increase its Handling, and in some cases, can result in increasing its Strain Threshold or even Speed.

On any Horse with the Gentle Temperament, any successful [[Riding]] [[Skill Checks|Check]] will increase Bond by 1. Additionally, spending 2 [[Advantage]] on any Mount-related Check where Handling would apply, increases Bond by 1.

Additional actions, such as bathing a Mount, feeding it, or taking care of it, will also increase the Bond. As a general rule, Mounts with the Wild Temperament will increase their Bond by half the amount than a Mount with the Gentle Temperament would to the same act.

Some actions, such as the horse being hurt under a Character’s watch, overworking the horse, or forcing it to carry heavy loads, may decrease a horse’s Bond.
#### Training
Building a Bond with a Horse is also, effectively, training it. A Horse’s Bond with a new Character will be functionally a *half* of what its Bond with its most favored rider is, so long as it has seen or interacted with that rider recently. If the Mount has the Wild Temperament, this is only a *quarter*, not a half.

If a Mount or Horse is well acquainted with a person, or they are related in some way to the favored rider (in such a way that the Mount could smell or tell), then increase their starting Bond with that Mount by 10.

A Mount that is particularly well-trained, and gone through a program for it, may have a default Bond with new riders equal to *three quarters* of its Bond with its favored rider.
