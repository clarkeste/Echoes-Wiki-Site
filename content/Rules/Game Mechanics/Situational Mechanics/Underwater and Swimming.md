Some situations in [[Echoes of the Departed]] will result in [[Character|Characters]] being forced underwater, or implored to explore it and map out the bottoms of lakes or rivers.

In narrative time, the question of maneuvering underwater is primarily one of time and holding one’s breath—and making the most of the time one has underwater. [[Swim]] is used in situations where the watery path is precarious enough, or requires enough lung-capacity, that it becomes dangerous to complete or in which Success is in doubt.

In [[Combat|structured time]], a [[Character]] can breath underwater for rounds equal to their [[Willpower]] or [[Swim]] [[Skills|Skill]] (whichever is higher), although [[The GM]] may decide to increase this duration if the task is not particularly physically taxing. Additionally, using a [[Maneuvers|Maneuver]] while underwater causes the Character using the Maneuver to suffer 1 Strain.

### Swimming Check
At any point in [[Combat]] or structured time, unless a [[Character]] has already succeeded on this same Check, a Character may use an [[Actions|Action]] to make a Swim [[Skill Checks|Skill Check]]. The baseline [[Difficulty]] of this Check is Average (2p) but the GM may increase it depending on the circumstances.

[[Success]] on this Check means the Character may take an immediate free [[Maneuvers|Maneuver]], and will not suffer any more [[Strain]] when using a [[Maneuvers|Maneuver]] underwater. Advantage may be spent in the usual manner, with 2 [[Advantage]] able to spent to increase breath duration by 1 Round per purchased. A [[Triumph]] can be used to automatically gain a second [[Maneuvers|Maneuver]] on every Turn while in underwater during the [[Encounter]].

A [[Gorons|Goron]] will simply sink to the bottom and be able to make Maneuvers to Move on the ground as if on land. However, due to the slowing effect of water, they can only make one Move Maneuver per turn. If they suffer 2 Strain and succeed on a Hard (3p) Swim Check, they will be able to Swim.

### Exhausting Air, Suffocating
When a Character runs out of air while swimming, they begin to **Suffocate**.

When Suffocating, a Character suffers 6 Strain at the end of every Round in structured time, or every 10-20 seconds in narrative time. Once they go over their [[Strain Threshold]], they become [[Incapacitated|incapacitated]] and then begin to suffer a [[Critical Injury]] instead of suffering more Strain, until they die.

If a Character leaves water while Suffocating, this effect will stop, of course. In addition, when returning from Suffocation, heal half of the Strain suffered from it; however, do not heal any of the Critical Injuries suffered.

### Encumbrance of a Person
In some situations, it may be important to determine the Encumbrance of a person, such as when Swimming. Thankfully, this is easy to determine, and is decided by their [[Silhouette]].

A [[Character]] with Silhouette 1 is determined to weigh 5 Encumbrance. A Character with Silhouette 2 is determined to weigh 10 Encumbrance. Each additional Silhouette after that adds +10, so a Silhouette 3 Character would be 20 Encumbrance.

A Silhouette 0 Character weighs 3 Encumbrance. A Silhouette 00 person weighs 0 Encumbrance.
