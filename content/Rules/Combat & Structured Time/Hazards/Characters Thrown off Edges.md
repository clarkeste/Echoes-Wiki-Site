When using the Shove [[Maneuvers|Maneuver]], two [[Advantage|Advantages]] for the same purpose, [[Manipulate]], or any other number of things that may move another [[Character]] against their will, it may be beneficial to throw them off an edge. This can be out a window, off a balcony, or into a fast-running river.

However, these tossed Characters are not completely helpless to protect themselves from this potential fate. Minions and some Rivals, as decided by [[The GM]], simply fall without any check and suffer the consequences; usually, exiting the [[Combat]] and suffering [[Fall Damage]].

[[Nemesis]] enemies, more important Rivals and [[PC|PCs]], however, will get the chance to roll to save themselves from falling, and from the potential disaster that awaits them.

First, the Character in question being knocked off rolls a [[Coordination]] or [[Athletics]] [[Skill Checks|Check]] to decide if they manage to grab hold of something or not. Usually, this thing they’re grabbing hold of is the ledge they were just knocked off of, but not necessarily. 

The [[Difficulty]] of this Check is determined by the circumstances. Usually, a minor enemy will be Easy (1p) Difficulty, a moderate enemy will be Average (2p) Difficulty, and a powerful enemy might be Hard (3p) Difficulty. Other things will impact the Difficulty calculation, however, such as the terrain and what is being grabbed on to. [[Setback Dice|Setbacks]] will also be applied based on the intensity of the [[Combat]] or environmental factors, such as slippery terrain from rain.

If the Check is a Success, then the Character manages to grab onto the ledge. A [[Maneuvers|Maneuver]] on their next turn, or a Maneuver from a nearby friend, will allow them to be pulled up back onto stable footing. Alternatively, on a [[Failure]] with [[Advantage|Advantages]], the Character might hold but their grip is slipping, meaning someone else will have to make a Check or a Maneuver to save them.

If a Character who succeeded on their Check is Shoved again before they use a Maneuver to get back up, they will fall without being given a second Check or chance to resist. This cannot be done on the same turn as when the Character was first Shoved off the cliff.

If the fall off the edge is particularly long and the Character is an PC or a Nemesis, the GM may give them a second chance to catch something as they fall, and therefore reduce their [[Fall Damage]].

If a Character is already [[Incapacitated]], they cannot roll to grab a hold of a ledge.

In rare circumstances, this may apply when being Shoved into particularly dangerous [[Hazards]], or if the Character being Shoved has Adversary 2 or higher.
