## Swimming and Moving
When a [[Character]] enters water, movement is done as normal. However, just as sometimes [[Athletics]] [[Skill Checks|Checks]] are required if the surrounding space is difficult to navigate, additional [[Swim]] Checks may be required for tricky terrain, or when trying to get somewhere fast. 

However, for most beings, moving in water is harder and much more challenging and tiring than on land. Due to this, all Checks done underwater add two [[Setback Dice]], and when suffering [[Strain]] to gain an additional [[Maneuvers|Maneuver]], a Character suffers 3 Strain instead of 2. A Character can also [[Water, Sinking, and Drowning#Drowning|drown]] if they spend too much time under the water.

However, during [[Combat Skills|Combat]] or structured time, a Character may, as an [[Actions|Action]], choose to do a [[Difficulty|Hard]] (3p) Swim Check when in the water, or when preparing to enter. If successful, all the aforementioned debuffs are ignored.

## Drowning
If a [[Character]] spends an extended period under the water, it is possible that they will begin to suffocate and—eventually—die.

Under the water, a [[Character]] can hold their breath for an amount of Rounds equal to their [[Brawn]] [[Characteristics|Characteristic]]. To refill their air, they must surface at some point, at which they can survive another number of Rounds equal to their Brawn Characteristic.

If a Character stays underneath the water more time than they are allowed, they begin to suffocate. Each turn they end suffocating, they suffer 3 [[Strain]]. Once they are over their [[Strain Threshold]], they stop suffering Strain and instead suffer a [[Critical Injury]] at the end of each turn they spend suffocating, until the Character is either dead or rescued.

## Sinking
In some case, when a [[Character]] is particularly heavy, usually due to what they are carrying, they may sink into bodies of water. 

Determining if a Character should sink or not is quite simple. If a Character has 12 or more [[Encumbrance]], they begin to sink. Wooden [[Items]], or Items made out of other buoyant materials, are excluded. A Character can make a [[Swim]] [[Skill Checks|Check]] when they begin to sink to avoid their fate. The [[Difficulty]] starts at [[Difficulty|Easy]] (1p) and increases by one per Encumbrance after 12. After [[Difficulty|Formidable]], the Check is impossible and cannot be done until weight is removed.

It should noted that if a Character is a [[Gorons|Goron]], they are much more likely to sink. For the purposes of determining if a Character should sink, Gorons add +10 Encumbrance to their total.
