The Attack Check is a type of [[Skill Checks|Skill Check]] done during [[Combat|Combat]] with the intent to deal [[Damage|Damage]] to another [[Character|Character]]. It is usually done with a [[Weapons|Weapon]] like a Sword or Bow, but can be done unarmed with just fists as well.

Unlike most other checks, the [[Difficulty|Difficulty]] of Attack checks is determined by the distance of the active character to the [[Adversary|target]] they are Attacking, determined by [[Range Bands|Range Bands]]. 

To see how exactly to roll on [[RPGSessions]], see the [[Attack Check#Rolling Attack Checks from Game Table|Rolling Attack Checks from the Game Table]] part of this article. However, please read the general explanation of the process first.

### Determine Weapon and Skill
Every weapon will have a [[Skills|Skill]] associated with it that determines what Skill must be rolled when the Weapon is used.

First, navigate to the [[Game Table|Game Table]] and select your Character from the top-left, to see your [[Weapons|Weapon]] as that will decide some of the parameters of your roll.

![](https://i.imgur.com/59AMwnF.png)

Once it opens the character sheet, scroll down past the Skill section until you reach a part that says “WEAPONS”. This section lists any and all weapons you have previously assigned to your character. It lists things such as the Damage, the [[Critical Rating|Critical Rating]], the Range of the Weapon, and the [[Qualities|Qualities]] it has.

![](https://i.imgur.com/z5616yx.png)

Damage, as one might imagine, indicates how much Damage a Weapon does. Higher is often better. 

‘Crit’ refers to the Critical Rating of a weapon. A number of [[Advantage|Advantage]] equal to the Critical Rating must be spent to activate it, thereby dealing a[[Critical Injury| Critical Injury]]. A [[Triumph|Triumph]] can activate a Critical Injury no matter what. When it comes to Critical Ratings, lower is better.

The Range column shows the maximum Range Band a Weapon can be used in. In the case of the Soldier’s Broadsword, this range is [[Engaged Range|Engaged]]. It cannot attack further than this range.

The Qualities section lists any special modifiers the weapon may have. In the case of the Soldier’s Broadsword, this is Accurate 1 and Quick Strike.

## Range Bands and Determining Difficulty
In Attack Checks, the Difficulty is determined by Range. While the GM may still add [[Setback Dice|Setbacks]], [[Boost Dice|Boosts]], upgrade some [[Difficulty Dice|Difficulty Dice]] to [[Challenge Dice|Challenge Dice]], or various other modifiers could impact it such as [[Moving Target|Moving Target]], the base Difficulty itself tends to be a little more objectively determined.

There are six Range Bands in [[Echoes of the Departed|Echoes of the Departed]], each conferring a different Difficulty. To read about Range Bands in depth, including what each one means, click on Range Bands. This section will only go over how they impact difficulty.

##### Engaged
Attacking a [[Character|Character]] within [[Engaged Range|Engaged Range]] is 2 [[Difficulty Dice|Difficulty Dice]], or “[[Difficulty|Average]]” [[Difficulty|Difficulty]]. Weapons using the [[Gunnery|Gunnery]] Skill cannot attack a target within Engaged Range.

![](https://i.imgur.com/SKderG4.png)

##### Short
Attacking a [[Character|Character]] within [[Short Range|Short Range]] is 1 [[Difficulty Dice|Difficulty Die]], or “[[Difficulty|Easy]]" [[Difficulty|Difficulty]].

![](https://i.imgur.com/czQ7j3h.png)

##### Medium
Attacking a [[Character|Characte]]r within [[Medium Range|Medium Range]] is 2 [[Difficulty Dice|Difficulty Dice]], or “[[Difficulty|Average]]” [[Difficulty|Difficulty]].

![](https://i.imgur.com/SKderG4.png)

##### Long
Attacking a [[Character|Character]] within [[Long Range|Long Range]] is 3 [[Difficulty Dice|Difficulty Dice]], or “[[Difficulty|Hard]]” [[Difficulty|Difficulty]].

![](https://i.imgur.com/zC2vSyb.png)

##### Extreme
Attacking a [[Character|Character]] within [[Extreme Range|Extreme Range]] is 4 [[Difficulty Dice|Difficulty Dice]], or “[[Difficulty|Daunting]]" [[Difficulty|Difficulty]].

![](https://i.imgur.com/a839KN4.png)

##### Strategic
Attacking a target within [[Strategic Range|Strategic Range]] is often impossible, as very few [[Weapons|Weapons]] reach this distance. When it is possible, it is—by default—5 [[Difficulty Dice|Difficulty Dice]], or “[[Difficulty|Formidable]]” [[Difficulty|Difficulty]].

![](https://i.imgur.com/rZtb3Vd.png)

### Rolling and Results
Once you’ve assembled the [[Dice Pool]], it’s time to roll and let fate decide what happens next.

Once a dice pool is rolled, its results are displayed both on the right side of the screen in “History & Messages” (see later section for more details), and the Dice channel on the Discord server. This is where the core of the [[Narrative Dice|Narrative Dice]] system comes into play—determining what the results of a roll mean.

While [[Success|Success]] and [[Failure|Failure]] are simple to understand, other symbols—[[Advantage|Advantage]] and [[Threat|Threat]], [[Triumph|Triumph]] and [[Despair|Despair]]—represents additional side-benefits or complications in what is already a Success or Failure.

By default, the [[The GM|GM]] determines how Threat and Despair are spent, although in some cases (such as checks made by [[NPCs|NPCs]] where the GM wishes to give [[Player|Players]] an option) they may give the players the option to spend these instead.

## Rolling Attack Checks from Game Table
To roll from the [[Game Table]], click on the [[Dice Pool]] icons in Weapon menu, right underneath where it says the Skill required. This automatically opens and adds the correct positive dice to the Dice Panel. 

This gives us two further options we must pick to continue. Firstly, we have to pick the [[Character|target]] of our Attack Check from the [[Character]] list. Then, we have to pick the [[Range Bands|Range]] we are attacking them from. The Range determines the [[Difficulty]] of the Attack Check.

![](https://i.imgur.com/7LzihPG.png)

Obviously, the target will depend on who you wish to Attack, and who you are capable of attacking; [[Ranged|Range]] is dependent on their distance from you, as determined by [[The GM|the GM]], the [[Encounter]], etc. Feel free to ask the GM for the Range. Likewise, remember your Weapon has a maximum Range and not every Character in the target list is actually a valid target.

After successfully selecting both options, the Rolling modal will appear. This has the [[Narrative Dice|Dice]] pre-prepared for us, including any [[Defense]] or [[Adversary]] the target may have.

![](https://i.imgur.com/L6LFUir.png)

Firstly, you can add additional Dice to the roll. This is particularly helpful if, as is common, environmental or situational factors as determined by [[The GM|the GM]] add additional [[Setback Dice]] or [[Boost Dice]]. If the GM told you to add additional symbols of any type, add them here by clicking on the relevant Dice or symbol icons.

Additionally, you can see Pre-Roll Activations. These are determined by RPGSessions [[Qualities]]/Modifiers added by [[Talents]], [[Weapons]], etc. If you click on their corresponding “Stage” button, they will change the Attack Check in some way relating to the rules around them. They are often automated to some extent. For example, a [[Talents|Talent]] that requires you to suffer Strain to use may automatically add the Strain to your [[Character Sheet]]. Don’t worry about this too much; nothing here will appear unless you buy an applicable [[Talents|Talent]] or [[Weapons|Weapon]], in case you will know the options ahead of time.

Finally, once everything looks good, hit the “Roll as (Character)” button at the bottom-right of the Rolling modal.

Once you do so, on the right-side of the screen where “History & Messages” is displayed, you will see the Roll and its results. It will also be shown on the relevant Discord channel. However, depending on what you’ve used, you may see extra options available in the “History & Messages” pane.

![](https://i.imgur.com/z2IPfJ3.png)

Beneath the main roll results, you can see it lists your “remaining” symbols, and options to spend them. In this case, we have 3 [[Success|Successes]] and 3 [[Advantage]] left over. [[Success|Successes]] can’t be spent on any additional effects in most [[Skill Checks]], but Advantages can.

I can spend those 3 Advantage on a variety of things or a combination. For example, on various effects as is common and encouraged with [[Narrative Dice]], including examples, described further down, or I can use 2 of them to trigger one of the relevant [[Qualities]] on [[Weapons|Weapon]]. [[RPGSessions]] lets you declare them to be spent them on those Qualities right here.

As you can see, the Attack Check was done using a [[Whip]], which has [[Ensnare (Active) (Quality)|Ensnare]] 3 and [[Knockdown (Active) (Quality)|Knockdown]] 1. I can spend 2 Advantage to activate either one. In this case, I will pick Knockdown. I’ll have to click twice, to confirm. When I do, the roll updates to show that Knockdown was activated on this roll, and that the 2 Advantage spent on it is no longer considered “Remaining”. Instead, now 3 are “remaining”

Of course, RPGSessions does not usually automate the *results* of Knockdown being activated. For example, if a [[PC|PC]] does it to an [[NPCs|NPC]] and that NPC is knocked [[Prone]], it is up to [[The GM|the GM]] to make sure that is remembered and honored.

There are additional options you can see after a Check has been rolled, that can be relevant in some circumstance. One of them, “Boost”, is highlighted as an example.

![](https://i.imgur.com/cMnf6Ff.png)

You will see four options. Firstly, “Re-roll Die/Dice”. This is useful if you use a [[Talents|Talent]] such as [[Natural]], or some other ability, to be able to choose to re-roll Checks. The option allows you to either re-roll all Dice, or just some of them. This is rarely used, but is handy for when the need arises.

Then there is “Upgrade”. This is useful if you intended to either [[Dice Upgrade|upgrade]] the [[Difficulty]] or the Ability of the [[Skill Checks|Check]], but forgot to do so. You can do so here without re-rolling all the Dice; only the relevant one or two.

And “Boost”! I cannot begin to tell you how many times people have forgotten one [[Boost Dice|Boost]] or two, and this button makes it very easy to simply add a Boost Die to the Check without re-rolling.

Finally, “Edit Die”, which goes largely unused in [[Echoes of the Departed]]. It allows you to change a Die to an adjacent face.

At some point, the [[RPGSessions]] team may also add a “Setback” option, as they have hinted. If they do so, this button is essentially the useful opposite to “Boost”, allowing you to add a [[Setback Dice|Setback Die]] to a roll if you forgot.

These options also exist if you roll on Discord, using the [[RPGSessions Bot|Discord bot]].


### Changing Dice Naming Scheme
First, navigate to your profile from the [[RPGSessions|RPGSessions]] website.

![](https://i.imgur.com/LM8Bd7Y.png)

Once there, hit the “Dice Input Style” button in the user settings category.

![](https://i.imgur.com/VIGpTkX.png)

Select ‘Color’ or ‘Game’ from the drop-down menu depending on which one you want.
