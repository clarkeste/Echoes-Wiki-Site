Not to be confused with [[Sunder (Modifier)|Sunder (Modifier)]].

A [[Weapons|Weapon]] with the [[Sunder (Modifier)|Sunder]] [[Qualities|Quality]] may damage other Weapons and [[Items|Items]], with some exceptions, such as [[Armor|Armor]]. Every 2 [[Advantage|Advantages]] spent will damage the target Item one stage. Stage 1 through 4 damage the Item, but it is still intact and can be repaired; Stage 5 completely destroys a weapon. It is difficult to repair in this state and does not confer any benefits.

Sunder can be activated multiple times during an [[Attack Check]] to damage an Item further. Every subsequent activation after the first costs 1 Advantage. The owner of the damaged Item can spend a [[Sacred Points|Sacred Point]] if it moves from Stage 3 to Destroyed in a single Attack to reduce the damage to Stage 4. Sunder cannot be used on Armor.

### Damaged Items
All Items or objects, even including walls or floors, can sustain Damage, although not all can be damaged with Sunder. Unless it is a particularly powerful Sunder effect and [[The GM|the GM]] agrees, Sunder can usually only affect Items of [[Silhouette]] 1 or smaller, and cannot be used on areas which are part of a larger whole, such as a wall.

Items at Stage 0 are Items that are in good shape; their durability is not threatened at all. Most Items are like this.

Items at Stage 1 are damaged in some way. Every time they are used, a [[Setback Dice|Setback Die]] is added to the Dice Pool.

Items at Stage 2 are significantly damaged. Every time they are used, add an additional [[Difficulty]] Die to the Dice Pool.

Items at Stage 3 are difficult to properly use, and any pretense of it being a functional product is gone. In addition to the Stage 2 effect, suffer 2 [[Strain]] whenever it is to be used.

Items at Stage 4 cannot be used. They can still be repaired, but cannot be used until that is the case.

Items that go beyond Stage 4 are destroyed. All destroyed Items or objects, such as Destroyed walls or floors, can be considered beyond Stage 4. Nothing can regenerate an Item or Object at this point, short of something that can undo what has been done.

When [[Armor]] is damaged in this way, it is considered to be ‘used’ when it is worn. That means that when wearing a piece of Armor that has been damaged to Stage 1, a [[Character]] must add a Setback to every Check.

Repairing an Item at Stage 1 is a simple Average (2p) [[Craftsmanship]] [[Skill Checks|Checks]]; Stage 2 is a Hard (3p) Craftsmanship Check. For most Silhouette 0 and 1 Items, this does not require any Downtime. Stage 3 and 4 require Daunting (4p) and Formidable (5p) Craftsmanship Checks respectively, and must be done at a worktable or workshop. Stage 3 requires 1 Day to repair; Stage 4 requires 3 Days to repair.

A Stage 1 broken wall or floor indicates noticeable cracks or structural issues. With proper funding and maintenance, this is the sort of damage that would be noticed and fixed with some haste, but not urgency.

A Stage 2 broken wall, ceiling, or floor, indicates an extremely uneven and clearly damaged structure. Wooden structures may have splinters on them which could be painful to step on.

A Stage 3 broken wall, ceiling, or floor, has long cracks running all across it. If it is stone or metal, it is likely it could be blown open with an explosive. If it’s wood or a material of similar strength, it could be easily shoved broken.

A Stage 4 broken wall cannot be wood; wood and materials of similar strength go straight from Stage 3 to Destroyed. Stage 4 walls are extremely flimsy; even strong stone or metal ones might collapse with a strong pushed, and floors could fall if one stepped on them.
