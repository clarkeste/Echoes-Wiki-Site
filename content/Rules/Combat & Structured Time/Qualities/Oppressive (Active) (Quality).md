Not to be confused with [[Oppressive (Modifier)|Oppressive (Modifier)]].

When using this [[Qualities|Quality]], add an extra [[Difficulty Dice|Difficulty Die]]. On a Successful [[Skill Checks|Check]], may spend 2 [[Advantage|Advantages]] to add [[Failure]] on the target’s [[Attack Check|Attack Checks]] and [[Social Checks]] for turns equal to Oppressive rating, one failure for every 2 Advantages spent. A [[Character|Character]] suffering from this has the [[Oppressed|Oppressed]] [[Status Effects|Status Effect.]] Multiple uses of the [[Qualities|Quality]] stack.
