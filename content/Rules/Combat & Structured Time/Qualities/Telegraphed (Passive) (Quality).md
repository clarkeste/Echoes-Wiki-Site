This Quality is almost exclusively used by [[Adversary|Adversaries]].

To use a [[Weapons|Weapon]] with the Telegraphed [[Qualities|Quality]], the [[Character|character]] in question must first have used an [[Incidentals|Incidental]] on a previous turn to declare that, on their next turn, they will use a certain Weapon. If certain conditions are met, they may be exempt from this requirement, such as a self-detonation pausing if its target moves out of range.

A Nemesis may elect to not use an [[Actions|Action]] or Weapon they Telegraphed, but this *could* come with a [[Strain]] cost. The cost is equal to the Telegraph rating of the Weapon. For example, if a Weapon has a rating of Telegraph 3, it will cost 3 Strain to cancel it. If an instance of the Telegraph Quality has no rating, then it costs nothing to cancel it.

If a Weapon reads “Telegraph (Longer)”, then it must be Telegraphed for 2 Turns in a row before it can be used.
