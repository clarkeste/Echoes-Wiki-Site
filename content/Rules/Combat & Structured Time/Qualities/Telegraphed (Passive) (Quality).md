This Quality is almost exclusively used by [[Adversary|Adversaries]].

To use a [[Weapons|Weapon]] with the Telegraphed [[Qualities|Quality]], the [[Character|character]] in question must first have used an [[Incidentals|Incidental]] on a previous turn to declare that, on their next turn, they will use a certain Weapon. If certain conditions are met, they may be exempt from this requirement, such as a self-detonation pausing if its target moves out of range.

A Nemesis may elect to not use an [[Actions|Action]] or Weapon they Telegraphed, but must suffer 2 [[Strain]] for doing. If this is done, or it the Weapon isn’t used for whatever reason, the requirement to Telegraph it ‘resets’, meaning it must be done again.

The amount of Turns where the Telegraph Incidental must be used before the Weapon in question can be used is dependent on the Telegraph rating. For example, a Weapon with Telegraph 3 would have to, on three consecutive turns, use an Incidental to Telegraph its use, before it could be used.
