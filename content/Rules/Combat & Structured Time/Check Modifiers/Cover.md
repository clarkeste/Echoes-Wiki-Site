Cover in [[Echoes of the Departed|Echoes of the Departed]] is protection offered by the environment or location, that when hidden behind, makes it harder for [[Attack Check|Attacks]] to hit their target. This is relevant in [[Combat|Combat]], where it adds [[Setback Dice|Setbacks]] to [[Attack Check|Attack Checks]] targeting an enemy who is behind Cover. From which direction(s) Cover protects a player is dependent on the cover, as is the amount of Setbacks on the check.

Cover is usually ignored if a [[Character|Character]] is [[Flanking|Flanked]], as this represents a Character being attacked from behind or a disadvantageous direction.

There are four levels of Cover:
### Minor
Minor Cover grants one Setback to attacks targeting a [[Character|character]] hidden behind it. 

Additionally, it blocks 4 points of [[Blast (Modifier)|Blast]] [[Damage]] unless the attack destroys the Cover source.
### Medium 
Medium Cover adds two Setbacks to the checks of attackers.

It blocks 6 points of Blast Damage unless the attack destroys the Cover source.
### Large 
Large Cover adds three Setbacks to the checks of attackers.

It blocks 8 points of Blast Damage, unless the attack destroys the Cover source.
### Complete
Complete Cover means you cannot be targeted if the cover applies—essentially, this means you are not in their line of sight. Technically, walls or any other complete barrier is Complete Cover.

Complete Cover completely blocks any Damage from Blast, unless the attack destroys the Cover source.

## Engaged Range Attacks
If a [[Character]] behind Cover is attacked from within [[Engaged Range]], the Cover does not usually apply. It may still apply if the Cover does not represent a literal blockage, but more of a defensive or advantageous position.
