## Physical and Cultural Traits
Zuna are [[The Humans|humanoids]] with vibrant green or blue skin and thin bodies and faces with a small nose. Some may describe them as cactus-like—a reference to their origins as a people from the Desert.

The Zuna Republic, where the majority of Zuna live now, is a tropical island and one of the only democracies in the world. They are, in many ways, a nation of underdogs, with many marrying [[Gerudo]] banished from Hyrule.

## History
The history of the Zuna is often unreliable, as few had contact with this tribe that lived deep in the desert. However, by their surviving landmarks and inscriptions, we know that they were a great people who called themselves the pyramid-builders. They conquered the harsh barrens and built great structures.

At some point, likely due to a collapse, conditions forced the Zuna to leave their desert. They settled the island where they live now, escaping the barrens. There, they eventually formed the [[Zuna Republic (Historia)|Zuna Republic]]. To this day, they are an isolated nation, with limited [[Economy|trade]] fueled by their exotic fruits and spices. To most outside of the Republic, it is but a mere curiosity, the Zuna a strange people. This is further enhanced by their close relationship with the Gerudo culturally. Many Gerudo live in the Zuna Republic and vice-versa, the two groups inter-marrying. About a fifth of all families in the Zuna Republic have a Gerudo mother.

The Republic is often credited as the first stable democracy. Zuna tend to passively appreciate their democracy, seeing it as a way for every citizen, regardless of status, to have a say. But democracy is a process, not a static condition. It is becoming, rather than being. It can be lost, but never won. Its essence is an eternal struggle. Particularly when democracy is threatened not only from outside, from within; when its only disciple is an ant in a game played by Kings…
