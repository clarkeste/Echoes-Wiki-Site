## Traits
Humans are similar to Hylians in nearly every way, except that their ears are significantly shorter, and more like an animal's. 

While there are some non-Hylian humans in Hyrule, most of the human population lives in other countries.

Culturally, humans have great variety. Whereas in Holodrum, relative peace and stability has led to a thriving Imperial intellectualism—carefully monitored by the Emperor’s Palace, of course—instability, irredentism, and incompetence has led to failures and resentment in Labrynna.

## History
#### Holodrum 
Holodrum is an Empire of which few can compare to. Its greatest rival, Hyrule, is engulfed in Civil War. Its businessmen, military leaders, and naval officers have a world which is little more than their oyster. 

Holodrum, under the watchful eye of Emperor Kanadei of the Holodramn dynasty, support the Hyrulean Royalists. They wish to take advantage of the situation as it occurs to grow their Empire, an Empire grand in technology, military, and naval prowess. Holodrumian culture largely values honor and honesty, and has a distinct architectural style. That architecture, and the nationalistic ideology behind it, may soon dominate the world….

#### Labrynna
Following the example of their fellow Revolutionaries in Hyrule, Labrynnan dissidents launched their own coup. Since then, Labrynna has been mired in a brutal war where the Revolutionaries, despite Holodrum’s support for their enemies, have broken every rule in the book to put their enemies on the brink.

Labrynna has often played third fiddle to Holodrum and Hyrule. Yearning to be a great empire but always falling short, it over-extends and pays the price again and again. Enough embarrassments have led to the current Revolution, and refugees attempting to escape this dying, self-destructing country….

*Read more: [[Labrynna]]*
#### Calatia
A minor power to the east of Hyrule, Calatia has nearly always been good allies to Hyrule. Yet, their own monarchy was deeply frightened when the Revolution killed their counterparts in Hyrule. Governmental repression and a state of militarized vigilance kept the country’s dissidents at bay.

Now, Calatia, in its own minor way, is together with Holodrum in supporting the Royalists. Its largely coastal country, now missing its ally in Hyrule, has found a new partner in Holodrum. Its cultural beliefs of order and stability are held together with duct tape and glue. Chaos, criminality, or Revolution, may be just around the corner. These sentiments exist, they are only suppressed….

*Read more: [[Calatia]]*

#### Koridai
An island nation in the Great Sea, the center of global shipping and trade. Due to its location, and due to three world powers—Hyrule, Holodrum, and Labrynna—vying over it, Koridai has become extremely wealthy. Its merchants and small monarchy have more wealth than any other country of Koridai’s size could dream of. Despite its small size and population, its location and trade power gives it untold influence and leverage.

Yet, this leverage only exists if that competition between global powers continues. The people of Koridai have enjoyed peace and economic prosperity; its rulers have enjoyed making greater powers bend over backwards to please them. And yet, two of those great powers are lost in a great struggle of identity, dying countries in the midst of Civil Wars. The shadow of a great power unimpeded by any other, an Imperialist Empire which will do anything to spread its ideology and further its power, looms over Koridai. Holodrum looms over Koridai. And every day, their shadow becomes harder to ignore….

*Read more: [[Koridai]]*

#### Hytopia
Hytopia exists to the north of Hyrule. Its own Rebels were inspired by the actions of their counterparts to the south, and attempted to overthrow their own monarch. A brutal civil war with the King’s successor and the Rebels have followed, which has left entire forests scorched and refugees fleeing south to Hyrule.

With great support from Holodrum and Calatia, the Hytopian Royalists are close to winning their Civil War, and completely exterminating the Revolutionaries. Already, the creative culture of Hytopia is being suppressed in areas controlled by their monarchy. Their newspapers are carefully controlled, their citizens surveilled with secret police, their famous fabrics colored dull grays. All to stop the thought of Revolution from killing another hundred-thousand Hytopians….
