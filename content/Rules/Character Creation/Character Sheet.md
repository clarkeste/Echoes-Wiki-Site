The Character Sheet is the core of what happens in the [[Echoes of the Departed|Echoes of the Departed]] game. All the information on a [[Character|Character]]’s mechanical abilities, as well as most of their [[Burdens and Backstory|Backstory]], should be on the Character Sheet.

To access your Character Sheet, go the [[RPGSessions|RPGSessions]] website. In the top-right of the screen, select the tab that says “Library”.

![](https://i.imgur.com/faFBNkb.png)

Then, the “My Library” page will open. Here is a list of all the characters you have created. If you wish to create a new character, hit the “New” button beneath the Logout button.

Select one of the characters in the library page to enter their Character Sheet.

![](https://i.imgur.com/QbIB7SF.png)

After you’ve clicked on a Character, the Character Sheet will open. There, you can edit the Character’s information, such as their [[Characteristics|Characteristics]], [[Skills|Skills]], etc. When making a character, make sure to follow the [[Character Creation Guide|Character Creation]] rules.

Make sure to set your [[Character]] up with the [[RPGSessions Data Library|Data Library]], as it makes adding [[Talents]] and [[Items]] directly from the website incredibly easy and fast. 

If you wish to open the sheet in the [[Game Table|Game Table]], first navigate to the Game Table. The exact Game Table UI is changing recently, including the [[RPGSessions]] team experimenting with a new design. Regardless, though, you should see a button with a + that says “Add” somewhere in the top-left part of the screen. This will then let you pick your [[Character]] from the list. After picking them, they will appear in the list!

Click on your Character’s icon to open their Character Sheet on the Game Table.

![](https://i.imgur.com/bBsJ26g.png)

Once you select your character, it will appear on the Game Table, allowing you edit it on the spot. This can be useful for updating [[Wounds|Wounds]] or [[Strain|Strain]] immediately after healing or suffering them, for instance.

## Editing a Character Sheet
### Characteristics
To edit [[Characteristics|Characteristics]], simply click on the big number beneath the big “[[Brawn]]”, “[[Agility]]”, etc, text, and change it. The [[Characteristics]] are found at the top of your Character Sheet, just beneath the “More Character Information” section which itself is nearby to the Character’s name.

### Skills
To edit [[Skills]], click on the pencil button in the top-right corner of the “Skills” panel of your Character Sheet, near the top of it. 

![](https://i.imgur.com/sYViE6k.png)

After doing so, this will allow you to add or remove Ranks in a Skill. Each filled section of a bar here is a Rank in the [[Skills|Skill]] the bar is next to. For example, the [[Character]] in this image has a [[Charm]] Skill Rank of 1. If you click to fill 2 notches of the bar, it will modify your Character Sheet to show you have 2 Skill Ranks.

![](https://i.imgur.com/VauE9vL.png)

The little box next to the name of the Skill indicates whether it is a [[Career Skills|Career Skill]] or not. If a Skill is a Career Skill, make sure to tick the box.

The text in parentheses next to each Skill name, such as (BR) or (INT), refers to what [[Characteristics|Characteristic]] the Skill is tied to. INT, for example, refers to [[Intellect]], and BR refers to [[Brawn]].

Make sure to hit save when you’re done!

### Items, Weapons, Gear, Armor
See the [[Items]] article.

### Critical Injury
It is rare you need to manually add a [[Critical Injury]]. When your [[Character]] suffers a [[Critical Hit]], simply hit the red “Roll Crit” button, adding or subtracting 10 with the adjacent buttons if the GM says you should or it is relevant due to the presence of something like [[Vicious (Passive) (Quality)|Vicious]]. Feel free to ask if you are not sure. See the [[Critical Injury]] article for more information.

#### Backstory, Burden, Beliefs, Notes
The [[Burdens and Backstory|Backstory]] and notes section of a Character Sheet are at the bottom, and can be edited or added to by hitting the pencil icon.
