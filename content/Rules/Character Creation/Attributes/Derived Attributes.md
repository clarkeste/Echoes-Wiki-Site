Derived Attributes refers to stats, such as [[Wound Threshold|Wound Threshold]], [[Strain Threshold|Strain Threshold]], [[Encumbrance|Encumbrance Threshold]], [[Soak|Soak]], and [[Defense|Defense]] that are not directly bought or upgraded, but are instead increased or decreased as a results of other stats, such as [[Characteristics|Characteristics]] or [[Talents|Talents]].

### List of Derived Attributes:
[[Wound Threshold]]
[[Strain Threshold]]
[[Soak]]
[[Defensive Step]]
[[Encumbrance|Encumbrance Threshold]]

Additionally, [[Wounds]] and [[Strain]] are like the ‘health’ of a [[Character]] and change regularly. How much Wounds or Strain a Character can suffer before becoming [[Incapacitated]] is determined by Wound and Strain Threholds.
