This [[Skills|Skill]] is a measure of the [[Character|Character]]'s overall fitness and physical condition. Used to demonstrate a character's physical strength and athletic ability. It is tied to [[Brawn|Brawn]], and is a [[General Skills|General Skill]].

It may be used for rolls regarding:
- Any attempts at vertical movement; including climbing, leaping, falling and so on. 
- Jumping over pits, gaps, or hazards.
- Prolonged physical efforts and situations where time is of the essence, such as carrying a heavy load, or chasing an escaping suspect down.

Specific instances that may call for an [[Athletics]] [[Skill Checks|Check]] directly:
- Mitigating [[Fall Damage]]
- Escaping [[Immobilized]] [[Status Effects|Status Effect]]
- Grabbing an [[Characters Thrown off Edges|edge when tossed off]]
- Mitigating some [[Hazards]]
