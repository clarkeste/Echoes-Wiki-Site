This [[Skills|Skill]] represents the [[Character|Character]]’s prowess at knowing their way around machinery, contraptions and complicated vehicles. It is tied to [[Intellect|Intellect]], and is a [[General Skills|General Skill]].

It is used in rolls involving:
- Actions on a ship, such as steering
- Maintaining, repairing, or designing machinery, contraptions, or ships, such as cannons
- Understanding, disarming, using, setting, or designing primarily modern contraptions, including traps or industry
  
Specific instances that may call for a [[Operating]] [[Skill Checks|Check]] directly:
- Making a Template, such as in [[Item Creation]]
- Various actions, such as repairing or steering, in [[Ship Combat, and Vehicles]]
