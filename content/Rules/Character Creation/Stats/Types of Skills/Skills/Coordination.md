This [[Skills|Skill]] measures a [[Character|Character]]'s nimbleness and swiftness. While it overlaps with [[Athletics|Athletics]], [[Coordination|Coordination]] focuses more on quick reaction times rather than strength. It is tied to [[Agility|Agility]].

It is used in rolls regarding:
- Reaching handholds, climbing, or crawling through tight spaces
- Balancing on an unstable or narrow surface
- Escaping restraints, or reacting to situations fast, like dodging a trap

Specific instances that may call for a [[Coordination]] [[Skill Checks|Check]] directly:
- Mitigating [[Fall Damage]]
- Escaping [[Immobilized]] [[Status Effects|Status Effect]]
- Grabbing an [[Characters Thrown off Edges|edge when tossed off]]
- Putting out [[Burn (Active) (Quality)|Burn]]
- Mitigating some [[Hazards]]
