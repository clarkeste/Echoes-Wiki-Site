This is the [[Spell Creation|Magic]] [[Skills|Skill]] of the [[Echoes of the Departed|Echoes of the Departed]] game. It is extremely valuable, and not all [[PC|PCs]] have access to it; but for those who do, it allows them to cast [[Spell Creation|Spells]]. It is tied to [[Willpower|Willpower]], and does not exist in any Skill category.

To buy any ranks in the Force Skill, it must be a [[Career Skills|Career Skill]]. Additionally, Force cannot be increased past Rank 2 unless your character has some sort of mentor. Whether that mentor is a person or an object, such as a large trove of books, is up to the GM and the Player to decide.


### Narrative Symbols
Since Force is used for a variety of different [[Spell Creation|Spells]], it makes little sense to list out a table of potential options, as the range of [[Skill Checks|Checks]] it can be used on are simply too large.

However, for Failed Checks, I’d like to provide some advice. Try to consider what your Character was hoping to achieve using the Spell, and consider how, despite failing, they might learn information relevant to that goal, or make a small step towards achieving it without the Spell. Or trigger a small portion of the Spell. A good standby, with 2 [[Advantage]], would be to learn what the Spell would’ve done if it succeeded, if you’re testing new combinations.
