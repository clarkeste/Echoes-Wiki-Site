This [[Skills|Skill]] is a [[Character]]'s ability to remain steadfast when faced with a surprising force, or resist the influence of cowardly instincts. It is tied to [[Willpower]], and is a [[General Skills|General Skill]].

It is used in rolls regarding:
- Mastering doubts, temptations, and fears, maintaining focus and mental fortitude
- Resisting some [[Spell Creation|Spells]] or external affects
- Resisting the effects of [[Coercion|Coercion]] and [[Leadership|Leadership]] (and [[Charm|Charm]] attempts that appeal to baser instincts, such as fear of the unknown)

Specific instances that may call for a [[Discipline]] [[Skill Checks|Check]] directly:
- Opposing or breaking out of [[Spell Creation|Spell]] effects targeting you, such as resisting [[Endow]] or breaking out of [[Manipulate]]
- Healing [[Strain]] at the end of an [[Encounter]]
- Resist the effects of some [[Hazards]] like [[Gas]]
