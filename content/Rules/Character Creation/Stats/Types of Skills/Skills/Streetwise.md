This [[Skills|Skill]] is representative of a [[Character|Character]]'s knowledge and understanding of more informal urban areas or villages, including how to navigate them.

It is used in rolls regarding:
- Navigating through urban environments or villages, particularly illicit or informal ones 
- Knowledge and subtle clues of the criminal underworld

- Networking; selling or buying illegal or unsavory [[Echoes of the Departed/Wiki Stuff/Game Wiki/Rules/Items/Items]]
