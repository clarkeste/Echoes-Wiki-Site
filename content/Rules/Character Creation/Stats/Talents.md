Talents are special abilities or powers that your [[Character|Character]] has. These can range massively, from the ability to block [[Damage|Damage]] to a blow, to removing [[Setback Dice|Setbacks]] from [[Skill Checks|Checks]] of a particular [[Skills|Skill]], to powerful abilities that let you deal additional Damage in [[Combat|Combat]], or execute particularly daring tactics.

Talents function on a tier system, with each tier costing 5 more [[Experience Points|Experience Points]] than the previous. Tier 1 Talents cost 5 Experience, Tier 2 Talents cost 10, Tier 3 cost 15, Tier 4 cost 20, with the maximum tier being Tier 5, which cost 25. You must have more Talents of the next lowest Tier to purchase a Talent on the next tier. For example, if you have five Tier 1 Talents, you may only have four Tier 2 Talents.

Additionally, there are some Ranked Talents. For example, the [[Parry]] Talent, which is Tier 1. If you purchase Parry, you can then purchase an additional rank. However, this additional rank is Tier 2. Likewise, purchasing a third rank would count as a Tier 3 Talent.

Here is a is the navigational [[Talent Navigation Guide]]. Echoes of the Departed has 120 regular Talents, and 32 Magic Talents.

### Magic Talents
Magic Talents act as regular Talents in every day, and count as Talents on the ‘pyramid’. The core difference, however, is that only [[Character|Characters]] with the [[Force]] [[Career Skills|Career Skill]] can purchase or benefit from them.

## Adding Talents to a Character Sheet
To add a Talent to your [[Character Sheet]] on [[RPGSessions]], you must first set up the [[RPGSessions Data Library|Data Library]]. Follow the steps listed in that article to import the [[Echoes of the Departed]] kit and then assign it to your [[Character]].

Once you’ve done that, open your Character sheet, either using the [[Game Table]] or in the Library.

Once open, scroll down to just below the [[Weapons]] and [[Critical Injury]] section, to where it says “Talents”. Just beneath that, hit the “Add New Talent Group” button, *only if* a Talent Group does not already exist (PC templates should come with a Talent Group already present, usually just named “Talents”). In the resulting menu, select the “Pyramid” option.

Once you have added it, or if it was already present, click on it in the list to extend it open.

![](https://i.imgur.com/IZEuSSn.png)

This will show the Talent “pyramid”, which is a visualization of the Tier system we mentioned previously. Hit the pencil icon on the top-right of the Talent pyramid to begin editing the Talent pyramid.

This will let you change the name, if you wish. However, it will also let you add Talents or edit existing ones. Right now, we have no Talents, just an empty slot. However your mouse over the empty slot and then click on it.

This will open the [[RPGSessions Data Library|Data Library]] menu. From there, find the Talent you want to purchase. You can do this by searching for it with the top-left menu bar and then hitting “Apply Filters”, or by applying criteria with the left-side controls. You can filter by Tier, Activation type, etc. Just make sure you hit “Apply Filters” after modifying your search criteria!

Here, I’ve decided I want to add the [[Durable]] Talent. It a Tier 1 Talent, and this is a Tier 1 Talent slot, since it is the first Talent I am buying for this Character. Hit “Purchase”. The other option, “Add”, can be used to add a Talent that is currently inactivated due to being unpurchased, appearing greyed-out on your sheet. Useful if you’re trying to plan ahead.

![](https://i.imgur.com/qdN7QuU.png)

After hitting “Purchase”, this menu will appear. It allows you to add a % modifier on how much XP the Talent costs, or allows you to add a custom cost. This cost will then automatically be deducted from your Character’s [[Experience Points|XP]].

However, Talents in [[Echoes of the Departed]] have set costs the [[RPGSessions]] Data Library usually automatically updates the XP cost whenever it should, meaning that you should basically always simply go with the default option, and hit “Purchase”. Buying a Talent in a Tier 1 slot is only 5XP, so [[Durable]] is only costing us 5XP to purchase.

![](https://i.imgur.com/eOWtqGE.png)

After you’ve done that, congratulations! You’ve added your first Talent, a Tier 1. Let me add another Tier 1 Talent, “[[Brace]]”, as an example.

You may see after adding 1 or 2 Talents, the Talent pyramid graphic will update, showing more slots. Below, each row of Talent slots is listed as Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3, etc. 

![](https://i.imgur.com/MUO9016.png)

Remember that as a pyramid—a strange, horizontal pyramid—Talents in Echoes of the Departed operate in a certain way. You *must* have more Tier 1 Talents than Tier 2 Talents, for instance. Consider it as a pyramid with 5-layers; each layer must be slightly larger than the one beneath it. If you want to increase a higher layer, you need to increase all the layers beneath it as well, unless you already had space to spare. The full explanation of this is at the beginning of the article.

Regardless, all this means is that RPGSessions easily visualizes this for you. In the above image, you can see I have two Tier 1 Talents; this means I can add a single Tier 2 Talent, in the next column.

Make sure to never accidentally add a Tier 3 Talent, for example, in a Tier 1 or Tier 2 slot, as it doesn’t belong there. Likewise, unless you’re purchasing another Rank of a Ranked Talent, make sure you don’t put a Tier 1 in a Tier 5 slot, or any slot higher than it ought to be.

That is everything you need to know about adding Talents to RPGSessions! If you encountered errors, such as seeing Talents which don’t exist in Echoes of the Departed, it is likely that this is because you forgot to assign the Echoes of the Departed kit to your Character Sheet, as instructed in the [[RPGSessions Data Library|Data Library]] article.
