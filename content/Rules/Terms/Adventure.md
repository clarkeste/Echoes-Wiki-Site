Adventures, or what is usually called “sessions” in the [[Genesys Core Rulebook]] and other supplementary [[Genesys]] material, is a measure of out-of-game time in [[Echoes of the Departed]].

It measures a ‘Storyline’ or ‘Episode’ that takes place in the game; essentially, an Adventure with a beginning, middle, and finish. The finish does not necessarily end a plot-line or leave a [[PC|PC]] relaxed and happy, but is still a break in the overall Story, denoting a section of it has come to an end.

Many abilities or [[Talents|Talents]] have effects that can only be activated once per Adventure. Additional meta-measurements of time are [[Encounter|Encounters]], which refers to an individual scene with a challenge in it. Additionally, for in-world time, there is [[Downtime]] and the [[Hylian Calendar]]. It should be noted that meta out-of-game Time is rarely going to align with in-game Time. An Adventure could be up to a month or more, or just a few days or hours.
