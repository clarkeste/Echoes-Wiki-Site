Labrynna is a nation to the east of both Hyrule and Holodrum, separated by the latter country by a strait. Despite beiong a similar size to the two aforementioned countries, it has often lagged behind Hyrule and Holodrum in prosperity, influence, and domestic development.

Labrynna has historical been a monarchy, with constitutional elements added on after various successful protests and revolts. While its reasons for never being successful are likely varied, many scholars disagree on the exact reasons. Some say it was their consistent concessions to popular will, while others say it was a matter of geography. Regardless, while usually being considered the third most influential country in the world, it greatly lagged behind the top two, and was only slightly ahead those below it. This, in turn, often fed even more discontent among the people.

Three years ago, Labrynna attempted to throw their hat into the ring when it came to gaining influence over Koridai, an island that serves as the hub for all International trade. They established a full embassy in Koridai, filled with the very best diplomats. It took only months for vigilantes among Koridian security forces to murder diplomats, staff, and politicians that both served at the embassy and were visiting. Labrynna immediately demanded that Koridai extradited those responsible, but the island-nation refused not only to extradite them, but did not press any charges against them. Tensions escalated drastically in the months after, as it became clear that Koridai was intending on dropping the issue entirely.

With both the leaders and people of Labrynna wanting justice to be done--and aiming to avoid appearing weak on the international stage by simply allowing over a dozen murders to happen--Labrynna blockaded Koridai using their navy. The island quickly retaliated, and so began the Koridai War. Hyrule and Holodrum, two countries courting Koridai's favor, immediately joined the war on the side of Koridai.

It took less than a week for the Labrynnan navy around Koridai to be destroyed and routed, with all attempts to land at the island turning into massacres for the Labrynnan forces. Both the navies of Hyrule and Holodrum continued to pummel Labrynna's ships, trade, and coastline--eventually landing on the western coast and capturing the capital, Lynna City.

In a humiliating defeat, the Labrynnan government and monarchy capitulated, and accepted a conditional surrender. Discontent grew and grew, as the people of Labrynna felt even more pathetic on the world stage, and that the current government could not be trusted not just to maintain any degree respect, but could not even provide for its people, or provide justice for its citizens who were murdered overseas. Many began to agree that Labrynna's attempts at domination were an attempt to distract the populace from the numerous domestic issues.

The Revolution in Hyrule occurred only a few months after the Labrynnan defeat. It was the final inspiration needed for there to be a revolution in Labrynna. Weeks later, after arranging enough support, rebels within Labrynna staged their own revolution, and began the Labrynnan Civil War. 

The Civil War rages to this day. Despite the neighboring Holodrumians supporting the Labrynnan royalists, the Rebels are clearly the side with the upper-hand--hatred for the old government running strong among the populace, giving the Rebels popular support. There are rumors that the Queen of Labrynna is not even any longer in the country, and has fled to Holodrum for safety.
