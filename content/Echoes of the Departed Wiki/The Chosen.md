The Chosen were the leaders of the Followers of Dragmire. The group was led by the Pragmatist, who themselves was a Chosen. While there have been various past individuals who were Chosen who bore the title Sorcerer or Believer, there is--as far as we know--only one Pragmatist, who took control of the Followers about thirty years ago.

The Chosen consisted of four individuals: The Believer, the Sorcerer, the Confessor, and the Pragmatist.

Each Chosen led their own branch of the cult, except for the Pragmatist, who organized all branches together.
