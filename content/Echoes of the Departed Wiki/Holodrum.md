The Empire of Holodrum, colloquially known as just Holodrum, is one of the most powerful nations in the world. Located to the east, across the ocean from Hyrule, it has historically been the world's most influential country, trading the spot with Hyrule depending on the circumstances of any given time.

Prior to the Revolution, Holodrum and Hyrule were rivals for influence, however this rivalry never broke out into armed conflict. They competed for influence in various nations and islands, and clashed most recently in Koridai. However, both united to support Koridai militarily when Labrynna attempted to invade the country after the island-nation executed Labrynnan diplomats and visiting politicians.

It is usually thought that having such a troop presence in Koridai is one of the reasons why, during the Revolution, there was a lack of troops to stop the Followers. Indeed, after the Revolution split Hyrule in half, Holodrum became the uncontested champion on the world stage. They have exerted influence over countries prior firmly in the Hyrulean sphere of influence, such as Hytopia and Calatia.

They have supported the Royalists in the Second Hyrulean Civil War, as well as the Royalists in the Civil War in Hytopia, hoping to gain governments favorable to them. In Hytopia, a large reason for the victories--and predicted total wipeout of the Rebels--is attributed to not just the support of Holodrum in the form of munitions, but direct military intervention on the behalf of the Royalists, leaving the soon-to-be undisputed government of Hytopia in their debt. 

Despite not doing anything quite so bold in Hyrule yet, they have consistently sold weapons, equipment, and warships to the Royalist faction at extremely low prices--some even gifted, or leased without immediate charge. They have collaborated with Calatia in delivering these munitions and maintaining ships at the Royalist coast of Hyrule, often-times escorting Royalist ships to dissuade Follower raiders. There are even rumors of Holodrum hiring privateers to disrupt trade and sink ships delivering supplies in and out of Kakariko City port. 

The young Emperor of Holodrum likely seeks not just ideological consistency in the factions he supports, but to build governments that will be indebted and under heavy influence of the Empire of Holodrum.
