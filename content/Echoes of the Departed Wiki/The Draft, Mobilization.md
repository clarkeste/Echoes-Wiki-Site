Colloquially, the draft refers to a decree issued by the King days before his death and the Revolution, in which he ordered the manpower of the Hyrulean military to be increased threefold to maintain order in Hyrule, and authorized the use of conscription and drafting to achieve this end. While tens of thousands were levied to this end, the goal was not met before the capital was overwhelmed.

Under the Royalist forces, a continuation of the old government, this decree has remained in effect. While the leader of the Royalist military, General Noril, has not attempted to reach the number, he has used random lotteries and other such conscription tactics to fill the gaps in the army. However, both sides of the military attempt to primarily recruit volunteers into the military, with promises of various benefits.
