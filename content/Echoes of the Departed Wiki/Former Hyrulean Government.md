How long the Royal Family and previously sole Hyrulean government reigned is unknown; however, most historians agree that it began long before the first Hyrulean Civil War, putting it at hundreds of years. However, despite its ideals being succeeded by the Royalist faction, the Royal Family, its council, and other institutions prior to the Revolution, are no longer surviving or in power.

The Royal Family is, in legend, thought to be of divine lineage. Their wisdom is said to be one of the reasons for Hyrule's success. Additionally, a piece of the Golden Power--the Triforce of Wisdom--has been carried down their ancestry, manifesting itself in the female members of the Royal Family. Zelda, supposedly the name of the first Princess of Hyrule, is also a name given to many women in the family.

The Royal Family has always been the final authority in Hyrule, except for when they have been deposed--such as Dragmire's seven year reign, or the current day. Therefore, the consequences of Hyrule's policies have often been laid at their feet. Among these was a policy that is still criticized to this day; the expulsion of the Gerudo from Hyrule and their desert, and the Hylian colonization of the area. Following the Hero of the Flood's victory over Ganondorf Dragmire, the Kingdom was devastated; its King and Princess both died at the hands of Ganondorf Dragmire. The Princess' brother took the throne and sought revenge, as did most of Hyrule.

Seeking to prevent another Dragmire from ever existing, the newly-crowned King invaded the Gerudo Desert and razed their structures, forcing the Gerudo to flee deeper into the Desert. Their forces were pursued by Hylians, eventually leading to a climatic battle at the Dunes of the Dead. Since then, the land the Gerudo once lived in has been settled by Hyruleans, creating Calditin City, and the Gerudo have been cut off from most of the world.


In the three centuries since, more and more criticism for the Royal Family has emerged. While it was mostly tolerated by those in power, certain organizations had, in the eyes of the Royal Family, gone too far and merited police action by the King and his ministers, known as the council. However, the actions of the Royal government to maintain order was clearly not enough, as just one year ago, the King was overthrown and Hyrule Castle. Just as when Dragmire attacked three hundred years ago, the Revolution killed both the King and the Princess, Zelda.

Currently, the most direct heir to the throne is the King's son, who lives with his mother in Holodrum. It is thought by gossip-mongers that the King divorced his wife because he believed it was possible that his son was conceived with another individual, but there is no evidence that this is the case. General Noril and the Royalist faction in the civil war has announced its intentions to invite the King's son to the country as soon as it is safe.
