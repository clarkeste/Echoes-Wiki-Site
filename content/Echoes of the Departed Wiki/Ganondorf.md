Due to the Gerudo's isolation from the rest of the world, little is known about the boy who would eventually become the Gerudo Chief and travel to Hyrule to die nobly for our monarchy, spurning his name-sake. However, from what was told to us by this Ganondorf and our knowledge of their past customs, we can paint a reasonably accurate picture of Ganondorf's early life. The life of an individual whose long-dead namesake, the Demon King Dragmire, hung over him like a shadow.

Males among the Gerudo are extremely rare. Therefore, Ganondorf's birth was likely an important event, and he was decided to be their ruler from birth. Taken away from his biological parents--who likely were loyalists to the Demon King and named their son after him--he was raised in the Royal Palace, along with an adopted older sister. Ganondorf was educated in the history of Hyrule, the Gerudo people, and the actions of his namesake. Eventually, at the end of his teenage years, he and his sister were captured by the Followers of Dragmire. Attempting to complete a dark ritual to revive their Dark Lord, they spilled the blood of Ganondorf's sister. This caused the Triforce of Power to manifest itself within Ganondorf, ending the lives of the Followers who had murdered his sister.

Sometime later, Ganondorf found a device he called the Timekeeper deep in the desert. Using it, he saw the past and hundreds of possible futures. He resolved to control his anger and direct it towards two things: Eliminating the Followers of Dragmire, who had left a permeant scar on his life, and recover Triforce so that he may end a dark curse upon his life and Hyrule Kingdom, revealed to him by the Timekeeper.

In his mid-twenties, aged by the harsh sun of the desert, Ganondorf traveled to the Kingdom of Hyrule to petition aid in his quest. He arrived in Castle Town during the Festival of the Flood, causing turmoil at his appearance. During his visit to Hyrule Castle, the Followers took his appearance as sign from their Dark Lord--the previous man named Ganondorf--and launched a raid on the Castle. Among the many loses suffered during this raid, one of them was nearly the King of Hyrule; the Sorcerer, a leader of the Followers, was ready to launch the finishing strike before Ganondorf protected the King, and killed the Sorcerer.

After gaining the King's trust, the royal leader of Hyrule agreed to help him find the shards of the Triforce spread across the land, and access to the Triforce of Wisdom when--or if--it manifested in his daughter. Alongside Link Sahon, Impa, and Astor--despite not having the trust of the last two--Ganondorf traveled to Kakariko City. While the others recovered the piece, Ganondorf caught sight of a Follower and pursued him back to a base at Lon Lon Ranch. After a battle with the Confessor and an individual later revealed to be Link Steadfast, the Gerudo King burned down the ranch and trapped the Followers inside, for the authorities to arrest.

 Later, after reconvening with his teammates and Princess Zelda, Ganondorf took the wary gang to Zora's Domain to recover an additional piece. Due to reverse-psychology on behalf of intervening Followers, Ganondorf was led to believe that pulling the Triforce Shard in the Temple underneath the Domain would have no effect; unfortunately, it did, collapsing a large part of the land that Zora's Domain rested upon above. Later, Ganondorf and the team traveled to the Lost Woods, where Ganondorf fought the Confessor once more and recovered a shard. While it is hard to know, sources inside the Castle at the time reported that Astor and the other members of the team had sought the Master Sword inside the woods, much to the frustration of Ganondorf. To recover the second-to-last shard, Ganondorf went deep into the Desert with a sage medallion he recovered from the Lost Woods.

When Ganondorf returned, Hyrule was in chaos. Bystanders report he rode into the Follower army on horseback and single-handedly broke their attack, killing dozens of Followers in a single attack and defeating their general, the Believer. Once he had satiated his need to kill the rebels and his horse was injured, Ganondorf hurried into Castle Town and ran for Hyrule Castle. Despite being interrupted by the so-called Hero, Link Steadfast, Ganondorf pushed him aside and arrived at the Castle.

Once inside, he did not find whatever it was he sought. Instead, the Confessor and Steadfast attacked him. In a climatic battle inside the Castle as it was shelled, Link Steadfast ran his sword through the chest of the Gerudo, and killed him.

The story of Ganondorf is a tragic one. Marred since birth by bearing the name of a tyrant, he did everything he could to save the Kingdom which his namesake tried to destroy. Unfortunately, that attempt to repent cost him his life.
