Kakariko City is the biggest, most diverse city in Hyrule, judged by land that is considered a part of the city zone. It is a close second in terms of population, with Castle Town barely beating it out by most estimates.

Kakariko City is in the south of Hyrule, and is the country's biggest, and most important port, handling the vast majority of international commercial activity in the country. It was founded shortly after the time of the Hero of Time, named after Kakariko Village--an old Sheikah settlement near Death Mountain--by its founders, that contained many Sheikah among them. It soon began to flourish due to its proximity to its coast, thanks to the new concept of intercontinental trade. By the time of the Hero of the Flood, it was among the ten largest cities in Hyrule. In the centuries since, it has grown to be second only to Castle Town. Due to its initial founding by Sheikah, it has been considered an Autonomous Zone, like Goron City or Zora's Domain, albeit with less privileges than the latter two.

Kakariko City retains a significant Sheikah population--often attracting Sheikah with more liberal sentiments, as opposed to the traditional conservatism of smaller Sheikah villages in the north. The majority of individuals are still Hylians, with a Goron and Zora minority. Kakariko City is often stereotyped as a place of business and hard-work. Kakariko City has historically been considered the most willing to accept anti-Royalist ideals, and was the starting place of the Revolution. It has a wide variety of individuals, but tends to lean towards those wanting political change and improved worker's rights. Kakariko City was the first city to decide to host elections for its governor and city government.

Kakariko City, years prior to the Revolution, had its beloved governor--Arandin--forcefully removed by the King, and replaced with one loyal to the King. This was officially due to security threats caused by Arandin publicly associating with dissident groups. This fostered a dislike among the population, both for the King and the new Governor, Kakan Rarurn. Later, days prior to the Revolution, the Governor closed off the port to prevent escaped Followers from escaping the country under the orders of the King and Nophandus.

Kakariko City was where Link Steadfast and the Confessor where during their infamous 'Broadcast' that revealed that Steadfast held the Master Sword. As a result, the people of Kakariko rose up against the Royalist government, the city falling into the possession of the Followers. Using Kakariko City as a staging ground, they invaded Castle Town and killed the King.


Today, Kakariko City is still under the control of the Revolutionaries. Its former beloved governor, Arandin, has returned to politics as Governor. Kakariko City is generally supportive of the Followers, but there is still much doubt--in a city so diverse, the opinions, too, are diverse. Some believe they have gone too far by killing members of the Royal Family, while others wish they had gone further; more, still, simply blame them for the ill-effects of the Civil War on their livelihood.
