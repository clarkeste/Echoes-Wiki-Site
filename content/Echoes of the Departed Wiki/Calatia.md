Calatia is a minor power to the west of Hyrule. It has always been an ally of Hyrule; its loyalty has always been assured by Hyrule, both diplomatically and militarily, due to Calatia's strategic position. The country of Calatia controls various naturally-formed mountain passages that, if in the wrong hands, could be used to launch a swift and likely successful invasion of the Hyrulean mainland.

Calatia has its own monarchy, and so, was appalled at the actions of the Revolutionaries in Hyrule, and the death of the Royal Family. Exercising their control, Calatia suppressed any revolutionary sentiment in their own country that may have been arose from the actions of the Followers in Hyrule. They have supported the Royalists in the Hyrulean Civil War, but without the power or influence Hyrule once wielded, they are no longer bound to be such strong allies with Hyrule. Instead, Holodrum has been filling the roles that Hyrule once did, with Holodrum and Calatia now forging a strong partnership. Both countries have collaborated with one another to support the Royalists in both the Hyrulean and Hytopian Civil Wars.

There are some concerns that Calatia may use the mountain passages it controls to invade the Follower-controlled parts of Hyrule, entering the war on the side of the Royalists. However, that has not yet occurred--the Calatian government and monarchy may be concerned that such a direct intervention will herald the return of revolutionary sentiment in their own country.
