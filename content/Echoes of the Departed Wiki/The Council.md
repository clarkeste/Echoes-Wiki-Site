The Council was a group of the King's highest-ranking subordinates that existed during the reign of the monarchy in Hyrule. It consisted of five people: the King himself, the Minister of Justice, the Lord Chancellor, the Minister of Hylia, and the Minister of the Interior. Sometimes, the second-in-command of these various members were involved in council meetings, but their opinion was not held with as much weight.

While the Kings and Queens of Hyrule most often used the Council as an advisory body and to coordinate the various branches of government, it was not uncommon for actions or orders seen as contentious or controversial among the people to be voted on by members of the Council.

In the last decade of the proper Hyrule Kingdom, here were the members of the Council, and each role's purpose

Lord Chancellor - the individual ultimately in charge of economics, supply, labor-relations, guild-relations, and the market. This position was held by Maren Auranli, before being handed to the wizened Lan Potho, who retired and nominated his protege, Collin Phurn, as his replacement--whom the King quickly approved of. Collin died during the Revolution.

Minister of Hylia - Nophandus held this role for twelve years before his death during the Revolution.

Minister of Justice - Frendell held this role, before dying under strange circumstances. The role was given to the next-in-command, the loyal Inl Astor. Astor died during the Revolution.

Minister of the Interior - Katoke of Kakariko City held this role, until suspicions of her loyalty as a Sheikah became too great to ignore. Six years ago, the role was handed to Lakoh. He survived the Revolution by defecting and aiding the Revolutionaries. Lakoh now holds a higher position in the Revolutionary government.
