The Kokiri, also known as the Kokiri Tribe or the Children of the Forest, or Fairy Folk, or the Children of the Forest, are, according to myth, small child-like beings that inhabit the Lost Woods (or 'Korkiri Woods' as some call them when referring to the Kokiri'). There, legends state, they live among planted houses within the woods' acres and borders.

The Kokiri are supposedly an ageless race that takes the form of human children. Like Hylians, they possess long, pointed ears. It is said that one of the seven sages was a Kokiri, a girl named Saria. Despite their agelessness, Kokiri are still mortal beings that can be killed.

It is said--through some unknown process--the Great Deku Tree gave life to the Kokiri. The Deku Tree, their guardian, watches over their home in the Lost Woods. It is believed that the Hero of Time was raised as a Kokiri and grew up among them, before leaving them to save the Kingdom of Hyrule in its darkest hour. While there is large amounts of evidence to indicate that the Hero of Time lived with a forest-dwelling tribe, the Kokiri themselves and the characterization of a living tree can seem a little far-fetched. Despite its unconfirmed status, it has not been completely dismissed as false.
