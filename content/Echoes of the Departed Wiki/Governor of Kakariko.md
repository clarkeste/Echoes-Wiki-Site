The former Governor of Kakariko--real name Kakan Rarurn--is currently one of the two recognized leaders of the Royalist movement in Hyrule. He is described by those who know him as a mild-mannered politician, and was a part of the civil service for many years in Kakariko City. However, after the Governor before him, Arandin, got on the bad side of the King, the monarch chose to unilaterally appoint Rarurn to the position of Governor of Kakariko, where he remained for four years prior to the Revolution.

Rarurn's ascension to Governor was controversial, as Arandin was well-loved in Kakariko City. However, the King thought it necessary, as Arandin often resisted measures to increase security in the city, whereas the new Governor of Kakariko deferred and cooperated with the Royal government much more.

Less than a week prior to the Revolution, the Governor was visited by Nophandus, an agent of the King's, who ordered him to shut down Kakariko City's port due to the increased activity of Followers. Despite the economic issues, the Governor agreed, and also let soldiers be stationed in Kakariko City to maintain order in case of turmoil.

Such an order turned out to be fortuitous, but not nearly enough. Days later, during the Confessor's public announcement of Link Steadfast as the wielder of the Master Sword which heralded him as the successor of the Hero, the Revolution began. The forces in Kakariko City were overwhelmed, with many defecting. The Governor fled the city during this, quickly traveling to the capital on the orders of the King.

In the final day, the Governor was sent along with General Noril and an army to rebuff the Follower advance. However, instead he and Noril were routed, and forced back to Hyrule Castle. Despite being present in the Castle during the assault on it, the Governor and General Noril were among the lucky few who managed to escape the Followers during the assault.


Soon after, the Governor joined as the main political leader of the Royalists who continued to fight on. To this day, he acts as a negotiator to gain support from foreign nations, such as Holodrum and Calatia, and to keep the civilian government of the Royalists together. There has been some talk of who will succeed the King in the monarchy, and sources say that the Governor is pursuing the subject with the utmost scrutiny; however, a decision has not yet been reached.
