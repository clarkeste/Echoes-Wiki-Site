Autonomous Zones are areas in Hyrule that have been given more independence by the central government. This can be due to various reasons, but was often a concession in exchange for the given zone to willingly join Hyrule, or for giving minority groups more independence.

There are three autonomous zones in Hyrule: Kakariko City, Zora's Domain, and Goron City.

Kakariko City: Kakariko City was founded shortly after the time of the Hero of Time, named after Kakariko Village--an old Sheikah settlement near Death Mountain--by its founders, that contained many Sheikah among them. At the time not expecting Kakariko to grow into the massive city it is today, the King of the time granted the settlement autonomy. Whether this was due to an attempt to make sure that the Sheikah had a place where they would be safe from discrimination, or was an attempt at some type of segregation, is up to interpretation. As the city grew, it became politically unviable to revoke their autonomy clause, despite the fact that Kakariko City became more and more vital to the Hyrulean economy and trade.

The Last King decided to attempt to rectify this in recent times, by violating parts of the Clause to remove the Governor chosen by Kakariko City to install one who was willing to cooperate more with the crown. As a result of what many saw as the King overstepping his bounds, Kakariko City was the first to 'fall' to the Revolution, with widespread support at the time.

Zora's Domain: The Zora have long been an ally of Hyrule. After the Hyrulean Civil War, they agreed to join Hyrule proper, under the condition of autonomy, that allowed them to retain their own monarchy and government. Over time, the Zora government moved to a constitutional monarchy system, where representatives to their government were elected. These representatives, as well as the Zora Kings and Queens, slowly grew to disagree more and more with the choices of the Hyrulean Royal Family. The Royal Family never attempted to jeporadize the autonomy of the Zora, likely either due to respect for the aquatic people, or fear that it would turn into a costly war. In the years prior to the Revolution, the Zora began to use their autonomy to shelter dissidents, such as the Followers of Dragmire. They maintained their allegiance to the Revolutionaries up until the very end, when they joined the Revolution. Today, they are still partners with the Revolutionary government.

Goron City: This place is home to the Gorons, and is placed on Death Mountain. After the Hyrulean Civil War, the Gorons pledged allegiance to the Royal Family, in exchange for their own Autonomy Clause, just like the Zora. Since then, the Gorons have used a semi-democratic Council of Elders, with a younger leader who is usually called the Patriarch, Chief, or Boss depending on the preference of the leader.

Goron City never acted against the Royal Family, but were unable or unwilling to carry out many of the harsher measures that the Royal Family demanded of them to limit and capture dissenters, which put the two into conflict. During the Revolution, Goron City did not immediately provide aid to the Royal Family, and--after Castle Town fell--proclaimed their neutrality in the affair, wanting their home to remain peaceful. Therefore, Goron City is now largely independent, where no fighting is allowed.

Gerudo Town and the areas around it were also once considered an autonomous zone, following the seven year reign of Dragmire, but they were invaded and the area's autonomy revoked following the return of Dragmire, in the name of revenge.
