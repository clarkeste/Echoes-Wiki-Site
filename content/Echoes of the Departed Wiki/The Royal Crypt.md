While there are rumors of a special crypt created specifically for the Royal Family to bury their dead, as well as that of their most vital servants, it has never been confirmed and remains a fanciful legend. That said, documents declassified by the Followers featured several mentions of non-standard burials for the Royal Family, but never described details or the location of this supposed crypt.

Most recently, Princess Zelda—killed during the Revolution one year ago—was buried underneath the Sacred Grounds just south of Castle Town.
