According to Royal documents that were declassified following the Revolution, Ganondorf spoke of a Temple deep in the desert. He believed it to hold a Triforce Shard, but was unable to enter without a Sage Medallion.

After traveling with the Commission for the Reclamation of the Golden Power, Ganondorf recovered a Sage Medallion from inside the Lost Woods. How is unknown. After he gained it, he traveled to the Temple in the Desert. He presumably opened it with the Sage Medallion, for he returned to Hyrule days later with the Triforce Shard.

When Ganondorf returned, the Revolution had reached Castle Town. Unfortunately, the Gerudo died attempting to stop the Revolution.
