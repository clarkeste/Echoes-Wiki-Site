Sooga was a member of the Followers of Dragmire, holding the rank of Messenger, and is now a member of the new Revolutionary government. He was the right-hand man of the Believer, and is a Sheikah, therefore part of the Yiga movement. He uses electrical and lightening magic, and is agile despite his very large and muscular stature.
