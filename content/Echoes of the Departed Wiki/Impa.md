A Sheikah who was once the lead bodyguard for Princess Zelda. Her role in history would be minor, as she was later replaced by Link Sahon, if not for her returning to royal employ shortly before the revolution. She was a part of the Commission for the Recovery of the Golden Power, likely returned to service for her great prowess and cunning, despite the stigma against the Sheikah for their lack of loyalty.

Since she was a young girl to when she was 15, Impa served as Zelda's maid, bodyguard, and--according to many sources--her closest confidant. However, only days after the Kingdom had been shaken by the skirmish between Parliamentarians and the authorities in the capital, Impa was removed from active duty along with many other Sheikah. While the King never publicly commented on why, it is widely assumed that it was due to fears over the secretive and shadowy nature, or at least reputation, that caused distrust in the Sheikah.

Impa was brought back into the fold when Zelda captured, just over a week before the revolution. This was at the request of Sahon, who had served under her. Despite her apprehension at working with Ganondorf and frustration with Astor, she joined the Commission and journeyed to Kakariko City with the rest of the team. Once there, she guided them into the catacombs. Leading them through various trials and difficulties, she was able to assist them in finding the Triforce shard that they had set out to find. Later, on the path to Zora's Domain, Impa and Zelda had a reunion, before quickly continuing into the underwater temple near Zora's Domain, and all the way to the Lost Woods, where she met and attempted to defeat the Confessor, but was unable to. She logged a report when she returned to Hyrule Castle the next day.

When at the capital, her and Zelda were finally able to catch up. Whether it was a happy, bittersweet, or tragic reunion we do not know. However, it seems it would be among the last time the two of them would enjoy together. Impa was soon ordered by the King to apprehend the Confessor and retrieve the Triforce Shard at Death Mountain; within hours, she was successful. Upon returning to Hyrule Castle with the Confessor as her captive, little is known about what Impa did as the revolutionaries approached and invaded Castle Town. However, the most likely explanation is that she fought amongst Royalist Forces before retreating when the battle was lost. Most sources would indicate that she survived, but her exact whereabouts are unknown.
