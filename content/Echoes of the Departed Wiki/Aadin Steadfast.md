Aadin Steadfast was a blacksmith who lived in Castle Town, and the father of Link Steadfast. His wife died in complications during childbirth, leading him to raise his son alone. Those who knew him say he was a soft-spoken, apologetic, but kind man who deeply believed in certain principles. If those principles led his son to join the Castle Town Parliamentarians, we cannot say.

Aadin was something of an 'underrated' blacksmith. Not large enough to be noticed and put into the employ of the government, he served independent contracts, such as creating gleaming weapons for nobility, or designing practical blueprints for blade manufacturing companies. At some point when he visited with a noble client, his young boy--Link Steadfast--met a wandering Princess Zelda. The two got along excellently, with Aadin reportedly not even realizing the girl was the Princess until the very end. However, a friendship had been made, and the two young children met many times--until Zelda's scheduling and allowed areas came under heavier restrictions for for her safety.

Later, when both children were approximately fourteen, Zelda appeared at Aadin's workshop. She and the blacksmith's son hit it off yet again, even when Zelda learned that Steadfast had joined the Castle Town Parliamentarians.

However, things went so very wrong when the Castle Town Parliamentarians went out of line, resulting in arrests of their members and the burning of the building they met in. Aadin's son was forced to flee to avoid arrest and imprisonment. Neighbors reported that Steadfast visited his father many times, but only for short periods of time, before leaving. 

Aadin was made a pariah in society and economics by the King's office, following his own link to the Parliamentarians, through his son. Despite Princess Zelda's attempts to support Aadin financially and her visits with him, the elder Steadfast was a man with no work, and his son a criminal on the run. Four years later, he contracted an illness and died peacefully.
