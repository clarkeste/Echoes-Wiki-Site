The Flood was an event three hundred years ago that refers to a torrential downpour from the heavens, coinciding with Dragmire's return from banishment, that threatened to bury the Kingdom of Hyrule and the other countries of the continent under the waves of the ocean.

What caused the Flood is unknown--however, it is usually attributed to the actions of Ganon Dragmire. It began shortly before Dragmire and his minions attacked Castle Town and kidnapped the Zelda of the era, and King Daphnes. It showed no signs of stopping, and was seemingly supernatural in nature--as no storm since or before has been recorded to be able to maintain such a large amount of rain for such a period of time. It is estimated that the world was covered in perpetual rain for over a week.

Finally, when the Hero of the Flood found Ganon in the Forbidden Fortress and slayed the Demon King, the rains soon began to clear. Unfortunately, it was not without a cost: both Dragmire's rampage and the Flood had destroyed so many settlements, and killed so many. Among these casualties were Daphnes and the Zelda of the era, who both died while in Dragmire's Fortress.
