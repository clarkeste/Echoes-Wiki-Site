The Great Deku Tree is, according to legend, the guardian of the Lost Woods. He is a large tree that can speak and watches over the forest, and protects the Kokiri who live inside the forest. It is said that he guided the Hero of Time during the young Hero's childhood and adolescence, and that the Deku Tree was incredibly wise.

It is unknown if there is any grain of truth to this story not. Due to the Lost Woods' puzzling nature, it is almost impossible to properly navigate and map out, meaning that technically there could be a large tree within the woods that had the same abilities as the Deku Tree.
