The "Timekeeper" was a mysterious device used by the Ganondorf of the modern day, of which little is known. It is believed that Ganondorf found the Timekeeper in an ancient structure in the desert, and that it allowed him to see the past and various possible futures. It is believed that Ganondorf used this to live and watch through the lives of great warriors--perhaps his past lives--thereby learning their skills.


When Ganondorf traveled to Hyrule and allied with the King, and joined the Commission for the Reclamation of the Golden Power, Ganondorf used the Timekeeper. Using the device, Ganondorf was able to locate where the shards of the Triforce of Courage might be. This formed the basis of the locations that the Commission visited, such as Kakariko City, Zora's Domain, and the Lost Woods.

What happened to the Timekeeper following Ganondorf's death is unknown.
