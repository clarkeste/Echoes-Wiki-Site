Lanayru Citadel is the current headquarters of the Royalist faction. It exists atop one of the largest mountains Hyrule, the snowy eastern peak of Mount Lanayru. Its height and impressive construction makes it an almost impenetrable fort. It previously acted as a prison for the most dangerous criminals, but was repurposed shortly after the Revolution.

Years before the revolution, when the Castle Town Parliamentarians were arrested, their leaders were sent here as a message. As expected, many of them died inside of the prison. Closer to the Revolution, a Follower by the name of Sooga was sent to Lanayru Citadel following his capture. He and many other dissident prisoners were liberated by Link Steadfast, the Confessor, and the Believer during a raid on Lanayru Citadel.

Two months after the Revolution, Lanayru Citadel's role was changed on the behest of General Noril, the acting leader of the Royalist forces. Prisoners formerly kept there were loaded onto Hyrulean ships and taken to Koridai, while the Royalists transformed Lanayru Citadel into a base of operations, from which to manage the war. It remains in this role to this day, with General Noril acting as the master of the fort, along with its former Warden. It is near King's Port, which is the political capital of the Royalists.

It is considered by almost all military analysts to be a structure that is essentially impossible to conquer. It is reported to have three years worth of stockpiled food and supply, and Mount Lanayru's proximity to the coasts means that it could easily be resupplied in full even if a siege were disrupted for only a few hours.
