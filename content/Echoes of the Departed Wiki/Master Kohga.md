The Believer, also known as Kohga of Kakariko Village, or Master Kohga, is a member of the Followers of Dragmire and the new Revolutionary government.

The history of Master Kohga can be traced back to when he was born in Kakariko City. As was not uncommon for middle-class Sheikah, he was enrolled into various programs to learn Sheikah history and culture. He excelled in many areas, including both physical disciplines and the liberal arts, for which he became a professor of. As he continued his career as a professor, he became a significant figure within the Kakariko City Sheikah community, and Kohga hoped to help the Sheikah escape from the stigmas and 'otherness' they were subjected to in much of Hyrule. However, his hopes were dashed when his life took a turn for the worse due to increased anti-Sheikah sentiment. Various conspiracies, usually related to the Sheikah's stereotypically aloof and shadowy nature, indicted Kohga and his people in a conspiracy that included the Royal monarchy. The monarchy's response, however, was to recuse responsibility and thereby fan the flames against the Sheikah, re-directing any ill-will from themselves.

It was during this that Kohga became disillusioned with the Royal Family, for using the Sheikah as a scapegoat. His position as a professor--or Master, as was the term used in universities at the time--was lost due to this increase in stigma against the Sheikah religious minority. It was during this time that Kohga became more distant from society, and no longer attempted to continue his professional career. It is suspected that it was around this time that Kohga was radicalized and joined the Followers of Dragmire. 

Eventually, likely due to his knowledge of Sheikah mystic arts and combat techniques, Kohga was promoted to a Chosen--and took the name of the Believer. While there is no evidence he was as dedicated as some of his colleagues, he seemed to believe in Dragmire as, at least, a symbol of resistance change in the face of the Royal Family.

As a Chosen, he was given his own branch of the Followers to control, along with the Sorcerer. The Believer's branch is considered to have been significantly more lax than that of the Sorcerer's, with members rarely being punished for smaller infractions. While this led to the Believer's branch being less effective in missions than that of the Sorcerer's, it also maintained a larger, constantly growing membership, when the Sorcerer's branch often stagnated. Additionally, disgruntled Sheikah were almost always directed to the Believer's branch. The Confessor and the Believer, however, got along much better with each other than either did with the Sorcerer, and the two branches worked together much more often.

It was about this time that it is thought that the Believer created the Ahoga persona. Ahoga was an alias made by the Believer that acted as an entirely new identity, without any connections to the real Kohga; this alias joined the Hyrulean Military and quickly rose through the ranks due to the Believer's own knowledge and expertise in the subject, becoming a General shortly before the final Festival of the Flood.

The Believer was present the raid on Hyrule Castle that happened during the Festival, weeks before the Revolution. While he faced Ganondorf to try and convince the Gerudo to join them, he fled before battling him, escaping with the Princess as his captive. His ally who stayed behind--the Sorcerer--was not so lucky, seemingly dying in a battle with Ganondorf. Members of the Followers report that the Believer was really not that broken up, as he did not enjoy the company of the Sorcerer.

The Believer was present later at Lon Lon Ranch, but escaped, later launching a mission inside of Castle Town to rescue as many of his captured comrades as he could. He was participated, during the day of the Revolution, in a prison break at Lanayru Citadel along with the Confessor, and was there when Link Steadfast was announced to be the Hero. He later led the Revolutionary army during their march to Castle Town. When their army was devastated by Ganondorf, the Believer was heavily injured and taken out of the battle; but despite his injuries, the Followers pulled through and won the battle, with Link Steadfast killing Ganondorf.

Now, the Believer is in charge of security and bodyguards for Hyrule Castle under the new regime. He reports directly to the Confessor, and is technically on a separate hierarchy from Lakoh and the Sorcerer--meaning, he no longer has to listen to his annoying "friend".
