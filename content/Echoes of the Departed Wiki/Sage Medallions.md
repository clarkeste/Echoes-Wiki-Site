Sage Medallions are objects that, according to legend, were tied to a Sage and awarded to the Hero of Time as a token of their power. It is believed that they were used, in ancient times, to prove that a person had authorization or approval from a Sage. In modern days, sources say that Ganondorf recovered a Sage Medallion from the Lost Woods that he used to enter a Temple deep in the Desert of Doubt. In that Temple, he found a Triforce Shard, before returning to Hyrule and dying by the blade of Link Steadfast.
