Crafting refers to the art of creating something and new and useful out of raw materials. Often, this is with metallurgy to create weapons, worked by a Blacksmith.

Gorons are usually considered among the best blacksmiths, but skilled smiths can be found in all peoples, in all places, from all walks of life. In recent years, with mass production, the production of many weapons of medium quality have been largely streamlined, using manual laborers following precise instructions. However, blacksmiths still find work in constructing unique blades for individuals, or creating new designs that they can sell to a Guild for them to produce.
