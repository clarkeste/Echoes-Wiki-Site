Hytopia is a nation to the north of Hyrule, separated by the Great Hyrulean Mountains. It was, for many years, a country that had strong trade ties with Hyrule, but maintained its independence due to its natural defenses. Hytopia was always a minor power, but its culture--particularly, its architecture and sense of fashion--influenced many other nations in the world. 

However, shortly after the Revolution in Hyrule, revolutionary and democratic sentiment too soared in Hytopia. What was previous just a pipe-dream for the downtrodden became a real possibility. Soon after, Hytopia was overwhelmed by its rebellion. The Rebels made quick progress and destroyed much of the Hytopian Castle City, leaving mines and explosives all over it. However, the Royalist factionr etaliated and began pushing the Rebels west.

During the Civil War, countless villages and forests were charred and burned, with both sides willing to sacrifice the populace to meet their strategic goals. Eventually, as the Rebels seemed to gain the upper hand, Holodrum--who had previously only been supplying weapons--chose a policy of direct intervention, and landed troops in Hytopia. In the last months, they have used their navy to create various landing points, stationing their troops in Hytopias and pushing forward to defeat the Rebels and support the Royalists. Calatia, despite not directly intervening, has also supported the Royalists with aid.
