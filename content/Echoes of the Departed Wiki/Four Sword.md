A legendary blade that supposed creates three phantoms of its user. Myths say it was wielded by a hero, clad-in-green, who defeated an evil spirit named Vaati, long before the era of the Hero of Time. The truth of this, however, cannot be validated or proven, as no primary sources of this event have survived the ages.

Its hilt is supposedly made of gold, encrusted with a green jewel. In legends, it resembles the Master Sword in that it has the sacred power to repel evil.
