Hyrule Castle is a fortress overlooking Castle Town, Hyrule's capital, and is visible from almost anywhere in Hyrule Field. It is the center of Hyrulean power, an icon for many years. Since time immemorial, it served as the seat of power for the Royal Family. However, during the Revolution, it was captured by the Revolutionaries, and it served its masters faithfully, as a grave for the King and the Princess. 

Since then, the Castle has served as the main base of the Follower Revolutionaries. It is arguably the most important symbol in all of the Kingdom. If you control Hyrule Castle, you control Hyrule--as far as many are concerned.
