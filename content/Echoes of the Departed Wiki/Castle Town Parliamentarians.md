The Castle Town Parliamentarians were a group who sought to carry out peaceful change in Hyrule's government, by transitioning the government to a constitutional monarchy in the same style as Zora's Domain. However, a Royal-commissioned investigation found that they were engaging in a treasonous conspiracy, and they were outlawed, their meeting place set aflame and leaders imprisoned.

The Parliamentarians began approximately three decades ago, founded by young activists who sought change and planned to advocate for more democratic government, inside of Castle Town. Over the twenty-five years of its existence, this organization grew larger and larger. A few years before its end, a particularly notable member joined its ranks: Link Steadfast. At the time, Steadfast was only a blacksmith's son, and middle-class at that--but he was unique in that he had been friends with the Princess at a younger age, before being torn apart by duty and safety concerns. Steadfast, despite his violent future, contented himself to the same activities as all his compatriots in the Castle Town Parliamentarians. Along with his friends, he protested, he offered flyers, he sent mail to nobles, and various other activities that could be considered annoying or disruptive, but not illegal.

A little over a year before the Castle Town Parliamentarians were outlawed, Link and Zelda reunited for the first time in years. Whether the Princess felt hurt by Link's belief that her birthright should be taken away is unknown; however, regardless, they attempted to reignite their friendship.

When Zelda's father, the King, caught wind that she was involved with the Parliamentarians, however, issues began to arise. The King became suspicious that the Parliamentarians were attempting to corrupt or convince his daughter, so he ordered an investigation that revealed many illegal actions by the Parliamentarians. They were subsequently arrested. After resisting arrest, their building was set ablaze. Link Steadfast, who was able to avoid the arrests, fled town, and at some later date joined the Followers of Dragmire.

Other members of the Parliamentarians were arrested, with the Royal Constables estimating that less than fifteen people escaped incarceration or identification. Some of those who did, however--such as Steadfast--revealed their true colors by joining the Followers of Dragmire, a group with violence as its main intent.
