The Black Tower is a structure in Labrynna that was created by the country’s previous monarch, Queen Ambi. While it was initially referred to only as Ambi’s Tower, it got a new name by the workers who spent long, painful  hours on the project: The Black Tower.

Why exactly the Queen decided to construct the tower is unknown, and her advisors were giving varying reasons, from security concerns to vanity. Regardless, however, the tower stands strong today, a symbol of military prowess and historical achievements for the Royalists in Labrynna, and a symbol of oppression and tyranny for the Rebels.

The Black Tower, during the current Labrynnan Civil War, acts as an important fortress. At the time of publication, it is under the control of the Royalists, and close in proximity to the capital of Lynna City. While the tower was not particularly open to visitors before, its shroud of secrecy has grown even thicker during the Labrynnan Civil War, with no one in the civilian populace, includihng the author of this book, having much idea what—if anything—exists inside the seeming void of the Black Tower.

Workers toiled long hours, day and night, to build the labyrinthine citadel that now towers over Labrynna, casting its dark and imposing shadow over the nation.
