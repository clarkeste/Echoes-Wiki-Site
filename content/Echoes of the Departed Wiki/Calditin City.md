Calditin City is a city in the Gerudo Desert, currently controlled by the Followers despite overall supporting the Royalists.

Calditin City was built following the Hylian-Gerudo War by Hyruleans who wished to colonize the land they had expelled the Gerudo from. Despite the difficulty of the desert, the superior technoplogy of the Hylians and support from the larger Kingdom allowed them to build a settlement far more impressive and beautiful than the paltry towns of the Gerudo that had previously inhabited the region.

Despite its distance from Central Hyrule, Calditin became a large city. It is a hub of tourism, from those seeking to look and touch ancient, damaged Gerudo architecture that remains buried in the sand. The city has also marketed itself to those who supported the monarchy and felt tired or alienated of more liberal cities, such as Castle Town and Kakariko City.

Due to its geography, Calditin was forced under the control of the Revolutionary government, despite majority support in its populace for the Royal Family. There are fears among its people that the Revolutionary Followers may yet try to invite the Gerudo back into the lands they now inhabit, which many local leaders threaten would lead to a rebellion against the Followers. Many citizens of Calditin, particularly those above the age of twenty, yearn for the day when the Royalists will liberate their city and bring them back into the fold of the Royal Family, with all the security and liberty they believe it will secure them.
