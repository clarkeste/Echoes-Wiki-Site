Nophandus was a man who served over a decade as the Minister of Hylia, a position involving the supervision and approval of thing such as books and newspapers, as well as handling the military, war efforts, and espionage. He was among the longest-serving members of the King's Council by the time of the Revolution. However, despite his zeal, there are records indicating that Astor believed him to be involved with the Followers of Dragmire; such a thing, however, is unlikely, as Nophandus died along with the other councilmen during the Revolution. He was described as hardened, loyal and with an unspoken zeal.

In the final days of Hyrule, Nophandus worked to mobilize the Hyrulean army after a draft was announced. He was present during the Follower raid at Hyrule Castle, and oversaw both the interrogation and executions of the Followers captured at Lon Lon Ranch. While it is unclear what happened in the last day of the Royal Government, it is believed that Nophandus most likely died to an assassin before the Followers arrived and shelled the city. Like the other council members, his body was recovered from the rubble and cremated.
