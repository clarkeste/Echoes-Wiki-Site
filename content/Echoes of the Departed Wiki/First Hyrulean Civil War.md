This entry refers to the first Hyrulean Civil War that occurred shortly before the adventures of the Hero of Time, over four hundred years ago.

The Hyrulean Civil War was a bloody civil conflict inside of the Kingdom of Hyrule. 

Approximately four hundred and fifty years from today--and at the end of this nearly thousand-year long era of growth--the Kingdom was rocked by the Hyrulean Civil War. This Civil War lasted for decades. Various groups, even within the Hylians themselves, sought dominance, with the eventual victors--the Royal Family--forced to ally with neighboring peoples to win a bloody victory. As a result, the country eventually unified to become the Hyrule we know today with its modern borders.

The cause of this war is usually attributed to an attempt by the monarchy of the time to consolidate power from various lords and their domains. Pressured by an economy struggling from taxes and a population with extremely limited movement and rights due to competing domains, the King of the time attempted to reel in the independence of these domains. Despite attempting peaceful attempts, much of these landowners staged an armed insurrection, beginning the war.

Despite this being the cause, it is not what made the war rage on for as long as it did. This dispute was resolved in years; however, other groups, seeking to place separate monarchs on the throne, or extremists from neighboring tribes such as the Zora, or raids from the Gerudo that led to large battles, caused the entire land of Hyrule to be dangerous. The war finally ended when Ganondorf Dragmire--the leader of the Gerudo--agreed to assist the Kingdom, invading the flank of the Hylian dissidents and decisively ending the Civil War. Over the next decade, the King sought to prevent a future war from breaking out by making sure that all lands in the Kingdom, now including the Gerudo Desert, would answer to the monarchy. The Gorons, Gerudo, and even the Zora were forced to pledge themselves as autonomous regions under the Kingdom of Hyrule. With Ganondorf Dragmire having been the deciding factor that won the Civil War, his pledge of loyalty was the most important of any.


However, Ganondorf was a cunning man. Using his status as a decisive ally during the Civil War, he manipulated the monarch into allowing him to usurp the Kingdom. Using his free access into Hyrule, his agents assassinated the King and took over Castle Town. Somehow, Dragmire managed to enter the Sacred Realm and gain the Triforce of Power for himself. With it and his control of the country, he plunged it into seven long years of despair. This destruction resulted in the ravaging of Hyrule Castle and its surrounding city, Castle Town. All peoples were forced to act subservient to Ganondorf; while the King of Evil--as he has been called--claimed to seek new, better lands for his people, the time where he allowed the Gerudo to enter the occupied Hyrule never came. According to Gerudo documents recovered following their banishment, it appears that the land was considered too dangerous by Ganondorf up until the very end of his reign.

Ganondorf's reign was overthrown after seven years by the Princess-in-hiding, and the Hero of Time--a child of the Civil War. With the rebuilding that happened afterwards, the finale echoes of the Civil War were put to rest.

This Hyrulean Civil War which happened four hundred years ago, also referred to as the First Hyrulean Civil War, is distinct from the current Civil War. The current Civil War is happening in the modern day.
