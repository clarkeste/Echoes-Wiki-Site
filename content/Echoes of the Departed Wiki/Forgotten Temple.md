The name ‘Forgotten Temple’ refers to the decaying structure located at the north end of the Tabantha Canyon. While its origins are unknown and entry discouraged due to its derelict nature, scholarly pursuits have explored it and confirmed that it contains the oldest and largest statue of the Goddess Hylia. Other ancient symbols and murals line the walls; however, their meaning is unknown.

The Forgotten Temple has been largely buried under dirt due to time, but its entrance remains unearthed at the north end of the Tabantha Canyon, its large size taking up the entirety of the canyon path.

Archaeologists and other researchers have surmised that the Forgotten Temple predates Hyrule itself. Despite its impressive and long-lasting architecture, it is considered to be in danger of collapses, and therefore it is officially closed off the public and research trips have been unable to explore too far or checked for collapsed pathways, lest they damage another part of the building.
