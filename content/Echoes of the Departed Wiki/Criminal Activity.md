As with any society, crime has been present in Hyrule since the very beginning. While the Hyrulean government and authorities have often been good at busting organized crime, or keeping crime syndicates small, some always slip through the cracks. In particular, in the modern day--with the Royal government overthrown by the Revolutionaries--crime and smuggling becomes more and more profitable and prevalent.

While little is known about the rising underworld--in particular, since it is constantly shifting during the time of writing--it is known that, since the Civil War, smuggling has become far more prevalent, to both the Followers and the Royalists. Additionally, these groups will often accept contracts from the different factions in the war, exchanging money for weapons, or an advantage.
