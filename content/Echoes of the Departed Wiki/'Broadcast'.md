'Broadcast' was the name given to the Confessor and Link Steadfast's message that set off the Revolution.

At the eve of that Revolution, he and the Confessor staged the now famous--or infamous--speech which was broadcast into every water reflection and fiery torch in every major city in Hyrule, where he showed that he had gained the Master Sword. This was the last push the Revolutionaries needed to launch their attack and gain enough popular support to make victory possible.

It is believed the Confessor achieved this goal by having various Follower sympathizers place a magic powder into fountains, torches and other sources of fire and water. Then, using magic, the Confessor telepathically connected with the sources of fire and water, facilitated by the powder, and 'broadcasted' the events into a vision and image anyone could see by looking into the flames. A similar technique was implemented at Lanayru Citadel by the Warden to quickly deliver orders, as revealed in Royal documents that were unclassified following the establishment of the Revolutionary government.
