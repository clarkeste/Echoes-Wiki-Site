Coltari Erbin: 
Coltari Erbin was the third writer to write the 20th edition of the Hyrule Historia. She was the most business-oriented of them, and was involved heavily in the research process. While the other two writers contributed, she was the most important when writing the 21st edition of the Hyrule Historia that released nearly a year after the Follower Revolution.

Gismis Martine:
Gismis Martine was another one of the writers to write the 20th edition of the Hyrule Historia. He was often considered to be the most writerly and scholarly of the group.

Cosmar Carawn
Carawn was one of the three new writers that, a decade ago, wrote the landmark 20th edition of the Hyrule History. Attempting to return to the roots of the early editions of the Historia, they sought to write a new, informative, enclyepedic Hyrule Historia. Carawn was considered to be the most excited in the project, often attempting to weave narratives into the text--a technique previously used by Gemea, but to lesser effect.

Quaru Gemea
Gemea, an independent author, bought the rights for the Historia from Surglan. Under them, two more editions of the Historia were produced. However, Gemea's insistence that they have the final say on these editions, as well as their questionable history, led to the Historia losing credibility. However, editions were still consistently published by ghost-writers, until Gemea gave up the rights.

Planutir Surglan
Surglan later took over the Hyrule Historia following the passing away of Baumber and Makel. He acted as a custodian, and bought the rights to the Historia following the shutdown of the company that owned it. He would edit and outsource the additional materials for a 17th edition.

Lettruan Baumber
Lettruan Baumber later became the project lead, and was a contemporary of Makel for most of his life. He was known to be among the most competent editors and writers of the era, being able to juggle many projects, but with little tolerance for those who annoyed or contradicted his usually sound judgements.

Dylane Makel
After the passing of Eastings, Makel was assigned to to continue writing for the Historia by the publisher entrusted with continuing its legacy. He wrote the eleventh version along with a team, but is believed to have burned the original drafts of the twelfth following a dispute with the publisher.

Sethris Eastings
Eastings was a contemporary of Ertay, and worked with him on various editions of the Hyrule Historia, co-writing the sixth to ninth editions. After Ertay pased away, Eastings' last project was to finish writing the tenth edition.

Vitou Ertay
A hundred years later, shortly after the Flood, Vitou Ertay would write the fifth edition of the Hyrule Historia, using notes from Tubo and Arfrisk. He was described to be mild-mannered, but prolific in his work, using a dryer style.

Minaris Arfrisk
Arfrisk's childhood was marred by the last gasps of the Hyrulean Civil War. During her early life, she worked as a guard, providing security at the Castle Town jail mostly petty criminals. Later, she became an author and co-wrote with Tubo the fourth edition of the Hyrule Historia.

Drazix Tubo
Drazix was an author who, following the early retirement of Toncele, wrote the second and third editions of the Hyrule Historia. He is remembered for his prolific writing, having penned many more texts than just the Hyrule Historia in his lifetime.

Duffan Toncele
A wise author who lived during the era of Hyrule's first Civil War, Toncele was the one to write an edition of the Hyrule Historia. He, as all contributors, are credited with every new edition of the book.
