The Hero of the Flood is an individual who lived approximately three hundred years ago, and who saved Hyrule from the Demon King, Ganondorf, and a torrential downpour that could've eliminated the entire Kingdom. He was a tradesmen from Lurelin Village who, on his first visit to the Castle, successfully dueled and bested a drunk guard who attempted to harass him. During his visit, he was noticed by Princess Zelda, whose unit hired the guard the soon-to-be Hero bested. They grew close, and most historians agree that there may have been a blooming romantic love between them, particularly when the Hero's tradesmen family chose to stay in Castle Town for business reasons. At some point during this, the Hero recovered the Master Sword, but it was kept a secret from the public and everybody except Princess Zelda.

However, after years of this, things went horribly wrong. Ganondorf, the being who had conquered Hyrule a century prior, returned with a vengeance. Quickly after his return, he conquered the Gerudo Desert, recruiting an army of Gerudo who still supported him, and monsters he created from the purest of Malice. He invaded Hyrule and reached Castle Town by destroying any forces in his way, capturing both Princess Zelda and her father, King Daphnes, returning them to the Forsaken Fortress in the Gerudo Desert.

When the Hero of Floods arrived, he had found that the two had died, with Ganondorf failing to extract the Triforce from Zelda. Seemingly believing that only his death could stop the downpour he most likely caused, he allowed the Hero to slay him with the Master Sword.

After this, the Hero of Floods went into a reclusive life. Despite his fame and acclaim, he lost much of what he held dear. The only appearances he made between finishing his journey and his death were when he publicly spoke out against the Hylian-Gerudo war that the King, after Zelda and Daphnes' deaths, waged in response to Ganondorf's return. However, despite his disapproval, the Hyrulean government finished that war to its conclusion. The Hero of Floods is thought to have returned to the Master Sword to the Temple of Time within the Lost Woods, and died a peaceful death.

The Hero of the Flood was named Link after the Hero of Time. As was not uncommon at the time, his full name was simply "Link of Lurelin Village".
