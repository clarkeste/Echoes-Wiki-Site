The Arbiter’s Grounds was a prison used by the Royal Family during and after the Hyrulean Civil War, until it was abandoned during Dragmire’s return. The Arbiter’s Grounds was located in the Gerudo Desert, and built off ancient ruins made by the Gerudo Tribe.

Only the worst criminals were sentenced to imprisonment in Arbiter’s Grounds—almost always, criminals sent to the Artbiter’s Grounds were awaiting trial, execution, or had been given a life-sentence.

The ancient Sages were said to be connected to the site, with there being recorded instances of—when particularly dangerous criminals were tried—the Sages appearing to act as the jury and decide the fate of the indicted criminal. This ceased after Ganondorf Dragmire killed the ancient Sages at the beginning of his seven year dictatorship. The new Sages never attended a trial in the Arbiter’s Grounds.  Perhaps they never saw a criminal that required their attention, or the Sages died after their task was complete, unlike the ancient ones.

Some executions were carried out by dropping convicts into pits with large beasts native to the desert, or various other unusual means. It is believed that these beasts starved or were killed by invading forces when the Hylians were forced to flee during the Flood. The Arbiter’s Grounds was damaged and its prisoners freed or killed, and the facility was left derelict until the modern day.

The appearance of the Arbiter’s Grounds is that of a coliseum, with the interior consisting of a mix of Hylian architecture and ancient Gerudo sandstone, particularly in the lower floors. Much of its interior, especially its bottom half, is likely collapsed and heavily worn. While there are some accounts of daredevils who enter, the accounts of such people are usually untrustworthy and embellished.
