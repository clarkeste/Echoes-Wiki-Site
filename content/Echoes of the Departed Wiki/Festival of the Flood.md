The Festival of the Flood is an annual holiday celebrated on the fourth of Asinober every year. It celebrates when the Hero of the Flood defeated the Demon King and ended the torrential downpour that threatened to wash Hyrule under the waves, forever. It is often celebrated in Castle Town, and features a speech from the current head of the Royal Family.

During the Festival of the Flood last year, Ganondorf made his first appearance in Hyrule. Later in the day, the Followers raided Hyrule Castle and attempted to kill the King--but were foiled by Ganondorf, who saved the King of Hyrule.
