The Commission for the Reclamation of the Golden Power, sometimes called the Commission for the Recovery of the Golden Power, and abbreviated as the CRGP, was a team created by the Royal Family to recover the Triforce in the last days of the monarchy.

Following Ganondorf's appearance at the Festival of the Flood, and when the Gerudo saved the King's life from an attack by the Followers of Dragmire, the Last King of Hyrule agreed to help Ganondorf with his request: Find the Triforce pieces to undo a wicked curse placed upon him, the Gerudo, and all of Hyrule since time immemorial.

The members of the Commission were Zelda's current lead bodyguard, Link Sahon, Ganondorf himself, Inl Astor--who was the Minister of Justice--and Impa, Zelda's previous head bodyguard. Impa had been fired due to suspicions against the Sheikah, but at Sahon's request, she was recruited to serve in the Commission. Despite the capture of his daughter at the same time the Castle was attacked by the Followers, the King told the Commission to focus on recovering the Triforce pieces and leave the recovery of Zelda to his agents. Ganondorf used a strange device he referred to as the Timekeeper to help locate the Triforce shards.

The Commission first traveled to Kakariko City and recovered a piece in the Sheikah catacombs there.

During that, however, Ganondorf had tailed a member of the Followers who had saved one of his own in the fire to Lon Lon Ranch. Who is unknown, however most agree it was a skilled warrior of theirs--likely, Steadfast or the Confessor. Once there, Ganondorf discovered it had been nothing more than a front for Follower operations, and burned the ranch to the ground, trapping the Followers inside and requesting the Royal authorities recover them. It is thought that he battled the Confessor here, but the woman escaped without giving up the piece of the Triforce she stole from the Princess.

Later, the Commission once more united and began their journey towards Zora's Domain. On the way, they found Princess Zelda, having escaped from the shackles of the Followers with her own skill. She accompanied them to the temple beneath Zora's Domain, where Ganondorf claimed about shard hid. Despite Follower attempts to gain the Triforce shard from the structure beneath the Domain before they could, they were foiled. 

The following events have only one source--Impa--as all others on this mission are now dead. Ganondorf took the Triforce Shard despite the warnings of a Follower boy, matching the description of Steadfast. Pulling it out, as the boy had warned, caused the ground the city rested on above them to shake and collapse, destroying large parts of the city. Most engineers believe that the Triforce was powering a device that kept the ground steady, but how is not known. Other observers believe that it was the Followers present who took the piece out first, damaging the Domain. The truth is unknown.

Following attempts to limit the suffering and calling in Royal humanitarian aid, the Commission left Zora's Domain for their next destination: The Lost Woods. Once passing through Minishi Town, the Commission was joined by General Ahoga. Inside the woods, they split up. Following details are scarce; however, we know that Ganondorf entered deep into the forest and recovered a shard of the Triforce, while other members--including Astor, Sahon, and General Ahoga--entered the Temple of Time. After discovering the Master Sword's pedestal was empty, Ahoga betrayed them by killing Sahon and fled.


Hours before the Revolution, as the Commission arrived at Hyrule Castle. The King, believing correctly that conflict was near, ordered most of the members of the Commission to stay and fulfill their usual roles. Ganondorf was the only one who continued seeking the Triforce Shards, heading to the Gerudo Desert to recover the last one. Upon his return, Hyrule was in the flames of its Revolution.
