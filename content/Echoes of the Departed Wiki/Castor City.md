Castor City is a city in the west of Hyrule, close to the Tabantha Frontier Bridge. It is the furthest west large city in Hyrule, founded centuries ago during an attempt to better develop the lands to the west. It was conquered by the Revolutionaries after the Royal forces guarding it fled further west, into Tabantha and Hebra. 

Castor City, as a large city, naturally leans towards change, despite its radical nature. Polls show that the denizens of Castor City support the Followers over the Royalists. Despite this, Castor City is very close to the partisan movement in the country, in the Hebra and Tabantha regions. This group--the Patriotic Royalist Partisans--disrupt Follower trade, supplies, and infrastructure, often destroying important routes like bridges. Taking advantage of the cold, snowy, and misty mountains of Hebra, they avoid and kill the Followers sent to deal with them.
