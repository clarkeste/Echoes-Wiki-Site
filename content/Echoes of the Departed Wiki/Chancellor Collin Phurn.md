Collin Phurn was the Lord Chancellor of Economics up until the Revolution, where he perished--a tragic end to a life-long career in economics and service tp the crown.

As a young man, Phurn worked for various Guilds, before eventually becoming a statistician and auditor. He jumped around various economic jobs in the government. This included: budgeter for the Central Hyrulean region, advising on tax policy, managing and overseeing grain supply lanes, a position in labor-dispute in which he was very successful for many years, master of Guild-government relations, budgeter for the entire Royal government, and many other positions. Finally, towards the end of his long and storied career, he was made a Royal Administrator, directly the Chancellor of Economics. When his superior on the council resigned--which was shortly before the death of Frendell--Phurn was handpicked by the King for his long resume to replace the retiring councilman. Collin became Chancellor Phurn, a member of the council. He served honorably and stably in that position. Phurn became a friend of Princess Zelda in this position, greatly respecting her wisdom and measured nature.

However, as it was for many things, the Followers of Dragmire were the beginning of the end for Phurn. The Chancellor was present when Ganondorf appeared at the Festival of the Flood, and survived the raid on Hyrule Castle. Rumors say he knocked the Sorcerer unconscious during a fight, but such a thing is almost certainly false. Members of the court acknowledge that he was skeptical about Ganondorf and his motives.

Later, despite his concerns, the Chancellor authorized emergency economic measures during the mass conscription. Chancellor Phurn, unfortunately, was at Hyrule Castle during the Followers' attack on the Castle, and died along with his King, and the Princess he admired.
