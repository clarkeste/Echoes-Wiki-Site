Mount Lanayru is one of the taller mountains in Hyrule, and the second tallest in eastern Hyrule. It is a snowy peak near the coat of the country. Due to its rugged and naturally defensible nature, as well as the snowy mist that obfuscates the view of those on the mountain, it was selected by the Royal Family to be the site of Lanayru Citadel, an impenetrable fortress. It overlooks Naydra Snowfields, and the Lanayru Promenade.

Some say that a water spring representing one of the Golden Goddesses, and blessed by the Goddess Hylia, exists somewhere on the Mountain, but there has been no conclusive evidence for this. The legends, persistent still, claim that those pure of heart--when wandering the mist--will eventually come upon it.
