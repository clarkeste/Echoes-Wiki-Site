The Royalists is the colloquial name for the side in the Civil War that claims to be the legitimate government, upholding the ideals of the monarchy. Their current leaders are General Noril and the former Governor of Kakariko; their stated goal is to take the country back from the Royalists, and they have stated that they intend to invite the next-in-line to the throne to the country once they can guarantee his safety.

Following the death of Zelda and her father at Hyrule Castle, the Royal Military was expelled from the capital of Hyrule. The Revolutionaries successfully rebuffed the attempts of General Noril to retake the city, and pushed the General's army across the river to Mount Crenel. There, Noril communicated with other military commanders, and they agreed to promote General Noril as Chief of Staff to prosecute the war. Thus, the Royalist movement was born--with the humble General Noril handling the military aspects, and the Governor of Kakariko--one of the few experienced politicians who still lived and remained loyal to the crown--handling political aspects, such as petitioning Calatia and Holodrum for supplies and equipment.

The ever-increasing theater of battle demanded that he moved to a more central spot; seeing nowhere better, Noril turned Lanayru Citadel into a fortress and headquarters for the Royalist military. Nearby, King's Port serves as a political capital, where the former Governor works with foreign diplomats to gain support. The Royalists have successfully forced the war into a near stand-still, with them controlling much of western Hyrule. Mountains and rivers make it challenging for the Followers to progress, bleeding out the forces of their enemies while the Royalists gather more and more supplies, munitions, and legitimacy on the world stage.
