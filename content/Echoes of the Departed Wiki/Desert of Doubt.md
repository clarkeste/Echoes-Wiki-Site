The Desert of Doubt refers to the deeper, most isolated regions of the Gerudo Desert. It is where the Gerudo were forced to flee after the Hylian-Gerudo war forced them to leave their charred homes. It is usually considered even more dangerous than the parts of the Gerudo Desert closer to Hyrule--now controlled by the Kingdom of Hyrule--due to its distance from civilization, clearer skies, and more dangerous wild-life. In the Desert of Doubt today exists the Gerudo settlements.

Up to a thousand years ago, before they lived on Zunari Island, it is believed that the Zuna people lived in the Desert of Doubt and constructed various grand structures, some of which still exist today.
