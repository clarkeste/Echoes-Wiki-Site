King Harkinian was one of the many monarchs Hyrule had after the Flood. He is most known for being the first King to seriously recognize the importance of relations with Koridai, and peaceful foreign policy, using influence instead of weapons in striving for peace. This is despite his past in the military as a warrior and General before his older brother died and he became the heir to the throne.

Harkinian is also known for reorganizing the Hylian Calendar. The month  “Harkun” is named after him.

Despite his militaristic past and harsh name, Harkinian is usually considered one of the wiser Kings in Hyrule’s history. Despite this, he is still criticized for allowing the exile of the Gerudo to continue. It was also believed he was less harsh on those he knew, usually those of higher status in society, opting for community service in cases of even treason. In his defense, he was more lenient on lower-class criminals as well—although not nearly as much.
