The Warden of Lanayru Citadel, who was formerly in command of the fortress before it was reappropriated as a base for the Royalist movement. Now, the Warden acts as its head of security.

The Warden was reportedly the first person outside of the Followers to see that Link Steadfast wielded the Master Sword. He was present during the raid on the Citadel preformed by Steadfast, the Believer, the Confessor, and some of their allies to liberate the prisoners there. Following his intervention, the Confessor and Steadfast arrived in his office to confront him. Despite being ready to fight, Steadfast showed the Warden the Master Sword. Stunned and not understanding the situation, the Warden allowed them to leave the office.

He has since been pardoned for gross negligence by General Noril, who is now the ultimate authority at Lanayru Citadel.
