General Noril is the current leader of the Royalist Armed Forces. He is a veteran of the Hyrulean Army, and was for a long time a Captain, and therefore the leader of, the King's Royal guard. In the years leading up to the Revolution, he was changed to independent command, becoming a General in the panic shortly prior to the Revolution. He was tasked with leading the armed forces in Castle Town to meet the marching revolutionaries; however, his army was routed due to a sneak attack, and he was forced back to Hyrule Castle.

He was taken prisoner during the assault on Hyrule Castle, but in the chaos, managed to escape and regroup near a garrison outside of Castle Town. There, he took command of forces that attempted several times to re-take the city, before eventually his troops fled across the river and announced that they would continue to fight.

With much of the Hyrulean chain of command either dead or serving in Koridai, Noril was made the Chief of Staff, in charge of all Royalist operations. After months of fighting, the ever-increasing theater of battle demanded that he moved to a more central spot; seeing nowhere better, Noril turned Lanayru Citadel into a fortress and headquarters for the Royalist military.

Today, Noril still leads the Royalist military. Despite being outranked by four generals stationed in Koridai, they and their allies in Holodrum have been hesitant to send them, for fear of assassination by the Followers; and Noril's experience and familiarity with the war currently makes him the best pick.
