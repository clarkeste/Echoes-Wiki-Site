The Confessor is the leader of the Followers and of the Revolutionary government; an individual with seemingly no name or history. She first appeared six years ago when she intervened during the Royal Army's attempt to quell a protest in Kakariko City. She engaged the Royalist Forces and destroyed much of their equipment, foiling their attempt. Bearing the same symbols and appearance as the Followers of Dragmire, she was identified as another Chosen.

Later that week, councilman Frendell, the Minister of Justice who had been assigned to the case, died under mysterious circumstances in his study. Some have proposed the theory that the Confessor was involved in the murder, but there is no evidence of this being the case.

The Confessor spent the following years attacking Royalist targets, and recruiting her own group of the Followers, creating another branch in addition to the Believer's and the Sorcerer's. This branch is considered to have been more liberal, less strict, and more individualistic compared to the other branches of the Followers, and included Link Steadfast as the Confessor's right-hand man. The Confessor and her minions spent many months attacking Royalist forts, transports, and supply lines; often targeting their ability to respond to civil unrest.

In the days before the Revolution, the Confessor fought Ganondorf and the Commission for the Reclamation of the Golden Power, attempting to stop them from gaining the Triforce, likely believing that the monarchy gaining the Triforce would be the end of their revolutionary movement. After being unsuccessful and as the King became more militant, violent, and dedicated in crushing the Followers, the Confessor--perhaps under the orders, or after conspiring with the Pragmatist--began the Revolution. To start this, she issued a 'broadcast' by using magic to show moving images in every torch and watery reflection in every major city in Hyrule. This broadcast showed Link Steadfast, wielding the Master Sword; this was intended to reduce the divine legitimacy of the Royal Family, and it succeeded.

At the same time, the Confessor used her resources and contacts to stage a revolution in Kakariko City that forced the governor to flee and captured the city for the Revolutionaries. Using the city as a base, the Followers--all branches now united, including the Believer's--marched on Hyrule Castle Town.  There, they sieged the city and using spies inside Hyrule Castle, killed the King and Princess Zelda. It is believed that the Confessor and Steadfast were among those inside the Castle, and that the two of them dealt the final blow to Ganondorf Dragmire, who died attempting to protect the King.


In the weeks following, the Confessor became the acting Head of State when stabilizing and creating a new government. Afterwards, she appointed the defector, Lakoh, and--after political pressure--the Sorcerer, among others, to high-ranking political positions in the new government.  The Confessor still acts as a leader for the New Hyrulean government, but her role is becoming less and less important as she seeks to soon host democratic elections, without herself in the running.

The Confessor is still the ultimate authority in the Revolutionary government, however. She knows the secrets of the Royal Family and is a respected member. She is considered to be the Follower leader who has most pushed for democracy and freedom; with Lakoh being too unwilling to make large changes, and Sorcerer too radical and too willing to burn everything of the old government down, regardless of who suffers.
