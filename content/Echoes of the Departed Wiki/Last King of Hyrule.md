The King of Hyrule was a controversial, but oft looked up to man, whose life met a sudden end less than one year ago when he was killed during the Revolution. He is always referred to as 'the King' or 'King Hyrule'. He is only monarch much of Hyrule remembers.

The King came to power in his thirties, following the death of his father, and ruled for nearly fifty years. He was credited for various things, such as criminalizing various fraudulent activities, and, on a separate note, reducing the overall crime rate. He also oversaw the newest wave of increased Hyrulean presence in world politics. However, he was not completely beloved; some criticized his actions as authoritarian, often citing the harsh response to disruptive and sometimes violent, but rarely homicidal, protests.

This came to a head as, in the decades before the revolution, sentiments against the monarchy began to grow more and more. Around this time, the King had a daughter, who he would name Zelda. The girl's mother and he divorced, however, when the King had suspicions that infidelity induced the mother's second pregnancy. The King continued to raise Zelda strictly, encouraging her to be the best leader she could be. However, as the threat of rebels became more and more pressing, King Hyrule became more focused on dealing with them.

When Ganondorf interrupted the last Festival of the Flood the King oversaw, a little more than a week before the Revolution, things began to change for the King. While initially fearing that Ganondorf would be like his name sake, the Gerudo--that is, Ganondorf--saved the King from an assassin during a Follower raid on the Castle. After that, the King agreed to help Ganondorf with what he wanted: finding the Triforce, to undo a curse that Ganondorf claimed binded him, Hyrule, and the Gerudo into endless conflict.


While Ganondorf's team were recovering the pieces, they also exposed a Follower base at Lon Lon Ranch. Taking those captured there, he engineered a public execution. However, it went awry when Steadfast, the Believer, and others escaped, saving many of their comrades who fled Castle Town.

Later, when Ganondorf's team arrive back at Hyrule Castle, the King saw Ganondorf depart for the Desert. Shortly after, the Revolution began in Kakariko City, heralding the King's end. As the Revolutionary forces approached, the King ordered Impa to apprehend the Confessor and redoubled his efforts to find the leader of the Followers, whom he believed was living a double life as a member of his Council. However, despite capturing the Confessor, the King's men were not able to kill her. What happened in the final hours at Hyrule Castle is unknown; but the next day, the King was discovered having died from a dagger stab to the back, right through his heart.

Two days after the assault on Hyrule Castle, the King's body was paraded through Castle Town inside of a transparent coffin, and publicly burned in a central square, before his ashes were spread to the wind, to confirm that the man had died.

The King’s name is King Aldriech Calodmir Hyrule.
