Akkala City is a city in the north-east part of Hyrule, located in the Akkala region. It is located in Torin Valley, straddling Lake Akkala. Nearby, between it and the Follower frontlines, it is protected by Akkala Citadel, an imposing fortress manned by the Royalists.

Akkala City is controlled by the Royalists. As a city primarily made up of Hylians and still under Royalist control, it is more supportive of the Royalists than most other cities. It is one of the larger cities in Hyrule, only a little bit smaller than Castor City.
