Currently, the economy of the Revolutionary-controlled areas of Hyrule are already struggling heavily to the war, with the Royalists fairing much better due to the economic aid received from Holodrum and Calatia.

The international economy is also suffering greatly from the Civil Wars in various countries, the most damaging being Hyrule. Places like Koridai hope the crisis is resolved quickly so that the economic woes they are suffering quickly pass.
