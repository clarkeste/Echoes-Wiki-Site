Kakariko Village is an old Sheikah settlement that was around by the time of Ganon’s seven year reign, and accepted many Hylian refugees. It is located in the north of the country, at the base of Death Mountain.

Since then, it has gradually become depopulated as most of its citizenry moved elsewhere. One of these places is Kakariko City, a new bustling city in far in the south named after Kakariko Village.

The Sheikah who remain in Kakariko Village tend to be conservative, and the town is largely a monoculture. The vast majority of its inhabitants are Sheikah.

Kakariko Village is in the Goron Neutral Zone due to its proximity to Death Mountain, with the frontline not far from the village. While most denizens of Kakariko Village support the Royalists, they still appreciate and prefer their neutrality.

Most of the Sheikah who remain believe that the reason for anti-Sheikah bigotry is disloyal Sheikah, such as the Followers. That there could be harmony if other of their kind were not always seeking to break it. Suffice to say, many of the more liberal Sheikah that live far outside of the Village do not agree.
