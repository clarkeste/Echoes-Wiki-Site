King Arkenalan Bosmison Hyrule was a King that reigned during the latter half of the Hyrulean Civil War. Under his reign, the Civil War ended with a Hyrulean victory. Unfortunately, he was betrayed and murdered during Dragmire’s coup. His daughter was forced into hiding as a result. 

Still, he is known as an important King in Hyrule’s history, even if his legacy is tainted by his naive trust of Dragmire, which led to the seven year dictatorship. He is well-respected for unifying Hyrule into its modern borders, integrating Zora’s Domain, Goron City, and the Gerudo. He is also seen as a symbol of the failures of pacifism, and attempting to pursue peace at all costs.
