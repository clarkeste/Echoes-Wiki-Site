Rito are a sapient, avian species that live in the cold eastern reaches of northern Holodrum. They are masters of the sky, and often use bows. Using their wings--which have finger-like appendages on them that allow them to manipulate objects just as well as a Human or Zora--they can fly in the air, gliding on currents and weaving in and out of various obstacles with precision. They are considered to have the best bow craftsmen among them; Rito bows are considered premium and are in high demand by nearly the whole world. They also fetch an extremely nice price.

While Rito can fly forward, they cannot ascend on their own and rely on the wind to lift them upwards. The art of creating an updraft to soar high into the sky without the assistance of a tool is considered to be a masterpiece of aerial techniques by the Rito.

Rito have poor night vision and struggle to see in dimly-lit places, causing problems in their frigid climate at night. However, their feathers retain heat, helpful for both the chilly climate they inhabit, and for when they fly at high altitudes. A Rito's feathers molt once per year, and the feathers have formed a thriving industry for the Rito who use them to craft clothes for others, or create particularly soft mattresses and sheets. Despite their harsh terrain, the Rito manage excellently, maintaining trade through the world with the help of companies in Holodrum and Labrynna, and surviving fairly comfortably in the cold. Their little corner of the continent is filled with tall, stout plateaus and over upraised platforms, allowing them to weave between massive pillars. Their largely vertical home of Rito Village is on one such pillar.


The Rito are considered to be a gentle but adventurous people. Despite their geographical isolation, they tend to be welcoming of visitors to their home, which has a significant human population, and many Rito travel the world to spread their wings--literally! However, despite this, Rito foreign policy has always valued their independence. Despite strong ties with Holodrum a cultural friendship with the people of Holodrum, the Rito have never agreed to a proposal that they believed could make them a vassal of Holodrum. However, in recent years, Rito and many geopolitical experts in Holodrum and the rest of the world have warned that Holodrum seems to be taking steps in an attempt to monopolize their trade with the Rito, and limit their ability conduct actions without first going through the Holodrumian Royal Family. The Rito have fervently tried to oppose these actions, but often they are left with little choice. A particularly notorious example in recent memory was when Holodrum required that all foreign trade that had a stopover in Holodrum, exit the country from a warm-water port. This has made it effectively illegal for the Rito to continue working with the previous companies they partnered with, and instead work with corporations further south that have stronger ties with the Holodrumian government and monarchy. Additionally, tourism to the colder areas of Holodrum has been further restricted, isolating the Rito.

Despite this, Rito culture remains strong. Rito pass down legends in the form of songs, and have libraries that have remained well-preserved in the cold. Unfortunately, they are not particularly long-lived--their average life-span a little bit short than a Hylian's.
