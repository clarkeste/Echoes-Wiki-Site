Kanadei Holodramn is the current Emperor of the Empire of Holodrum. He is young, in his mid-twenties, but is considered to be one of the most competent and cunning monarchs in Holodrum’s history for those who know him. Despite his young age, he has been Emperor for a decade and has a wife alongside an infant child.

Kanadei has overseen the increasing influence of Holodrum on the world stage—beginning with an aggressive campaign to win over Holodrum, and now to move into Hyrule’s sphere of influence during their civil war. He is considered a smart, quiet, authoritative man, with his critics referring to him as cruel for his policies—but others point to the Civil Wars in Hytopia, Labrynna, and Hyrule as proof of the necessity of a firm hand to maintain peace. He has supported the Royalists in the Hyrulean and Hytopian Civil Wars, and—despite their previous rivalry—is now the number one ally of the Royalists.

The Emperor is equally liked and feared by his people, who view him as a cold but necessary protector who is strong beyond his years, and who is responsible for the success of Holodrum.


Emberline is Kanadei’s wife, or the Empress-consort, or just Empress. Less is known about her compared to her husband, but it seems likely that she and the Emperor share many attitudes when it comes to ruling.
