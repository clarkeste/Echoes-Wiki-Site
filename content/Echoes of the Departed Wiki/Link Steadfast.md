Link Steadfast was a child born to a blacksmith of middling class called Aadin Steadfast. Due to coincidence and the adventurous nature of both children, as well as Aadin taking a few contracts for swords for Royal functionaries, he and Zelda met. Despite reportedly being great friends, concerns over the security of the Princess led to her no longer being allowed to see Steadfast. 

In their early teen years, the two met once more. However, by this time, Link Steadfast had joined a group of dissidents called the Castle Town Parliamentarians. While this did not seem to initially come between their very close friendship--or romance, if the gossipmongers are to be believed--as soon as a report was filed to the King reporting of the clandestine activities of the Parliamentarians and the retalitation was carried over, Zelda ceased her relationship with Steadfast.

Steadfast, with most of his colleagues arrested, fled, unable to take his father. His father fell into poverty despite the support of Zelda and later died. His neighbors, however, reported that his son visited him often, under aliases--not realizing that Steadfast was a wanted man they should've reported.

There are no official or unofficial records of what happened to Link Steadfast during this period. It is likely, as he was a wanted criminal, he used aliases to evade the authorities. However, it is now known that sometime during this period, he joined the Followers and became the right-hand man of one of its leaders, the Confessor. Steadfast was likely corrupted into a cynical man during his time on the run, choosing to ally with a group that wanted to kill a friend who trusted in him, Zelda, and her entire family. He chose the Parliamentarians over our Princess. Ganondorf reported fighting who would later be identified as Steadfast when he discovered the Follower base at Lon Lon Ranch.

On the day of the Revolution, hours before it began, Steadfast was in a raid along with the Confessor and the Believer on Lanayru Citadel to free prisoners, including some of his old Parliamentarian compatriots. At the evening of that day, he and the Confessor staged the now famous--or infamous--speech which was broadcast into every water reflection and fiery torch in every major city in Hyrule, where he showed that he had gained the Master Sword. This was the last push the Revolutionaries needed to launch their attack.

Link Steadfast was present at the Battle for Hyrule Castle; whether or not he met the Princess one last time, or was the one to kill her, is unknown. However, it is known that--alongside the Confessor--he fought Ganondorf as the Castle collapsed around them, only barely managing to slay the Gerudo King.


Since then, less is known about Steadfast's activities. He was present in the early months, still serving as a bodyguard and right-hand man for the Confessor. However, approximately eight months after the Revolution, he has not been seen at official meetings or appearances. Whether he was quietly dealt away with by the Followers or is carrying out another mission, we cannot say. The issue of whether he truly is the reincarnation of the Hero, however, is in great contention.
