The Pragmatist is an alias of the individual who led the Followers of Dragmire during and prior to the Revolution. They were one of the Chosen, the highest rank in the Followers of Dragmire. Their real identity remains unknown, and they seem to have disappeared from history following the capture of Hyrule Castle, their purpose likely complete.

The name first appeared in intercepted letters to and from other Chosen in the Followers. They was cruel, and often cunning. How the Followers of Dragmire came to be, and therefore how the Pragmatist came to wield such power, is not completely known; however, it is most likely that the Pragmatist was an influential individual even before becoming a cult leader, who used various agents and proxies to construct an organization that would carry out his orders, bound together with a radical ideology.

Eventually, over a decade and a half the Followers grew and grew, under the watchful eye of the Pragmatist, who continued to give their lieutenants orders and instructions. 

This led to an attempt to capture the the only Gerudo male at the time--Ganondorf--to complete a dark ritual to revive Dragmire, their worshipped lord. Such an attempt failed, but spawned a dedicated enemy for the Followers of Dragmire, and the Pragmatist themself.

It is believed that the appearance of Ganondorf in Hyrule during the Festival of the Flood expedited the plans of the Pragmatist. They attacked Hyrule Castle immediately, perhaps hoping that the Gerudo would support them; instead, however, Ganondorf killed one of the Chosen, the Sorcerer.

With the King and this new Ganondorf now working together to undo the legacy of their dark lord, the Pragmatist ordered the Followers to move quickly. After failing in their attempts to delay Ganondorf from recovering the Triforce Shards, they revealed their trump card: Link Steadfast, wielding the Master Sword. With this rallying symbol behind them undermining the divine legitimacy of the monarchy, the Revolution began in Kakariko and overthrew the city government. During this time, the whereabouts of the Pragmatist are unknown; the individuals leading the Revolutionary armies were the Confessor and the Believer. However, the King and his councilmen suspected that someone high-ranking inside Hyrule Castle was acting as a spy, and was indeed the Pragmatist.

When Ganondorf died inside Hyrule Castle and the city was taken by the Revolutionaries, further mentions of the Pragmatist disappeared. It is rumored that the Confessor told her colleagues that the Pragmatist had deemed that their goal was complete: that the business of civil war and what came after the death of the King was not his responsibility. Some, however, speculate that the Pragmatist died during the assault, or that they took another name.

The most popular theory accuses Lakoh of being the Pragmatist, and therefore the spy inside Hyrule Castle. However, this seems unlikely, as there is little evidence of dissidence on the part of Lakoh before the final days of the Royalist government.
