Akkala Citadel is a tall structure built near Akkala City, in the region of the same name. It is on top of a small plateau that overlooks the valley path towards Akkala proper, as well as the parade grounds directly south. Therefore, it is in an extremely strategic position to cut off access in or out of the Akkala region. It is currently controlled by the Royalist faction, and is considered to be almost impossible for the Followers to take. It is currently the head-quarters for the Royalists in the north.
