Arandin is a veteran politician. An activist when he was young, he was elected to be Governor of Kakariko fifteen years ago, becoming one of the most well-known and famous governors in all of Hyrule, and had extremely high approval from his citizenry. The citizens of Kakariko had always been more progressive than other places in Hyrule, and Arandin represented that hope for a better future, and was consistently re-elected. He even spoke in support of the Parliamentarians in Hyrule Castle.

However, after he refused to institute a curfew and authorize a program to have every individual's house searched once following a rebel attack, the King chose to remove him from his position. The King ruled that the law that allowed Kakariko to have elections for its governors was not allowed within their Autonomy clause; and therefore, appointed the next Governor directly. The Governor after Arandin was named Kakan Rarurn, and served as Governor for four years up until the Revolution. 

Arandin lived the intervening years as a private citizen, occasionally endorsing certain movements or petitions, but largely staying out of public life--understanding that there was little he could do. 

After the Revolution in his old city threw out his replacement, and soon overthrew the King, Arandin was instrumental in preventing chaos in the city. Due to his legacy, the Revolutionaries agreed to put Arandin back in control shortly after the revolution. As of now, Arandin has been Governor of Kakariko again for a year. The veteran, cunning, but according to his friends, compassionate politician has struggled with his city, but is reportedly glad he is the one entrusted with the city in this difficult time.

However, his position has only been temporary; the intention of the Revolutionary government is to soon host an election in Kakariko City. Arandin has expressed his interest in re-election, but it seems clear he will have competition.
