Ealden Frendell was the Minister of Justice before Inl Astor, who died under mysterious circumstances. He served as the Minister of Justice for many years, responsible for criminal justice and collaborated with his compatriot, Nophandus. As a Minister, Frendell was a member of the Council and one of the highest-ranking individuals in the government. Astor was his protégé, and took over after Frendell's death.

Frendell died shortly after the Confessor appeared to the world by attacking Royal Forces at Kakariko City. While some suggest the two are connected, there is little evidence of this; while details were not released by the Royal government, it seems likely that Frendell died in his office due to a poison, heart attack, or some other damage that does not leave a visible wound or scar. After the Royal documents were declassified by the Revolutionary government, it was revealed there was another individual with him in his office, also dead. This person could not be identified, but the prevailing theory is that Frendell's death was a murder-suicide on the behalf of an assailant whose identity had been wiped. Despite this theory, documents note that there was no sign of a struggle at all.
