 The Great Sea is the name of the ocean of the world. The most well-explored part is to the east of Hyrule, and to the west of Holodrum and Labrynna. In this part of the Great Sea, resides many islands, such as the important trade hub of Koridai. The Great Sea is littered with often independent islands, and is extremely important for international trade. Despite this, due to its massive nature, many parts of it remain unexplored, even between Hyrule and Holodrum.

Individual islands are intentionally not mentioned in the Hyrule Historia, due to the countless number, and their limited relevance to the history of Hyrule, the main purpose of this volume. The exception to this rule are Koridai and Zunari, due to their important relations to trade and the Gerudo respectively.
