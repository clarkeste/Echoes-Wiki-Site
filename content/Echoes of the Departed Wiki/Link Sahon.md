A bodyguard who served under Lady Impa as a member of Zelda's royal guard; he was promoted to being the leader of her bodyguard unit, after large amounts of Sheikah were removed from government. He was close to the King and a loyal member of the Royal government. In the last days of the Hyrulean Kingdom, Sahon was announced by the Royal government to be the most likely candidate for the reincarnation of the Hero of Time and Flood. However, such a reality seems unlikely now, as he died before pulling the Master Sword and was not involved in the defeat of Ganondorf, and was not able to save the Kingdom from the Revolutionaries.


Link Sahon was present during the Follower raid on Hyrule Castle, and attempted to protect Princess Zelda. However, despite his skill, he was little matched for the Follower's warriors, like the Sorcerer, and was unable to reach Zelda in time. Following the raid, the King--on the behest of Ganondorf--created the Commission for the Recovery of the Golden Power, and unassigned Sahon from searching for Zelda, instead wanting him to find the Triforce.

Sahon later traveled along with the other members of the Commission, including Ganondorf and his old boss, Impa--who he recommended for the job. At the Sheikah Catacombs, Impa led her team through the Sheikah church and the catacombs underneath, until eventually reaching an area with various trials. While Ganondorf was distracted with a fire outside the forest, Impa and the others recovered the Triforce Shard.

Later, the team once more united and began their journey towards Zora's Domain. On the way, they found Princess Zelda, having escaped from the shackles of the Followers with her own skill. She accompanied them to the underwater temple, where Ganondorf claimed the shard hid. The Followers attempted to gain the piece for themselves, but were foiled. Ganondorf took the Triforce Shard despite the warnings of a Follower boy, matching the description of Steadfast. Pulling it out, as the boy had warned, caused the ground the city rested on above them to shake and collapse, destroying large parts of the city. Most engineers believe that the Triforce was powering a device that kept the ground steady, but how is not known.

Following attempts to limit the suffering and calling in Royal humanitarian aid, the Commission left Zora's Domain for their next destination: The Lost Woods. Once passing through Minishi Woods, the Commission split up. Following details are scarce; however, we know that Ganondorf entered deep into the forest and recovered a shard of the Triforce, while other members--including Astor, Sahon, and General Ahoga--entered the Temple of Time to find the Blade of Evil's Bane. After discovering the Master Sword's pedestal was empty, Ahoga revealed himself to be an agent of the Followers and betrayed them by killing Sahon and fled.
