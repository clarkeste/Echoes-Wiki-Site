The Hylian-Gerudo War was an event shortly after the Era of the Flood, when the then monarch of Hyrule--the Prince ascendant, after his father and sister were killed by Ganon--ordered an invasion of the Gerudo in retaliation of the actions of their former leader, Ganondorf Dragmire. While the majority of then-living Gerudo did not support Dragmire, the Royal Army rolled into the desert all the same.

Despite being a warrior people used to surviving, the Gerudo were no match for the overwhelming firepower of the Royal army, including their heavy artillery and mass number of troops. The Gerudo were soon forced out of Gerudo Town, the Hyruleans reducing their former homes to nothing more than smoking ruins. Despite significant casualties relative to the amount of enemy troops they faced, the Hyrulean army continued on, pursuing the Gerudo as the warrior tribe fled further and further into the desert.

As the chase continued, the Hyrulean soldiers inflicted severe casualties on the Gerudo. It is estimated that forty-percent of the Gerudo population was slain during these attacks. However, the harsh desert and dunes was a terrain that the Hyruleans were unfamiliar with, allowing the Gerudo to slowly pick off their opponents, and theoretically being able to outlast them as many died of heat strokes, or dehyderation.

The Hyruleans, however, were determined to end the war before the conditions deterred them. The Hyruleans caught up to the Gerudo at what is now called the Dune of the Dead, and barred the Gerudo's escape with well-placed artillery blasts. From there, the Hyruleans killed thousands upon thousands of the Gerudo. Eventually, the Gerudo gained the upper-hand in the battle and destroyed much of the siege equipment, forcing the Royal Army to flee. So did the Gerudo flee, deeper into the desert--what is now called the Desert of Doubt. It is believed that the Gerudo were nomadic for a time, losing more people to the harsh desert, before eventually forming the settlements that they are reported to inhabit now. However, despite the survival of some of the Gerudo, the King was satisfied that they had been taught a lesson, and recalled the troops. The Gerudo Desert was later settled by Hylians, who founded Calditin City. The ruins of Gerudo Town is now a regulated tourist attraction.


During the war, there were attempts by those on the Hylian side to convince the government to end it. Everything from the group that would eventually become the Followers of Dragmire, to the Hero of the Flood, publicly requested the Royal government to cease. However, they--and many of Hyrule--saw only red after Dragmire attempted to flood the world, and wanted revenge on the Gerudo who had spawned him, and who Dragmire had once led.
