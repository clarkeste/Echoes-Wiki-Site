The Six Sages are a group of individuals recruited by the Hero of Time and who assisted him in battling and sealing away the Demon King, Ganon. They were usually notable persons among their people, who had no knowledge of their power and destiny as a Sage until it was awoken during a particularly dire event. They assisted the Hero of Time at Hyrule Castle, and were joined by a seventh Sage, who was none other than the Princess of that era four hundred years ago.

Some believe that the Sages still watch over us to this day, with most surviving text indicating that the Sages become ageless when awoken. If they do, then they likely live inside the Sacred Realm, and a building called the 'Temple of Light', where the Hero of Time first met Rauru.

The following is information about each Sage.

Rauru: Rauru was the Sage of Light, and an Ancient Sage. This means he pre-dates all the others, and was possibly the only surviving Sage. It is believed that Ganon killed the other five Ancient Sages during his seven year reign. Rauru was an old Hylian man, who wielded great magical power--however, his most important trait was his wisdom and guidance for the Hero of Time.

Saria: Saria was the Sage of the Forest, and the first Sage awoken by the Hero of Time. The exact nature of Saria's life beforehand is unknown, however it is believed she was a childhood friend of the Hero of Time. Some believe she was a Kokiri, however that is not confirmed.

Darunia: Darunia was the Sage of Fire, and the second Sage awoken by the Hero of Time. Prior to his awakening, the Goron named Darunia was a mighty and beloved chieftain of his people, who led them through the tribulations of the Hyrulean Civil War and the cruel reign of Ganon. When his people were at the most desperate--starved and trapped by Ganon--the Hero of Time saved them. In the process, Darunia awoke as a Sage.

Ruto: Ruto was the Sage of Water, and the third Sage awoken by the Hero of Time. Prior to her awakening, she was a headstrong Zora Princess, growing up in the difficult times of the Hyrulean Civil War and the seven year reign of Ganon. For the subversive resistance of Ruto, her people, and her father, Ganon froze Zora's Domain. However, with the Hero of Time's help, Ruto was able to free her people from the ice, and awoke as the Sage of Water.

Nabooru: Nabooru was the Sage of Spirit, and the fourth Sage awoken by the Hero of Time. Prior to her awakening, the charismatic Nabooru was the second-in-command of the Gerudo, and Ganon's right-hand 'man'. However, she grew disillusioned and attempted to move against Ganon shortly before his rise to power after discovering of his plans to conquer and dominate Hyrule. Unfortunately, the plan was for naught, and she spent the next seven years in her former headquarters that had been turned into a penal colony. However, the Hero of Time soon helped Nabooru escape and defeat her captors, leading to her awakening as a Sage.

Sheikah Sage: The name of the sixth Sage awoken by the Hero of Time is lost to entropy. However, we know that she was a Sheikah--among the last of the Sheikah operating Hyrule during this time--and was a bodyguard for Princess Zelda, who now protected Kakariko Village. Alongside the Hero of Time, she descended down the Village's well and defeated a dark demon who threatened to destroy Kakariko. She then became the Sage of Shadow.

Zelda: Zelda as the final Sage. She, the Princess of the Hyrule on the run, likely knew of her destiny for many years; however, she was awakened during the final confrontation with Ganon. What she was the Sage of is unknown.
