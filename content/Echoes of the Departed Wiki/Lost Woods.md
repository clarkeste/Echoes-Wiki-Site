The Lost Woods are a mysterious woods in the central east of Hyrule, oft the focus of many folk stories. It is the largest forest in Hyrule, and its shroud is largely impenetrable; its inside is dark due to its overwhelming canopy and blueish fog. Tourists have reported that it is very easy to get lost, and it is suggested to enter with a guide. However, even the guides often cannot pierce fall into the forest. While people sometimes find landmarks, such as the Temple of Time or other ruins, very few people can successfully recreate their journeys and discoveries of certain locations. It is said that the Hero of Time lived near, or inside, the Lost Woods for much of his early life. Legends from this time speak of a protector of the forest, named the Great Deku Tree, and  fairy children that inhabited the forest. These urban legends continue to propagate to the modern day, although there is some evidence in reliable historical documents that some sort of Deku Tree did indeed exist, or perhaps still does.

In the last days of the monarchy, the Commission for the Reclamation of the Golden Power traveled to the Lost Woods to recover a shard of the Triforce. It is here that the group found the Temple of Time, with the Master Sword missing, and encountered one of the leaders of the Followers of Dragmire--the Confessor. Despite the Confessor's escape, Ganondorf and the Commission managed to recover the Triforce Shard and returned it to the Hyrule Castle.

Now, during the Civil War, tourism to the Lost Woods has largely dried up due to it being uncomfortably close to the frontline of the war. Those who do enter report that the forest feels darker than ever, some even saying that inexplicable scorch mark exists on the forest floor. There have been some reports, additionally, of increased dangerous wildlife, and the majority who enter have been logged as permanently missing after not returning from the Lost Woods for months, assumed dead. The official statement of the Royalist movement urges citizens to not enter the Lost Woods or approach it at all.
