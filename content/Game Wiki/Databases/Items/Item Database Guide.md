Welcome to the Item Database of [[Echoes of the Departed]]! This is a section designed to help you figure out what [[Items]] to purchase, and how to find what you’d like.

Firstly, the Item Database is split into three different categories. [[Armor]], which includes clothing worn for various purposes, usually defensive. [[Gear and Equipment]], which are tools usually needed to do a job properly, or make certain tasks easier. And finally, [[Weapons]], which exist to be used in attack, although they may have further utility purposes.

The Item Database Guide is split into three sections: [[Item Database Guide#Starter Items|Starter Items]], [[Item Database Guide#Items by Affiliation|Items By Affiliation]], and [[Item Database Guide#All Items|All Items]]. The first section goes over Items that are particularly relevant or are purchasable when [[Character Creation Guide|creating a character]]; the second lists all Items by their [[Affiliations|Affiliation]], and the third is an alphabetically listing of all Items, separated by their categories.

### Starter Items
Not every Item listed in this wiki is purchasable right off the bat; usually, because the asking price for them is more than the price a [[Character]] gains at [[Character Creation Guide|Character Creation]]. Therefore, we’ve curated a list of [[Items]] that could be worth consideration when starting, starting with [[Gear and Equipment]].

#### Weapons (Starter)
##### One-Handed Weapons
[[Dagger]]
Small and effective, Daggers excel at stabbing and dealing [[Critical Injury|Critical Injuries]]. They can also be thrown, and have [[Swift Strike (Passive) (Quality)|Swift Strike]].

[[Traveler's Sword]]
Simple, inexpensive Sword often used for self-defense, or to fend off bandits and thieves.

[[Soldier's Broadsword]]
An all-rounder blade, designed to be, reliable, easy to use and still be decently sharp. Became common in Hyrule among civilians, despite initially being made for soldiers.

[[Noble's Rapier]]
A luxurious Rapier with [[Defensive (Passive) (Quality)|Defensive]], made for the nobility. It is both for gentlemanly duels, and self-defense, all while commanding respect.

[[Parrying Dagger]]
A variant on the regular [[Dagger]], this weapon has cross-guards designed for blocking, giving it [[Defensive (Passive) (Quality)|Defensive]] 2. Many Parrying Daggers also have ‘sword breakers’ designed to catch and hook onto an enemy blade, allowing you to disarm the enemy.

[[Sheikah Kodachi]]
The Kodachi boasts a sharp blade, and is of significant cultural importance to the [[Sheikah]]. While its [[Unwieldy (Passive) (Quality)|Unwieldy]] rating makes it challenging to use, its low [[Critical Rating]] and respectable [[Damage]] make it frightening to fight.

##### Two-Handed Weapons
[[Traveler's Claymore|Traveler’s Claymore]]
A hefty, if unrefined and inferior blade, with an emphasis on damage.

[[Double Axe]]
A weapon of decent quality; its size and weight focused in the tip makes it hard to use, but it is highly versatile.

[[Soldier's Greatsword]]
An all-arounder Claymore that deals serious [[Damage]] and can be used even by novice warriors. Just like the Broadsword, it became common among civilians after the Civil War.

[[Cobble Crusher]]
The Cobble Crusher is a heavy weapon with a blunt edge, meaning it has a low [[Critical Rating]] but high [[Damage]] due to its crushing power.

[[Stone Smasher]]
An upgrade to the Cobble Crusher, this weapon deals immense amounts of damage, but is very heavy.

##### Bows
[[Hyrulean Longbow]]
A long-range Bow that allows an archer significant [[Damage]] at the cost of being a little difficult to use.

[[Soldier's Bow]]
An accessible, easy-to-use bow that is used in most modern armies have archers engaging in short-range [[Combat]].

[[Swallow Bow]]
An extremely versatile bow that is easy to use, but—if one is skilled—can hit extremely hard, due to its fast rate of fire.

[[Traveler's Bow]]
A basic but trusty bow. While it may be lacking in damage, it is reliable and useful in a pinch.

##### Pole-arms
[[Traveler's Spear]]
A very cheap Spear, usually used in self-defense or as a thrown weapon to attack foes from a distance due to its low price.

[[Heavy Lance]]
An old weapon that once saw use in war. Nowadays, those who prefer a longer range pole-arm at a modest price use it.
Should be lower-priced, compare to soldier spear

[[Soldier's Spear]]
The Soldier’s Spear, now common thanks to the Civil War, is an easy to use, middle-of-the-road, but still effective weapon. Even the most unexperienced individual could pick this weapon up and have a good chance in a battle.

[[Drillshaft]]
A specialized mining device used by the [[Gorons]], the Drillshaft has extremely powerful piercing capabilities that breaks down both rock and steel alike.

[[Hyrulean Pike]]
Used in the Hyrulean militaries, this Pike is long-range and often used in warfare due to its longer range and piercing power.

[[Zora Spear]]
A spear can be used defensively to ward off attackers, and with a maneuverable shiny shaft that can be used to deal non-lethal injuries.

##### Shield
[[Traveler's Shield]]
Sturdy, light reliable, and useful in a fight—the Traveler’s Shield, despite its small stature, can effectively protect against many melee blows.

[[Bulwark Shield]]
Heavy, but made of cast iron. It is bulky and hard to use against ranged attacks, but will repel swords with a clang. Its users sometimes make use of it to slam its metal frame into enemies.

[[Soldier's Shield]]
An all-around Shield that can block strikes from bow and sword alike. It is often issued to soldiers, but is common enough among civilians.

[[Shield of the Mind's Eye]]
A Shield originating from the Sheikah Tribe. Its design decreases blind spots and its wooden frame makes it easy to swing around towards incoming projectiles.

##### Brawl Weapons
[[Garrote]]
The Garrote is a specialized weapon; a thin wire used to strangle victims, and a favorite of assassins who prefer a quiet job.

[[Sheikah Tekkō]]
A weapon used by Sheikah martial artists. They are shaped like horse-shoes, and go on each hand. They can both cut, punch better with their metal frame, and block mighty strikes, giving them [[Defensive (Passive) (Quality)|Defensive]].

[[Spiked Handwear]]
A catch-all for gloves or push-daggers that can strike at an enemy target and cut them, allowing this weapon to have a low [[Critical Rating]] and increased [[Damage]].

[[Steel Gauntlets]]
A strong and mighty gauntlet that allows for its user to deliver resounding punches that [[Staggered|stagger]] and [[Disorient (Active) (Quality)|Disorient]] their enemies. It also serves as valuable hand-protection in a fight, and its superior traction lets somebody pick up heavy objects.

[[Steel-Laced Gloves]]
Simple gloves that have are lined with steel on the inside, causing a punch to hurt significantly more than normal.

##### Utility
[[Boomerang]]
A useful and classic toy-turned-weapon that will disorient targets, and return to its owner when thrown.

[[Hammer]]
A sledgehammer that can be used as a tool and a weapon alike. Its crushing power, while hard to wield, can [[Staggered|knock an enemy senseless]].

[[Whip]]
Whips can be used in a variety of ways, such as a replacement for [[Rope]], or to [[Immobilized|Immobilize]] targets, or to crack it faster than the speed of sound to strike targets.

#### Armor (Starter)
[[Assassin's Garb]]
Allows a nimble combatant to weave between attackers due to increased [[Defense]], at the cost of [[Soak]]

[[Chainmail Armor]]
Significant armor grants two [[Soak]] and can be worn under lighter clothes.

[[Hebra Coat]]
A thick coat that protects against cold climates and can be worn over other clothes.

[[Hylian Calditin Clothes]]
Simple clothes designed to help a person stay cool in the searing desert.

[[Metal Vambraces]]
The favorite of many non-human species, such as the [[Zora]] or [[Gorons]], this armor is made up of scattered pieces of steel that covers areas such as the shoulders, elbows, head, and kneecaps.

[[Noble's Finery]]
While not much in the way of defense, this garb commands respect in most social situations and adds a [[Boost Dice|Boost Die]] to many [[Social Checks|Social Checks]].

[[Runner's Attire]]
Clothing designed to help runners, athletes, and others. It manages sweat and helps a person push their body further for longer.

[[Traveler's Clothes]]
The favorite of the majority of humans who need something more than daily clothing. Provides one [[Soak]] and has additional pockets.

#### Gear and Equipment (Starter)
[[Belt]]
A useful [[Items|Item]] to increase [[Encumbrance|Encumbrance Threshold]].

[[Bomb (Small)]]
An explosive device that can be used once.

[[Bomb Bag (Small)]]
A bag used to store specifically bombs.

[[Bottle]]
The only way to store various important drinks or liquids, such as potions.

[[Container]]
Allows you to combine similar [[Items]] into a container to lessen the cost on [[Encumbrance]].

[[Hood, Stage Mask, Face Cover, Disguise]]
Cheap and useful for hiding your identity, or seeming mysterious.

[[Hylian Glider]]
While not as effective as the [[Rito Paraglider]], this [[Items|Item]] can help when you’re in a… vertical situation.

[[Lantern]]
Useful, long-lasting, and reusable source of light and small amounts of warmth. Comes with oil, but refills are purchased separately.

[[Lockpick]]
Need to open a locked door without having its key? A lockpick allows you to try to unlock it.

[[Pack]]
Like a [[Belt]], increases [[Encumbrance|Encumbrance Threshold]] by even more.

[[Potion Creation|Potions]]
Various different types of Potions can be bought, and are the most efficient way to heal [[Wounds]] or [[Strain]]. They must be stored in the aforementioned [[Bottle|Bottles]].

[[Quill (Medium)]]
When you run out of arrows, having a larger Quill lets you refill your bow with a [[Maneuvers|Maneuver]].

[[Rope]]
An all-purpose tool for adventuring or various other tasks, like tying up a prisoner.

[[Rupee Wallet (Small)]]
Important for all but the poorest citizens, allowing you to hold more [[Rupees]] on your person.

#### My Character is Gerudo! (Starter Still)
Great! That’s a daring choice. Gerudo, by their exiled nature, have some unique opportunities. They can purchase unique Gerudo Weapons. Here are some good ones to purchase at Character Creation:

[[Gerudo Scimitar]]
Somewhere between a dagger and a sword, this Scimitar can cut deep. It is comparable to the Soldier’s Broadsword, but requires more skill to wield.

[[Gerudo Spear]]
A three-pronged Spear, allowing a strike to deal more injuries with [[Prepare (Passive) (Quality)|Linked]]. It’s light, adapted to the desperate conditions of the desert.

[[Gerudo Bow]]
A bow created to hunt what little game existed in the Desert, the Gerudo Bow excels at a distance.

[[Gerudo Shield]]
While still serving a defensive purpose, this Shield is far more offensive in purpose than most in Hyrule, fitting the Gerudo’s style of battle.
